## The US Flipped an AI Kill Switch and the World Noticed

On June 13, the US government's export controls took Anthropic's two most powerful models — Fable and Mythos — offline for users globally, briefly including Anthropic's own foreign-born employees. It's triggered alarm bells in Europe and Canada about who actually controls the AI infrastructure their companies and governments depend on. Now dozens of cybersecurity veterans have petitioned the White House to reverse the restrictions, arguing that defenders need these models more than attackers do.

**Why it matters:** This is the first time a US government directive has physically yanked frontier AI access mid-deployment, turning "AI sovereignty" from a theoretical concern into a live emergency for every country that built on American models.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/15/cybersecurity-vets-protest-dangerous-us-government-ban-on-anthropics-most-powerful-models/) / [AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/anthropic-export-controls-ai-sovereignty/)

## AI Is Displacing Workers While Making Insiders Filthy Rich — That's a Problem

TechCrunch's analysis puts it bluntly: tens of thousands of workers are being laid off in the name of AI efficiency while a tiny cohort of AI insiders is accumulating generational wealth at a pace that's hard to comprehend. The wealth gap isn't just economic — it's accelerating faster than any political or social safety net can respond.

**Why it matters:** When the people losing jobs and the people cashing in are this visible to each other, the backlash won't be abstract — it'll be loud, organized, and aimed directly at the AI industry.

## Salesforce Drops $3.6B on AI Customer Service Startup Fin

Salesforce is acquiring Fin, an AI-powered customer service platform, for $3.6 billion. The plan is to fold Fin's technology and team into Agentforce, Salesforce's enterprise agent-building platform. This is a pure capability acquisition — Salesforce is buying its way to better agents rather than building from scratch.

**Why it matters:** A $3.6B price tag for a customer service AI company signals that enterprise agentic AI is no longer a "wait and see" market — the consolidation phase has begun.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/15/salesforce-acquires-ai-customer-service-platform-fin-for-3-6b/)

## AI Agents Need Passports Now — NewCore Raises $66M to Build Them

NewCore just came out of stealth with $66M to solve what it calls the next enterprise security crisis: AI agents acting autonomously inside company systems with no formal identity, access controls, or audit trails. As agents start doing the work of employees, they're being provisioned like rogue contractors — no HR record, no accountability chain.

**Why it matters:** Every company deploying AI agents is quietly accumulating a shadow workforce with privileged system access and zero identity governance — NewCore is betting that fixing this will be a mandatory spend, not a nice-to-have.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/15/ai-agents-are-becoming-employees-newcore-emerges-with-66m-to-give-them-identities/)

## A Satellite Hunted Down Its Target Autonomously for the First Time

In April, an Earth observation satellite used onboard AI to independently identify and locate a target without any ground-based instructions. Until now, satellites have been dumb cameras pointed by humans — this is the first demonstration of a satellite reasoning about what it's looking at and acting on that in real time.

**Why it matters:** Autonomous target acquisition in orbit has enormous implications for both commercial remote sensing and military surveillance — this capability won't stay experimental for long.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/15/a-satellite-just-learned-to-find-things-on-its-own-heres-what-that-means/)

## Huawei Walks Into the AI Gap Apple Left in China

Four days after Apple confirmed Siri AI won't launch in China, Huawei unveiled HarmonyOS 7, explicitly framing it as the start of the "agent era." The OS is built around agentic AI workflows from the ground up — not bolted on. Huawei isn't just filling a product gap; it's positioning itself as the default AI platform for the world's largest smartphone market.

**Why it matters:** Apple's regulatory paralysis in China hands Huawei a rare chance to define what AI-native mobile looks like for a billion users — a lead that will be very hard to claw back.
