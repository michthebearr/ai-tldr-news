## Authors Are Fighting Publishers Over Anthropic's Settlement Money

Anthropic reached a settlement with authors over AI training data, but the drama isn't over — now authors are accusing publishers and agents of swooping in to claim disproportionate cuts of the payout. The dispute exposes a messy question at the heart of AI copyright settlements: who actually owns the harm done to a writer's work, the writer or the intermediaries who sold it? This could set a precedent for how future AI settlements are distributed across the publishing ecosystem.

**Why it matters:** If publishers and agents successfully claim large portions of AI copyright settlements, individual authors — the people whose work was actually used — could end up with almost nothing.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/06/authors-push-back-as-publishers-and-agents-seek-share-of-anthropic-settlement/)

## The Seattle Times and Newsday Just Sued OpenAI and Microsoft

Two more regional news publishers — the Seattle Times and Newsday — have filed copyright infringement suits against OpenAI and Microsoft, joining a growing pile of media litigation against the AI industry. The suits arrive just as OpenAI rolled out GPT-6 Astra, its most advanced model yet, making the timing especially pointed. Regional outlets are increasingly unwilling to watch their archives get vacuumed up for model training while their ad revenue crumbles.

**Why it matters:** The steady drumbeat of publisher lawsuits is building toward an industry-defining legal reckoning over whether AI companies can train on copyrighted news content without compensation.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/990932/seattle-times-newsday-lawsuit-openai-microsoft)

## MG Ship Deploys AI Route Optimization as Logistics ROI Gets Real

Shipping platform MG Ship has launched an AI-powered module that automates route selection and carrier recommendations across international trade corridors. Enterprise shippers are reportedly seeing measurable cost and time savings, signaling that supply chain AI is moving past the hype phase into genuine operational deployment. The system targets global retailers dealing with the complexity of multi-carrier international freight.

**Why it matters:** When niche logistics players start shipping working AI products — not pilots — it signals the technology is quietly reshaping physical supply chains at scale.

**Source:** [AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/mg-ship-ai-route-optimisation-logistics-returns-accelerate/)
