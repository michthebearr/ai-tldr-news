## Musk v. Altman Closing Arguments Were a Demolition Derby

Closing arguments wrapped today in the most-watched tech trial of the year, with Musk's lawyer stumbling badly — mislabeling co-defendant Greg Brockman as "Greg Altman" and incorrectly telling the jury Musk wasn't seeking money before being corrected by the judge. The jury will now decide whether OpenAI's pivot to a for-profit structure breached a founding agreement with Musk, who co-signed a nonprofit mission he claims was later abandoned for commercial gain.

**Why it matters:** The verdict could force OpenAI to revisit its corporate structure at the worst possible time — mid-conversion to a full for-profit entity.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/931006/musk-v-altman-closing-arguments-analysis)

## Cerebras IPO Pops 108% — AI Chip Darling Finally Goes Public

AI chip startup Cerebras raised $5.5 billion in its long-awaited IPO, and the stock promptly doubled on its first day of trading. The company, which builds wafer-scale AI processors designed to compete with Nvidia, had its IPO delayed for over a year due to national security reviews of its ties to a UAE-backed investor.

**Why it matters:** It's the first major tech IPO of 2026 and a green light signal for the AI infrastructure investment wave — expect a parade of AI companies to test the public markets next.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/cerebras-raises-5-5b-kicking-off-2026s-ipo-season-with-a-bang/)

## OpenAI Is Preparing to Sue Apple Over the ChatGPT Integration

OpenAI is actively exploring legal action against Apple after the ChatGPT integration Apple announced with iOS 18 failed to deliver the subscriber growth and product prominence OpenAI says it was promised. The deal was supposed to put ChatGPT in front of hundreds of millions of iPhone users — instead, OpenAI claims Apple buried it.

**Why it matters:** This would be a stunning rupture between two of tech's biggest power players, and it signals OpenAI is done being patient with distribution partners who don't deliver.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/openai-is-reportedly-preparing-legal-action-against-apple-it-wouldnt-be-the-first-partner-to-feel-burned/)

## Microsoft Is Quietly Killing Claude Code Access for Its Own Employees

Microsoft gave thousands of its developers access to Anthropic's Claude Code in December as an internal experiment — and it worked so well that non-engineers started using it too. Now Microsoft is pulling those licenses, likely because the tool's popularity inside the company got awkward given Microsoft's own Copilot products and its deep OpenAI partnership.

**Why it matters:** When even Microsoft employees prefer a competitor's AI coding tool, it tells you everything about where the quality bar actually sits right now.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/930447/microsoft-claude-code-discontinued-notepad)

## OpenAI Brings Codex to Mobile — Chasing Claude Code's Momentum

OpenAI is adding Codex, its AI coding agent that can write code and operate your computer autonomously, to the ChatGPT iOS and Android apps. The move is explicitly reactive: Anthropic's Claude Code has surged in popularity, and OpenAI has reportedly redirected internal resources — including shutting down Sora development — to accelerate its coding tools.

**Why it matters:** The AI coding agent wars are now playing out on your phone, and OpenAI is clearly feeling the heat from Anthropic's lead.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/930763/openai-codex-chatgpt-ios-android-app-preview)

## SpaceXAI Is Hemorrhaging Talent Post-Merger

More than 50 employees have left Elon Musk's newly merged SpaceXAI entity since February, according to new reporting. Reasons reportedly include burnout, leadership turbulence following the SpaceX-xAI merger, aggressive poaching from rival AI labs, and the fact that liquidity events may have removed a key retention incentive for early employees who've already cashed out.

**Why it matters:** Losing 50+ people in three months at a frontier AI lab isn't attrition — it's a structural signal that the merger created more chaos than synergy.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/elon-musks-spacexai-has-been-bleeding-staff-since-its-merger/)
