## OpenAI Wants to Be Your Banker Now

OpenAI launched a personal finance feature for ChatGPT that lets you connect bank accounts via Plaid — the same infrastructure used by Schwab, Fidelity, and 12,000 other financial institutions. Once connected, you get a dashboard showing portfolio performance, spending patterns, subscriptions, and upcoming payments. Your AI chatbot now knows your credit card debt.

**Why it matters:** This is OpenAI's clearest move yet into high-stakes, high-trust territory — if users bite, ChatGPT becomes a financial OS, not just a chatbot.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/931122/openai-chatgpt-financial-accounts-plaid-connection)

## The Musk vs. Altman Trial Is Over — The Trust Question Isn't

The Musk v. Altman trial wrapped up this week, with closing arguments revolving around a single uncomfortable question: can we trust the people running the most powerful AI companies? The jury now has to decide what obligations OpenAI's founders actually had when the nonprofit pivoted toward a for-profit structure — and whether Musk got burned in the process.

**Why it matters:** Whatever the verdict, this trial has put AI governance and founder accountability in front of a jury for the first time, setting a precedent the entire industry will feel.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/what-the-jury-will-actually-decide-in-the-case-of-elon-musk-vs-sam-altman/)

## OpenAI Goes All-In on Agents, Shuffles the Org Chart Again

OpenAI reorganized — again — this time putting president Greg Brockman in charge of all product decisions. The explicit strategy: go all-in on AI agents by merging ChatGPT and Codex into a single agentic platform. Brockman's memo frames 2026 as the year OpenAI bets everything on autonomous, multi-step AI doing real work.

**Why it matters:** Every major AI lab is racing to own the "agent layer," and OpenAI is signaling it'll sacrifice internal stability to win that race first.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/931544/openai-keeps-shuffling-its-executives-in-bid-to-win-ai-agent-battle)

## Runway Isn't Just Making Pretty Videos Anymore

Runway — the AI video startup that began as a tool for indie filmmakers — has a bigger ambition: use video generation as the path to building world models that understand physics, causality, and how reality works. The company argues that being an outsider to the big-lab ecosystem is a competitive edge, not a handicap.

**Why it matters:** If Runway's bet pays off, video generation stops being a novelty and becomes the training ground for the next generation of general-purpose AI.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/runway-started-by-helping-filmmakers-now-it-wants-to-beat-google-at-ai/)

## AI Is Torching the U.S. Power Grid and the Bill Is Coming Due

Power prices on America's largest grid are up 76%, and a grid watchdog is explicitly pointing at AI data center demand as a primary driver. The problem isn't temporary: the U.S. grid was built for a different era, and the gap between what it can deliver and what an AI-driven economy needs is widening fast. Even Lake Tahoe — Silicon Valley's weekend retreat — is bracing for a new utility provider and spiking electricity costs.

**Why it matters:** AI's energy hunger is no longer an abstract ESG talking point — it's crashing into electricity bills and grid stability in real time, and there's no quick fix.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/power-prices-are-up-76-on-americas-biggest-grid-and-a-watchdog-is-pointing-fingers/)

## ArXiv to Researchers: Clean Up Your AI Slop or Get Banned

ArXiv, the go-to repository for academic preprints, announced it will ban researchers for a year if their papers contain "incontrovertible evidence" of unchecked AI-generated content — think hallucinated citations or LLM meta-comments left in the final text. The policy targets authors who are clearly using AI to generate research without bothering to verify what it produced.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the most concrete enforcement actions any major academic platform has taken against AI slop, and it could pressure other journals and repositories to follow.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/931766/arxiv-ai-slop-ban-researchers)
