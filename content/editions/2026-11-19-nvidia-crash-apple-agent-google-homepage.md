## Nvidia Stock Has Lost 40% — And the Interim CEO Just Said "We've Got This"

Nvidia's stock is sitting around $150/share after a brutal post-earnings slide — a nearly 40% drop from recent highs. Interim CEO Colette Kress closed the Q3 earnings call with a confident "we've got this," which, in context, is doing a lot of heavy lifting. The slide reflects mounting investor anxiety about whether AI hardware demand can sustain Nvidia's sky-high valuation.

**Why it matters:** When the company synonymous with the AI boom drops 40%, it's a stress test for the entire AI investment thesis.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1038063/nvidia-stock-market-price-earnings)

## Apple Wants to Build Its Own AI Agent — and It's Hiring Now

Apple posted a job listing for an engineer to lead development of an agentic AI assistant capable of browsing the web, writing code, working across apps, and calling external services. This is Apple's clearest signal yet that it's gunning for the same territory as OpenAI's Operator, Google's Project Mariner, and Anthropic's computer use. Siri's decade of underperformance makes this a high-stakes bet.

**Why it matters:** Apple controls the most valuable consumer hardware platform on earth — if they ship a capable agent, distribution is already solved.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1038227/apple-ai-agent-engineer-job-listing)

## Google's Homepage Is Now a Chat Interface With a Search Attachment

Google is adding a collapsible AI chat sidebar directly to its homepage, letting users view and continue AI conversations alongside traditional search results. The feature surfaces from a gray bar on the left side of the screen. It's a small UI change with a large symbolic weight: Google is slowly inverting its core product.

**Why it matters:** When the search box stops being the front door, Google's entire ads-and-links business model starts bending under pressure.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1038059/google-search-homepage-ai-chats)

## Meta's Muse Charm AI Gadget Will Run You $300

Meta's Muse Charm — a small screen-equipped device for chatting with its Muse AI agent — is reportedly priced at $300 and targeting a December holiday launch. Zuckerberg teased the device earlier this year but kept the price quiet. At $300, it sits in genuinely awkward territory: more than an Echo Show, less capable than a phone you already own.

**Why it matters:** This is Meta's first real swing at selling standalone AI hardware to consumers, and the price point will determine whether it's a product or a press release.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1038087/meta-muse-charm-ai-price)
