## Jensen Huang Is Out at Nvidia — and Already Angling to Get Back In

Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang stepped down as CEO following revelations about his personal relationships, including one with an executive at a company suspected of routing chips to China. His security chief had quietly departed in August — months before the scandal broke publicly. Now, sources say Huang is exploring a path back to the CEO seat even as the fallout continues to escalate.

**Why it matters:** The head of the world's most important AI chip company was forced out over a suspected China-linked security breach — that's not a corporate drama story, it's a national security story.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/column/1033943/jensen-huang-nvidia-china-spying-scandal-resignation)

## Congress Sees Nvidia Scandal as the Opening It Needed on Chip Exports

Both Democrats and Republicans are pushing new legislation to tighten AI chip export controls following the Huang scandal. Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer is among those demanding Huang and other Nvidia executives testify before Congress. Bipartisan agreement on tech policy is rare — this is moving fast.

**Why it matters:** The scandal gives lawmakers a concrete, politically digestible reason to pass export restrictions that could reshape how AI chips flow to China and reshape the entire semiconductor supply chain.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1033984/congress-export-rules-ai-chips-china)

## Nvidia's Security Team Was Asleep at the Wheel — An Intel Vet Explains Why

A former intelligence officer told TechCrunch that the kind of leak that brought down Jensen Huang — one that runs through personal relationships and casual conversations — is exactly what most corporate security teams are built to miss. Nvidia's security chief left in August, leaving a gap right as the most damaging period apparently unfolded.

**Why it matters:** This isn't just an Nvidia problem: virtually every major AI company has the same blind spot, and their chips are just as strategically valuable to adversaries.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/where-was-nvidias-security-team/)

## 115,000 Signatures Want Jensen Huang Back — Including Elon Musk

A public petition calling on Nvidia's board to reinstate Jensen Huang crossed 115,000 signatures by Saturday, with Elon Musk and other prominent tech executives among the signatories. The tech industry's reaction has been notably sympathetic toward Huang rather than alarmed by the security implications.

**Why it matters:** Silicon Valley rallying around a CEO ousted amid a China chip-smuggling scandal says a lot about how the industry weighs personal loyalty against geopolitical risk.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/elon-musk-other-tech-execs-sign-petition-calling-on-nvidia-to-reinstate-jensen-huang/)

## China's Self-Driving Industry Is Already Building Its Nvidia Backup Plan

With Nvidia's leadership in chaos and export restrictions looming, China's autonomous vehicle sector is accelerating efforts to find alternative chip suppliers. TechCrunch Mobility reports that Chinese AV companies have been quietly stress-testing contingency plans for a world without Nvidia hardware access.

**Why it matters:** The Huang scandal may have just turbocharged China's push for domestic AI chip independence — the exact opposite of what export controls are meant to achieve.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/15/techcrunch-mobility-the-nvidia-backup-plan-for-chinas-self-driving-industry/)

## Apple's Smart Glasses Are Real, and They're Coming to WWDC

Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reports Apple's first smart glasses — described as slightly thinner than Meta's Ray-Ban glasses — will be announced at WWDC in June 2027. The first model won't have augmented reality features, positioning it squarely against Meta's current hardware rather than the Vision Pro tier.

**Why it matters:** Apple entering the smart glasses market validates the category and sets up a direct hardware war with Meta at a price point millions of consumers might actually consider.
