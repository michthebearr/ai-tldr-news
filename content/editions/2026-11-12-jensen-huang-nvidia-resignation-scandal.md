## Jensen Huang Is Out. Nvidia Has Never Done This Before.

Nvidia CFO Colette Kress is now interim CEO after Jensen Huang resigned following reports of multiple affairs — including one with a woman suspected of being a Chinese intelligence asset. The board moved fast: the New York Times broke the initial story just days ago, two more affairs emerged in rapid succession, and Huang agreed to testify before Congress after lawmakers threatened to subpoena him. For a company with no succession plan and a founder who'd run it for 33 years, this is not a small bump.

**Why it matters:** Huang wasn't just Nvidia's CEO — he was its chief lobbyist, open-source advocate, and strategic visionary; losing him mid-AI-arms-race creates a leadership vacuum at the worst possible moment for the industry.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huangs-affair-with-alleged-chinese-spy-a-timeline/)

## The National Security Angle Is the Part Everyone Should Be Watching

The affair that triggered Huang's exit wasn't just a corporate governance problem — his alleged closeness with a suspected Chinese spy raises real questions about whether sensitive information about Nvidia's chip roadmap, export strategies, or government relationships was ever compromised. Congress clearly thinks it's serious enough to demand testimony. The U.S.-China chip war has been the defining geopolitical tech battle of the decade, and Nvidia sits at its center.

**Why it matters:** If there's any substance to the espionage angle, this stops being a scandal and starts being a national security incident with consequences for U.S. AI policy and chip export controls.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1032069/jensen-huang-resignation-affair-chips-smuggling)

## Hugging Face's Future Under Nvidia Just Got Murky

Huang was the driving force behind Nvidia's $12.9 billion acquisition of Hugging Face and its public commitment to open-source AI. With him gone, Hugging Face CEO Clem Delangue took to the internet to pledge he'd keep fighting for open source — which reads less like a victory lap and more like a man nervous about what the new regime thinks of his company. Nvidia's new interim leadership has no track record on open-source advocacy.

**Why it matters:** Huang's exit could quietly kill the most influential open-source AI platform's independence, chilling the entire ecosystem of researchers and startups that depend on it.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/hugging-face-ceo-vows-to-keep-fighting-for-open-source-ai-after-jensen-huangs-exit/)

## Google Drops Gemini 4 Hydrogen Into Search

Google upgraded its AI Mode in Search with Gemini 4 Hydrogen, its latest model, which is designed to handle genuinely complex, multi-part queries — the kind where current AI search falls flat. The update also surfaces more relevant links and richer visual responses. It's available today in Search, the Gemini app, Google AI Studio, and via API.

**Why it matters:** Google is quietly making "AI search" actually useful for hard questions, which is the main thing that's kept power users on traditional search — and on competitors like Perplexity.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/googles-latest-search-upgrade-promises-better-answers-to-your-most-complicated-questions/)

## SoftBank Lost $3 Billion Betting on Natural Gas for AI Data Centers

SoftBank pre-ordered a massive fleet of natural gas turbines to power AI data centers and is now sitting on a $3+ billion loss as that bet unraveled. The implications stretch beyond SoftBank's balance sheet — it signals that the "just burn more gas" approach to solving AI's power hunger has real financial risk, and may slow the aggressive energy buildout that hyperscalers have been banking on.

**Why it matters:** This is a cautionary tale that could make investors and operators far more conservative about locking in fossil fuel infrastructure for AI — accelerating the pivot toward nuclear and renewables by necessity, not virtue.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/softbanks-billion-dollar-loss-may-have-put-a-cap-on-ais-natural-gas-ambitions/)

## United Airlines Tells AI Agents: You're Not Welcome Here

United Airlines announced it will not allow AI agents to book flights or manage reservations on behalf of customers, citing cybersecurity risks. It's one of the first major public pushbacks from an incumbent industry against agentic AI — and sets a precedent other travel, finance, and retail companies will likely watch closely.

**Why it matters:** As AI agents become capable of autonomous action across the web, the question of which gatekeepers will block them — and on what grounds — will define how much of the economy agents can actually touch.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/united-airlines-blocks-ai-agents-from-booking-flights-citing-cybersecurity-risks/)
