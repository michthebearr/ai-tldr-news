## Jensen Huang Is Back at Nvidia — and It Took a President to Make It Happen

After resigning over reports of multiple affairs — including one with an alleged Chinese spy — Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has been reinstated, thanks in no small part to pressure from President Trump and VP JD Vance. The board that accepted his resignation apparently didn't have much choice once the White House started making calls. Huang is back in the leather jacket like nothing happened.

**Why it matters:** When the most powerful chip company on the planet has its leadership decisions made by the Oval Office, the line between private industry and government in the AI race has effectively disappeared.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1030422/nvidia-jensen-huang-ai-china-alice-huang)

## AI Data Centers Are a Wrench in the Global Climate Machine

Delegates at the UN's COP31 climate summit in Antalya couldn't escape a growing contradiction: the clean energy transition requires electrifying everything, but AI data centers are gobbling up power faster than renewables can scale. The surge in AI infrastructure is forcing climate negotiators to rethink electricity demand projections that were already ambitious.

**Why it matters:** AI's energy appetite isn't an abstract future problem — it's actively complicating the global agreements we need to avoid catastrophic warming right now.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/1040497/ai-data-centers-electricity-un-climate-summit)

## Ladybird Is the First New Browser Engine in Years — and You Can Test It Now

After four-plus years of development, Ladybird — a browser with a completely from-scratch engine — has launched in alpha on macOS and Linux. Developer Andreas Kling built it independent of any Big Tech backing, making it the first genuinely new browser engine to reach this milestone in a long time. Windows support is planned for later stages.

**Why it matters:** Browser engine diversity has been collapsing for years; a credible independent engine matters for the long-term health of the open web.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1040872/ladybird-indie-browser-engine-alpha-testing)

## The White House Engineered Jensen Huang's Nvidia Return

The backstory on how Huang got his CEO title back is even wilder than the headline: Trump floated Huang's reinstatement publicly on a radio show, signaling to Nvidia's board which way the wind was blowing before the decision was formalized. For a company whose chips are central to US AI strategy and export controls, the government clearly views Huang as too strategically important to let go.

**Why it matters:** This sets a precedent where federal AI priorities can override corporate governance at the country's most critical semiconductor company.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1040652/jensen-huang-nvidia-trump-reinstated)

## Kagi Is Building a Privacy-First Email to Take on Gmail

Kagi — the paid, ad-free search engine that's quietly built a loyal following — is now launching a public beta of Kagi Mail. You get an @kagimail.com address, unlimited aliases, custom domain support, and a hard promise of no surveillance and no ads. It's another brick in Kagi's anti-Google stack, which already includes search and the Orion browser.

**Why it matters:** Kagi is one of the few companies actually trying to build a coherent privacy-respecting alternative to Google's entire product suite, and email is the hardest piece of that puzzle.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1041000/kagi-mail-email-google-gmail-alternative)

## Opinion: Jensen Huang's Reinstatement Is Elite Impunity, Plain and Simple

The Verge's editorial take on the Huang saga cuts straight to it: a CEO who resigned under a cloud of scandal is back in power because the sitting US government wanted him there, free market principles be damned. **Why it matters:** If AI's most powerful executives are effectively government-protected assets, accountability in the industry becomes structurally impossible.
