## AI Inference Is Minting Giants: Fal Just Hit a $16B Valuation

Five-year-old inference startup Fal raised $1.3B led by Bessemer, doubling its valuation as demand for running AI models at scale continues to outpace supply. Fal sits in the picks-and-shovels layer of the AI stack — the pipes that actually serve model outputs to applications — a segment that's growing as every company scrambles to ship AI features.

**Why it matters:** Inference infrastructure is quietly becoming one of the most valuable layers of the AI stack, and Fal's doubling valuation in a single round signals investors think demand has much further to run.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/ai-inference-startup-fal-doubles-its-valuation-to-16b-as-demand-surges/)

## Anthropic's Compute Costs Are Getting Worse, Not Better

At a stage where scaling efficiencies should be kicking in, Anthropic's compute margins are moving in the wrong direction. The company is spending more per unit of output even as it grows — a troubling sign for a business that needs massive revenue to justify its eye-watering infrastructure spend.

**Why it matters:** If Anthropic can't bend its compute cost curve, it faces a structurally brutal business regardless of how good its models get.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/anthropic-is-still-grappling-with-the-economics-of-compute/)

## ChatGPT Now Handles 20% of Google's Search Volume

OpenAI's consumer product lead announced at a press event that ChatGPT search queries now equal 20% of the volume processed by Google — a milestone that would have sounded absurd two years ago. The company defines a "search" as any query a user inputs that results in a web lookup, meaning the comparison isn't apples-to-apples, but the trajectory is undeniable.

**Why it matters:** "Ask Chat" is genuinely replacing "Google it" for a meaningful slice of users, and this is the first hard number that quantifies just how much ground Google has ceded.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1025482/openai-says-chatgpt-is-catching-up-to-google-in-search)

## Google Tells Schools: Disable AI or Lose Search Entirely

Google updated its policy for educational accounts to allow schools to restrict AI tools — but there's a catch. Because AI is now baked directly into Google Search, schools that opt out of AI features lose access to Search altogether. It's an all-or-nothing deal that puts administrators in an impossible position.

**Why it matters:** This is a preview of how deeply AI bundling will reshape platform leverage — Google is essentially forcing schools to accept AI or abandon one of the most used tools in education.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/google-will-let-schools-disable-its-ai-tools-but-not-without-sacrificing-search/)

## CrowdStrike Drops $500M on Two AI Security Startups

CrowdStrike is acquiring Fiddler AI (AI agent observability) and Dreadnode (automated AI security testing) for nearly $500M combined. The move is a direct bet that securing AI agents is the next major frontier in cybersecurity — and that companies need dedicated tooling to monitor and red-team their AI systems before attackers do it for them.

**Why it matters:** As agentic AI proliferates, the attack surface explodes — CrowdStrike is positioning early in what could be a massive new security category.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/crowdstrike-to-shell-out-nearly-500m-for-2-ai-security-startups-as-the-market-for-securing-agents-takes-off/)

## Apple Opens Siri to Third-Party Apps — and Locks Down AI Companions

Two Apple stories worth watching together: iOS 27.3 now lets developers integrate Siri AI across all third-party apps, giving the assistant personal context, natural language understanding, and on-screen awareness to complete tasks anywhere. Separately, Apple updated App Store rules to require 18+ ratings for AI companions and dating apps offering romantic or sexual content.

**Why it matters:** Apple is simultaneously expanding Siri's reach as a platform and drawing a hard line on how AI relationship products can operate — both moves will reshape the third-party AI app ecosystem on iOS.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1025694/apple-siri-ai-third-party-apps) | [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/apple-restricts-dating-apps-and-ai-companions-to-users-18-and-up/)
