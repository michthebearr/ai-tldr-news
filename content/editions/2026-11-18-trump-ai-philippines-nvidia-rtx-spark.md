## Trump Wants the Philippines to Be America's Next AI Colony

During a state visit to Manila, Trump announced a deal with the Philippines centered on building US-backed AI infrastructure — data centers, chips, and the whole stack — under a venture called Pax Silica. It's framed as a bilateral tech partnership, but the substance is American companies planting AI capacity in Southeast Asia with geopolitical cover. The Philippines gets investment; the US gets another node in its AI supply chain far from China.

**Why it matters:** The race to lock up AI infrastructure globally is accelerating, and the US is now explicitly using presidential diplomacy to do it.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/1036749/trump-ai-chip-data-center-us-philippines-pax-silica)

## Nvidia Makes $8,300 in Pure Profit Every Single Second

The Verge published a data visualization piece on Nvidia's staggering financial ascent — $8,300 in profit per second, with revenue charts that look less like a business and more like a rocket trajectory. The numbers reflect Nvidia's stranglehold on AI training and inference hardware, a position that hasn't meaningfully loosened despite AMD and Intel pushing hard.

**Why it matters:** Nvidia isn't just winning the AI chip war — it's lapping the field so fast that the concept of competition feels theoretical at this point.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1035924/nvidia-visualized-revenue-profit-earnings-after-huang)

## Nvidia RTX Spark Mini PCs Are Now Shipping From Asus, Dell, and MSI

The RTX Spark N1X chip — Nvidia's compact powerhouse combining a 6144-core Blackwell GPU with a 20-core Grace CPU — is now inside desktop mini PCs from three major OEMs. Acer, Gigabyte, and HP have more Spark desktops coming, and Microsoft's Surface RTX Spark Dev Box ships this month too. This follows RTX Spark laptops that launched earlier this year.

**Why it matters:** RTX Spark brings serious local AI compute to the desktop form factor, which could shift how developers and prosumers think about running models without the cloud.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1036880/nvidia-rtx-spark-ai-mini-pc-desktops)

## Congress Is Coming for Deepfake Creators and Forced Arbitration Clauses

With weeks left in the legislative calendar, a Senate Judiciary subcommittee held hearings targeting two related issues: deepfake harms and the platforms that bury victims in forced arbitration to prevent lawsuits. Roblox was singled out as a specific offender on the arbitration front. The proposed Defiance Act would strip platforms of that shield for certain online harms, including AI-generated content.

**Why it matters:** If it passes, the Defiance Act would be one of the first laws with real teeth against deepfake creators and the platforms that protect them.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/1036935/congress-forced-arbitration-deepfakes-defiance-act)
