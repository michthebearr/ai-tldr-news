## Apple Finally Fixes Siri (For Real This Time)

WWDC 2026 is here, and Apple's marquee AI story is the long-delayed Siri overhaul — now powered in part by Google Gemini. The revamp has been two years in the making since its WWDC 2024 announcement, and Apple is also rolling out updates to Apple Intelligence across iOS 27 and macOS 27 Golden Gate. New CEO John Ternus is staking his first major keynote on making Apple's AI story feel coherent after years of playing catch-up.

**Why it matters:** If Apple actually ships a competent Siri this cycle, it puts AI assistance in front of a billion-plus iPhone users overnight — dwarfing the install base of every standalone AI app combined.

As OpenAI, Anthropic, and others eye IPOs, analysts expect API token prices to rise sharply. ## Meta's AI Chatbot Had a Bug That Let Hackers Steal 20,000 Instagram Accounts

A bug in Meta's AI support chatbot allowed attackers to hijack over 20,000 Instagram accounts — no two-factor authentication required. Hackers simply asked the chatbot to trigger a password reset, and it complied. Meta confirmed the breach in a filing with the state of Maine and is blaming it on a bug, not the model itself.

**Why it matters:** This is a textbook example of why giving AI systems the ability to take account actions — not just answer questions — requires airtight security review before deployment.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/945658/meta-ai-support-chatbot-exploit-instagram-accounts)

## Google Upgrades NotebookLM With Gemini 3.5 and a Cloud Computer

Google's NotebookLM is getting an across-the-board upgrade: Gemini 3.5 is now the default model, promising more accurate responses. The update also lets users build a source repository directly from chat conversations and adds a cloud computer feature for handling more complex research tasks.

**Why it matters:** NotebookLM is quietly becoming Google's most compelling consumer AI product — and swapping in Gemini 3.5 keeps it ahead of competing research tools from Perplexity and OpenAI.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/944325/google-notebooklm-ai-gemini-update)

## London's Robotaxi War Is On: Uber, Waymo, and Wayve Are All Showing Up

The UK capital is becoming the next major battleground for autonomous vehicles. Uber has opened an interest list for Wayve robotaxi rides, while Waymo is also preparing a London push — setting up a three-way fight for one of the world's most complex urban driving environments.

**Why it matters:** London's dense streets, unpredictable traffic, and strict regulatory environment make it a genuine stress test — whoever cracks it gains a massive credibility boost for global expansion.
