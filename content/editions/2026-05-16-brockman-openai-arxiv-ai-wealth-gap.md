## Greg Brockman Is Back — and Now Running OpenAI's Product Vision

OpenAI co-founder Greg Brockman has taken charge of product strategy in the company's latest internal reshuffling. The move coincides with reports that OpenAI plans to merge ChatGPT and its coding product Codex into a single unified offering.

**Why it matters:** Brockman stepping into a product leadership role signals OpenAI is consolidating power at the top just as competition heats up — and folding Codex into ChatGPT could be a play to own the full developer workflow.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/openai-co-founder-greg-brockman-reportedly-takes-charge-of-product-strategy/)

## ArXiv Will Ban You for a Year if You Let AI Write Your Paper

The preprint research repository ArXiv is cracking down hard on AI-generated content: authors caught letting LLMs do all the writing face a one-year ban from submitting. The policy targets wholesale AI authorship, not AI-assisted editing or grammar checks.

**Why it matters:** ArXiv is the backbone of AI and ML research publishing — if this holds, it sets a precedent that could ripple across academic publishing just as AI writing tools become table stakes in research workflows.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/research-repository-arxiv-will-ban-authors-for-a-year-if-they-let-ai-do-all-the-work/)

## Cerebras Nearly Went Broke Building a Chip Everyone Said Was Impossible

Before its $60B IPO — the biggest tech listing of 2026 so far — Cerebras Systems was burning $8 million a month and nearly ran out of road. The company bet everything on a wafer-scale chip that most of the industry thought couldn't be built, and almost didn't survive long enough to prove them wrong.

**Why it matters:** The Cerebras origin story is a reminder that the AI chip winners weren't obvious bets — and that the enormous capital requirements of frontier hardware mean most competitors won't get a second chance if they stumble.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/60b-ai-chip-darling-cerebras-almost-died-early-on-burning-8m-a-month/)

## The AI Gold Rush Has a Serious Inequality Problem

Even inside the tech industry, the mood around AI's economic boom is souring. A new TechCrunch analysis finds a sharp divide forming between companies and workers who are cashing in and those being displaced or left behind — and the gap is widening faster than most predicted.

**Why it matters:** When the vibes turn negative even among tech insiders — not just critics — it's a signal that the political and regulatory backlash against AI's economic disruption is about to get louder.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/the-haves-and-have-nots-of-the-ai-gold-rush/)

## YouTube Is Rolling Out AI Deepfake Detection to All Adults

YouTube is expanding its AI likeness detection tool — previously limited — to all users over 18. The feature uses a selfie-style face scan to monitor the platform for potential deepfakes, then alerts the user and gives them the option to request removal.

**Why it matters:** Scaling deepfake detection to hundreds of millions of users is a meaningful defensive move, but it also hands YouTube enormous new biometric data — raising real questions about what happens to those face scans long-term.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/news/931884/youtube-likeness-detection-ai-deepfake-expansion-all-adults)

## Menlo's Anthropic-Linked Fund Backs AI Marketing Platform Nectar Social

Nectar Social, which bills itself as an AI-powered "marketing operating system," raised a $30M Series A led by Menlo Ventures through its Anthology Fund — the vehicle Menlo created specifically alongside Anthropic. The round signals continued investor appetite for vertical AI apps built on top of frontier models.

**Why it matters:** The Anthology Fund's involvement shows Anthropic's ecosystem strategy in action: backing application-layer companies that drive Claude usage, mirroring what OpenAI has done with its own portfolio bets.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/marketing-operating-system-nectar-social-raises-30m-series-a-in-round-led-by-menlo/)
