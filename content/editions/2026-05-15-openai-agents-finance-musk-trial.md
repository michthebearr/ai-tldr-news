## OpenAI Bets the Company on AI Agents, Merges ChatGPT and Codex

OpenAI is reorganizing again — this time with purpose. President Greg Brockman is now the official head of all product, and the company is merging ChatGPT and Codex into a single agentic platform. The strategy is simple and loud: agents are the only product that matters in 2026.

**Why it matters:** This is OpenAI making a structural bet, not just a roadmap slide — if the agent wars are real, they just picked their general.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/931544/openai-keeps-shuffling-its-executives-in-bid-to-win-ai-agent-battle)

## ChatGPT Now Wants Access to Your Bank Account

OpenAI launched a personal finance feature inside ChatGPT that lets users connect their bank accounts and investment portfolios. Once linked, you get a dashboard covering spending, subscriptions, upcoming payments, and portfolio performance — all queryable in plain English.

**Why it matters:** This moves OpenAI from answering questions about money to sitting inside your financial life, which is a very different trust relationship — and a very different regulatory exposure.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-personal-finance-will-let-you-connect-bank-accounts/)

## The Musk v. Altman Trial Is Over, and the Real Question Was Always Trust

The Elon Musk vs. Sam Altman trial wrapped this week, with closing arguments that kept returning to one core question: are the people running the most powerful AI labs actually trustworthy? The case has been as much a public referendum on AI governance as a legal dispute.

**Why it matters:** Whatever the verdict, the trial has put AI leadership accountability in front of judges, journalists, and the public in a way no congressional hearing ever managed.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/podcast/the-openai-trial-wraps-up-and-the-musk-founder-machine-keeps-spinning/)

## Runway Isn't Just Making Videos — It's Building a World Model

AI video startup Runway has a bigger pitch than "text-to-video." The company believes video generation is the fastest path to true world models — AI that understands how physical reality works. They're positioning their outsider status (no Google, no OpenAI lineage) as a strategic advantage, not a handicap.

**Why it matters:** If Runway is right that video is the training ground for world models, the race for general AI might be won in Hollywood, not a research lab.

## ArXiv Will Ban Researchers Who Submit AI Slop

ArXiv is done playing nice. The preprint platform announced it will ban researchers for one year if their papers contain "incontrovertible evidence" of unchecked LLM output — think hallucinated citations or LLM meta-comments left in the text. The policy is about accountability, not banning AI assistance outright.

**Why it matters:** Science's most important rapid-publication venue drawing a hard line on AI carelessness sets a precedent that peer-reviewed journals will feel pressure to follow.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/931766/arxiv-ai-slop-ban-researchers)

## America's Biggest Power Grid Is Up 76% — and AI Is a Prime Suspect

A grid watchdog is pointing fingers at surging AI data center demand after power prices jumped 76% on the U.S.'s largest electricity grid. The core problem: the grid was designed for a pre-AI economy and the gap between supply and what the industry now needs is accelerating faster than new capacity can be built.

**Why it matters:** AI's energy crisis is no longer abstract — it's showing up in electricity bills, and the infrastructure timeline to fix it is measured in decades, not quarters.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/power-prices-are-up-76-on-americas-biggest-grid-and-a-watchdog-is-pointing-fingers/)
