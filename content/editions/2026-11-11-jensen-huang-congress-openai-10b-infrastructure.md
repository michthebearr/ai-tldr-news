## Jensen Huang Has Until Midnight to Answer Congress on the Alleged Chinese Spy Affair

House Republicans have given Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang a hard deadline to agree to a closed-door briefing over his relationship with an alleged Chinese spy. The scandal has already rattled Nvidia's board and left corporate governance experts calling it unprecedented — one said flatly that nobody has seen anything like this unfold at a company of Nvidia's scale. A separate report suggests Huang's behavior may be an open secret, with senior industry figures aware and "effectively condoning it."

**Why it matters:** The world's most important chip company — the one underpinning the entire AI infrastructure buildout — is now entangled in a national security investigation at the worst possible time.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/congress-to-call-jensen-huang-in-to-answer-for-alleged-chinese-spy-affair/)

## OpenAI Just Posted Its First $10B Quarter — Up 50% in Three Months

OpenAI's Q3 revenue crossed $10 billion for the first time, a staggering jump from the $6.7 billion it recorded in Q2. That's nearly 50% quarter-over-quarter growth, the kind of number that makes most SaaS companies look slow. The company still isn't profitable — its costs are immense — but that revenue trajectory is hard to argue with.

**Why it matters:** This cements OpenAI as the fastest-growing enterprise software company in history and makes the case that AI is a real business, not just a hype cycle.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/openais-quarterly-revenue-reportedly-crosses-10b-for-the-first-time/)

## The AI Data Center Boom Has a Reality Problem

Billions of dollars in AI data center projects have been announced, but a significant chunk may never get built. Local opposition, byzantine permitting processes, power grid constraints, and a shortage of everything from transformers to cooling equipment are creating a massive gap between press releases and shovels in the ground. Post-"Blue Wave," the political environment has shifted, but the bureaucratic obstacles haven't.

**Why it matters:** If the infrastructure can't keep pace with AI demand, it creates a hard ceiling on the industry's growth — and hands an advantage to whoever figures out how to build more efficiently.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/podcast/how-backlash-and-red-tape-continue-to-slow-data-centers-post-blue-wave/)

## CoreWeave Is Making Customers Pay Upfront to Fund Its Own Growth

CoreWeave's Q3 earnings reveal a creative — and slightly worrying — funding strategy: asking customers to prepay, borrowing against specific contracts, and shifting to shorter-term deals that could ultimately cost customers more. It's a sign that even one of the best-capitalized AI cloud players is straining under the weight of its infrastructure ambitions.

**Why it matters:** When a company starts using customer prepayments as working capital, it's either a sign of massive demand or financial stress — and CoreWeave's situation looks like both at once.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/coreweave-turns-to-customer-prepayments-shorter-contracts-to-fund-ai-growth/)

## UK Chip Startup Fractile Raises $720M to Take On Nvidia

British AI chip startup Fractile has closed a $720 million funding round, vaulting it into the top tier of Nvidia challengers building purpose-built AI silicon. Details on the chip architecture remain sparse, but the funding size signals serious institutional conviction that the AI chip market won't be a winner-take-all Nvidia monopoly forever.

**Why it matters:** With Nvidia under political and legal pressure, the timing couldn't be better for a well-funded European alternative to gain credibility with skittish enterprise buyers.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/with-720m-round-britains-fractile-gets-a-leg-up-in-the-race-for-ai-chips/)

## Google DeepMind's AlphaGenome 2 Maps How DNA Changes Affect Individual Cells

DeepMind has released AlphaGenome 2, an AI model that predicts how specific DNA variations affect gene activity across different cell types. This is a materially harder problem than the protein folding that made AlphaFold famous — the same mutation can behave completely differently depending on the cell — and cracking it could reveal the cellular roots of diseases like cancer and diabetes.

**Why it matters:** This is the kind of AI application that could actually change medicine: not a chatbot writing discharge summaries, but a model helping scientists understand why genes malfunction in the first place.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1031524/google-deepmind-alphagenome-2-genetic-ai-model)
