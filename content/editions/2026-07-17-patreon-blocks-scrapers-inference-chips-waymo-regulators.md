## Patreon Is Done Politely Asking AI Bots to Leave

Patreon has partnered with Cloudflare to actively block AI training bots, abandoning the honor-system approach of robots.txt. This is a direct enforcement move — if a bot ignores the "no scraping" request, it now gets stopped at the door rather than politely reminded.

**Why it matters:** This signals a broader shift from passive opt-outs to active technical enforcement, and if other major platforms follow, AI labs will have a genuinely harder time finding fresh training data.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/17/patreon-stops-asking-ai-bots-not-to-scrape-and-starts-blocking-them/)

## The Smart Money Is Moving from GPUs to Inference Chips

The same financiers who pioneered GPU-backed loans are now structuring a $400 million deal around inference chips — the silicon that runs AI models in production rather than training them. This is a bet that the build-out phase is maturing and the "run it" phase is where the next infrastructure boom lives.

**Why it matters:** When the people who correctly called the GPU financing wave pivot to inference, it's a strong signal that AI infrastructure investment is entering a new, deployment-focused era.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/17/why-the-first-gpu-financiers-are-turning-to-inference-chips-in-a-400-million-deal/)

## Google Vids Wants You to Clone Yourself for Work Videos

Google is rolling out personalized AI avatars inside Google Vids, letting Workspace users generate videos starring a digital version of themselves. The feature pairs with Gemini Omni-powered tools for prompt-based video creation and editing from reference images.

**Why it matters:** Google is aggressively embedding generative AI into its productivity suite, and self-avatar video generation at enterprise scale raises immediate questions about consent, deepfake misuse, and where corporate communications are headed.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/google-vids-now-lets-you-star-in-your-own-ai-videos/)

## Roblox Lets You Build a Game With One Sentence

Roblox launched "Build," a mobile feature that generates a basic playable game from a single text prompt. It's live in the Roblox app now, targeted squarely at the platform's young, creator-curious user base who want to make something without learning Lua.

**Why it matters:** Roblox has 88 million daily users, most of them under 17 — democratizing game creation at that scale could reshape who makes games and flood the platform with AI-generated content in ways that are hard to predict.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/roblox-launches-an-ai-powered-game-creation-feature-in-its-mobile-app/)

## Zoox Quietly Recalled Its Robotaxi Software After It Choked on Smoke

Zoox issued a software recall after one of its autonomous vehicles got confused by heavy smoke and behaved unexpectedly. The recall comes as federal safety regulators are specifically warning AV companies about vehicles that interfere with first responders during emergencies.

**Why it matters:** A robotaxi that can't handle a wildfire smoke event — an increasingly common condition in the Western US — is a serious reliability gap, and federal scrutiny of AV edge cases is only intensifying.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/17/zoox-issues-software-recall-after-a-robotaxi-got-confused-by-heavy-smoke/)

## San Francisco Is Fed Up With Waymo and Wants the State to Step In

After a major hours-long gridlock event caused by Waymo robotaxis, San Francisco Mayor Daniel Lurie sent a formal letter to state regulators demanding tougher requirements on autonomous vehicle operators. He wants more city-level control over when and how robotaxis operate on SF streets.

**Why it matters:** Cities have largely been spectators while state regulators decide AV policy — Lurie pushing back hard is a sign that local governments are ready to fight for a seat at the table before robotaxi fleets scale further.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/san-francisco-mayor-pushes-for-tougher-rules-after-the-waymo-traffic-fiasco/)
