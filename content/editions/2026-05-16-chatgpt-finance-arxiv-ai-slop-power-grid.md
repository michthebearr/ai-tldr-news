## OpenAI Wants to Be Your Financial Advisor — and Your Bank

OpenAI launched a personal finance feature inside ChatGPT that lets users connect their bank accounts, investment portfolios, and credit cards. You get a dashboard showing spending patterns, subscriptions, portfolio performance, and upcoming payments — all queryable in plain English.

**Why it matters:** This is OpenAI's most direct move into high-stakes personal data yet, and it puts them in direct competition with fintech players like Mint, Copilot, and even the banks themselves.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-personal-finance-will-let-you-connect-bank-accounts/)

## America's Power Grid Is Already Cracking Under AI Demand

Power prices on the US's largest grid are up 76% and a watchdog is calling out AI data centers as a major driver. The grid simply wasn't engineered for the load that hyperscale AI training and inference requires, and the gap between supply and demand is getting worse, not better.

**Why it matters:** Energy infrastructure is fast becoming the binding constraint on AI scaling — no amount of GPU investment matters if there's no power to run them.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/power-prices-are-up-76-on-americas-biggest-grid-and-a-watchdog-is-pointing-fingers/)

## ArXiv Will Ban Researchers Who Upload AI Slop for a Year

ArXiv is done playing nice. The preprint server will now ban authors for 12 months if their papers contain "incontrovertible evidence" they didn't review LLM-generated content — think hallucinated citations or LLM meta-comments left in the final text. The policy comes from ArXiv's own advisory board.

**Why it matters:** Academic publishing has a generative AI garbage problem, and ArXiv drawing a hard line with actual consequences is the most credible enforcement action the research community has seen so far.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/931766/arxiv-ai-slop-ban-researchers)

## YouTube Rolls Out AI Deepfake Detection to All Adults

YouTube is expanding its AI likeness detection tool — previously limited — to every user over 18. You submit a selfie-style face scan, and YouTube's system automatically hunts for deepfakes of you across the platform. Matches trigger an alert, and you can then request takedowns.

**Why it matters:** Putting deepfake detection directly in users' hands at scale is a meaningful shift from the reactive "report and wait" model, and sets a precedent other platforms will feel pressure to match.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/news/931884/youtube-likeness-detection-ai-deepfake-expansion-all-adults)

## The Musk v. Altman Trial Is Over — and the Core Question Remains Unanswered

The Musk v. Altman courtroom battle wrapped up this week, with closing arguments zeroing in on one uncomfortable question: can the people running frontier AI labs actually be trusted? No landmark ruling has changed the governance reality on the ground.

## Deloitte to Enterprises: Stop Playing With Chatbots, Start Deploying Agents

Deloitte's latest report argues that companies stuck in the "generate text and summarize emails" phase of AI adoption are leaving real money on the table. The consultancy is pushing a shift toward "autonomous intelligence" — AI systems that can independently execute multi-step business processes, not just assist with them.

**Why it matters:** When Deloitte talks, Fortune 500 boardrooms listen — and this report will accelerate enterprise pressure to move from AI pilots to agentic deployments in 2026.

**Source:** [AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/deloitte-scale-autonomous-intelligence-for-real-growth/)
