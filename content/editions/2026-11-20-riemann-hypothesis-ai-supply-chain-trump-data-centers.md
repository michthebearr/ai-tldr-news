## An Anthropic Mathematician May Have Just Solved Math's Hardest Problem — With Claude's Help

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge claims he's proved the Riemann hypothesis, one of the seven Millennium Prize Problems that has stumped mathematicians for over 160 years. He used Claude as a collaborator in producing the proof, which has not yet been independently verified. If it holds up, this would be the most significant mathematical achievement in modern history — and a landmark moment for AI-assisted research.

**Why it matters:** A verified proof would cement AI as a genuine scientific partner, not just a productivity tool — and net someone a $1 million prize.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1010051/anthropic-riemann-hypothesis-millennium-prize-problem)

## A Researcher Planted 2 Million Canaries in the AI Supply Chain. It Was a Privacy Disaster.

A security researcher seeded AI pipelines with 2 million uniquely trackable "canary" prompts containing fake secrets to see where data ends up. The results confirmed what many feared: private user data doesn't stay private, leaking across the AI supply chain at an alarming rate. This is the most systematic proof yet that the infrastructure underpinning AI products has serious data hygiene problems.

**Why it matters:** Every enterprise and consumer using AI tools should assume their inputs may be exposed downstream — and demand supply chain transparency from vendors.

**Source:** [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/a-leaky-ai-supply-chain-is-exposing-secret-user-data-at-an-alarming-rate/)

## Trump Threatens a "Tremendous Tax" on Companies That Build AI Data Centers Abroad

At a press conference following his summit with China's President Xi, Trump said he will not allow US companies to build data centers outside the country and threatened steep tariffs on those that do. This comes as domestic data center buildout faces pushback from local governments restricting new construction permits. The two pressures are now on a collision course.

**Why it matters:** Forcing AI infrastructure onshore while blocking it locally creates a genuine bottleneck — and could slow the US compute expansion that everyone from OpenAI to Google is counting on.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/1038892/trump-foreign-data-centers-tax)

## Google Is Killing Google Assistant on Smart Speakers

Google announced it will retire Google Assistant on smart home devices next year, alongside shutting down the Nest app on January 25th, 2027. Users will be migrated to the Google Home app and presumably a Gemini-based assistant experience going forward. It's a quiet but definitive end to one of the original voice AI platforms.

**Why it matters:** Google Assistant's death signals the complete generational turnover from first-wave voice assistants to LLM-native AI — and leaves millions of Nest device owners scrambling to adapt.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1038834/google-assistant-smart-speakers-nest-app-killed-shut-down)

## YouTube Is Settling School District Addiction Lawsuits for $3 Billion

YouTube reached an agreement in principle to pay $3 billion to thousands of US school districts that accused the platform of using addictive design features that harmed children. The deal will also include new teen safeguards. It's one of the largest settlements ever tied to platform design liability.

**Why it matters:** This sets a precedent that algorithmic product design can carry massive legal liability — a signal that will ripple into how AI-powered recommendation systems are built and regulated.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/streaming/1039096/youtube-social-media-addiction-school-districts-settlement)
