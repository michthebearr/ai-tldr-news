## Anthropic Is Going Public at a $1.4 Trillion Valuation

Anthropic set its IPO price range at $70–$78 per share, implying a fully diluted valuation of up to $1.404 trillion. All seven co-founders are selling exactly the same number of shares — netting roughly $250M each — while retaining voting control of the company.

**Why it matters:** This would be one of the largest tech IPOs in history, cementing AI as a generational wealth-creation machine and setting a benchmark that every other AI lab will be measured against.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-of-70-to-78-per-share-to-reach-an-implied-valuation-of-1-404-trillion/)

## Jensen Huang's Affair With an Alleged Chinese Spy Is an AI National Security Crisis

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang admitted to an affair with a woman suspected of being a Chinese intelligence operative. Huang reportedly hired a crisis PR firm — one specializing in high-profile sex scandals — over a year ago, shortly after early reporting surfaced the relationship.

**Why it matters:** Huang controls the chokepoint of global AI infrastructure; if China gained any intelligence access through this relationship, the consequences for the US-China AI race could be severe and long-lasting.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huangs-affair-with-alleged-chinese-spy-a-timeline/)

## Anthropic's Claude Meetings Wants to Kill the Follow-Up Email Forever

Anthropic launched Claude Meetings, a real-time AI that listens to workplace conversations and simultaneously drafts plans, maps timelines on a whiteboard, answers questions in chat, and pulls data from connected work apps — all while the meeting is still happening.

**Why it matters:** This is a genuine shot at replacing the entire post-meeting workflow, and it's a direct challenge to Microsoft Copilot and Google's Workspace AI on their home turf.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-claude-meetings-to-turn-workplace-discussions-into-plans-and-tasks/)

## OpenAI Bought a Travel Startup. Its Plans Are Anyone's Guess.

OpenAI acquired Odessia, a travel startup founded by Francis Davidson — the entrepreneur behind short-term rental company Sonder. OpenAI hasn't disclosed what it intends to build, but Davidson is a heavyweight in travel tech.

**Why it matters:** OpenAI's consumer product ambitions are clearly expanding well beyond chat; a travel vertical would put it in direct competition with Google Flights, Booking.com, and every AI travel agent startup that's raised money on this exact thesis.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-acquires-odessia-travel-startup-created-by-sonder-founder/)

## New York Just Made AI Companies Register, Disclose, and Report — Or Pay Up

New York's sweeping AI safety law is now in effect, requiring major AI developers like OpenAI and Anthropic to formally register with the state, publicly disclose their safety practices, and report critical incidents. Non-compliance means fines.

**Why it matters:** With federal AI regulation still gridlocked, New York is effectively writing the rulebook for the whole industry — and if it sticks, every major AI lab now has a new compliance burden regardless of where they're headquartered.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-anthropic-face-new-oversight-as-new-yorks-ai-safety-law-takes-effect/)

## Grok Build Left 35,000 Apps Wide Open — Including SpaceX Data

A security researcher discovered that Grok Build, xAI's coding agent, exposed roughly 35,000 apps to anyone who knew where to look, leaking confidential information including data tied to SpaceX.

**Why it matters:** Agentic AI systems that autonomously build and deploy apps create a massive new attack surface — and this breach shows the security infrastructure hasn't kept pace with the product ambitions.

**Source:** [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/grok-build-coding-agent-leaks-oodles-of-confidential-info-some-belonging-to-spacexsi/)
