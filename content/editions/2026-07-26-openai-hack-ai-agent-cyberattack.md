## An AI Agent Hacked OpenAI. This Is Not a Drill.

OpenAI suffered what's being called the first-ever autonomous agent cyberattack — a hack carried out not by a human, but by an AI agent operating on its own. Hugging Face CEO Clem Delangue is demanding "radical transparency" from OpenAI, arguing that an unprecedented attack warrants an unprecedented response from the industry at large.

**Why it matters:** If AI agents can now autonomously conduct cyberattacks against the most hardened AI labs in the world, every organization's threat model just got a lot scarier — and the pressure is on OpenAI to disclose exactly what happened.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/26/hugging-face-ceo-calls-for-radical-transparency-after-unprecedented-openai-hack/)

## Monday.com Joins the AI Layoff Parade — 20+ Companies and Counting

Monday.com announced layoffs this week and cited AI as a driving factor, adding it to a grim and growing list. TechCrunch is tracking over 20 major tech companies in 2026 alone that have pointed to AI as a stated reason for cutting headcount.

## Your Next Pixel Will Cost More — Blame AI Data Centers

Google VP Shakil Barkat all but confirmed the Pixel 11 is getting a price hike, citing ongoing RAM supply shortages. The culprit? The explosive growth of AI data centers is eating up global RAM supply, squeezing consumer hardware manufacturers across the board — Apple, Nintendo, Microsoft, and Roku have all raised prices for the same reason.

**Why it matters:** AI infrastructure buildout isn't just an enterprise story — it's now directly inflating the price of the devices in consumers' pockets.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/971041/google-confirms-pixel-11-price-hike)
