## ChatGPT Can Now Pull Up Your Patient Chart

OpenAI's ChatGPT Health product just integrated with Epic, the dominant electronic health records system used by most major hospitals. Clinicians can now import patient data directly into ChatGPT for read-only access, meaning the AI can see your records but can't write anything back. This is OpenAI's clearest move yet into clinical workflows — putting ChatGPT at the bedside, not just the consumer's phone.

**Why it matters:** Epic integration is the key that unlocks the U.S. hospital system — if this gains traction, ChatGPT becomes infrastructure for healthcare, not just a curiosity.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/chatgpt-health-adds-epic-integration-for-clinicians-to-import-patient-data/)

## Apple Says OpenAI's Side Destroyed Evidence — and Has the MacBook to Prove It

Apple's lawsuit against OpenAI over alleged trade secret theft just got a lot more dramatic. Apple says a former employee at the center of the case had discussions about "destroying forensic data" on a MacBook that OpenAI only recently handed over. Apple is now pushing for expedited discovery, arguing that evidence is actively disappearing.

**Why it matters:** If the destruction-of-evidence allegations stick, OpenAI faces legal exposure that goes well beyond the original theft claims — this could get ugly fast.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/987575/apple-openai-destroying-evidence-trade-secrets-lawsuit)

## Waymo Goes Offense on Two Fronts: Three New Cities and a Shot at Tesla

Waymo announced robotaxi expansions into Denver, San Diego, and Tampa — its biggest geographic push yet. At the same time, the company went on the record arguing that Tesla's pure end-to-end AI approach (the kind powering Cybercab) isn't safe enough, and that real autonomy requires a mix of sensors. The timing, right before Tesla's Cybercab launch, is not subtle.

**Why it matters:** Waymo is moving fast to establish market presence before Tesla arrives — and framing the safety debate in its favor while it still can.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/waymo-accelerates-robotaxi-expansion-with-launches-in-denver-san-diego-and-tampa/)

## Google Pics Wants to Kill Canva With a Prompt Box

Google launched Google Pics, a Gemini-powered design tool built into Workspace that lets users generate and edit professional images by describing what they want. It's built on Google's Nano Banana generative model and targets the same business users that Canva and Adobe have been fighting over for years — but replaces drag-and-drop with a prompt interface.

**Why it matters:** Google has distribution advantages Canva can't match; if Pics is good enough, it could commoditize an entire category of creative software overnight.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/987423/google-pics-ai-image-editor-generator)

## $50M Raised to Be the Security Guard for Your AI Agents

AIR just closed a $50M round to tackle one of enterprise AI's messiest problems: no one really knows what their AI agents are doing. The platform automatically discovers agents running inside a company, vets the third-party skills and plugins those agents use, and blocks behavior that falls outside policy. Think of it as an IT firewall, but for agent behavior.

**Why it matters:** As companies deploy hundreds of autonomous agents, the attack surface explodes — AIR is betting that agent governance becomes as essential as endpoint security.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/air-raises-50m-to-help-companies-vet-the-skills-and-add-ons-ai-agents-use/)

## Alexa's New Feature Is Basically an AI Shopping Stalker (But Make It Polite)

Amazon's "Update Me When" feature lets Alexa users set personalized alerts for product launches, new book releases, tour announcements, and anything else that might trigger a purchase. You tell Alexa what you're waiting for, and it nudges you when the moment arrives. It's framed as a convenience feature, but the business logic is obvious.

**Why it matters:** Amazon is turning Alexa into a proactive purchase funnel — the assistant that was once reactive is now hunting for your next buy before you even think of it.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/amazon-alexa-can-now-alert-you-when-something-new-might-tempt-you-to-shop/)
