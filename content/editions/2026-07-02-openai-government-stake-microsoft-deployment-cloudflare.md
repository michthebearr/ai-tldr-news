## OpenAI Wants to Give the U.S. Government a 5% Cut of the AI Boom

Sam Altman reportedly floated giving the U.S. sovereign wealth fund a 5% equity stake in OpenAI — framed as a way to let the public share in AI's upside. The pitch appears designed to ease friction with the Trump administration and blunt growing public backlash against AI enriching a handful of private actors. No deal has been struck, but the fact that Altman is floating this at all signals just how much political pressure OpenAI is under during its for-profit conversion.

**Why it matters:** If this goes through, it sets a precedent for how AI companies negotiate with governments — equity stakes instead of regulation.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/02/openai-proposed-donating-5-of-its-equity-to-a-us-sovereign-wealth-fund/)

## Microsoft Joins the AI Deployment Arms Race With $2.5B on the Table

Microsoft has launched its own dedicated AI deployment company, committing $2.5 billion to the effort. It's following a now-familiar playbook: Amazon, OpenAI, and Anthropic have all stood up similar deployment-focused groups. The move signals that the major players see deployment — not just model building — as the real moat.

**Why it matters:** When every major AI player is building its own deployment arm, the "build vs. buy" calculus for enterprises just got a lot more complicated.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/02/microsoft-launches-its-own-ai-deployment-company-with-2-5-billion-commitment/)

## Cloudflare Gives AI Crawlers Until September 15 to Pay Up or Get Blocked

Cloudflare is drawing a hard line: AI companies must separate crawlers used for search from those used for AI training and agents by September 15, or publishers on Cloudflare's network can block them by default. The policy is a direct push to force AI companies into content licensing deals with publishers. It's one of the most concrete enforcement mechanisms the web infrastructure layer has deployed against AI scraping.

**Why it matters:** Cloudflare sits in front of a massive chunk of the internet — this policy has real teeth, unlike most publisher robots.txt complaints.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/01/cloudflares-new-policy-pushes-ai-companies-to-pay-for-publishers-content/)

## Together AI Raises $800M, Valuation More Than Doubles to $8.3B

Together AI, the neocloud specializing in hosting open-source models, just closed an $800M round — more than doubling its valuation from $3.3B in early 2025. The raise reflects booming demand for alternatives to the big proprietary model providers, particularly from enterprises that want to run open-source models without building their own GPU clusters.

**Why it matters:** A $5B valuation jump in roughly 18 months shows the open-source AI infrastructure layer is now a serious business, not just a hobbyist playground.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/01/neocloud-together-ai-raises-800m-leaps-to-8-3b-valuation/)

## Anthropic's Claude Science Gets a Biotech Brain with NVIDIA BioNeMo

Anthropic has integrated NVIDIA's BioNeMo Agent Toolkit into Claude Science, its AI workbench for scientific research currently in public beta. The integration lets scientists direct AI agents with natural language to run end-to-end computational biology workflows. It's a meaningful step toward AI that doesn't just summarize papers but actually runs experiments.

**Why it matters:** Pairing a frontier language model with specialized biocompute tooling is how AI actually breaks into serious drug discovery pipelines — not via chatbot wrappers.

**Source:** [AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/nvidia-bionemo-accelerates-anthropic-claude-science/)

## SpaceX Is Building an AI Phone. Yes, Really.

SpaceX reportedly showed investors a "handset-like" AI device prototype ahead of its eventual IPO. Details are thin, but paired with Starlink's existing direct-to-cell ambitions, this looks like a play to own the full wireless stack — satellite network, device hardware, and presumably AI assistant layer. Think less iPhone competitor, more Elon Musk's version of a vertically integrated telecom.

**Why it matters:** If SpaceX ships an AI device tied to Starlink connectivity, it could compete in markets where traditional carriers don't reach — which is most of the planet.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/01/spacex-has-an-ai-device-prototype-and-it-sure-sounds-phone-ish/)
