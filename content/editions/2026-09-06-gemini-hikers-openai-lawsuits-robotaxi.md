## Google Gemini Gave Hikers Bad Advice. They Nearly Died.

A group of hikers had to be rescued after relying on Google Gemini to plan their trip — and the AI told them to bring far less food and water than they actually needed. The sheriff's office confirmed the AI's advice was the root cause of the dangerous situation. This wasn't a subtle hallucination; it was life-threatening misinformation delivered with confidence.

**Why it matters:** This is the clearest real-world example yet of what happens when people treat AI chatbots as authoritative trip planners — and it will add fuel to the growing push for AI safety disclosures and liability frameworks.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/05/hikers-rescued-after-using-google-gemini-for-planning/)

## The Media vs. OpenAI War Has Two New Combatants

The Seattle Times and Newsday have filed suit against OpenAI and Microsoft, alleging their journalism was used without permission to train AI models. They join a growing list of publishers — including the New York Times — making the same core argument: scraping copyrighted content for commercial AI training isn't fair use.

**Why it matters:** As the case count climbs, the legal pressure on OpenAI and Microsoft is compounding — and a ruling against them could force a fundamental rethink of how frontier AI models are trained on web data.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/05/seattle-times-and-newsday-are-the-latest-publications-to-sue-openai-and-microsoft/)

## Travis Kalanick Wants Back Into the Robotaxi Game

Uber's controversial co-founder is reportedly eyeing the robotaxi market through his new company, Atoms. Kalanick has framed it as "unfinished business" — a nod to the fact that Uber sold its self-driving unit, ATG, to Aurora back in 2020 under financial pressure. Details on Atoms' technology or funding are thin, but the direction is clear.

**Why it matters:** If Kalanick is serious, Atoms enters a market already crowded with Waymo, Tesla, and deep-pocketed incumbents — his real edge, if any, would be the regulatory and operational playbook he built at Uber.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/06/travis-kalanicks-atoms-might-be-getting-into-the-robotaxi-business/)

## Tesla's Cybercab Is on the Road — With Problems

Tesla's long-awaited Cybercab robotaxi has officially launched on public roads, but it hasn't gone smoothly. TechCrunch Mobility reports the rollout has already hit a snag, though details on the exact nature of the issues are still emerging. This is the vehicle Musk has staked Tesla's autonomous future on.

**Why it matters:** A rocky launch for the Cybercab would be more than a PR headache — it would raise fresh questions about whether Tesla's vision-only, no-LiDAR approach to autonomy is ready for unsupervised commercial deployment.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/06/techcrunch-mobility-tesla-cybercab-hits-the-road-and-a-snag/)
