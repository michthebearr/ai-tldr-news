## Cognition Bought a Startup Just to Give Devin a Better Personality

Cognition acquired Poke — a conversational AI startup — not for its models or tech stack, but for its interaction style. The bet: that *how* an AI agent talks to you will soon matter as much as what it can do. Devin, Cognition's coding agent, is getting Poke's personality transplanted in.

**Why it matters:** This is the first high-profile acquisition where "vibes" is explicitly the product — a signal that UX differentiation, not raw capability, is becoming the next AI battleground.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/why-cognition-bought-poke-ai-personality-is-becoming-a-competitive-advantage/)

## Reid Hoffman's New Lab Thinks Automating Boring Computer Tasks Beats Coding

Prentis, a new AI lab co-founded by Reid Hoffman and Mark Pincus, is in talks to raise $100M. The lab's thesis: automating routine desktop and computer tasks — think clicking, form-filling, workflow grunt work — will eclipse coding assistance as AI's dominant use case. No product is public yet.

**Why it matters:** When the LinkedIn founder pivots from coding agents to task automation, it tells you where the smart money thinks the next hundred-million-user AI app is hiding.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/prentis-new-ai-lab-co-founded-by-reid-hoffman-mark-pincus-in-talks-to-raise-100m/)

## One Fallen Power Line Nearly Took Down Northern Virginia's AI Data Centers

A single downed power line in Northern Virginia — the world's densest concentration of data centers — revealed how badly the industry handles grid disruptions. Data centers lack coordinated failover, and their power demands have outpaced the grid's ability to accommodate sudden load shifts. The fix involves better islanding tech and smarter interconnection agreements.

**Why it matters:** AI's infrastructure is built on a fragile foundation — a regional grid hiccup in one corridor could ripple into latency or outages across half the internet's AI services.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/25/one-fallen-power-line-exposed-a-growing-ai-data-center-problem-heres-how-to-fix-it/)

## Libraries Are Packing Rooms With People Who Just Want to Avoid AI

"Avoiding AI" workshops at public libraries are filling up faster than anything librarians have seen before. Attendees want practical help opting out — alternative search engines, non-AI writing tools, ways to stop their data from feeding training sets. The demand is described as unprecedented.

**Why it matters:** Consumer AI fatigue has graduated from Twitter complaints to real-world collective action — and librarians, not regulators, are leading the resistance.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/25/librarians-are-hosting-viral-avoiding-ai-workshops-for-people-who-are-fed-up-with-big-tech/)

## OpenAI's New AI Keypad Is a Developer Toy That Will Confuse Everyone Else

OpenAI shipped a physical AI keypad — a programmable input device designed to trigger AI actions and shortcuts from your desk. For developers and power users who live in the terminal, it's genuinely fun. For everyone else, it's a $X peripheral with no obvious on-ramp.

**Why it matters:** OpenAI is clearly probing the hardware category, and this keypad — however niche — is a test of whether developers will pay for dedicated AI-native input devices before a mainstream version ships.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/i-tried-out-openais-new-ai-keypad-which-will-be-fun-for-coders-and-slightly-mystifying-to-everyone-else/)

## Meta Backs Down on Charging $20/Month for Smart Glasses Audio Features

Meta quietly planned to put a $20/month subscription gate on a hearing-enhancement feature in its smart glasses — one that runs entirely locally and doesn't touch the cloud. After backlash, they've paused the plan. The company says subscriptions aren't dead, just delayed.

**Why it matters:** Meta's glasses are already under fire for privacy and harassment issues — slapping a paywall on local features would have been exactly the kind of move that accelerates the growing anti-wearables sentiment.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/970970/after-backlash-meta-pauses-plan-to-rate-limit-its-smart-glasses)
