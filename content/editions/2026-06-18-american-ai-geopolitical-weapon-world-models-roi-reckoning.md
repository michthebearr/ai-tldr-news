## G7 Leaders Are Terrified the U.S. Can Flip the AI Kill Switch

French President Macron and Indian PM Modi raised alarms at the G7 summit that the U.S. could shut off access to American AI overnight — and the recent Anthropic blackout of its Fable 5 model proved they're not being paranoid. The episode has crystallized a genuine geopolitical dilemma: countries want cutting-edge American AI, but not the sovereign dependency that comes with it. Expect this to accelerate interest in domestic AI infrastructure and open-source alternatives abroad.

**Why it matters:** The ability to revoke AI access is now a geopolitical lever, and the rest of the world is finally building policy around that reality.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/17/world-leaders-want-american-ai-they-just-dont-want-america-to-be-able-to-turn-it-off/)

## "Dangerous" AI Is Coming Whether Anyone Likes It or Not

Ars Technica's analysis cuts through the regulatory noise: AI models with advanced hacking and cyberoffense capabilities are on a trajectory to become mainstream, regardless of what any individual lab or government decides. The argument is that capability thresholds keep rising across dozens of competing actors, so voluntary restraint by one company mostly just means another fills the gap. This lands the same week Anthropic's Fable 5 was pulled after the U.S. government flagged it as a national security risk.

**Why it matters:** It reframes the AI safety debate — the question isn't whether dangerous models will exist, but who controls them and under what conditions.

**Source:** [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/06/dangerous-ai-models-are-coming-no-matter-what/)

## World Models Are the New LLMs — and VCs Are Throwing Money Accordingly

Two world model startups just hit major funding milestones in 24 hours. Odyssey, backed by Amazon, closed at a $1.45B valuation. General Intuition is in talks to raise $300M at a ~$2B valuation, training its models on 2 billion videos per year sourced from gaming clip platform Medal's 10 million monthly active users. World models — AI systems that learn physical and causal dynamics from video rather than text — are rapidly becoming the next frontier bet.

**Why it matters:** The world model funding surge signals that investors think the next AI capability leap will come from video-trained physical reasoning, not more text scaling.

**Sources:** [TechCrunch (Odyssey)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/17/world-model-maker-odyssey-nabs-1-45b-valuation-backed-by-amazon-and-other-big-names/) · [TechCrunch (General Intuition)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/18/general-intuition-in-talks-to-raise-300m-at-around-2b-valuation/)

## Enterprises Blew Their AI Budgets and Have Little to Show for It

NEA partner Tiffany Luck confirmed what many inside tech companies already know: the "tokenmaxxing" era — where CEOs pushed employees to burn as many AI tokens as possible — has hit a wall. Uber reportedly exhausted its entire annual AI budget in a few months, companies have quietly cut Claude licenses, and Meta killed its internal AI usage leaderboard. The ROI just isn't materializing fast enough to justify the spend.

**Why it matters:** The enterprise AI honeymoon is over — the next phase will be brutal rationalization of which AI use cases actually pay for themselves.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/podcast/neas-tiffany-luck-on-ai-ipos-personal-agents-and-the-roi-reckoning/)

## Amazon Is Firing Employees Who Testified Against Data Centers

Three Amazon software engineers who testified at Seattle City Council hearings in favor of a data center moratorium are now facing disciplinary action — potentially termination. They cited a Seattle law barring employment retaliation for political speech when they testified on June 3rd; Amazon moved against them the day after the City Council passed the moratorium on June 9th. The engineers are now formally accusing Amazon of illegal retaliation.

**Why it matters:** This is a rare, concrete test of whether tech workers can publicly oppose their employers' infrastructure expansion without losing their jobs — and it's being fought in one of the most AI-hungry cities in the country.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/952180/amazon-seattle-data-center-moratorium-aecj-disciplinary-action)

## Adobe Shoves AI Assistants Into Every Major Creative App

Adobe launched a public beta today putting conversational AI assistants inside Photoshop, Premiere, Illustrator, InDesign, and Frame.io. The chatbots are built on Firefly and let users describe edits in plain language rather than hunting through menus. Adobe is clearly racing to make AI feel native to professional creative workflows before someone else does.

**Why it matters:** Adobe has 30+ million Creative Cloud subscribers — this is one of the largest simultaneous AI assistant rollouts to professional users ever attempted.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/952099/adobe-ai-assistants-photoshop-premiere-illustrator-beta-launch)
