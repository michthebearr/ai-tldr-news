## Midjourney Is Making Hollywood Prove It Doesn't Use AI Either

Midjourney is locked in a legal battle with three Hollywood studios over alleged copyright infringement — but now it's going on offense. The image generator is seeking to compel the studios to disclose exactly how they use AI internally, a classic "you too" legal maneuver that could expose uncomfortable contradictions.

**Why it matters:** If studios are found to be using AI tools themselves while suing AI companies for infringement, it dramatically complicates their moral and legal standing — and could reshape the entire AI-copyright litigation landscape.

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/04/midjourney-wants-hollywood-studios-to-reveal-the-details-of-their-ai-usage/)

## Google's Fourth of July AI Ad Is Everything Wrong With AI Marketing

Google dropped a commercial imagining the Founding Fathers using Google Workspace and Gemini to co-write the Declaration of Independence. The Verge called it "infuriating" and "cringeworthy" — and the internet largely agreed.

**Why it matters:** When AI's biggest advocate can't sell AI without making people cringe, it's a reminder that the industry's perception problem is as real as its capability gains.

**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/961468/google-ai-commercial-founding-fathers-declaration-of-independence)

## 90 New Unicorns in Half a Year, and AI Is Writing the Checks

Nearly 90 startups have hit unicorn status in the first half of 2026, a pace fueled almost entirely by investor appetite for AI companies. The frenzy shows no signs of cooling as capital continues to flood into the sector.

**Why it matters:** The AI unicorn bubble is either the greatest value-creation event in tech history or the setup for a brutal correction — and we're moving fast enough that we'll find out soon.
