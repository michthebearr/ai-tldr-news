🏎️ Anthropic just made Fable 5 sweat — and it's doing it on price.

Claude Opus 5 dropped today, and the positioning is aggressive. Near-flagship performance across coding, reasoning, and real-world tasks — but cheaper and with fewer content restrictions than the models sitting above it. That's not a subtle move.

For developers, this is the one that actually ships. Fable 5 still holds the crown on raw benchmarks, but most enterprise teams don't need the crown. They need something fast, capable, and cost-effective enough to justify at scale. Opus 5 was built for exactly that calculation.

The ripple effect matters too. A cheaper, less restricted frontier-class model from Anthropic reshapes the economics for every team currently building on Claude. The ceiling just got lower to reach.
