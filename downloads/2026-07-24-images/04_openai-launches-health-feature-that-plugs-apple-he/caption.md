🏥 ChatGPT just got access to your medical records — and that's either brilliant or terrifying.

OpenAI has launched a Health feature inside ChatGPT that lets you connect your Apple Health data and actual medical records directly to the chatbot. It's live now for logged-in users 18+ across Free, Plus, and Pro tiers on web and iOS. The pitch: an AI that finally knows your full health picture before you even start typing.

On paper, this is genuinely useful. Imagine a chatbot that can cross-reference your bloodwork, your medications, your sleep data, and your symptoms — all in one place. That's not a gimmick. That's something most doctors don't have time to do in a 15-minute appointment.

But this is also ChatGPT. A general-purpose consumer chatbot now sitting on some of the most sensitive data you own. The line between "clinically helpful" and "catastrophically invasive" has never been thinner.

The upside is real. So is the risk.
