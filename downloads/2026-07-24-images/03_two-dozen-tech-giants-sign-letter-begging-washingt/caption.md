💡 Two dozen tech giants just sent Washington a message: don't you dare ban open-weight AI.

Meta, Microsoft, Nvidia, IBM, Hugging Face, Mistral, Palantir, and 17 others signed an open letter this week urging US policymakers to hold off on sweeping restrictions targeting open-weight AI models. The timing isn't random — DC is actively debating how to respond to Chinese AI advances and the controversy around "model distillation," where foreign labs allegedly train on outputs from American models.

The industry's argument: locking down open-weight models doesn't stop China — it just kneecaps American innovation. These companies are drawing a clear red line before regulation lands, and the stakes couldn't be higher. How Washington responds will decide whether open-source AI has a future in the US.

This is the tech world playing defense. And they're doing it loudly.
