🔐 AI safety filters are blocking the people trying to keep us safe — and the bad guys are getting through anyway.

Offensive security researchers — the ones who hunt zero-days, build exploit tools, and stress-test systems before attackers do — are increasingly hitting walls with OpenAI and Anthropic's content filters. Ask the wrong question in the wrong way, and you're flagged. Doesn't matter if you're a credentialed red-teamer doing legitimate work.

The core problem: the guardrails can't tell the difference between a security researcher and a threat actor. So the researcher gets blocked, files a frustrated support ticket, and loses hours of work. The actual attacker just finds a jailbreak and moves on.

Overcautious AI isn't safe AI. When the tools slow down defenders more than attackers, everyone loses.
