💡 Midjourney just bought an astrology app — and it's not as random as it sounds.

Co-Star, the AI-powered astrology app with tens of millions of users, has been acquired by Midjourney. No price tag announced. Just a quietly massive move from a company most people still think of as "that image generator."

Here's the thing: Co-Star isn't really about astrology. It's about personality-driven AI that people check every single day. High retention, emotional engagement, a massive consumer install base. That's a completely different moat than anything Midjourney has built so far.

Midjourney is playing a longer game than it's letting on.
