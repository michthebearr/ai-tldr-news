🤖 The robotaxi free pass is officially over.

For years, companies like Waymo have operated in a comfortable gray zone — expanding city by city, promising accountability later, and leaning on regulatory patience as a growth strategy. That patience is running out.

City officials and regulators are now demanding hard commitments: real safety data, defined coverage obligations, and timelines that actually mean something. No more "we're still in pilot mode" as a permanent excuse. The industry built its entire expansion playbook around moving fast and asking forgiveness — and that playbook is getting torn up in real time.

The next 12 months are the accountability squeeze. The companies that survive it will be the ones who stopped hiding behind promises.
