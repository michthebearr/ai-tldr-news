💡 Reed Jobs is betting AI can turn Big Pharma's biggest headache into biotech's biggest opportunity.

Over the next few years, dozens of blockbuster drugs — think billions in annual revenue — are losing patent protection at the same time. It's called the patent cliff, and for most of the industry, it's a crisis. For Reed Jobs and his VC firm Yosemite, it's a window.

His team is now using AI as a core tool — not a side experiment — to identify drug candidates faster, compress development timelines, and move from patent expiry to viable generic or successor compound at a speed that wasn't possible five years ago. Yosemite is 17 people. The drugs they're hunting? Billion-dollar categories suddenly up for grabs.

The real story here isn't the Jobs name. It's the thesis: AI doesn't just help you discover new drugs — it helps you move faster than incumbents when the old guard's moat disappears overnight.
