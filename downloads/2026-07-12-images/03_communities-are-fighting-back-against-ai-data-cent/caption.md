💡 AI's infrastructure boom has a NIMBY problem — and it's only getting louder.

Across the country, residents are showing up to town halls, planting yard signs, and filing legal challenges against the data centers being built in their neighborhoods to power the AI arms race. The concerns aren't abstract: we're talking real water consumption, grid strain, round-the-clock noise, and tanking property values.

And the pushback is getting organized. This isn't just isolated complaints — communities are coordinating, sharing playbooks, and finding real leverage against the tech giants trying to move fast and build things.

Here's the tension no one in Silicon Valley wants to talk about: you cannot train frontier AI without massive compute, and you cannot build massive compute without the communities who have to live next to it. That social license? It's quietly eroding.
