🏎️ Apple's car project failed — and somehow that's why their chips are winning the AI race.

Project Titan never put a single autonomous vehicle on the road. A decade of work, billions spent, and nothing to show at the finish line. By every traditional metric, it was one of Silicon Valley's most expensive flops.

But here's the twist: the engineers working on self-driving compute had to solve a nearly impossible problem — running AI at the edge, in real time, with no room for error. That pressure forged chip architecture that eventually became the neural engine powering Apple's entire M-series lineup. The car was never the product. The chip was.

Apple didn't plan to dominate edge AI hardware. They stumbled into it by trying to build something else entirely.
