💡 Smart glasses without a camera might be the most interesting bet in wearables right now.

Even Realities is launching AI-powered glasses that deliberately leave out the lens — no camera, no recordings, no making your coworkers or strangers feel like they're on a surveillance set. Instead, you get real-time translation, meeting assistance, and teleprompter-style prompts. Useful. Quiet. Intentionally limited.

The target isn't the average consumer. It's frequent travelers, presenters, and professionals who need subtle cognitive support without the social baggage that comes with strapping a camera to their face. That's a narrow lane — but it might be a real one.

While Meta keeps going all-in on camera-equipped Ray-Bans, this is the counter-thesis: that privacy isn't a missing feature, it's the product.
