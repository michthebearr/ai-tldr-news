💡 Meta just spent $900 million to hire one person's brain — and it might be the smartest thing they've done in India.

Meta is dropping $900M into Indian fintech CRED and pulling its founder, Kunal Shah, out of the company to run WhatsApp globally. Will Cathcart, who led WhatsApp for years, shifts to another role inside Meta. This isn't a routine leadership shuffle — it's a calculated bet on a single founder's market instincts.

Shah built CRED into one of India's most-used financial apps by understanding how Indian consumers think about money, trust, and status. That's exactly the playbook WhatsApp Pay has been missing. Payments and commerce on WhatsApp have been stalled in India for years despite 500 million users sitting right there. Meta has the distribution. It's never had the right person to unlock it.

Now it does. Or at least it paid $900 million to find out.
