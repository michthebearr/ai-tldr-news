💡 Amazon just made its boldest move yet to own the future of voice AI — and it starts with Hindi.

Amazon is quietly inviting Indian users to beta test Alexa+, its next-gen AI assistant, with Hindi support baked in. That might sound like a product update. It's actually a land grab.

India is the world's most populous country, one of the fastest-growing smartphone markets on the planet, and home to hundreds of millions of people who've been largely ignored by English-first AI products. Whoever cracks vernacular language support here doesn't just win India — they win the blueprint for every other underserved language market that follows.

Amazon is moving before Google can lock it in. That's the whole play.
