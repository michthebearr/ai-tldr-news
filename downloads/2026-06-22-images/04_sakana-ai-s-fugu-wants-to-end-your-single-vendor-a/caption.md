💡 Japanese AI lab Sakana just built the escape hatch enterprises didn't know they needed.

Sakana AI has launched Fugu, an orchestration model that coordinates multi-agent workflows across multiple AI providers — instead of funneling everything through a single vendor's API. Think of it as a smart switching layer that routes tasks to whichever model is best suited for the job.

The problem it's solving is real: if your entire operation runs on one provider and they raise prices, go down, or change their terms overnight, you're exposed. That's not a hypothetical risk anymore — it's a boardroom conversation happening right now.

As AI gets deeper into critical enterprise workflows, the companies that win won't just be the ones with the best models. They'll be the ones who control the plumbing.
