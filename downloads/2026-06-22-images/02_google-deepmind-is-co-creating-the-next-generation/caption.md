💡 Google just bought itself a seat at Hollywood's most credible table.

Google is investing ~$50 million into A24 and teaming up with DeepMind to build AI tools designed specifically for film production. The goal, officially, is to help filmmakers "expand their storytelling possibilities." The real goal? Get generative AI embedded into prestige cinema before the conversation turns hostile.

Smart move picking A24. Partnering with a major studio would have screamed corporate takeover. A24 — the house behind Everything Everywhere, Hereditary, and Midsommar — carries indie credibility that no amount of money can manufacture. This deal makes Google look like a collaborator, not a conqueror.

That framing matters, because this is one of the first times a frontier AI lab has gone all-in on prestige filmmaking. However it plays out, it sets the template for how Hollywood either learns to use these tools — or gets hollowed out by them.
