💰 A startup you've probably never heard of just committed $1.8 billion to SpaceX for AI compute — and that's only through 2026.

Reflection AI, a relatively quiet open-source lab, has signed a deal to pay SpaceX $150 million per month starting July 1 for access to Nvidia's brand-new GB300 chips inside the Colossus 2 data center near Memphis. Total bill through 2029: $5.4 billion. That's not a typo.

To put that in context — that level of infrastructure spend puts Reflection in the same breath as OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. Except most people couldn't have named this company last week. Top-tier GPU access right now is effectively rationed, so locking in this much compute isn't just a flex — it's a strategic land grab.

The real story here is twofold: the AI arms race is expanding fast beyond the household names, and SpaceX's data center business is quietly becoming one of the most important power brokers in the entire industry.
