🧠 The person who wrote OpenAI's safety reports just quit — and called the culture "broken."

David Robinson wasn't a peripheral critic. He was the staffer responsible for writing the safety disclosures attached to every major OpenAI model release. When he walks out the door and goes to The Atlantic to say the system is failing, that's not a disgruntled employee. That's an insider indictment.

His core argument: safety is being systematically sacrificed for shipping speed. He even acknowledges the pattern is becoming a cliché — safety researcher quits OpenAI, sounds the alarm. But that's exactly his point. The cliché is the story. When this many people with this specific job keep leaving and saying the same thing, the pattern *is* the warning.

The most powerful AI lab in the world is racing to build systems it admits it doesn't fully understand — and the people hired to pump the brakes keep getting shown the door.
