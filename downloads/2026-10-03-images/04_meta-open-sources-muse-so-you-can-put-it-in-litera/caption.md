🏎️ Meta just open sourced Muse — and wants it running on your TV, your fridge, your Raspberry Pi, everything.

Meta dropped the code for its Muse AI agent this week, and the invitation couldn't be clearer: build with it, embed it, stick it on whatever hardware you can get your hands on. TVs, e-ink displays, HDMI sticks — Meta explicitly named the targets. This isn't a cautious release. It's a land grab.

The strategy is textbook platform playbook. Don't win by building every device — win by running on every device. If Muse gets embedded in a thousand third-party gadgets, Meta owns the ambient AI layer without spending a dollar on hardware. Sound familiar? It's exactly how Android ate the smartphone market.

Open sourcing the agent layer is how you win platform wars. And Meta just made its move.
