🎵 Sean Parker just went from burning the music industry down to handing it a match.

The Napster co-founder who once made record labels beg is now their AI business partner. Parker has pivoted the struggling Stability AI toward AI-generated music — and the major labels aren't fighting it. They're funding it.

Let that land for a second. The same industry that sued teenagers over MP3s is now writing checks to build the tool that could replace the artists on their own rosters. Stability had been losing ground fast after its image generation dominance collapsed, and music is clearly the new lifeline.

Here's why this matters: when the major labels stop suing AI companies and start bankrolling them, the "AI vs. artists" narrative doesn't just get complicated — it gets ugly. The economics of music creation are about to shift in ways most artists aren't prepared for.
