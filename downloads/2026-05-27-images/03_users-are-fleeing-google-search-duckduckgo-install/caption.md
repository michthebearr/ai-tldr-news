💡 Users Are Fleeing Google Search — DuckDuckGo Installs Up 30%.

After Google's I/O 2026 overhaul replaced traditional search results with AI agents, the backlash landed fast. DuckDuckGo saw a 30% spike in app installs as users looked for an exit from what they're calling "force-fed" AI results.

The blue link is apparently still something people want. Why it matters:Google is betting its entire search franchise on AI-first results — a 30% install spike for its scrappiest competitor suggests that bet carries real user-retention risk.
