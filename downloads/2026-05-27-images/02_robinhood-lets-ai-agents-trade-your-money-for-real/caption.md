💡 Robinhood Lets AI Agents Trade Your Money — For Real.

Robinhood is launching dedicated AI agent trading accounts. Users fund a separate account, hand it to an AI agent, and the agent executes trades autonomously — buying, selling, monitoring sectors — without needing human approval on each move.

It's pitched as automation for retail investors, not just hedge funds. Why it matters:This is the first mainstream consumer platform to formally give AI agents real financial autonomy, and it normalizes a world where software, not people, makes market decisions.
