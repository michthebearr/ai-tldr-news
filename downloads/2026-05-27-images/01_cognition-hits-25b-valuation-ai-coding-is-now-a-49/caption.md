💡 Cognition Hits $25B Valuation — AI Coding Is Now a $492M ARR Business.

Eight months ago, Cognition (makers of the Devin AI coding agent) was valued at $9B. Now it's raised $1B at a $25B pre-money valuation with $492M in annualized revenue.

That's not a rounding error — it's one of the fastest valuation climbs in recent startup history, and it signals that autonomous coding agents are generating real, recurring revenue at scale. Why it matters:When an AI coding startup doubles its valuation in under a year on the back of half a billion in ARR, the "AI is all hype" argument gets a lot harder to make.
