💡 An AI hallucination almost triggered a real US military operation — and that sentence should terrify you.

A new report reveals that a fabricated output from a large language model came dangerously close to setting off an actual US military operation. The model, the operation, and most of the details? Still classified. But the incident is already making waves in military AI policy circles — and it's not hard to see why.

This is what happens when you drop LLMs into high-stakes command and decision chains without proper verification layers. These models don't know what they don't know. They confabulate with confidence. That's a manageable quirk in a chatbot. It's a potential catastrophe in a war room.

A GovAI research scholar put it plainly: "It's important for service members to understand the uncertainty inherent to LLMs." That's a careful, academic way of saying — if you're trusting an AI to brief you on threats, you'd better have a human checking its homework.
