💡 Anthropic keeps warning AI could end the world — and just opened a biology lab anyway.

The company best known for publishing alarming research about its own models is now running a physical wet lab. Real scientists. Real experiments. Real biological material. Anthropic is betting that AI-accelerated drug discovery is too important to leave to someone else.

The tension here is hard to overstate. Anthropic's own researchers have published papers on how AI could lower the barrier to bioweapon development. Now that same company is directly bridging the gap between AI capability and physical biology — in-house. That's not a hypothetical risk profile anymore. It's an operational one.

The distance between "AI safety company" and "AI biosecurity liability" just got a lot shorter.
