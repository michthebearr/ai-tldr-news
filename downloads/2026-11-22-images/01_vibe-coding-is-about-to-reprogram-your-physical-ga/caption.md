💡 Vibe-coding just escaped your laptop — and it's coming for every gadget in your house.

LLMs made writing software embarrassingly easy. Now that same energy is targeting hardware. TVs, cameras, embedded devices — manufacturers are opening up APIs, and AI tools are getting sharp enough to work inside constrained environments. The wall between "I had an idea" and "I built a thing that runs on a physical device" is quietly crumbling.

David Pierce at The Verge draws a clean line to what happened to web development — except this time the timeline is compressed beyond recognition. What took the web a decade, vibe-coding is doing to hardware in real time.

The upside: millions of people building custom firmware and device integrations with zero traditional programming. The downside: security researchers are about to have the most stressful — and interesting — few years of their careers.
