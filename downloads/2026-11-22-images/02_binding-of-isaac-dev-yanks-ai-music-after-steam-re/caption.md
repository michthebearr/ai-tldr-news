🎵 A Reddit user found hidden AI music metadata in Binding of Isaac — and the internet immediately burned it down.

Buried inside audio files from *Binding of Isaac: Repentance Plus* was Suno metadata — the fingerprint of AI-generated music. One sharp-eyed Reddit user spotted it, the community spread it fast, and Steam reviews cratered almost overnight.

Creator Edmund McMillen didn't fight it. No statement about "augmenting creativity" or "early development tools." Just a quick announcement that the AI tracks would be pulled — full stop.

That's the real story here. The gaming community didn't need a regulator or a lawsuit. They had the review-bomb, and they used it. AI-generated content is increasingly detectable, and the reputational cost of getting caught is arriving faster than most studios seem to expect.
