💡 AI's energy bill is about to become everyone's problem.

A new analysis projects that US data centers could consume more natural gas than Germany and Japan *combined* by 2035. Let that sink in. Two of the world's largest economies — outpaced by servers running AI models.

Here's the core issue: each new generation of AI requires exponentially more compute, and the grid simply can't add renewables fast enough to keep up. So we're filling the gap with natural gas. A lot of it. The clean energy narrative the tech industry has been selling doesn't quite hold when you zoom out to these numbers.

This isn't an ESG footnote anymore. It's a geopolitical and infrastructure story — one that will shape energy policy, international relations, and how we build AI for the next generation.
