💡 Jensen Huang just told Trump — and the entire AI safety crowd — exactly where he stands.

While on a live call with President Trump, Nvidia's CEO made one thing unmistakably clear: AI is not slowing down. Not on his watch. It's a direct shot across the bow at Dario Amodei, and reportedly Altman and Musk too, who've been pushing for a more cautious pace of development.

Here's what makes this moment different from a typical CEO soundbite: Jensen Huang isn't just expressing an opinion. He controls the chips. The GPUs powering virtually every major AI lab on the planet run through Nvidia. When he says there won't be a slowdown, he's not forecasting the future — he's engineering it.

The optics were loud. The most powerful hardware supplier in AI, publicly aligned with a go-fast political agenda, on a live call with the sitting president.
