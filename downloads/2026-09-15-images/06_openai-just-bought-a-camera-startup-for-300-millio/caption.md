💰 OpenAI just dropped $300 million on a camera startup — and it says a lot about where they're headed.

Glass Imaging isn't a random bet. It was founded by former Apple engineers who literally built Portrait Mode — the feature that made iPhone cameras feel magical. OpenAI quietly acquiring that team for nine figures is a signal, not a coincidence.

Here's the context that makes this interesting: this deal comes right after OpenAI's "super app" launch landed with a thud. The company is clearly searching for a hardware wedge — some way to get GPT-level intelligence into your hands without depending on Apple or Google to let them play. A world-class computational photography engine paired with frontier vision AI? That's a real product kernel.

Could just be an expensive acqui-hire. But $300M is a lot to spend on talent alone.
