🏎️ Meta wants you to pay a monthly fee for AI — and with 3 billion users, it doesn't need a high conversion rate to win.

Meta One is the company's new subscription tier, bundling premium AI access across Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp into a single monthly fee. It builds on the rollout of Meta's Muse AI assistant and signals something bigger: Meta is done leaving money on the table with ads alone.

This is the same playbook Google and Apple are running — make AI the center of the platform, then make the free tier just uncomfortable enough that paying feels worth it. The difference is Meta's scale. Even a 1% conversion rate on 3 billion users is 30 million paying subscribers. The math writes itself.

A company that built its empire on free and ad-supported is now quietly becoming a subscription business. That's not a small pivot.
