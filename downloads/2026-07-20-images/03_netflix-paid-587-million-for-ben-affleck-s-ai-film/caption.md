💰 Netflix just dropped $587 million on Ben Affleck's AI filmmaking startup — and it tells you everything about where Hollywood is heading.

The acquisition is called InterPositive, an AI-assisted production company Affleck co-founded. Netflix didn't license the tech. They bought the whole thing outright for over half a billion dollars in cash. That's not a hedge — that's a bet.

The move signals that Netflix wants AI production tools baked into its own pipeline, not rented from a third party that could sell the same edge to Amazon or Disney tomorrow. Control the tools, control the economics.

Half a billion dollars for an AI Hollywood startup is Netflix saying the future of content production won't look like today — and they refuse to be caught paying someone else to figure that out.
