💡 China just dropped the biggest open-weight AI model in history — and they did it twice in one week.

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 landed with 2.8 trillion parameters, making it the largest open-weight model ever released. The same week, Alibaba's Qwen dropped its own frontier-class model. Both claim to match OpenAI and Anthropic's best. Both are cheap to run.

Kimi K3's edge isn't just size — it's architecture. Instead of throwing more compute at the problem, it bets on memory efficiency. That's a meaningful design choice, and if it holds up, it changes the cost equation for running frontier AI at scale.

America's lead is compressing. Fast.
