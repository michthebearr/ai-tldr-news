🔐 Hugging Face got breached — and if you have an access token, you need to act right now.

The AI world's most important platform just confirmed a security breach. Hugging Face — the GitHub of AI, where millions of models and datasets live — had internal systems compromised, exposing user credentials and sensitive dataset information stored on the platform.

This isn't just a "change your password" situation. Hugging Face sits at the center of thousands of research projects, enterprise pipelines, and production AI products. If tokens leaked, attackers could potentially access private models, poison datasets, or quietly compromise downstream systems that nobody's watching closely enough.

The blast radius here could be massive — and most affected users won't know it yet.

The company is urging everyone to rotate access tokens immediately and audit their account activity. Do it now, not later.
