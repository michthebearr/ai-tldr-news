💡 Jensen Huang just turned a Tokyo trip into a full-stack takeover of Japan's AI future.

Nvidia's CEO wrapped a high-profile Japan visit this week — and the deal sheet reads like someone bought the whole country's tech ecosystem in one checkout. Semiconductors, cloud, robotics, sovereign AI infrastructure. Every major player in Japan's tech stack now has Nvidia's fingerprints on it.

This isn't just business development. Japan is making a serious national bet on AI sovereignty — building the kind of domestic infrastructure that doesn't depend on foreign goodwill to stay online. And Nvidia just volunteered to be the backbone of all of it.

Getting in early matters enormously here. Every partnership Jensen signed makes it harder for AMD, Intel, or any other challenger to get a foothold. Japan didn't just pick a chip supplier — it picked a long-term infrastructure partner. That's a very different kind of lock-in.
