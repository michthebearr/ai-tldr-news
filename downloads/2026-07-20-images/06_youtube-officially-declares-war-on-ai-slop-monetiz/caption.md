💡 YouTube just made AI slop a lot less profitable.

YouTube updated its ad monetization policies this week to explicitly ban low-quality AI-generated content from earning revenue. We're talking the mass-produced, algorithm-gaming, copy-paste garbage that's been flooding the platform — gone from the money side of the equation.

The updated rules also flag repetitive and distressing videos that exist purely to rack up views. YouTube isn't just annoyed by this content — they're cutting off its oxygen.

This is the strongest platform-level crackdown on AI junk content we've seen yet. And if YouTube sets the standard, expect Instagram, TikTok, and others to follow fast. The era of printing money with AI-generated nothing is getting a hard expiration date.
