🧠 ChatGPT just got a glow-up — and the text-box era of AI is officially over.

OpenAI is rolling out what it's calling an "Intelligent UI" update, and it's a bigger deal than the name suggests. Instead of dumping walls of text on you, ChatGPT can now respond with interactive diagrams, charts, forms, and tappable buttons — built dynamically based on what you actually asked.

The quiet part? GPT-6 shipped alongside it. And it was trained specifically to know *when* to go visual versus when to just talk. That's not a UI tweak — that's a fundamentally different model of what an AI assistant should be.

Every competitor now has to answer a very uncomfortable question: why does yours still look like a text box?
