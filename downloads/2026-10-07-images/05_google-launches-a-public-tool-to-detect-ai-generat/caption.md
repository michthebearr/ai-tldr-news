💡 Google just made it easier to spot AI-generated content — with one major catch.

SynthID is now a public website. Upload an image, video, or audio clip, and Google's tool will tell you whether it was made by AI. It works by detecting invisible watermarks baked into content at the moment of creation — no metadata stripping, no workarounds. Clean, fast, accessible to anyone.

The problem? It only reliably catches content made with Google's own AI tools. Generate something with Midjourney, Sora, or literally anything outside Google's ecosystem, and SynthID shrugs. It's a real solution to a partial problem.

That's the story of AI detection right now — powerful in pockets, fragmented everywhere else.
