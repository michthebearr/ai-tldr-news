💡 Nous Research just raised $90M, hit a $1.5B valuation, and is coming for your company's workflow.

The open-weights lab behind the beloved Hermes model series closed a Series B this week and launched a suite of enterprise AI agents in the same breath. For a community-first lab that made its name giving powerful models away for free, this is a significant turn.

The pitch to business users puts Nous in direct competition with Cohere and Mistral — two companies that have been fighting for enterprise AI dollars for years. The difference is Nous arrives with serious street cred from the open-source world, which is a rare asset when you're trying to get developers to trust your paid products.

The real story here: the free-model era was never the end game — it was the on-ramp. Open weights built the audience. The enterprise product is how you cash the check.
