💡 Microsoft just dropped its MacBook Pro killer — and it starts at $2,599.

The Surface Laptop Ultra is here, powered by Nvidia's new RTX Spark Arm-based chip and shipping October 16th. Dell, Lenovo, Asus, HP, and MSI are all following with their own RTX Spark machines — top configs push all the way to $7,000. This isn't a refresh. It's a full bet on AI hardware as a premium category.

The bigger story is what's running on these chips. Microsoft's upgraded Copilot can now access your local files and take actions across Windows — what they're calling "Hybrid Intelligence." Local AI inference is no longer a spec sheet bullet point. It's the product.

Microsoft is being blunt about who they're coming for. The MacBook Pro comparison is their framing, not ours.
