💡 Meta is using AI to stop predators from hiding child abuse material behind normal-looking ads.

Here's the tactic: bad actors were running ads that appeared completely legitimate on Meta's platform — but the moment you clicked, you were redirected to child sexual abuse material hosted somewhere else entirely. The ad itself passed moderation. The destination didn't.

Meta has now built and deployed new AI detection models trained specifically to catch this redirect gap — analyzing not just what an ad looks like, but where it actually sends you. That's a meaningfully different technical challenge than standard content moderation.

The uncomfortable truth in this announcement: the tactic was already being used at scale before it was caught. Which means the gap was real, it was known by the people exploiting it, and it took a major platform this long to close it.
