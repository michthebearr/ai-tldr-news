💡 Firefox just added two privacy features Chrome should've shipped years ago.

Mozilla dropped Firefox 161 this week, and it's a quiet but meaningful update. The headline additions: per-site VPN rules and a one-click history wipe. Small on the surface. Big in practice.

The VPN control is the smarter feature. Instead of toggling your VPN on and off every time you switch between sites, you can now set permanent always-on or always-off rules per domain. It's the kind of friction removal that makes a tool actually usable. The "Fire" button does exactly what it sounds like — one click, history gone. No menus, no confirmation spirals.

Chrome has the market. Firefox is building the features. At a moment when browser market share is finally showing some cracks, Mozilla is making a clear play for privacy-conscious power users who want tools that respect them.

Sometimes the underdog earns it.
