💡 Jensen Huang is back at Nvidia — and already doing damage control.

Huang held his first all-hands meeting since returning as CEO, apologizing to staff and flatly denying reports that Trump and Silicon Valley insiders engineered his comeback. His message: "not true." The problem? The timing, the optics, and the company he's been keeping make that a very hard sell.

The meeting was leaked almost immediately — Bloomberg, NYT, and The Handbasket all had it within hours. So not only is Huang fighting the political entanglement narrative, he's now dealing with the fact that someone in that room didn't want his version of events to be the only one out there.

This matters beyond the drama. Nvidia is the most strategically important AI chip company on the planet. Any whiff of political strings attached to its leadership makes foreign partners nervous and gives regulators in Brussels and Beijing fresh ammunition. Governance questions at Nvidia aren't just corporate gossip — they're a geopolitical event.
