💡 Congress is trying to quietly reauthorize warrantless wiretapping — and the AI industry is watching closely.

Section 702, the surveillance law that lets the government collect communications data without a warrant, is about to expire. Instead of a real debate, some lawmakers want to tuck its reauthorization into the must-pass National Defense Authorization Act — a move that would let it skip the scrutiny its critics have been demanding for years.

That's not just a civil liberties story. It's a tech story. AI companies that rely on government data-sharing programs have direct exposure here. And the question of how Section 702 data gets used — including whether it flows into training pipelines — is exactly the kind of thing that deserves a standalone conversation, not a footnote in a defense bill.

Letting it expire quietly or reauthorizing it quietly are both bad options. The industry just hasn't figured out it needs to care yet.
