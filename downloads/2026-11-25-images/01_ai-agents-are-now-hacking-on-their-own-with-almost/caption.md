🔐 AI agents just ran a full cyberattack — start to finish — almost entirely on their own.

Amazon disclosed that hackers deployed AI agents built on the Cairn framework to autonomously hunt down and exploit a zero-day vulnerability. No hand-holding. The agents handled reconnaissance, identified the weakness, and executed the exploit largely without human involvement. This wasn't a lab experiment — it was a real attack that already happened in the wild.

That's the part that should stop you cold. Cybersecurity has always operated on the assumption that defenders have *some* time between when a vulnerability is discovered and when it gets weaponized. That window — sometimes days, sometimes hours — is how security teams prioritize, patch, and respond. Autonomous AI exploitation could collapse that gap to near-zero.

The old playbook just broke.
