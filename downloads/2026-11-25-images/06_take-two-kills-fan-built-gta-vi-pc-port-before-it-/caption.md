💡 A 15-year-old built a GTA VI PC port — Take-Two killed it before it even launched.

A teenager reverse-engineered a leaked Xbox build of GTA VI and started porting it to Windows natively. The project was called Leonida. Take-Two hit it with a DMCA takedown fast enough to make it clear they were watching.

Rockstar hasn't announced a PC release for GTA VI. That silence is a business decision — staggered launches mean double the sales window, and PC is where modding and piracy eat into revenue hardest. A working fan port threatens that entire strategy before it can even play out.

What's new this time: AI-assisted modding tools are making these projects faster and more viable than ever. Take-Two isn't just swatting one kid — they're signaling to an entire community that they'll litigate the PC timeline into existence on their terms.
