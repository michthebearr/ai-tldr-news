💡 Huawei just got hit with a criminal conviction for stealing trade secrets — and the timing couldn't be worse for the company.

A federal jury in New York found Huawei guilty of stealing proprietary tech from T-Mobile, Cisco, and Motorola. We're talking source code and core networking IP — the kind of technology that doesn't just power today's telecom infrastructure, it's increasingly foundational to AI infrastructure too.

This wasn't a quiet civil settlement. It's a criminal conviction, and that distinction matters enormously. The DOJ first indicted Huawei back in 2018, and six years later, the verdict lands like a hammer.

The ripple effects are the real story here. US regulators and allied governments now have hard legal cover to keep Huawei locked out of American chips, cloud services, and global telecom markets — not just political justification, but a courtroom verdict to point to.
