🧠 OpenAI complied with a government AI restriction — then immediately went on record saying it was bad policy.

GPT-5.6's rollout was quietly pulled back after a government request. OpenAI didn't just accept it — they posted a public statement calling this kind of gatekeeping out by name, warning that restricting access "keeps the best tools from users, developers, enterprises, cyber defenders, and global partners who need them."

That's a pointed message. OpenAI has spent months building bridges with the current administration — White House meetings, infrastructure deals, the whole thing. Now they're publicly signaling that government-controlled AI access crosses a line they're not willing to quietly accept.

Comply and complain. That's a strategy.
