🏎️ OpenAI just built its own chip — and Nvidia should be paying attention.

The chip is called "Jalapeño." It's OpenAI's custom inference processor, built in partnership with Broadcom, and it joins a roster that already includes Google, Apple, and SpaceX. Everyone, apparently, is done waiting on Nvidia.

Here's the key detail: this isn't about training models. Nvidia still owns that game. This is about inference — the moment an AI actually responds to your prompt — which is where the real money gets spent at scale. Custom silicon can slash those costs dramatically, and when you're running millions of queries a day, that math changes everything.

When the biggest names in tech all independently reach the same conclusion — build your own chips — that's not a trend. That's a structural shift. Nvidia's pricing power just got a lot harder to defend.
