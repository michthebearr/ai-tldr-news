🧠 OpenAI just raided Uber India's top exec — and the reason why tells you everything about where AI is heading.

OpenAI has poached the head of Uber India to lead its India operations, its largest market outside the United States. This isn't a symbolic hire. It's a signal that India has moved from "nice to have" to the core of OpenAI's global growth strategy.

And the timing makes sense. U.S. growth is maturing. Export restrictions are making China and other markets complicated. India — with its massive young population, booming tech sector, and voracious appetite for AI tools — is the most important scale play left on the board.

OpenAI is finally staffing up like it actually believes that.
