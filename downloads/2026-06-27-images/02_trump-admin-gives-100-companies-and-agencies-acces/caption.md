💡 The Trump admin just handed 100+ companies and agencies the keys to Anthropic's most powerful model — and not everyone gets in.

The administration has quietly authorized over 100 U.S. companies and government agencies to access Mythos 5, Anthropic's latest model. That includes access for non-American employees. This isn't a public release — it's a curated list of approved insiders getting something the rest of the world can't touch.

That's the whole game here. A two-tier system where the best AI is a privilege, not a utility. Approved? You're in. Everyone else? Wait your turn.

And OpenAI is already calling this out publicly. They've been pushing back against exactly this kind of access regime — where government-sanctioned gatekeeping decides who competes and who doesn't. When the most capable models become political assets, the AI race stops being just about technology.
