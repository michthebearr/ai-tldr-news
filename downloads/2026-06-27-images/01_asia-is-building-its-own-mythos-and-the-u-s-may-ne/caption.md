🏎️ Asia stopped waiting for America's permission — and now the U.S. might never get those customers back.

Anthropic's export restrictions on Mythos were supposed to protect U.S. AI dominance. Instead, they handed well-funded Asian competitors exactly what they needed: time, motivation, and a market full of frustrated customers looking for alternatives.

New models are launching across Asia claiming Mythos-level performance — with zero export restriction baggage attached. The pitch is simple: same capability, no geopolitical strings. And it's working. Users aren't waiting around for Washington to sort out its policy. They're signing contracts with whoever shows up.

The window to reclaim that market isn't just closing. For some of those customers, it may already be shut.
