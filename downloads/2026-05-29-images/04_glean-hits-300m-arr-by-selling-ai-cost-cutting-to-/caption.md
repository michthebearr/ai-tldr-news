💡 Glean Hits $300M ARR by Selling AI Cost-Cutting to Budget-Squeezed Enterprises.

Enterprise AI search startup Glean tripled its annual revenue to over $300M, even as Microsoft, Google, and ServiceNow moved into its lane.

Its pitch has evolved: Glean now sells itself as a way to consolidate and eliminate other SaaS tools, not just search them. Why it matters:The fact that "help us cut AI spend" is now a growth vector proves enterprises are drowning in redundant AI tools — and whoever unifies that mess wins.
