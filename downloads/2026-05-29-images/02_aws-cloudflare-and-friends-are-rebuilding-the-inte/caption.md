💡 AWS, Cloudflare, and Friends Are Rebuilding the Internet for AI Agents.

AWS, Cloudflare, and other infrastructure giants are redesigning the cloud for a future where machine-generated traffic dwarfs human traffic.

That means new protocols, new routing logic, and new pricing structures built around agents calling APIs — not people clicking links. Why it matters:The infrastructure layer is where the real power concentrates, and whoever owns the pipes for AI agent traffic will have enormous leverage over how the agentic internet works.
