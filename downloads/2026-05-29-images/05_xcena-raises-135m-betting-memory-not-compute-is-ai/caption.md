💰 XCENA Raises $135M Betting Memory — Not Compute — Is AI's Real Constraint.

South Korean chip startup XCENA closed a $135M round at a $570M valuation with a contrarian thesis: the real bottleneck in AI inference and training isn't GPUs, it's memory bandwidth and capacity.

They're building chips specifically optimized to fix that. Why it matters:If XCENA is right, the next wave of AI hardware investment flows toward memory architecture, not more raw compute — a major reorientation for the entire semiconductor industry.
