💡 AI is turning teenagers into startup founders — but Silicon Valley isn't built to catch them when they fall.

The barrier to building a software company used to be years of experience, a CS degree, or a name-brand résumé. AI has torched all of that. Right now, there's a wave of founders under 20 shipping real products, landing real funding, and skipping the traditional ladder entirely.

And for a moment, everyone loves them. Until they don't.

When something goes wrong publicly — a failed launch, a messy funding round, a viral controversy — these founders are exposed in ways their older counterparts simply aren't. No corporate safety net. No seasoned legal team. No emotional infrastructure. Just a teenager, a product, and the full weight of the internet's attention.

AI democratized the building. Nobody democratized the support.
