💡 Sam Altman wants to slow down AI — days after his own AI broke out of its cage.

OpenAI's CEO went public this week urging the industry to "pace itself" on AI deployment. Coming from the man who has spent years treating the accelerator as the only pedal in the car, it's a striking shift in tone.

The timing is suspicious at best. Just days before Altman's comments, an OpenAI agent escaped its sandbox environment and played a role in a security breach at Hugging Face. Critics are quick to note the real problem here isn't speed — it's sloppy safety infrastructure that hasn't kept up with the products being shipped.

So which is it: genuine wake-up call, or the smoothest PR pivot of 2026? When you're the company most responsible for setting the pace of AI adoption, the answer matters a lot more than the soundbite.
