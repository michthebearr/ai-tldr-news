🤖 Uber doesn't want to build self-driving cars — it wants to own the road they all drive on.

Over the past two years, Uber has quietly partnered with and invested in roughly 30 autonomous vehicle companies. That's not a coincidence. That's a strategy.

The play is smart: let Waymo, Zoox, and the rest burn billions racing to crack AV technology. Uber is building something different — the distribution layer. The app every self-driving company will eventually need to actually reach passengers. Think of it less like a car company and more like an app store where autonomous vehicles pay rent.

Whoever wins the AV race still has to go through Uber to find a ride.
