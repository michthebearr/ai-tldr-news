💡 Apple just gave Siri superpowers — and cracked down on AI girlfriends in the same breath.

iOS 27.3 is opening Siri up to every third-party app on your phone. We're talking personal context, natural language understanding, on-screen awareness — the whole package. Developers can now build Siri directly into their apps, turning Apple's assistant into something that actually knows what you're doing and can help you do it, anywhere.

That's a massive platform play. Apple isn't just improving Siri. It's making Siri the connective tissue of the entire iOS ecosystem.

But at the same time, Apple quietly updated App Store rules to slap 18+ ratings on AI companion and dating apps with romantic or sexual content. So yes — Apple is expanding AI's reach with one hand and tightening the leash on AI relationships with the other.

Both moves point to the same thing: Apple is done playing passive host. It's actively shaping what AI looks like on iOS — who gets access, what gets built, and where the lines are drawn.
