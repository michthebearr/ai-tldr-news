🔐 CrowdStrike just dropped $500M to own the future of AI security — before the hackers do.

The company is acquiring two AI-focused startups: Fiddler AI, which monitors AI agents in real time, and Dreadnode, which automates security testing against AI systems. Half a billion dollars. Two bets. One very clear message.

The AI agent era is here — and it's a security nightmare waiting to happen. As companies hand more autonomy to AI agents, the attack surface doesn't just grow, it transforms. Traditional security tools weren't built for this. CrowdStrike is betting that dedicated observability and red-teaming infrastructure becomes non-negotiable for every enterprise running agentic AI.

This is a land grab. The category barely exists yet — and that's exactly the point.
