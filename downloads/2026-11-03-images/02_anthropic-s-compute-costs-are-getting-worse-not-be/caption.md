💡 Anthropic is spending *more* per unit of AI output as it scales — and that's the opposite of how this was supposed to work.

At this stage of growth, efficiency gains should be kicking in. Compute costs should be falling relative to output. That's the standard playbook for tech infrastructure at scale. Anthropic's numbers are moving in the wrong direction.

That matters enormously because Anthropic's entire business model rests on a bet: that future revenue will eventually justify today's eye-watering infrastructure spend. If compute margins keep deteriorating, that bet gets harder to win — no matter how impressive Claude becomes or how many enterprise deals they close.

Great models don't save a structurally broken cost curve.
