💡 Google just told schools: take the AI, or lose Search entirely.

Google updated its policy for educational accounts this week — technically giving schools the ability to restrict AI features. Sounds reasonable. Here's the problem: AI is now so deeply embedded in Google Search that opting out means losing Search access altogether.

That's not a choice. That's a trap.

Administrators are stuck between accepting AI tools they may not have evaluated for their students, or walking away from one of the most used platforms in modern education. And this is bigger than schools — it's a preview of how AI bundling will quietly redraw the lines of platform power across every industry.
