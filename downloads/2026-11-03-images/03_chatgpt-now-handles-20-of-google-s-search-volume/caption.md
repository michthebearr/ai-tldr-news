🧠 ChatGPT is now handling 20% of Google's search volume — and that number should terrify Mountain View.

OpenAI's consumer product lead dropped this stat at a press event this week, and it landed like a grenade. Two years ago, this comparison would've been a joke. Today, it's a milestone. ChatGPT search queries now equal one-fifth of what Google processes daily.

The fine print matters: OpenAI counts any query that triggers a web lookup as a "search," so it's not a perfect apples-to-apples comparison. But here's the thing — the methodology debate misses the point entirely.

"Google it" is quietly becoming "ask ChatGPT." That behavioral shift is real, it's accelerating, and this is the first hard number that puts a figure on exactly how much ground Google has already lost.
