💡 The picks-and-shovels of AI just minted another giant — and most people have never heard of them.

Fal, a five-year-old inference startup, just raised $1.3B led by Bessemer at a $16B valuation. That's a double in a single round. If you don't know what inference infrastructure is, here's the short version: it's the plumbing that actually delivers AI model outputs to real applications. Every time an app "runs" an AI feature, something like Fal is making that happen.

Everyone's racing to ship AI products. That means demand for inference capacity is exploding — and supply hasn't caught up. Fal sits right at that bottleneck, which is exactly where you want to be when billions in enterprise budgets are suddenly chasing the same scarce resource.

The quiet layer of the AI stack is getting loud.
