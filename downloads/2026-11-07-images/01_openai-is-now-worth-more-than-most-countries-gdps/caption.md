🧠 OpenAI is now worth more than most countries' entire economies.

To put the number in perspective: OpenAI is now valued higher than ExxonMobil, JPMorgan, and Walmart. And Bloomberg reports the round hasn't even closed yet — and they're already hunting for more capital on top of it.

The AI money printer is not slowing down.
