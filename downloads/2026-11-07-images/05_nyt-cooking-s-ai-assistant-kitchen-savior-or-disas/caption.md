💡 NYT Cooking just added an AI assistant — and it's one bad baking tip away from ruining your life.

The New York Times Cooking app now has a built-in AI helper, and The Verge ran it through a full stress test. For weeknight improvisation — swapping ingredients, scaling recipes, figuring out what to do with half a zucchini — it was genuinely impressive. The kind of useful that makes you think AI in the kitchen might actually stick.

Then came the baking test. The assistant gave confidently wrong guidance on a recipe, the classic hallucination problem dressed up in an apron. And here's why that's a bigger deal than it sounds: savory cooking is forgiving. Baking is chemistry. Wrong ratios aren't a vibe issue — they're a collapsed cake or a ruined birthday issue.

This is the exact tension defining AI assistants right now. Not whether they're useful — they often are. But whether users know when to trust them.
