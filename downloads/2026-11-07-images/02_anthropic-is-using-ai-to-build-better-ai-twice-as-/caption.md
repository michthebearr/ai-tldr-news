💡 Anthropic is using AI to build better AI — and it's accelerating faster than anyone expected.

A new 18-month evaluation from AI safety org METR found that Anthropic has been using its own models to automate AI development internally. The outcome? Its models are improving roughly twice as fast as they were just a year ago — with zero signs of slowing down.

This is the recursive self-improvement loop researchers have been warning about for years. AI helping build smarter AI, which then helps build even smarter AI. It's not a thought experiment anymore. It's Tuesday at Anthropic HQ.

The fact that this is coming from a safety-focused lab makes it both reassuring and deeply unsettling. Anthropic knows the risks better than almost anyone — and they're still hitting the gas.
