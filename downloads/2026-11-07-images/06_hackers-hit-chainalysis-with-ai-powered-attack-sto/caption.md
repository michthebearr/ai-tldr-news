🔐 Hackers used AI agents to breach the company that helps governments catch crypto criminals — and stole every customer's credentials.

Chainalysis, the blockchain analytics firm that essentially serves as the intelligence backbone for crypto crime investigations worldwide, just disclosed a serious multifront cyberattack. The attackers deployed AI agents alongside on-path data interception to rip out credentials and session tokens for their entire customer base. Not some. All of them.

That's a catastrophic outcome on its own. But the context makes it worse. Chainalysis's clients include law enforcement agencies, regulators, and financial institutions actively using the platform to trace illicit crypto flows. Whoever now holds those credentials has a roadmap into some of the most sensitive blockchain investigations on the planet.

This is what AI-augmented attacks look like when they graduate from the whitepaper to the real world.
