💡 Google just bought a data center project that was already struggling — and that tells you everything about the AI infrastructure race.

Crusoe Energy, the startup that went from mining flare gas to building AI data centers, has sold its Wyoming project to Google. The deal comes after the project hit serious development headwinds — cost overruns, construction delays, the brutal reality of turning gigawatt-scale ambitions into actual steel and concrete.

This isn't a story about one startup stumbling. It's a story about how hard it is to build AI infrastructure fast enough to matter. Even well-funded companies with strong backers are finding out that announcing a data center and actually delivering one are two very different things.

Google's move? Absorb the troubled asset and keep it moving. When you're desperate for capacity, someone else's problem becomes your opportunity.
