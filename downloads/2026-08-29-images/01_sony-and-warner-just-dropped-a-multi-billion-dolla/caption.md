💰 Sony and Warner just sued Anthropic for potentially billions — and the music industry isn't playing nice anymore.

Sony Music and Warner Chappell have filed suit against Anthropic in California federal court, claiming Claude was trained on and outputs "tens of thousands" of copyrighted songs without permission. The damages ask is staggering: up to $150,000 per infringed work, plus $25,000 every time copyright metadata was stripped from a song.

Do the math. It gets uncomfortable fast.

What makes this different from past AI copyright battles is the framing. This isn't a nuanced fair use debate — the labels are calling it piracy, full stop. If that argument lands in court, it doesn't just hurt Anthropic. It forces every major AI lab to rethink how they've been building their models from the ground up.

This is the music industry's most aggressive legal strike on an AI company yet. The precedent it could set might be the most expensive lesson Silicon Valley has ever had to learn.
