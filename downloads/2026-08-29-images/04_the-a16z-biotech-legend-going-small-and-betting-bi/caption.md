💡 The biotech VC legend who managed $4 billion just went small on purpose — and it says everything about where AI meets biology right now.

Vijay Pande built a16z's biotech practice into one of the most powerful in the game. Then he walked away to launch VZVC — a leaner, AI-native fund built on a very specific thesis: biology is no longer just a discovery science. It's becoming an engineering one.

That shift sounds subtle. It isn't. If you can engineer biology the way you engineer software, the entire investment logic changes. Smaller teams. Faster iterations. Asymmetric bets. Which is exactly why Pande thinks a nimble fund beats a mega-fund in this moment.

But he's not drinking his own Kool-Aid blindly. He's clear-eyed that AI can't fix the clinical trial bottleneck — the slow, expensive gauntlet that kills promising drugs regardless of how good your model is. His real conviction: open, shared medical datasets will do more for bio-AI than any proprietary walled garden ever could.

When someone this credible downsizes by choice, pay attention.
