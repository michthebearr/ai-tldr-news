💡 Nvidia isn't just building the fastest chips anymore — it's building a trap.

The company is quietly shifting its strategy beyond raw GPU performance. The new focus: network traffic management, system-level design, and making the entire data center — not just the processor — Nvidia's product.

That's a much harder thing to compete against. Any well-funded rival can eventually reverse-engineer a chip. But when Nvidia's software, networking, and silicon are all deeply entangled with each other? Ripping it out becomes an infrastructure nightmare. Switching costs go from annoying to existential.

This is what a real moat looks like.
