💡 Europe isn't asking how powerful AI can get — it's asking who's responsible when it goes wrong.

At TechBBQ, the Nordic region's biggest tech conference, one question kept surfacing in every panel, pitch, and hallway conversation: who's actually in charge here? Not in a philosophical sense. In a very practical, very urgent one.

While Silicon Valley obsesses over benchmarks and speed, Europe's startup ecosystem is locked onto governance and accountability. Founders and investors aren't just asking what their AI systems can do — they're asking whether anyone genuinely understands what those systems are doing right now. That's a different conversation entirely.

And it matters beyond Europe's borders. When an entire continent's tech culture is wired around control and accountability — and that continent also happens to be the world's most aggressive AI regulator — the philosophical becomes the political. Fast.
