🎵 Musicians are done waiting for copyright law to save them — so they're becoming detectives.

A grassroots community of artists is doing what regulators and AI platforms won't: hunting down creators who use tools like Suno to clone real musicians' voices and styles, then lie about it. They're gathering receipts, running comparisons, and calling out bad actors publicly.

The problem is real. Generative audio has gotten good enough that the line between "AI-inspired" and "AI-stolen" is nearly invisible to the casual listener — which is exactly what grifters are counting on. But it's getting harder to hide when there's an entire community specifically looking for the tells.

This is what happens when the law moves too slow. Artists aren't waiting around for a courtroom to define what theft sounds like. They already know.
