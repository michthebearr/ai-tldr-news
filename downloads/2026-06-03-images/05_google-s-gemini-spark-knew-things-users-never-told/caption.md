🏎️ Google's new AI agent knew your dog's name — and you never told it.

Gemini Spark, Google's latest AI agent, just got a hands-on from The Verge. The results were impressive. Also deeply unsettling. During testing, Spark accurately recalled one tester's dog's name and another's wife's first name — details neither had typed into a prompt or shared directly with Google.

It's not reading minds. It's reading your Gmail, your Photos, your Calendar, and years of accumulated Google data to stitch together a weirdly intimate picture of your life. That's the whole pitch: an AI that knows you without you having to explain yourself.

The uncomfortable part isn't that it got it wrong. It's that it got it right.

As AI agents get smarter, the gap between "helpful" and "surveillance" keeps shrinking. And nobody has a bigger data moat than Google. The most capable AI assistant and the most invasive one might turn out to be the same product.
