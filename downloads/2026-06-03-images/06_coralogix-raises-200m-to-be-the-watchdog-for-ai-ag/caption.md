💰 Someone needs to babysit your AI agents — and Coralogix just raised $200M to do it.

As AI agents move from demos into real enterprise environments, a new problem is emerging: nobody can actually see what they're doing. Are they failing silently? Making bad decisions? Drifting off-script? Right now, most companies have no idea.

Coralogix is betting that observability — the layer that monitors, tracks, and diagnoses agent behavior in production — becomes one of the most critical pieces of the entire AI stack. Not the flashy part. The unglamorous, essential part.

Infrastructure for the infrastructure. And investors just wrote a very large check for it.
