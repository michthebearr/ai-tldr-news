🧠 Microsoft just broke up with OpenAI — and launched a full-on rival in the same week.

At Build this year, Microsoft didn't just show off new features. It revealed a completely rebuilt AI stack: in-house reasoning models, autonomous agents, a "super app," and a new cybersecurity tool — none of it dependent on OpenAI. For years, Microsoft *was* OpenAI to most enterprise customers. That's over.

The Scout personal assistant, portable agent policy files, and an open-source eval framework called ASSERT all dropped in the same 48-hour window. That's not a product refresh. That's a declaration.

Microsoft went from biggest backer to biggest competitor. Build 2025 was the coming-out party.
