💰 Suno just raised $400M at a $5.4B valuation — while actively fighting copyright lawsuits from the biggest names in music.

Seven months ago, Suno was valued at $2.45B. Now it's $5.4B. That's more than double, mid-litigation, with major record labels still in the ring. By any traditional logic, that's not when you raise a monster round. Apparently nobody told the investors.

The message this sends to the broader AI content space is loud: legal risk is no longer the kill shot it used to be. If you're building fast enough, growing hard enough, and the product is compelling enough — the market will look past the lawsuits and write the check anyway.

Copyright liability used to be a dealbreaker. Now it's a line item.
