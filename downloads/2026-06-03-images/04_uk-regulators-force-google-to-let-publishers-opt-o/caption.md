💡 UK regulators just handed publishers a weapon against Google's AI search — and it could reshape the internet.

The UK's Competition and Markets Authority is forcing Google to build a dedicated opt-out tool — one that lets website publishers block their content from being used in AI-generated search summaries. Crucially, this is separate from existing web crawl opt-outs, meaning publishers finally get a real choice: appear in search, but not get harvested for AI Overviews.

Publishers have been furious for months. AI Overviews answer questions directly on the search page, meaning users never click through. Traffic disappears. Revenue follows. This is the first time a regulator has stepped in with actual teeth.

The tool gets piloted in the UK first — then goes global. That's the part Google should be nervous about.

If this becomes the standard in the UK, every other market will feel the pressure to follow. What starts as a British regulatory experiment could quietly rewrite the rules of AI search everywhere.
