🎵 Google is quietly turning your searches into AI training data — and you're opted in by default.

A new "Search Services History" setting is now live, and it saves everything: photos you search with Google Lens, real-time audio from Search Live, voice queries, and Translate recordings. The stated reason? AI training. The default setting? On.

You can turn it off. But most people won't — because most people won't even know it exists. That's not an accident.

Here's the bigger picture: Google processes billions of searches every day across text, image, voice, and now live video. No AI lab in the world has a data pipeline like that. By converting everyday user behavior into training fuel, Google is quietly building a structural moat that pure-play AI companies simply can't replicate.
