🏎️ This AI can simulate hours of photorealistic driving — no car, no road, no risk.

Decart just dropped Oasis 3, a real-time world model that generates photorealistic driving environments for autonomous vehicle testing. The big leap: it can now run for hours, not seconds. And it's available via API, meaning any AV team can plug in and start stress-testing edge cases without ever leaving the office.

That's a bigger deal than it sounds. Getting real-world miles is one of the most expensive bottlenecks in autonomous vehicle development — Waymo has spent years and billions accumulating them. A photorealistic, API-accessible simulator could hand smaller teams a serious shortcut.

The caveats are real — physical accuracy and long-run consistency still have limits. But the direction is clear: synthetic data is coming for one of AV's most stubborn cost problems.
