🔐 Anthropic's newest model shipped with two different groups already furious — and it's only been 24 hours.

Claude Fable 5 is Anthropic's most powerful release yet. It's also already a case study in how to alienate your most important users out of the gate. Microsoft quietly blocked internal employee access to the model over Anthropic's new data retention requirements — even while simultaneously rolling it out to GitHub Copilot and Azure Foundry customers. That's a awkward split.

Meanwhile, the cybersecurity community is louder about their frustration. Researchers doing legitimate pen testing and vulnerability work say Fable 5's guardrails are so aggressive the model is basically useless for professional security use. These aren't bad actors — they're paying customers with real workflows.

When a model manages to alienate enterprise buyers and a core technical user base on day one, that's not a guardrail problem. That's a product-market fit problem.
