🎵 Warner Music just stopped complaining about AI — and started building weapons against it.

Warner Music Group has acquired Sureel AI, a startup that detects when copyrighted music gets scraped for model training or used inside AI-generated content. This isn't a press release about "responsible AI." It's an infrastructure play — WMG now has the technical tools to track exactly where its catalog shows up in AI workflows.

That's a big shift. For the past two years, major labels have mostly fought AI through lawsuits and lobbying. Warner is now doing something different: building the detection layer first, so monetization — or litigation — can follow. If you can prove your music was used, you have leverage.

The music industry just grew a new set of teeth.
