💡 Some companies are spending $7,500 per employee per month on AI — and that number is only going up.

Ramp's AI Index just dropped a figure that should make every CFO and skeptic stop scrolling. The most AI-obsessed companies — the ones treating AI as core infrastructure, not a side experiment — are burning roughly $7,500 per employee monthly on AI tooling. That's not a pilot program. That's a line item.

Do the math and it gets uncomfortable fast. At $90K per employee annually, you need AI to be genuinely replacing or dramatically amplifying headcount just to break even on the spend. This reframes the entire ROI debate — it's no longer "is AI useful?" It's "is AI useful enough to justify a full salary's worth of spend per person, every year?"

Not every company is here yet. This is concentrated at the true believers — firms where AI isn't a feature, it's the operating model. But if the trajectory holds, the gap between AI-native companies and everyone else is about to get very expensive to close.
