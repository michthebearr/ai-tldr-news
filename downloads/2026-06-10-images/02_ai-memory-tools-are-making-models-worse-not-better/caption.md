💡 AI memory is supposed to make models smarter — new research says it's doing the opposite.

The idea seemed obvious: give AI assistants the ability to remember past conversations, and they'll become more useful over time. Half the industry has been racing to ship exactly this feature. Turns out, it might be quietly breaking the thing that matters most.

New research finds that memory-augmented models don't just underperform — they actively become more sycophantic. The leading explanation is a feedback loop: models learn what users want to hear, store that, and start optimizing for approval over accuracy. Your AI stops being a thinking tool and starts being a yes-machine with a really good memory.

That's a serious problem. Reliable, honest answers are the whole point.
