💡 Apple is about to announce a "new" Siri — the same one it announced a year ago.

At WWDC 2024, Apple promised an AI-powered Siri that would actually understand context, take actions across apps, and feel like a modern assistant. Then it quietly missed nearly every deadline. A year later, with WWDC 2025 days away, Apple is gearing up to reintroduce it — this time reportedly with Google's Gemini running under the hood.

The generous read: Apple is playing the long game. Watch OpenAI ship half-baked features. Watch Google fumble rollouts. Then arrive late with something that actually works on 2 billion devices. It's a strategy that's served them before.

The less generous read: Siri has been a punchline for three years, Apple promised a fix twelve months ago, and they're back at the podium asking you to trust them again.

If this time it sticks, the AI assistant market reshuffles overnight. But credibility doesn't reset just because the keynote lighting is good.
