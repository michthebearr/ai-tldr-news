💡 Meta built an AI-generated news feed — and it already can't tell there's only one Queen Elizabeth.

Meta's standalone AI app just launched a "For You" section that surfaces AI-generated articles, AI-generated images, and AI-generated text. All of it, fed directly into your feed. The debut content includes a photo of the British royal family featuring two Queen Elizabeths standing side by side.

That's not a glitch they caught in testing. That's how it shipped.

Here's why that should alarm you: we already watched Facebook's human-written clickbait era warp public perception at a global scale. Meta just automated that entire pipeline. No editors. No fact-checkers. Just an AI churning out plausible-looking content at industrial speed and dropping it in front of billions of people.

The misinformation problem didn't get solved. It got a factory.
