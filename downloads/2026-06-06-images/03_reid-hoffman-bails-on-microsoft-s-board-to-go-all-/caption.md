🏥 Reid Hoffman just quit Microsoft's board to go build an AI startup — and that tells you everything.

After a very profitable decade as a Microsoft board member, LinkedIn's co-founder is stepping down to go full founder mode at Manus, his AI drug discovery company. No more advising from a leather chair. He wants skin in the game.

This isn't just a career move — it's a signal. When one of Silicon Valley's loudest AI evangelists trades a cushy board seat to actually build something, it says the real upside isn't in oversight. It's in ownership.

Boardrooms are for the last wave. Founders are for this one.
