💰 Google is paying SpaceX $920 million *per month* for compute — and that number should terrify you.

That's $11 billion a year. Going to a rocket company. From the most powerful cloud infrastructure operator on the planet.

Google's own spokesperson called it "unexpected demand" from its recently launched AI products. Translation: the AI boom hit so fast and so hard that even Google — with its custom TPUs, its massive data centers, its decades of infrastructure investment — couldn't keep up. So they cut a check to Elon Musk's space company instead.

Let that sink in. When Google can't handle its own AI workload, the compute crisis isn't a startup problem anymore. It's an industry-wide reckoning.
