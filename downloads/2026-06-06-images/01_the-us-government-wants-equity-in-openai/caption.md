🧠 The US government wants a piece of OpenAI — and that should make everyone a little uncomfortable.

President Trump confirmed this week that his administration is in active talks to give the American public a financial stake in AI companies. OpenAI is reportedly at the top of the list, and the timing is deliberate — the company just completed its restructuring from nonprofit to for-profit, cementing itself as one of the most valuable private companies on the planet.

The White House pitch is classic populism: "the American people should benefit from AI's success." Hard to argue with the soundbite. But a government equity stake in a frontier AI lab is completely unprecedented — and the questions it raises are enormous. Who sets research priorities? What happens to regulatory oversight when Uncle Sam is a shareholder with skin in the game?

Oversight and ownership don't mix well.
