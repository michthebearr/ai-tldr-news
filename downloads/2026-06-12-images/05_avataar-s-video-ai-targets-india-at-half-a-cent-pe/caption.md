📱 Video AI just got a Jio moment — and it's coming out of India.

Avataar has launched a video generation model priced at $0.005 per second of output. Half a cent. For a second of AI-generated video. That's not a rounding error — that's a deliberate attack on the price barrier that's kept video AI out of reach for most of the world.

But this isn't just a cheap Western model with a new flag on it. Avataar built cultural awareness for Indian contexts directly into the model — targeting the creators, brands, and commerce platforms operating at India's scale. That's a meaningful distinction. Price without relevance doesn't move markets.

The Jio comparison writes itself. When Reliance dropped mobile data prices to near-zero, it didn't just make the internet cheaper — it rewired who got to participate in it. If Avataar's bet lands, video generation could follow the same curve.
