💰 Jeff Bezos just raised $12 billion to build an AI that designs bridges, chips, and drugs — not just writes emails.

His physical AI startup Prometheus closed a $12B round at a $41 billion valuation this week. The mission isn't another chatbot or coding assistant. It's an "artificial general engineer" — AI that can do the hard, physical work of designing molecules, semiconductors, and infrastructure at a level that currently takes armies of specialists decades to master.

That name is deliberate. Bezos is planting a flag right next to AGI and saying: automating the knowledge economy is the warm-up act.

While OpenAI and Anthropic battle over who owns the future of text and thought, Bezos is betting the real money is in the physical world — the stuff that's genuinely hard to build, not just hard to write. $12 billion says he's not speculating.
