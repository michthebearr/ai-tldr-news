🤖 Theker just raised $85M to build the factory robot that can do any job — not just one.

Most industrial robots are basically single-trick ponies. You buy one, you configure it for a specific task, and the moment your product line changes, you're looking at expensive retooling or a brand new machine. That's a painful reality for the long tail of manufacturers who can't afford to build out dedicated lines for every operation.

Theker's bet is different. Their robots are modular and reconfigurable — designed to be repurposed across tasks as needs shift. No humanoid gimmicks. No fixed-arm limitations. Just flexible hardware built for the messy, ever-changing reality of actual factory floors.

It's a quiet thesis with massive implications. If it works, automation stops being a luxury for only the largest players and becomes accessible to the manufacturers who've always been left out.
