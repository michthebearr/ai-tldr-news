🧠 FAANG is dead. Meet MANGOS — and Wall Street is already salivating.

The new power acronym making rounds in VC and finance circles: MANGOS. Meta/Microsoft, Anthropic, Nvidia, Google, OpenAI, SpaceX. Three are already public. SpaceX just debuted. Which means all eyes are now locked on the two AI giants that could define this generation's IPO moment.

Anthropic and OpenAI going public isn't just a Wall Street story — it's a reckoning. Private backers have been patient, but public markets don't do patient. They want revenue, margins, and a path to profit. Right now, both companies are burning cash at a scale that would make most CFOs sweat through their dress shirts, with no clear profitability timeline in sight.

The trillion-dollar AI valuation party is easy when the guest list is private. Opening the doors to public investors? That's when the bill arrives.
