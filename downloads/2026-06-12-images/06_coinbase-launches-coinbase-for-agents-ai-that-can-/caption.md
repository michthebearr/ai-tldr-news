💡 Coinbase just gave AI agents a credit card — and permission to use it.

Coinbase launched "Coinbase for Agents" this week, a product that connects AI directly to your crypto portfolio and lets it trade, pay, and execute — no confirmation pop-up required. LLMs could already read the market and tell you what to do. Now they can just... do it.

That's a bigger deal than it sounds. There's a massive difference between an AI that advises and an AI that acts. One is a smart dashboard. The other is an autonomous financial agent with real spending power. Coinbase just built the bridge between those two things — and opened it to the public.

The risk conversation hasn't caught up yet.
