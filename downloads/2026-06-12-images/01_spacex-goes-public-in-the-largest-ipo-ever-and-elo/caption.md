💰 SpaceX just went public in the largest IPO in history — and Elon Musk is now worth $1 trillion.

SpaceX priced at $135 a share, opened at $150, and raised $75 billion on day one. That's not a typo. The 11% first-day pop officially made it the biggest public debut ever — and pushed Musk's net worth past a number most economists thought was theoretical.

To put $1 trillion in perspective: it's more than the entire GDP of Ireland or Sweden. One person. More paper wealth than a nation-state. And that same person also runs the dominant U.S. rocket company, a social media platform, an AI lab, and the world's most valuable car company.

Robinhood crashed trying to keep up with retail demand. The market wanted in badly enough to break the pipes.
