🏎️ Nvidia just posted strong Q3 numbers — without the man who built the whole thing.

Colette Kress stepped up to deliver Nvidia's Q3 earnings on Tuesday, and the results met expectations. No drama. No miss. The machine Jensen Huang spent decades building kept right on humming.

But here's what everyone noticed: the word "Jensen" didn't come up once. No mention of his departure, the investigation, or what happens next. The board drew a hard line and stuck to it — numbers only.

The near-term story is fine. Hyperscalers are locked into massive AI spending commitments, so revenue isn't the immediate problem. The real question is whether Nvidia can hold onto its talent, its strategic edge, and its identity without its visionary founder calling the shots.

Strong quarter. Louder silence.
