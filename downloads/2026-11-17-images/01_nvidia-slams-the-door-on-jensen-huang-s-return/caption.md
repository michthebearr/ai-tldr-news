💡 Nvidia just told the world Jensen Huang isn't coming back — and they're not taking questions about it.

New board chair Harvey Jones opened the Q3 earnings call by slamming that door shut. Interim CEO Colette Kress followed with quarterly results that hit expectations. And when it came time for Q&A? The board stonewalled every question about Huang's resignation, the investigation, or who's running the world's most valuable chip company next.

That silence is loud.

Nvidia is printing money right now — AI infrastructure spending is at an all-time peak and every big player is lining up to buy their chips. But the man who built this company from zero is gone, the seat is empty, and nobody's talking. Whoever fills that role next will inherit one of the most powerful positions in tech history.

No pressure.
