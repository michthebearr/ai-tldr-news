💡 Hundreds of legitimate websites are secretly infecting your device the moment you land on them.

Researchers have uncovered a large-scale attack campaign compromising websites that run on Optimizely — a popular content management and marketing platform used by thousands of businesses. Visitors don't have to click anything. Just loading the page is enough to trigger the malware.

The payload is nasty: cryptocurrency theft tools and iOS exploits bundled together, hitting desktop and mobile users in a single drive-by. Your Bitcoin wallet and your iPhone in one visit.

This is the new playbook. Hackers aren't breaking down your front door anymore — they're poisoning the trusted infrastructure behind sites you'd never think twice about visiting. Supply-chain attacks through legitimate platforms mean even cautious, security-aware users are exposed. The website isn't the threat. The platform running it is.
