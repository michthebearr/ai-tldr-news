📱 Meta's own Chief AI Officer is posting undisclosed deepfakes on Instagram — of a competitor's CEO.

Alexandr Wang, the head of AI at Meta, shared an AI-generated video this week featuring synthetic versions of himself, Mark Zuckerberg, and OpenAI's Sam Altman. It was posted to Instagram — the same platform Meta keeps promising to clean up — with no prominent disclosure that none of those people were actually real.

The video was promotional content for Meta's AI agent Muse. So the company's top AI executive used a deepfake of a rival CEO as marketing material, on his own company's platform, without flagging it.
