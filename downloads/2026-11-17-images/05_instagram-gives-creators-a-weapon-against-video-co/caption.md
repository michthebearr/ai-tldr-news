📱 Instagram finally built the tool creators have needed for years — right when AI made stealing content effortless.

Meta is rolling out a "content protection" feature for eligible Instagram creators that automatically scans for visual matches of their original videos across Instagram and Facebook. When a copy is found, you get an alert and get to decide what happens next. Think YouTube's Content ID — but arriving about a decade late to the party.

Better late than never, though. Because the timing actually matters. AI tools have made it trivially easy to rip, re-edit, and repost video content at scale — stripping credit, gutting reach, and quietly siphoning ad revenue from the people who made the original. Platform-level detection isn't a nice-to-have anymore. It's table stakes.

For creators trying to make a living on these platforms, this is infrastructure. The question now is how well it works in practice — and whether Meta will keep expanding access beyond "eligible" creators before the damage is already done.
