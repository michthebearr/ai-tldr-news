💡 The AI safety movement just turned on itself — and it's getting ugly.

After a chaotic summer of rogue AI agents and escalating researcher warnings, the big US labs are finally admitting out loud that the pace of AI development needs to slow down. "Pacing the frontier" is now an actual, official talking point. Progress.

Except they can't agree on how to do it. Mustafa Suleyman — CEO of Microsoft AI — just went on record saying the risks are real and serious. He also used that same breath to call out Anthropic, one of the most safety-focused labs in the industry, for making things *worse*. Meanwhile, Microsoft quietly dropped a 37-page "Humanist AI Code of Conduct." Thirty-seven pages.

When the guy running AI at Microsoft is publicly torching Anthropic's safety strategy, this isn't a philosophical disagreement anymore. The AI industry's internal cold war just went fully public.
