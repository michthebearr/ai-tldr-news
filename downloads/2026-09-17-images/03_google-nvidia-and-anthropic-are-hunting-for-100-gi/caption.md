💡 Google, Nvidia, and Anthropic don't just want more power — they want to rewire the grid itself.

A new coalition including Google, Nvidia, Anthropic, and a company called Emerald AI is hunting for 100 gigawatts of electrical grid capacity to host the next wave of AI data centers. To put that in perspective: 100 GW is roughly the entire generating capacity of Germany.

Emerald AI's job is to identify grid locations that can actually absorb that kind of load — places where the infrastructure exists or can be built fast enough to keep pace with AI's appetite. This isn't a lobbying push or a policy paper. It's a coordinated land grab for energy.

The AI power crisis just got very real.

This story matters because it reframes the whole conversation. The industry isn't just asking utilities to flip a switch — it's trying to reshape national energy infrastructure from the ground up. That's a different kind of ambition entirely.
