💡 AI that makes your awkward phone calls for you just became a two-horse race.

Instinct and Meta's Muse both dropped voice-calling AI agents this week — on the same day. We're talking booking restaurant reservations, canceling subscriptions, sitting on hold so you don't have to. The kind of calls everyone dreads and endlessly postpones.

The timing isn't a coincidence. Both companies clearly see the same opening: phone-capable AI is the first feature that replaces something people genuinely hate, not just something they mildly tolerate. That's a much harder category to commoditize once someone owns it.

Two serious players. One week. The race for "AI that handles your phone life" just officially started.
