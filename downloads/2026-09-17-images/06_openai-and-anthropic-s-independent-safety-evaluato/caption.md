🏎️ OpenAI and Anthropic hired "independent" safety evaluators — but independent from what, exactly?

Both labs announced plans to embed external researchers inside their facilities, giving them rare access to study frontier models before release. On paper, it sounds like a meaningful step toward accountability. In practice, researchers are already raising the alarm.

The structural problem is glaring: these evaluators are funded by and physically housed inside the very labs they're supposed to scrutinize. That's not independence — that's a conflict of interest with a press release attached. And without any regulatory enforcement backstop, there's nothing stopping the labs from quietly shaping what gets published.

Safety theater is worse than no safety process at all. It hands the public a false sense of oversight while the labs keep both hands on the wheel.
