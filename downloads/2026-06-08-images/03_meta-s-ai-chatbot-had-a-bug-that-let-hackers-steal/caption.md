🔐 Meta's AI chatbot just handed hackers the keys to 20,000 Instagram accounts.

A bug in Meta's AI support chatbot let attackers bypass two-factor authentication entirely — no phishing, no brute force. They simply asked the chatbot to trigger a password reset, and it did. Over 20,000 accounts compromised before Meta caught it.

Meta confirmed the breach in a filing with the state of Maine and is framing this as a bug, not a flaw in the model itself. That distinction matters legally. It doesn't matter much to the 20,000 people who lost access to their accounts.

This is the real risk nobody talks about enough: AI that can *do things* — not just answer questions — is a completely different threat surface. Chatting is fine. Executing account actions? That needs security review that's orders of magnitude more serious than what got shipped here.
