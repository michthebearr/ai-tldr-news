💡 Microsoft's AI chief says superintelligence is coming — and no, you're not getting fired.

Mustafa Suleyman sat down for a wide-ranging interview this week and made two things clear: he thinks we're closer to superintelligence than most people realize, and he's not buying the automation apocalypse narrative. His case is that AI will augment workers, not replace them — a familiar argument, but it lands differently when it comes from someone actually building the thing.

He also took a direct shot at Anthropic, calling it irresponsible to talk about Claude as if it has real feelings or consciousness. For a field that loves to blur lines between capability and sentience, that's a notable rebuke — especially from a founder who's been in this game since DeepMind.

Suleyman is one of the rare people with a frontier lab background and a seat inside a trillion-dollar company. When he signals where Microsoft AI is headed, it's worth listening closely.
