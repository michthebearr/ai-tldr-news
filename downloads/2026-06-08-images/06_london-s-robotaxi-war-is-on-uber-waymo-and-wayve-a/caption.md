🤖 London's streets are about to become the world's most brutal robotaxi proving ground.

Uber just opened an interest list for Wayve robotaxi rides in London — and Waymo is reportedly close behind. That means two of the biggest names in autonomous vehicles are gunning for the same city, at the same time, on some of the most chaotic roads on Earth.

London isn't Phoenix. It's not San Francisco. It's centuries-old street layouts, double-deckers cutting in from nowhere, roundabouts every 200 metres, and regulators who don't hand out wins easily. If your AV can handle London, it can handle almost anything.

Whoever cracks this first doesn't just win a city — they win the argument.
