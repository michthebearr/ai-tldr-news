💡 The AI price hike is coming — and a lot of startups are not ready for it.

OpenAI, Anthropic, and the rest have been practically giving away API access. Cheap tokens, generous tiers, aggressive discounts. It felt like a golden age. It was actually a land grab.

Now, with IPOs on the horizon and real shareholders entering the picture, analysts are warning that the subsidy era is ending. These companies need margins. And the easiest place to find them is in the pricing they've been holding artificially low to win market share.

Here's the brutal part: an entire generation of AI startups built their unit economics around today's rock-bottom inference costs. When those costs double — or triple — a lot of business models quietly stop working.
