💡 Apple just rebooted Siri — two years late, powered by Google, and everything is riding on it.

WWDC 2026 is here, and new CEO John Ternus is making Apple's AI overhaul the centerpiece of his first major keynote. The long-awaited Siri revamp — first teased at WWDC 2024 — is finally shipping, now with Google Gemini under the hood alongside Apple Intelligence updates across iOS 27 and macOS 27 Golden Gate.

That's a lot of ground to cover for a company that's spent the last two years watching OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic eat its lunch in the AI conversation. Ternus needs this to land clean — not as a promise, but as a product people actually use every day.

Here's the number that matters: if Apple ships a Siri that genuinely works, it's instantly in front of over a billion iPhone users. Every standalone AI app combined doesn't come close to that install base. This isn't just an Apple story — it's potentially the moment AI assistance goes fully mainstream.
