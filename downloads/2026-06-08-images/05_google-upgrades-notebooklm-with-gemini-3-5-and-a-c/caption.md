🧠 Google just gave NotebookLM a serious upgrade — and it's quietly becoming the most underrated AI tool out there.

Gemini 3.5 is now the default model inside NotebookLM, bringing sharper, more accurate responses across the board. Google also added the ability to build source repositories straight from chat — so your research actually compounds as you work. And the new cloud computer feature handles the heavier lifting when tasks get complex.

This isn't a flashy product drop. It's Google methodically stacking advantages in a space where Perplexity and OpenAI are coming hard. NotebookLM has always had a loyal user base — now it has the model muscle to match.

Most people still sleep on this tool. They shouldn't.
