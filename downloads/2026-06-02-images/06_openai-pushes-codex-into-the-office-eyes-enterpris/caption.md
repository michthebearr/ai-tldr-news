🧠 OpenAI just told every enterprise software company: Codex is coming for your lunch.

OpenAI dropped expanded Codex capabilities this week — and this time, it's not just about writing code. The new push targets knowledge work: document workflows, internal automation, the kind of white-collar tasks that keep entire IT departments employed. They also released internal usage data showing Codex is already being used far beyond its original developer audience.

That last part is the tell. OpenAI doesn't release that kind of data by accident. It's a signal to the market — and a warning shot to Microsoft Copilot and Google Workspace AI, who've spent the last two years owning this exact turf.

The enterprise automation race just got a serious new entrant.
