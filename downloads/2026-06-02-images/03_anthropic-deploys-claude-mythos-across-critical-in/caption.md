🧠 Anthropic just put its most powerful AI in charge of protecting the infrastructure keeping 100 million people alive.

Claude Mythos — Anthropic's most capable model — is being deployed across 150 organizations in 15 countries. Power grids. Water systems. Hospitals. Telecom networks. This isn't a pilot program. This is the whole stack.

The initiative runs alongside Project Glasswing, Anthropic's security framework designed to find vulnerabilities before bad actors do. The scope is deliberate and a little staggering: they're explicitly targeting systems where a breach doesn't mean a data leak — it means lights out, taps dry, or worse.

This is the line between AI as a product and AI as infrastructure. Anthropic just crossed it.
