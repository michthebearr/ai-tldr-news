💡 Microsoft just built an AI operating system — and it's not running Windows.

At Build 2026, Microsoft unveiled Project Solara: a ground-up OS designed specifically for AI agent devices. Two concept hardware pieces came with it — a desk unit that looks like an Echo Show with a brain, and a wearable badge. Both running agents. Neither running Windows.

That last part is the quiet bombshell. Microsoft, the company that has spent 40 years defending Windows as the center of personal computing, just chose Android as the foundation for its agentic future. Pair that with Nvidia's new RTX Spark chips showing up in a Surface Dev Box, and you're looking at a full hardware-software stack being assembled in real time.

Redmond isn't just building products here. It's placing a bet on where computing actually goes next — and apparently Windows isn't invited to that table.
