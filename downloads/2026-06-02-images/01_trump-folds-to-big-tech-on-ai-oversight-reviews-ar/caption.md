🏎️ Trump just handed Big Tech the keys — AI oversight is now officially voluntary.

After heavy lobbying from the industry, Trump signed a revised executive order stripping out mandatory prerelease government reviews of advanced AI models. The earlier draft had real teeth. This one doesn't. Federal agencies no longer get a meaningful say before frontier models hit the market.

Voluntary oversight is a polite way of saying no oversight. If companies could self-regulate effectively, we wouldn't be having this conversation. The AI industry pushed hard for this, and it got exactly what it wanted — a wide-open runway with no control tower.

Meanwhile, the EU is moving in the exact opposite direction. The U.S. is making a clear bet: race first, clean up later. Whether that's strategic boldness or reckless negligence depends on what the next generation of models actually does.
