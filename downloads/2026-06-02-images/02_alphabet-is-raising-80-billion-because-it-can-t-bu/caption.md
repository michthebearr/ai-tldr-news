💰 Alphabet is borrowing $80 billion — because it literally cannot build fast enough.

Google's parent company is tapping debt markets for $80B to fund AI infrastructure. The reason? Their own words: demand for AI products and services is "exceeding available supply." One of the most powerful tech companies on the planet is publicly admitting it can't keep up.

Let that sink in. This isn't a startup scrambling for runway — it's Alphabet. A company sitting on hundreds of billions in assets, choosing to borrow because the pace of AI adoption is outrunning even their ability to build.

When the world's AI infrastructure leader needs $80B just to meet existing demand, the scale of what's happening still hasn't hit most people.
