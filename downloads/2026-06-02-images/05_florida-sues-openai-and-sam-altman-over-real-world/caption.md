🧠 Florida just sued OpenAI and Sam Altman over a real-world shooting — and the AI industry should be paying close attention.

Last year, a gunman opened fire at Florida State University. Now, Florida is arguing ChatGPT played a role — and they're suing OpenAI and its CEO directly to prove it. It's the first lawsuit of its kind in the country to link an AI company to physical violence.

No state has gone this far before. Most AI liability debates live in the abstract — bias, misinformation, job displacement. This one is about blood on the ground and who bears legal responsibility for it.

Win or lose, the template is now set. If courts decide AI companies can be held liable for what their models output in the real world, the entire industry gets repriced overnight — legally, commercially, existentially.
