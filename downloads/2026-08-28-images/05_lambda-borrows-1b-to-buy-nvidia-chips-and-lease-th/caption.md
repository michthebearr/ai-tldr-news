💡 Lambda just borrowed $1 billion to buy Nvidia chips — and lease them straight to Microsoft.

That's not a typo. Neocloud Lambda raised $1B in private debt — not equity — to purchase Nvidia GPUs and rent them back to one of the most cash-rich companies on the planet. It sounds backwards. It's actually a sign of how broken the compute supply chain still is.

Microsoft needs GPUs faster than it can build data centers to house them. Lambda is essentially acting as a middleman financier — buying the chips, owning the risk, and collecting the lease. It only works if GPU demand stays scorching and Nvidia supply doesn't suddenly flood the market. If either flips, Lambda is holding a billion dollars of depreciating hardware.

Debt-funded GPU arbitrage at this scale is a bet-the-company move. But the fact that a hyperscaler like Microsoft is signing on the other side tells you everything about how desperate the race for compute has become.
