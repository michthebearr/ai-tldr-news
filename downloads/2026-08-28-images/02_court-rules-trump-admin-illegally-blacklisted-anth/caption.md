💡 A federal judge just told the Trump administration it can't weaponize national security labels to punish AI companies for having principles.

Anthropic drew "red lines" around military AI uses it wasn't willing to cross. The Pentagon responded by slapping a "supply-chain risk" label on the company — a designation that could have tanked its government contracts and reputation overnight.

A federal court called it unconstitutional. You can't dress up political retaliation in national security clothing and expect it to hold up in court.

This is Anthropic's first legal win in a months-long fight with the Trump administration — and a second lawsuit in Washington is still live. The precedent here matters beyond just one company: the executive branch just learned it can't weaponize security labels to punish AI labs for their safety policies.

That's a ruling with real teeth.
