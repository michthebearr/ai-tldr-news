💡 Big Tech doesn't want to win the open-source debate — they want to buy it.

Open-weight AI startups are suddenly the hottest acquisition targets in Silicon Valley. These are the companies releasing their model weights publicly — letting anyone download, fine-tune, and deploy them freely. Sounds counterintuitive to acquire something you give away. That's exactly the point.

Strategics are circling because open models aren't a charity project — they're a distribution moat. Get your architecture embedded everywhere, and you own the ecosystem before the ecosystem knows it's owned. Add in the talent and the IP, and you've also bought yourself a hedge if the closed-model giants stumble.

The open vs. closed AI war was supposed to be ideological. Turns out it's just another M&A cycle.
