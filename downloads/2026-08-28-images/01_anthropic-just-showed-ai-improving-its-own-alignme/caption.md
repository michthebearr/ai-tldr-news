💡 Anthropic just built an AI that fixes its own alignment problems — and it actually worked.

Researchers at Anthropic published results from an automated system that improved AI performance across 10 misalignment benchmarks at the same time. That's not the headline. The headline is that it did this without breaking anything else — which is exactly what every previous attempt failed to do.

Self-improving AI has always had a catch: fix one problem, create three more. This demo suggests that trap isn't inevitable. The system course-corrected its own bad behaviors in a controlled setting while keeping general capability intact. Small result on paper. Massive implications in practice.

To be clear — this isn't shipped, it isn't in your Claude subscription, and it isn't a solved problem. But it's no longer theoretical either. AI that can reliably fix its own misalignment is either the most important safety breakthrough in years, or the most ironic way this all goes wrong.
