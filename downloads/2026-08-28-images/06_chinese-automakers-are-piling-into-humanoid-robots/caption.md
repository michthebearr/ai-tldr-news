🤖 Chinese automakers just found their next EV playbook — and it's humanoid robots.

Tesla's Optimus put humanoid robots on the map. Now a wave of Chinese automakers are piling in, betting that their manufacturing muscle and cost-efficient production lines give them a structural edge that pure robotics startups simply can't match.

Sound familiar? It should. This is exactly how Chinese EV makers blindsided Western rivals — move fast, cut costs, scale aggressively, and let everyone else scramble to catch up. The companies already tooled up for complex, high-volume hardware don't need to reinvent the factory. They just need to retool it.

If they crack the humanoid cost curve the same way they cracked EVs, Western robotics startups are in serious trouble.
