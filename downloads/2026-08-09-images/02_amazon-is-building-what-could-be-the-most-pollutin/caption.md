💡 Amazon may be building the single most polluting power plant in America — and it's just to run its AI.

To power a new data center in West Texas, Amazon is funding a dedicated gas-burning plant in Pecos County that won't even connect to the state grid. It exists for one purpose: feeding Amazon's AI infrastructure. That's it.

If completed, this plant could become the largest single source of greenhouse gas emissions in the United States. Let that sink in. Not a steel mill. Not a refinery. A data center.

The "green AI" pledge and the reality are now so far apart it's hard to look at both at the same time.
