💡 A printed sticker can make you invisible to AI surveillance — and that's both fascinating and terrifying.

A security researcher just built an algorithm that generates patterns you can literally print out and wear — and AI-powered surveillance cameras will fail to detect you. No hacking required. No special tech. Just a piece of paper.

The patterns exploit fundamental weaknesses in how computer vision models interpret visual input. The same systems being used by law enforcement to track people, identify faces, and flag vehicles can be defeated by something that looks like abstract art.

Here's the uncomfortable truth: this research doesn't stay in a lab. Privacy advocates will use it. Protesters will use it. And so will people who have very good reasons not to want to be caught.
