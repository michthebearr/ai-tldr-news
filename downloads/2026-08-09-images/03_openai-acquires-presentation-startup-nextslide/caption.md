🧠 OpenAI just bought a presentation startup — and it's playing a much bigger game than slides.

OpenAI quietly acquired NextSlide, an AI-powered presentation tool, and has already absorbed the team into ChatGPT development. The product page? Gone. Redirects straight to ChatGPT now. No price tag disclosed.

This isn't really about presentations. OpenAI is piece by piece assembling a full productivity suite — the kind of thing you currently open Microsoft Office or Google Workspace for. Slides today. Spreadsheets tomorrow. Docs after that.

Microsoft owns a massive stake in OpenAI and also sells Office. That's about to get very awkward.
