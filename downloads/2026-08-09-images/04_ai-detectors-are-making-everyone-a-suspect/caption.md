🏎️ AI detectors are flagging real humans as cheaters — and no one is being held accountable.

Schools, publishers, and employers are deploying AI writing detectors like they're infallible. They're not. These tools are generating false positives at scale, meaning actual students are failing assignments, workers are losing jobs, and real people are paying the price for software that simply can't do what it promises.

The core problem: these detectors are being used to make high-stakes decisions despite being notoriously unreliable. There's no standardized accuracy threshold, no appeals process, and no institutional accountability when they get it wrong. The burden of proof has been quietly flipped — you're guilty until you can prove you're human.

That's not a minor bug. That's a systemic failure being dressed up as due diligence.
