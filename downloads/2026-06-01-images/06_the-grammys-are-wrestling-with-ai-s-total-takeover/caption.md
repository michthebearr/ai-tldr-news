🎵 The Grammys just admitted what the music industry has been tiptoeing around — AI is everywhere.

Recording Academy CEO Harvey Mason Jr. sat down with The Verge and used one word to describe AI's role in music production right now: "omnipresent." That's a long way from the careful, noncommittal language we were getting 18 months ago. The shift in tone alone is a headline.

The bigger story is what the Grammys are trying to figure out next. Eligibility rules, attribution credits, what even counts as human authorship — none of it has clean answers yet. And the Recording Academy knows it's not just writing Grammy policy. It's writing the first draft of how the entire industry thinks about this.

Whoever defines authorship here sets the terms for contracts, royalties, and copyright battles for decades.
