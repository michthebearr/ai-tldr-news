💡 Microsoft Build kicks off this week — and the pressure is real.

Every year, Build is where Microsoft shows developers what it's actually been building. This year, the focus is tight: new AI models, Windows developer tools, and more GitHub Copilot integrations. The company is even staging it in a smaller venue — less spectacle, more substance.

That's a deliberate signal. After years of splashy announcements, Microsoft needs developers to believe the AI strategy is landing in tools they actually use day-to-day. Google I/O just wrapped. Apple's WWDC is right around the corner. The developer mindshare war is very much on.

Microsoft has the enterprise relationships and the OpenAI partnership. Now it has to prove that translates into things people open every morning.
