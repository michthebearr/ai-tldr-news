💡 Anthropic just filed to go public — and it's officially the most valuable startup on the planet.

Claude's creator quietly filed confidentially with the SEC on Monday, setting the stage for what could be one of the biggest tech IPOs in years. Coming off a fresh fundraise that pushed its valuation to the top of the startup world, Anthropic is now ahead of OpenAI in the race to public markets.

The confidential filing means we don't get the full S-1 yet — but the direction is obvious. Anthropic is done being a private AI lab and is ready to play on the biggest financial stage there is.

Here's why this is a bigger deal than just another IPO: for the first time, we're about to see what frontier AI actually looks like on a balance sheet. Revenue, burn rate, margins — all of it. The AI arms race is getting a public scoreboard, and the numbers won't lie.
