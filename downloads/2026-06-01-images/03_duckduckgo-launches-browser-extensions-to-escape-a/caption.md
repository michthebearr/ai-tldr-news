💡 Millions of people are actively fleeing AI search — and DuckDuckGo just built them an escape hatch.

DuckDuckGo dropped new Chrome and Firefox extensions this week that give users one-click access to its AI-free search experience. No Overviews. No hallucinated summaries. Just links. The move is surgical — it meets frustrated Google users exactly where they already are.

And the timing isn't random. DuckDuckGo is reporting a serious surge in traffic, and the culprit seems clear: users are burned out on AI Overviews getting things wrong and Perplexity confidently making things up. There's a growing crowd that just wants a straight answer from the actual web.

Turns out "no AI" is a feature now.
