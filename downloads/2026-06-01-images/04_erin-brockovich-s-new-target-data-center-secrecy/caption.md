💡 Erin Brockovich just made AI's dirty secret her next fight.

The activist who took down Pacific Gas & Electric is turning her attention to data centers — and the AI industry should be paying close attention. Brockovich is pushing hard for transparency around how much water these facilities consume, how much energy they burn, and what that actually means for the communities living next door.

Here's the thing: hyperscalers have been remarkably good at keeping this information buried. No disclosure requirements. No standardized reporting. Just quietly expanding infrastructure while local residents have little idea what's moving in next door.

A famous face with a proven legal and political playbook changes the pressure dynamics fast.

AI's physical footprint has been the industry's most convenient blind spot. Data centers are real buildings, in real towns, drinking real water. The moment that story gets a high-profile narrator, the regulatory window shifts — and companies that banked on opacity start scrambling.
