💡 A 10-person startup just outforecasted NOAA — by multiple days.

Windborne Systems quietly dropped a weather prediction model that's beating the best government forecasts on the planet. Not by a little. By days. The team deploys a fleet of high-altitude balloons to capture atmospheric data that ground-based systems simply can't reach — then feeds that cleaner, richer data into an AI model that's running circles around agencies with billion-dollar budgets.

This isn't a story about a better algorithm. It's a story about better data plumbing. Windborne didn't just build a smarter model — they built a smarter pipeline. That distinction matters more than most people realize.

It's the clearest signal yet that AI-native startups don't need to outspend legacy institutions. They just need to out-instrument them.
