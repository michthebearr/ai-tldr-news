💡 Samsung and SK Hynix just placed a $550 billion bet that AI's hardware hunger never stops.

The world's two biggest memory chipmakers are committing over half a trillion dollars to build out new fabs — and the reason is simple: the AI boom has outpaced the chips needed to run it. High-bandwidth memory is the unsung bottleneck behind every model rollout, every data center expansion, every "why is inference so slow?" complaint.

You can optimize your architecture all you want. You can't prompt-engineer your way out of a hardware shortage.

This is one of the largest infrastructure bets in tech history — a signal that Samsung and SK Hynix aren't just reacting to demand, they're betting it never meaningfully dips. If they're right, they own the backbone of the AI era. If they're wrong, that's a lot of empty fabs.
