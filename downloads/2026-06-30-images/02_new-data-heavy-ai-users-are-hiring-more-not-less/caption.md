💡 New data says heavy AI users are hiring more — not fewer.

A fresh report is throwing cold water on the "AI kills jobs" panic. Companies classified as high-intensity AI adopters grew headcount by 10.2%. Entry-level roles? Up 12%. That's the exact demographic everyone assumed would get hit first and hardest.

This is the most concrete counter-evidence yet to the displacement thesis. The dominant narrative has been that automation hammers junior employees — repetitive tasks, low complexity, easy to automate away. This data tells a different story entirely.

Worth noting: correlation isn't causation. Companies growing fast tend to adopt more tools — AI included. But still, if you've been doom-scrolling about robots stealing starter jobs, this is at least worth a pause.
