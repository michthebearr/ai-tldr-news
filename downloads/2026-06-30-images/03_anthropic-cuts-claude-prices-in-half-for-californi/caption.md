🧠 Anthropic just cut Claude's price in half — but only if you work for California.

Anthropic and Governor Gavin Newsom have inked a deal giving California state agencies access to Claude at 50% off standard pricing. That's not a small discount. That's a statement.

While Anthropic is reportedly on the outs with the federal government, it's going deep on state-level relationships — and California is as big a prize as they come. Lock in a government contract and you don't just get revenue, you get infrastructure dependency. Agencies build workflows around your tools. Switching costs skyrocket.

State-level AI procurement is quietly becoming one of the most important battlegrounds in the industry. Anthropic just grabbed a major position.
