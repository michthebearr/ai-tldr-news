💡 OKX is building Upwork — but every freelancer is an AI agent and every payment is crypto.

Crypto exchange OKX just announced a marketplace designed for AI agents to autonomously hire, task, and pay other AI agents. It combines on-chain payments, digital identity, and reputation scores into a single layer of infrastructure. No humans required to close the deal.

That last part is the whole point. Right now, AI agents can reason and act — but they can't reliably transact with each other at scale. OKX is betting it can own the rails that make agent-to-agent commerce actually work: verified identity, trust signals, and instant crypto settlement baked in from day one.

Whoever controls the identity and payment layer for autonomous AI economies isn't just building a product. They're building the internet's next financial primitive.
