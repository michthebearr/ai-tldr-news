🧠 OpenAI just launched an agent to take on Meta — and Meta doesn't even charge for theirs.

At DevDay 2026, Sam Altman unveiled Dots, a personal assistant agent that OpenAI is framing as its direct answer to Meta's Muse platform. The problem? Muse is already pulling massive numbers, and it costs users exactly nothing.

This isn't a developer tools competition anymore. The agent wars have moved to the consumer layer — your phone, your daily habits, your life admin. And that's a fight that looks very different when one side has a billion-user head start baked into apps people already open every day.

OpenAI is the incumbent in AI mindshare. But Meta owns distribution. That gap is hard to close with a price tag attached.
