💰 OpenAI just fired three of its own safety researchers — and that should worry everyone.

According to a WSJ report, the three departures followed an internal investigation into mishandling of sensitive company information. OpenAI confirmed the exits without getting into specifics, as they tend to do.

But here's the thing: this isn't just an HR story. OpenAI's safety team has been bleeding talent since Ilya Sutskever left and the Superalignment team quietly dissolved. The people whose literal job is to make sure the most powerful AI systems don't go sideways — they keep leaving, or getting pushed out.

When your safety org has a turnover problem, that's not a footnote. That's a red flag with a banner attached.
