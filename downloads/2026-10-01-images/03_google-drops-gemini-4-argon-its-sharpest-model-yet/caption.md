🧠 Google just shipped its sharpest model yet — and barely made a sound.

Gemini 4 Argon landed this week with a clear target: coding and cybersecurity. Google's positioning it as a workhorse, not a showboat — built for the developers and security teams who actually push these models to their limits.

But the bigger story isn't the model. It's the pace. The same day Argon dropped, Google also rolled out Guided Vision in Gemini Live — a real-time camera feature that narrates what your phone sees, out loud, as it happens. Two major releases. One day.

The gap between "most powerful model" announcements used to be quarters. Now it's weeks.
