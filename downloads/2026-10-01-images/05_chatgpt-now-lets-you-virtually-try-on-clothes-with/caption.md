🧠 ChatGPT just became your AI stylist — and that's a much bigger deal than it sounds.

OpenAI quietly rolled out virtual try-on in ChatGPT this week. Upload your photo, pick a piece of clothing or an accessory, and see how it looks on you. Oh, and you can now save items to a Favorites library too.

That's not a chatbot feature. That's a shopping assistant.

This is OpenAI's clearest move yet into territory that Google Shopping and Pinterest have spent years building. The combination of personalized try-on plus a save-for-later library is exactly the architecture of a commerce platform — and OpenAI just dropped it inside the world's most-used AI product.

E-commerce discovery has a new competitor. And it doesn't mess around.
