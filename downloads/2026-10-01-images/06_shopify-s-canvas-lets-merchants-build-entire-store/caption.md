💡 Shopify just made building an online store as easy as sending a text.

Meet Canvas — Shopify's new AI-powered store builder that lets merchants describe what they want to their AI agent, Sidekick, and watch it come to life in real time. No drag-and-drop. No templates. No developer on speed dial. Just type, and your storefront builds itself.

This is e-commerce's zero-interface moment. The barrier to launching a business used to be capital, then it was technical skill, then it was design sense. Shopify has been chipping away at each one for years. Canvas takes a sledgehammer to the last one standing.

If chat-to-storefront works reliably at scale, the question of who gets to be a merchant completely changes. Your ability to type a coherent sentence is now the only real prerequisite.
