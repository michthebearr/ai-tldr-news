💡 Microsoft just rebranded Copilot as the operating system for work — and the stakes couldn't be higher.

At a private enterprise event this week, Satya Nadella unveiled a major Copilot overhaul. Coding capabilities, agent features, baked directly into the product. The pitch: this isn't just an AI assistant anymore — it's the foundational layer your entire workplace runs on.

The repositioning is a tell. Copilot has had a hype problem since day one — big numbers, flashy demos, but lukewarm daily usage. Renaming the vision is Microsoft's way of saying the current version wasn't selling the right story.

The real pressure is on enterprise buyers. Microsoft has wired Copilot into the entire Office franchise — that's a trillion-dollar product suite riding on agents actually becoming indispensable. If this reframe doesn't land, they're not sitting on a missed opportunity. They're sitting on a very expensive mistake.
