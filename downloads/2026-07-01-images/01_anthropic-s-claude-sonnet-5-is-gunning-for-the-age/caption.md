🧠 Anthropic just made a play for every developer who wants powerful AI agents without the flagship price tag.

Claude Sonnet 5 dropped this week, and it's not trying to be the smartest model in the room — it's trying to be the most practical one. Anthropic slotted it deliberately below Opus, priced to compete directly with GPT-5.5 and Gemini Pro, and tuned it specifically for running agent pipelines at scale.

The pitch is simple: same agentic capability, better safety record, lower cost per run. For anyone building multi-step AI workflows, that math matters a lot more than benchmark bragging rights.

Because here's what's actually happening in the model wars right now — raw capability gaps are closing fast. The new battleground is price. Whoever makes capable agents cheapest wins the developer ecosystem, and Anthropic is betting Sonnet 5 is that product.
