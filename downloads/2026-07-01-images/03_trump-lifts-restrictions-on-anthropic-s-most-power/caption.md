💡 The Trump administration just lifted restrictions on Anthropic's most powerful AI models — with no real explanation.

Anthropic's Mythos and Fable models had export and usage restrictions slapped on them by the federal government. Now those restrictions are gone. Just like that. No clear reasoning, no policy framework — just a reversal that leaves everyone guessing what comes next.

This is becoming a pattern. Erratic AI policymaking from Washington isn't just frustrating — it's actively damaging. Enterprise buyers won't build on a foundation that could shift next week, and American companies can't compete globally when they're stuck navigating regulatory whiplash that their foreign rivals never have to deal with.

Uncertainty is its own kind of handicap.
