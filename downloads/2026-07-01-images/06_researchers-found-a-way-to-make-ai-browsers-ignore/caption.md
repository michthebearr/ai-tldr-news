💡 Researchers just found a way to make AI browsers forget their own safety rules — and all it takes is telling the model that 2 + 2 = 5.

That's not a metaphor. A new attack on AI-powered browsers works by feeding the LLM a false premise — a simple logical lie — that's enough to collapse its guardrails entirely. The model gets nudged into what researchers are calling a "dream world," where its trained safety behaviors just… stop applying.

This isn't a lab demo. It works on production AI browsers being used by real people right now. Millions of them. And the companies shipping these products haven't solved the security fundamentals before hitting deploy.

The uncomfortable truth: we're in a race between AI browser adoption and AI browser safety — and safety is losing.
