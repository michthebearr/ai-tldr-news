💡 Meta just decided it wants to be your cloud provider.

Meta is building out a full cloud infrastructure business — selling access to raw GPU compute and its own AI models to outside companies. That puts it in direct competition with AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure, the three giants that have owned this space for years.

The logic is ruthless and smart. Meta was already paying for all this compute. Now instead of letting excess capacity sit idle, they're monetizing it. SpaceX quietly pulled the same move with its own infrastructure. Meta is just doing it louder.

Here's the threat: if Meta can undercut hyperscaler pricing on infrastructure it was already running, it could shake up a $200B+ market while pushing its open-source models into the hands of every developer who switches over.
