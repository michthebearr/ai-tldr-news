💡 Venice AI just hit a $1B valuation — and it didn't sell a single piece of your data to get there.

The platform raised a $65M Series A this week, but the headline isn't the funding — it's that Venice is already pulling in over $70M in annualized revenue. For a unicorn, that's almost suspiciously healthy.

The pitch is brutally simple: your conversations stay on your device, nothing trains their models, no surveillance, no data harvesting. CEO Erik Voorhees built the whole thing around the idea that privacy shouldn't be a premium feature — it should be the foundation. Turns out a lot of people are tired enough of being the product that they'll actually open their wallets.

That's the real story here. Venice isn't just a win for one startup — it's proof that privacy-native AI is a legitimate market, not a niche concern for the tinfoil hat crowd.
