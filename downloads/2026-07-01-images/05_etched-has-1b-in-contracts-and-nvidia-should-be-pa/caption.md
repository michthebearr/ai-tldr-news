💡 A $1B order book isn't a pitch deck — it's a threat.

AI chip startup Etched just landed a $5B valuation, backed by something more convincing than hype: $1 billion in contracts already signed. Their chip is purpose-built for transformer inference, not the catch-all general compute Nvidia's GPUs are designed for. That specialization makes it faster and cheaper for the exact workloads AI companies are burning money on right now.

This is the part Nvidia should take seriously. Etched isn't trying to beat them at everything — just the one thing that matters most at this moment in AI. Inference is where the bills are being paid, and a purpose-built chip doing it better is a wedge, not a footnote.

A billion dollars in pre-sold product doesn't lie.
