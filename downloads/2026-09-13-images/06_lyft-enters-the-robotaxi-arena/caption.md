🤖 Lyft just placed its bet on the robotaxi future — and it might actually have an edge.

Lyft has officially entered the autonomous vehicle space, going head-to-head with Waymo and Tesla's FSD ambitions. The details on partners, tech stack, and launch timeline are still coming into focus — but the direction is clear.

Here's what makes this interesting: Lyft isn't starting from zero. It already has millions of riders, established driver infrastructure, and a brand people trust for getting from A to B. Pure AV startups have the tech. Lyft has the distribution.

That combination could matter a lot. Consumer adoption of robotaxis has been slow partly because new players have to build trust from scratch. Lyft skips that line entirely.
