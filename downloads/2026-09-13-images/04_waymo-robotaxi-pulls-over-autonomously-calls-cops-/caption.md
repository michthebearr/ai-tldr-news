🤖 A Waymo robotaxi pulled over mid-ride and called the cops — all on its own.

No driver. No human judgment call. The car detected something was off, stopped itself, and summoned San Francisco police. Officers arrived and arrested two juveniles carrying a loaded AR-style ghost gun. Waymo later confirmed the incident to the LA Times after a police report surfaced.

This is the part where it gets complicated. The car did what you'd arguably want it to do — it helped get a weapon off the street. But a vehicle autonomously deciding to involve law enforcement, without a human making that call, opens a door that's very hard to close. Who authorized that? What data triggered it? What gets flagged next?

Robotaxis were always going to be surveillance infrastructure. This just made it impossible to ignore.
