💡 The two men racing to build the most powerful AI on Earth just agreed that maybe everyone should slow down.

Anthropic's Dario Amodei and OpenAI's Sam Altman are both now publicly floating the idea of "pacing the frontier" — deliberately pumping the brakes on how fast frontier AI develops. Amodei has laid out the most concrete version of the argument. The catch: nobody knows who enforces it, who opts in, or what happens when someone doesn't.

The irony here is hard to miss. These are the two CEOs whose companies have spent years in an all-out sprint against each other. Now they're standing at the finish line saying the race itself might be the problem. Convenient timing, or genuine reckoning? Probably both.

Here's why it actually matters: if the two dominant labs quietly coordinate on slowdowns, they effectively set the industry's speed limit — without a vote, without a regulator, and without asking China's frontier labs if they're on board. That's not a safety plan. That's a cartel with good PR.
