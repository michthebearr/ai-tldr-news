💡 Obama just told Democrats: stop sleeping on AI — and come with a plan.

At a recent public appearance, the former president made it clear that AI needs to move from the back burner to the center of the party's agenda. Not as a talking point. As a platform. He specifically called out economic displacement and safety as the issues Democrats need to get serious about — fast.

He didn't hand anyone a policy roadmap. But he didn't need to. When a two-term president compares AI's societal weight to the forces that historically reshaped entire political coalitions, the signal is loud: this issue is about to stop being a tech-policy niche and start being an electoral battleground.

AI governance is entering the mainstream. The question is who shows up with actual answers.
