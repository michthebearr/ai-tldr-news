💡 Half the Fortune 500 runs on Hugging Face — and their CEO says open source AI has already won.

Hugging Face's Clem Delangue dropped a stat this week that should make every closed-model AI company nervous: roughly 500 of the world's biggest enterprises are now on Hugging Face's platform. And the pattern is almost always the same — companies start with OpenAI or Anthropic, then migrate to open models once they realize how much they're spending and how little they control.

That's not a niche trend. That's a playbook.

Delangue's argument is that open source AI is no longer the scrappy underdog option — it's becoming the default infrastructure layer for serious enterprise deployment. And if he's right, the real power in AI shifts away from whoever has the flashiest frontier model, and toward whoever owns the tooling, fine-tuning, and deployment stack. Hugging Face is very deliberately building exactly that.

The moat for closed model providers gets a little shallower every quarter. Hugging Face is betting the future belongs to the picks-and-shovels layer — not the model itself.
