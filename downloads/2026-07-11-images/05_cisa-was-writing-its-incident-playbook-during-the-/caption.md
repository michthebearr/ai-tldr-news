💡 The agency protecting America's digital infrastructure had no incident response plan — and found that out mid-incident.

CISA, the federal body responsible for defending US critical infrastructure from cyberattacks, just admitted it was writing its own breach playbook *while actively dealing with a breach*. A contractor employee uploaded exposed passwords to a public GitHub repo, and the agency tasked with telling everyone else how to handle this stuff had to improvise in real time.

Let that sink in. This isn't a small startup figuring things out. This is the US government's frontline cyber defense agency.

The timing couldn't be worse. AI-powered cyberattacks are getting faster, smarter, and harder to contain. The window between "breach detected" and "serious damage done" is shrinking — and CISA just revealed it was winging one of the most basic scenarios in the security playbook.
