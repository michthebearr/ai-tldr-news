💡 An AI shopping app co-founded by Phoebe Gates is accused of faking its own success — and the method is textbook dirty.

Bloomberg's investigation into Phia alleges the app used "cookie stuffing" — a scheme where it quietly planted tracking cookies to claim affiliate credit for purchases it had absolutely nothing to do with. In plain terms: the app was taking a cut of sales it didn't drive, while making its performance numbers look far stronger than they actually were.

For a startup selling itself on AI-powered shopping intelligence, that's a devastating allegation. Conversion metrics aren't just vanity stats — they're how these companies prove their value to brands, attract investors, and justify commission revenue. If those numbers are inflated, the whole pitch falls apart.

And the bigger question no one wants to ask: how many other "AI commerce" success stories are built the same way?
