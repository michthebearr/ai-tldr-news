🧠 Apple just sued OpenAI for trade secret theft — and it goes all the way to the top.

This isn't a rogue contractor story. Apple's lawsuit alleges the misconduct was directed by OpenAI's senior leadership, including a longtime former Apple employee who apparently brought more than just their résumé to the new job. The specific secrets stolen haven't been made public yet — but the fact that leadership is named means this was deliberate, not accidental.

The timing is brutal. Apple and OpenAI are already in bed together — ChatGPT is baked into iOS through their partnership deal. Now they're in court. That's a level of corporate awkwardness that's hard to overstate.

One lawsuit could quietly blow up the most visible AI deal in consumer tech.
