🧠 OpenAI wants ChatGPT to become a member of your family.

A new job listing reveals OpenAI is hiring a product manager specifically to build ChatGPT experiences for families, caregivers, and older adults. We're talking parental controls, kid-friendly modes, and features designed for aging parents — not just the tech-savvy power users who got them here.

This is a major strategic pivot. Right now, AI is mostly a tool for professionals and early adopters. But the real platform war is for the living room — and whoever earns trust across parents, kids, and grandparents at the same time builds loyalty that's nearly impossible to dislodge.

The family that ChatGPTs together, stays together.
