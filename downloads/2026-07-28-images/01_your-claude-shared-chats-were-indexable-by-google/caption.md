🧠 Your Claude "shared" chats weren't just shared with who you thought.

Anthropic's share-a-link feature — built to let users send conversations and Artifacts to others — quietly had a second audience: Google. Those public links were being crawled and indexed, meaning sensitive prompts and outputs could show up in search results for anyone to find.

Anthropic appears to have fixed it. What they haven't confirmed is how long the window was open — or how much got indexed before anyone noticed.

This is a classic "share via link" trap. Public link does not mean private link. It never has. And if your team has been piping confidential workflows, internal strategies, or client data through Claude and hitting share? It's time to find out exactly what's sitting in Google's index right now.
