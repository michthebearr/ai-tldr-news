💡 Microsoft's CEO just told you to stop depending on Microsoft — kind of.

Satya Nadella warned this week that companies routing everything through a single AI provider are making a serious strategic mistake. No in-house models, no AI gateway layer, no backup plan. That's not a tech stack — that's a liability.

An AI gateway sits between your app and whichever model it's calling. It abstracts away vendor lock-in, keeps your prompts out of the provider's hands, and gives you flexibility to swap models without rebuilding everything from scratch. Nadella's point: if you don't have that layer, you don't own your AI strategy.

Here's the wild part — this advice is coming from the CEO of a company that sells both Azure AI and Copilot. That's either genuinely self-aware leadership, a subtle pitch for Microsoft's own infrastructure stack, or both.
