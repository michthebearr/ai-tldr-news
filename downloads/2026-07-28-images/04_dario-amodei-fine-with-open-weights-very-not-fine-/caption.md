💡 Dario Amodei isn't anti-open-source. He's anti-China-getting-open-source.

The Anthropic CEO stepped into the open-vs-closed AI debate this week — and immediately reframed it. His position isn't that open-weight models are bad. It's that the debate everyone's having is missing the actual point.

The real issue, according to Amodei: geopolitical asymmetry. When frontier models are freely available, the question isn't just "who benefits?" It's *who benefits more?* And his answer is that adversaries — China specifically — may be extracting more value from open models than the open-source community realizes.

That's a distinction the loudest voices in this debate rarely make. Open vs. closed is a tech argument. This is a national security argument dressed up in AI clothes.
