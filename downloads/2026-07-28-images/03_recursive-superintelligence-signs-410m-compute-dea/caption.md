💡 A startup just signed a $410M cloud deal with Amazon — and it has almost no employees.

Recursive Superintelligence is betting that AI can build itself better than humans can. Their entire model: skip the engineering headcount, pour capital into compute, and let the system recursively improve its own architecture. It's not a metaphor. It's the actual product roadmap.

$410M to AWS is the operating budget. The engineers *are* the AI.

This is what an AI-native company looks like in 2026 — nine-figure infrastructure bills, near-zero payroll, and a thesis that self-improving systems will eventually compound faster than any human R&D team on earth. If they're right, it's one of the most important bets in tech. If they're wrong, it's the most expensive science project in cloud history.

The age of AI building AI isn't coming. It's already on the invoice.
