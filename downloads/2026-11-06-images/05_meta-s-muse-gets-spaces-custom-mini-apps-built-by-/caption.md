💡 Meta just turned its AI assistant into an app store — and Apple should be paying attention.

Meta quietly launched Spaces inside its Muse AI, and it's a bigger deal than the name suggests. Instead of downloading an app, you just describe what you want — a school dashboard, a shopping hub, a trivia game — and Muse builds it for you on the spot. No App Store. No developer. No waiting.

Connect your Gmail or Google Calendar and it pulls in live data to make these mini-apps actually functional, not just aesthetic. That's the move that separates this from a gimmick.

Meta isn't building a better chatbot. They're building a platform where the AI is the operating layer — and you just tell it what you need.
