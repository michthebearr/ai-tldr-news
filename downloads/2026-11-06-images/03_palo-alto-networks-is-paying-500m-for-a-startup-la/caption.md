💡 Palo Alto Networks is paying $500M for a startup last valued at $85M — and it makes complete sense.

Clutch is an AI agent security startup. It's backed by Lightspeed and SignalFire, and until recently, was worth $85 million on paper. Now Palo Alto Networks is reportedly set to acquire it for around half a billion dollars. That's nearly a 6x markup, and nobody in the room is blinking.

Here's why: AI agents are no longer a demo. They're inside real enterprise systems — reading files, executing tasks, making decisions. And the security infrastructure to handle that? It barely exists. Legacy vendors like Palo Alto can't afford to spend three years building coverage from scratch while their customers are already exposed.

So they buy. Fast. At whatever price it takes.

This is what happens when urgency outpaces the market. Agentic AI is moving so quickly that incumbents are essentially bidding against their own vulnerability. Expect more acquisitions like this — and expect the price tags to keep climbing.
