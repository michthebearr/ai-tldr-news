💡 Airbnb is building a real AI travel agent — and it wants to make Google and Expedia irrelevant.

The company just confirmed it's launching a full AI travel agent in the first half of 2027. We're not talking about a chatbot slapped onto a search bar. Airbnb is describing something that plans your entire trip, compares options, handles bookings, and stays with you on the ground — start to finish.

That's a fundamentally different product from what exists today. Most travel AI is still glorified autocomplete. Airbnb is betting it can build something that actually acts on your behalf — the kind of agentic AI that's been talked about for years but rarely delivered at consumer scale.

The stakes are enormous. Travel is a $1.5 trillion industry still dominated by search and aggregators. If Airbnb pulls this off, the trip-planning game changes completely.
