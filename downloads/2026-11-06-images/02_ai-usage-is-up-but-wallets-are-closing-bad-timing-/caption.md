💡 AI usage is surging — but companies are spending less. That's a problem nobody wants to say out loud.

New data from Ramp shows a widening gap between how much enterprises are *using* AI and how much they're actually *paying* for it. Price wars between the big labs plus smarter procurement from large customers are squeezing revenue even as adoption numbers look great on paper.

Great on paper is not the same as great for business.

This is exactly the environment Anthropic is walking into ahead of its IPO. If the market can't reconcile rising usage with flatlining spend, the hypergrowth valuations baked into these companies start looking shaky — and Anthropic's public debut becomes the first real stress test of that thesis in front of Wall Street.

The AI gold rush is real. The revenue model? Still being figured out.
