💡 Democrats just handed the AI industry its biggest political problem yet.

Tuesday's midterms weren't just a blue wave — they were a direct referendum on AI infrastructure. Voters weighed in on 41 data center-related ballot measures and shot down 40 of them. That's not a trend. That's a message.

And it wasn't limited to ballot measures. Candidates who ran against data center expansion — against the noise, the water usage, the power grid strain — won anyway. The AI boom has built its physical foundation in communities that are now actively voting against it.

Two years of a Democrat-led Congress could mean real legislative teeth: zoning restrictions, energy mandates, permitting delays. The companies racing to build out AI infrastructure just got a glimpse of what the next chapter looks like — and it's a lot more friction.
