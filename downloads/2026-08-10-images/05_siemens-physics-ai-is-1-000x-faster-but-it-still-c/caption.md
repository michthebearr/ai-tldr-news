💡 Siemens says Physics AI is 1,000x faster — but it still doesn't get the final say.

Siemens' Physics AI tools can tear through thousands of design variations in the time traditional simulation handles a handful. That's not incremental improvement. That's a different category of engineering speed entirely.

But CTO Sam Mahalingam made one thing crystal clear this week: no matter how fast or capable the AI gets, it will not be authorized to sign off on safety-critical components. If a part fails catastrophically, a human signed off on it. Full stop. That line isn't moving.

This matters beyond Siemens. A major industrial player is essentially drawing the boundary map for AI autonomy in physical engineering — and regulators, competitors, and customers are all watching how that line gets drawn.

Fast enough to reshape design. Not trusted enough to own consequences.
