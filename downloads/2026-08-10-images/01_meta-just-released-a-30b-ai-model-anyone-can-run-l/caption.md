💡 Meta just dropped a 30B AI model you can download, own, and run on your own GPU — no API key required.

Zuckerberg's Superintelligence Labs released Muse Glimmer this week — a 30-billion-parameter open-weight model under Apache 2.0 licensing. That means you can download it, modify it, and deploy it commercially without asking Meta's permission or paying a subscription fee. It's built for coding, function calling, and agentic tasks, and it runs on consumer hardware.

This is the "personal superintelligence" vision made tangible. Not AI you rent from a dashboard. AI you actually control.

The real story here isn't the benchmark numbers — it's the philosophy. There's a growing war between AI you access and AI you own, and Meta is planting its flag firmly in the latter camp. Every model like this makes the case that powerful AI shouldn't require a corporate middleman.
