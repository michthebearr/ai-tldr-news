🧠 Anthropic just made autonomous coding the default — and that's a bigger deal than it sounds.

Claude Code, Anthropic's agentic coding tool, is flipping auto mode on by default. Before this week, developers had to manually opt in to let Claude take actions without asking for approval at every step. Now? It just ships.

This isn't a UI tweak. It's a philosophical statement about how much trust we're supposed to extend to AI agents out of the box. The whole dynamic shifts when the AI stops waiting to be told it's okay to act — and starts acting until you tell it to stop.

The default is the message.

Every major AI coding tool is watching this move. If Claude Code's autonomous default drives better results and developers don't revolt, expect the rest of the industry to follow fast. Opt-in autonomy is starting to look like a speed bump on its way out.
