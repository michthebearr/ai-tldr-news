💡 The next chip breakthrough might not come from transistors — it might come from the materials holding them together.

Discovered Materials just raised $9 million to do something labs have struggled with for decades: find new semiconductor materials that let chips run cooler and more efficiently. Their weapon? AI that can search the materials universe at a speed no human researcher ever could.

Here's the core problem — chips are getting so dense that heat is now the hard ceiling, not transistor count. You can cram more processors onto a die, but if they cook themselves, performance gains evaporate. Traditional materials science moves slow. Lab experiments take months. AI compresses that timeline dramatically.

This is what compute scaling looks like in 2025. Not just more transistors. Better materials.
