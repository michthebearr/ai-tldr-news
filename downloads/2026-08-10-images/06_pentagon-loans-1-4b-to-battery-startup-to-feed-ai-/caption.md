💡 The Pentagon just handed a $1.4 billion loan to a battery startup — because AI's power problem is now a national security problem.

The U.S. Department of Defense is backing Sila, a battery materials company, to scale up production at its Washington State facility. The pitch: militaries need better energy storage. But so does every data center quietly straining under the weight of AI workloads.

This is the tell. When the Pentagon starts funding the energy supply chain, it's a signal that AI's infrastructure demands have moved well beyond Silicon Valley's problem to solve. Power reliability isn't just a tech inconvenience anymore — it's a strategic vulnerability.

The grid can't keep up. The batteries aren't there yet. And now the DoD is writing nine-figure checks to fix that.
