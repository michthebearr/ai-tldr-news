💰 Everyone's obsessed with the chips. Nobody's talking about what happens between them.

Cornelis Networks just raised $205 million to fix one of AI infrastructure's least glamorous — and most critical — problems: interconnects. The cables, switches, and networking fabric that let AI chips actually talk to each other. When you're running a massive training cluster, the chips themselves aren't always the bottleneck. The time they spend waiting on each other is.

Nvidia dominates the GPU layer. But InfiniBand and NVLink have real limits at scale. As clusters grow into tens of thousands of chips, the interconnect layer becomes the chokepoint — and that's exactly where Cornelis is planting its flag. The hyperscalers building the next generation of AI infrastructure need this problem solved badly enough that $205M is just the opening bid.

The picks-and-shovels play in AI isn't over. It's just getting more specific.
