💡 Waymo just landed in Las Vegas — and its 15-city streak is starting to look unstoppable.

The robotaxi company officially launched commercial operations in Vegas this week, marking its 15th market. And the choice isn't random. High tourist volume, clean grid-style streets, and a city government that's been rolling out the welcome mat for autonomous vehicles for years. It's almost a perfect testing environment.

But the bigger story isn't Las Vegas. It's the quiet, methodical playbook Waymo is running. Every new city adds operational data, regulatory experience, and institutional knowledge that can't be shortcut. Competitors aren't just behind on technology — they're behind on years of real-world reps.

That gap is getting harder to close.
