💡 Siri just had its biggest glow-up in five years — and reviewers are actually impressed.

Apple dropped iOS 27 and macOS 27 Golden Gate today, and the standout feature is a completely rebuilt Siri that people are genuinely recommending. Not "it's fine." Not "give it time." Actually recommending. It can answer real questions, manage tasks across apps, and integrate so deeply that third-party apps like Daydream let you search for products using photos from your camera roll — without ever opening the app.

That's a different Siri than the one we've been tolerating for a decade.

Here's the number that makes this bigger than just a software update: Apple has 1.5 billion active iPhones. If even a small slice of those users start relying on Siri daily, Apple's on-device AI deployment becomes one of the largest in human history — almost overnight. Oh, and Intel Mac support is gone. Apple Silicon only from here. The era of hedging is officially over.
