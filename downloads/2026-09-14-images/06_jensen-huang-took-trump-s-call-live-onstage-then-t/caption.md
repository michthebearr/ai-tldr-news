🤖 Jensen Huang took a live call from the President onstage — and that's somehow not even the most interesting part.

At the All-In Summit this week, Nvidia's CEO put Trump on speakerphone in front of a live audience. Classic Jensen. But beyond the spectacle, Huang used the moment to make a bigger argument: robots aren't coming for your job, and the AI apocalypse crowd is wrong.

Convenient take, given that Nvidia needs the world to believe more AI infrastructure spending is a feature, not a bug. The more fear you remove from the equation, the easier it is to sell the picks and shovels.

Here's what's actually going on: when the most important chip CEO alive is fielding presidential calls in real time and shaping the public narrative around robot risk, AI infrastructure isn't just a tech story anymore. It's geopolitics. It's policy. It's power.
