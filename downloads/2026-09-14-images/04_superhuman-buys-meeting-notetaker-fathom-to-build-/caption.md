💡 Superhuman just bought AI meeting notetaker Fathom — and the move says everything about where productivity software is headed.

Superhuman, the premium email app that made $30/month feel justified, has acquired Fathom, a YC-backed meeting notetaker with 400,000 monthly active users. On the surface, it looks like an email company buying a Zoom bot. Under the hood, it's a land grab.

Here's the real story: standalone AI notetakers are already getting crushed. Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams are baking transcription and summaries directly into their platforms for free. Fathom's window to survive as an independent product was closing fast. Superhuman needed a data layer that connects what you said in a meeting to what you send in an email — and what an AI agent does next. This acquisition builds that bridge.

The endgame is an agentic productivity stack. Email → meetings → autonomous follow-through. Whoever owns that full loop owns the workday.
