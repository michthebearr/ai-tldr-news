💡 Self-driving trucks just cleared one of their biggest legal hurdles yet.

Aurora Innovation and Kodiak AI both received permits from the California DMV this week to run fully autonomous trucks on public highways. California isn't known for handing these out easily — this is a state that has historically pumped the brakes harder than most when it comes to autonomous vehicle approvals.

Two major players. Same week. Same state. That's not a coincidence — that's a signal.

Trucking is an $800B+ industry that's been waiting on regulators to catch up with the technology. This might be the moment the dam starts to break. With California on board, the pressure on other states to follow suit just got a lot heavier.
