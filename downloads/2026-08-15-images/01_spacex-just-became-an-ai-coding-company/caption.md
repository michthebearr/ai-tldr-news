💡 SpaceX just acquired Cursor — and nobody really knows why.

The deal is closed. Elon Musk's rocket company now owns one of the most popular AI coding assistants on the market, used by developers everywhere. That's a strange sentence to type, but here we are.

SpaceX has always stayed in its lane — rockets, satellites, Starship. AI was supposed to be xAI's problem. So why is an aerospace company suddenly holding a mainstream developer tool? The options on the table: internal talent grab, a play to supercharge SpaceX's own engineering workflows, or the opening move in something much larger nobody's mapped out yet.

Any way you cut it, this is a weird power move.
