🔐 Your ChatGPT account is more dangerous to lose than your Netflix login.

TechCrunch just dropped a practical guide on how to check for unauthorized access across the major AI platforms — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and more. And the timing isn't random. These accounts are increasingly in attackers' crosshairs.

Here's the problem: most people treat AI chat accounts like throwaway subscriptions. But think about what's actually in there — business strategy, client details, personal conversations, saved workflows, and in some cases direct connections to your files, email, and apps. A compromised AI account isn't just embarrassing. It's a full data breach hiding in plain sight.

The guide walks you through exactly where to look and what to do. Worth five minutes of your time today.
