🔐 Iranian hackers just broke into US water plants — and that should terrify you.

Over the past few weeks, hackers with alleged ties to the Iranian government successfully breached multiple US water utility systems. Not probing. Not scanning. Actually inside.

The full scope of what they accessed — and whether they could flip a switch — is still being assessed. But the fact that state-sponsored actors are sitting inside critical water infrastructure isn't a warning shot anymore. It's the shot.

Water systems are notoriously under-resourced on cybersecurity. They're soft targets running legacy software, and everyone in the security world has known it for years. This is what state-sponsored cyberwar looks like when it stops being theoretical.
