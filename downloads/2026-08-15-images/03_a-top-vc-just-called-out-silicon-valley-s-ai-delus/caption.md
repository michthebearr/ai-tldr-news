💡 One of Silicon Valley's top VCs just told the AI hype machine to slow down — and he's not a skeptic.

Joshua Kushner of Thrive Capital dropped his first-ever investor letter this week, and the message is a gut check for his own industry. This is the guy who backed OpenAI. He's not anti-AI. He's anti-delusion.

His warning: the opportunity is real, but "it would be a grave error to let excitement weaken investment discipline." Translation — the valuations and deal terms being thrown around right now are being driven by fear of missing out, not fundamentals.

When a top-tier insider tells the room to calm down, you listen.
