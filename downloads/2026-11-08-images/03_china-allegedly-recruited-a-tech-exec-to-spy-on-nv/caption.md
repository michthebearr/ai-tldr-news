💡 China allegedly recruited a spy to get inside access on Nvidia's Jensen Huang.

A US intelligence memo reportedly circulated this year claims China's Ministry of State Security recruited a female executive at a company called Megaspeed — specifically to surveil Nvidia's CEO. That's not a cyberattack. That's a human intelligence operation aimed at the most powerful man in semiconductors.

Nvidia sits at the absolute center of the global AI arms race. Its chips are the scarce resource every country and company is fighting over — and the US has been aggressively cutting off China's access. If Beijing is now running spy operations directly targeting Jensen Huang, it tells you exactly how desperate and high-stakes this chip war has become.

This isn't corporate espionage. This is geopolitics with a human face.
