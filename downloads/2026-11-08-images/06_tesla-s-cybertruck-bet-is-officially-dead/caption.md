🔐 Tesla's Cybertruck experiment is officially over — and the fallout matters more than the truck ever did.

TechCrunch Mobility is calling it: the Cybertruck failed. Not just as a sales story, but as a strategic bet. The polarizing stainless steel gamble never converted mainstream truck buyers, and it quietly drained the engineering and marketing resources Tesla desperately needs right now.

Here's why this stings: Tesla's autonomous driving and robotics ambitions run on vehicle revenue. Every dollar the Cybertruck didn't earn is a dollar Musk can't throw at FSD, Optimus, or staying competitive in an AI-era car market that isn't waiting around.

Meanwhile, the competition isn't slowing down.
