💡 Elvis and Eminem's rights holders are suing Suno — and the walls are closing in on AI music fast.

AI music startup Suno is facing two new lawsuits this week. One comes from the rights holders of Elvis Presley's catalog. The other from the publisher behind some of Eminem's biggest records. Both allege Suno is illegally generating music and lyrics that mimic those artists without permission.

The Elvis suit goes a step further — it invokes Tennessee's ELVIS Act, a state law specifically designed to protect artists' voice and likeness from AI cloning. That's not just a copyright claim. That's a whole new legal weapon being pointed directly at generative audio tools.

Suno was already in a legal war with the major labels. Now add Elvis and Slim Shady to the plaintiff list. The window for AI music startups that trained on copyrighted material without licensing deals is getting very small, very fast.
