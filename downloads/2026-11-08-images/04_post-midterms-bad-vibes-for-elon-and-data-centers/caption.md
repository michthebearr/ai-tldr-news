💡 The midterms just made building the AI future a whole lot harder.

The political dust is still settling, but the tech industry isn't waiting around — and it's nervous. The new balance of power in Congress is throwing real uncertainty over two things that matter deeply to the AI boom: Elon Musk's standing inside the administration, and the future of large-scale data center expansion.

TechCrunch's Equity podcast broke down what the shifting landscape could mean for energy permitting, infrastructure funding, and the political relationships that have quietly been greasing the wheels of AI buildout. Musk in particular is facing headwinds — his political capital may not stretch as far as it did 48 hours ago.

Here's the thing people forget: AI isn't just software. It runs on physical infrastructure — massive data centers that need land, power, permits, and political will. Slow that down, and you slow down the entire industry.
