💡 Apple is quietly freaking out about Siri — and the pressure is coming from the top.

CEO John Ternus is reportedly pushing AI chief Craig Federighi to move faster on Siri's roadmap. The goal: ship a genuinely powerful AI agent that can actually compete with OpenAI and Meta. Apple has a functional version running internally — but the company knows it's not close enough.

When your hardware chief is walking into AI meetings demanding speed, that's not a routine check-in. That's a signal the gap is big enough to threaten the whole ecosystem. iPhones sell partly on the promise of a smart assistant. Right now, that promise is losing.

Two major capability upgrades are reportedly in the works. The question is whether Apple can move fast enough to matter.
