🧠 Apple may be about to let ChatGPT and Claude move into your home.

Code buried in the iOS 17.3 beta reveals a "Conversational Apps" category inside Apple's HomeView smart home interface. No official announcement. No press release. Just a quiet signal that third-party AI chatbots could soon be living inside your home hub — with bidirectional audio support to boot.

This is a bigger deal than it sounds. Apple's smart home ecosystem has always been a walled garden. Letting ChatGPT and Claude in would be one of the most significant platform openings the company has made in years — and a clear sign that even Apple can't afford to keep ambient AI at arm's length.

The home hub is the next frontier. Whoever owns that interface owns how people talk to their homes, their devices, and eventually, everything in between.
