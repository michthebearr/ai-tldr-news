🏎️ A nonprofit wants to build the "World Wide Web of AI" — open, free, and built for everyone the big labs are ignoring.

Current AI is working on an open AI infrastructure modeled on the early internet — shared protocols, multilingual models, and on-device deployment that doesn't require a subscription or a Silicon Valley data center. The goal is a commons, not a platform.

The timing is pointed. OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic are in a full sprint to lock users into proprietary ecosystems. If you speak English, have a credit card, and live somewhere with fast internet, that race mostly works out for you. For billions of people who don't? Not so much.

An open alternative with real backing and a credible mission isn't just idealistic — it's necessary.
