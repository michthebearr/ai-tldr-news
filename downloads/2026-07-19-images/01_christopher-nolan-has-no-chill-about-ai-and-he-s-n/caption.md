💡 Christopher Nolan just called AI a Trojan horse — and he's not pulling punches.

Speaking to press around his upcoming film *Odyssey*, Nolan offered one of the more memorable AI takes from any public figure in recent memory: it's an "obvious Trojan horse," and "everybody knows the Greeks are inside." Dry, precise, and a little devastating.

What makes this land harder than a think piece is who's saying it. Nolan isn't some random tech critic — he's one of the last directors with genuine institutional power in Hollywood. When he frames AI not as a creative tool but as a slow-moving capture of culture and media, that's not paranoia. That's pattern recognition.

The creative industries have been quietly building toward this consensus for a while. Nolan just said the quiet part loud.
