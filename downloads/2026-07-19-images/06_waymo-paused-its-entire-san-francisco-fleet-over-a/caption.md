💡 A power outage just took down Waymo's entire San Francisco fleet — and that's a bigger deal than it sounds.

Waymo paused all robotaxi service across the city after a power outage disrupted operations. About an hour later, service quietly resumed. No drama, no explanation — just a city-wide AV shutdown treated like a minor inconvenience.

It isn't one. This isn't the first time San Francisco's infrastructure has knocked Waymo offline, and that's the problem. A robotaxi network that goes dark during a routine urban power hiccup isn't ready for the unglamorous chaos of real city life. Reliability isn't just about the cars — it's about everything they depend on to function.

Every pause like this hands ammunition to critics who say AV deployment is sprinting ahead of the resilience work that actually needs to happen first.
