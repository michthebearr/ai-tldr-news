💡 A Chinese AI startup just priced its model so low, critics are calling it "full AI communism."

Moonshot AI dropped a new version of Kimi this week — and the pricing wasn't just competitive, it was aggressive enough to spark an entire discourse about what Chinese labs are actually trying to do here.

The phrase making the rounds: "full AI communism." The idea being that Chinese labs aren't trying to win on quality alone — they're trying to make frontier AI so cheap that Western companies can't sustainably compete. Kimi has already been quietly gaining traction outside China. This release makes that global push impossible to ignore.

And it puts OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google in an uncomfortable spot. Match the pricing and watch your margins collapse. Don't match it, and start explaining — convincingly — why your model is worth the premium.

That's a hard conversation to have when the gap keeps narrowing.
