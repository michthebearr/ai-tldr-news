🧠 Dave Eggers walked into OpenAI's headquarters and told 200 employees their product was destroying a generation of writers.

Sam Altman personally invited the novelist — founder of McSweeney's and multiple youth writing nonprofits — to speak to staff last year. Eggers didn't soften a word. He told the room that ChatGPT was "silencing an entire generation," stripping young people of the struggle that actually makes writers.

In the belly of the beast. To the people who built it.

The real question isn't whether Eggers is right — it's why Altman invited him in the first place. Genuine intellectual courage? A calculated move to let staff process their guilt and move on? Either way, the war between AI labs and the creative class just got a lot harder to ignore from inside Silicon Valley.

The critics are no longer just shouting from op-eds. They're standing at the podium.
