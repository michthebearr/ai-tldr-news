💡 Amazon is reportedly firing employees who testified against its data centers — and that should alarm everyone.

Three Amazon software engineers showed up to Seattle City Council hearings in June to support a moratorium on new data center construction. They cited local law. They spoke publicly. They did everything right.

Amazon moved against them the day after the moratorium passed.

The engineers are now formally accusing the company of illegal retaliation under a Seattle law that explicitly protects workers' political speech. This isn't a vague HR dispute — it's a direct confrontation between Big Tech's infrastructure ambitions and employees who dared to push back in the one place it counts: city government.

This is what the AI buildout actually looks like up close. Not just chips and server farms — but workers being made an example of for slowing things down.
