💡 Enterprises dumped millions into AI — and most of them have almost nothing to show for it.

The "tokenmaxxing" era is officially over. That's the term for the phase where CEOs told employees to use AI as much as humanly possible, burn the tokens, justify the contracts. Uber reportedly blew through its entire annual AI budget in just a few months. Companies have quietly started cutting Claude licenses. Meta killed its internal AI usage leaderboard. The vibe has shifted fast.

NEA partner Tiffany Luck confirmed what insiders already knew: the ROI just isn't materializing fast enough. Buying access to AI tools is easy. Turning that access into measurable business value? Turns out, extremely hard.

The honeymoon is over. What comes next is a brutal reckoning — companies will be forced to identify which AI use cases actually pay for themselves and kill everything else. The era of vibes-based AI spend is done.
