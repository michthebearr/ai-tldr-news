💡 G7 leaders are realizing American AI comes with an off switch — and they're not okay with it.

Macron and Modi both raised the alarm at this week's G7 summit: what happens when the U.S. decides to pull the plug on AI access overnight? It's not a hypothetical anymore. Anthropic's recent blackout of its Fable 5 model gave world leaders a preview of exactly how fast that can happen — no warning, no negotiation, no recourse.

The dilemma is real. Countries want frontier AI, but frontier AI is almost entirely American. That means depending on U.S. policy, U.S. companies, and U.S. goodwill to keep the lights on. That's not a technology strategy — that's a vulnerability.

Expect this to light a fire under domestic AI investment and open-source alternatives everywhere from Paris to New Delhi. The ability to revoke AI access is now a geopolitical lever. The rest of the world just figured that out.
