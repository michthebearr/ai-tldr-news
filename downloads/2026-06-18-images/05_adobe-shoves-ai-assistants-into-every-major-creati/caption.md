🏎️ Adobe just put an AI assistant inside every app you use to make a living.

Photoshop. Premiere. Illustrator. InDesign. Frame.io. All of them got conversational AI assistants today in public beta. Built on Adobe's Firefly model, the chatbots let you describe what you want in plain language — no more hunting through nested menus for a feature you use once a month.

This is a big deal. Adobe has 30+ million Creative Cloud subscribers. That makes this one of the largest simultaneous AI assistant rollouts to professional users ever attempted — not a prototype, not a waitlist. A live beta, right inside the tools people get paid to use.

The real story isn't the feature. It's the urgency. Adobe is sprinting to make AI feel native to creative workflows before a leaner competitor does it first.
