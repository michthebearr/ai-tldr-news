💡 The CEO of Hugging Face just made the most compelling case yet that the AI race is happening in the wrong direction.

Clem Delangue isn't arguing that GPT-4 or Claude aren't impressive. He's arguing they're increasingly irrelevant to how enterprises actually ship AI. Cost, data privacy, and the need to own your own stack are pushing companies toward open models — fast.

His point lands hard: if most real-world AI in production runs on open models, what exactly are the frontier labs racing toward? A market that's quietly moved on.

That's not just a philosophical jab. It's a direct challenge to the valuations sitting under OpenAI and Anthropic. If enterprises aren't the customer, who is?
