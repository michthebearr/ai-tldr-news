💡 New York just hit pause on the AI data center boom — and it could change everything.

Governor Kathy Hochul has signed a temporary freeze on all new large-scale data center approvals in New York State. The reasons: skyrocketing electricity costs, massive water consumption, and communities with zero say in what's being built next door. It's the first move of its kind from any U.S. state.

The AI industry has been operating on one core assumption — that infrastructure can expand as fast as ambition demands. New York just challenged that. The freeze isn't permanent, but the message is clear: build first, ask questions never isn't a strategy anymore.

If other states start borrowing from Hochul's playbook, the AI gold rush hits a wall it didn't see coming.
