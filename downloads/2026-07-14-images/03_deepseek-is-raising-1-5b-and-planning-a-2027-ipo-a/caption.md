💰 DeepSeek is heading to Wall Street — and it could change the AI race forever.

The Chinese AI lab that sent Silicon Valley into a panic earlier this year is reportedly raising $1.5 billion in pre-IPO funding, with a public debut targeted for 2027. The valuation? A cool $71 billion — which would make DeepSeek one of the most valuable AI companies on the planet, full stop.

This isn't just a funding story. A public DeepSeek means Chinese AI gets a permanent seat at the global capital markets table. Suddenly the US-China AI race isn't just a geopolitical talking point — it's a ticker symbol investors can bet on.

The company already proved it could compete on model quality at a fraction of Western costs. Now it's proving it can compete on ambition, too.
