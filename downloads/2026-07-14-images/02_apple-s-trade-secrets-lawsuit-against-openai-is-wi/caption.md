🧠 Apple is suing OpenAI — and the details read like a corporate espionage thriller.

The lawsuit, filed last Friday, alleges that former Apple employees walked out the door with confidential files and handed them straight to OpenAI. We're not talking about vague accusations either. Apple claims job candidates were instructed to bring Apple hardware to their interviews, and that workers were openly joking about unauthorized access to Apple's internal systems.

Oh, and one ex-employee allegedly exploited a rare software bug to keep pulling sensitive files from Apple's network — after he'd already left the company.

This isn't just a messy HR story. OpenAI is eyeing an IPO, and a serious trade secrets lawsuit could throw a wrench into that entire timeline. Investors don't love the words "stolen IP" in the headlines right before a public offering.
