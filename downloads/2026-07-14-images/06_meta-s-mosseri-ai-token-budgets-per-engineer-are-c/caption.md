💡 Your engineers might soon have an AI spending allowance — just like a payroll budget.

Instagram chief Adam Mosseri dropped a prediction this week that's going to make a lot of developers uncomfortable: companies will soon cap how many AI tokens each engineer can burn through, the same way they cap salaries or expense accounts.

As AI coding assistants go from novelty to standard issue, the costs are showing up hard on the P&L. Querying these tools at scale isn't cheap — and leadership is starting to notice.

This isn't just a finance story. Token budgets would introduce a real resource constraint into software development for the first time. Right now, a developer can prompt an AI as many times as they want. That era may be ending.
