💡 Satya Nadella is warning enterprises about AI vendor lock-in — and he's the last person you'd expect to say it.

Microsoft's CEO is publicly telling companies to be careful about getting too dependent on proprietary AI models from closed labs. The risk: the vendor you're relying on today could hold enormous leverage over your business tomorrow.

The subtext is doing a lot of work here. This is the man who wrote a $13 billion check to OpenAI delivering a quiet but clear message: don't let any single AI supplier own your stack. Whether that's strategic positioning, genuine concern, or both — enterprises should probably be paying attention.

When the guy funding the AI arms race tells you to hedge, you hedge.
