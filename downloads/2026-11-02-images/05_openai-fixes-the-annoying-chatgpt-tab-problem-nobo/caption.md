🧠 OpenAI finally fixed the ChatGPT tab mess — and it only took them way too long.

ChatGPT's separate "Chat" and "Work" tabs are getting merged into a single unified interface. On top of that, ChatGPT and Codex will now share one usage pool instead of running on separate quotas. Sounds boring. It genuinely isn't.

Power users have been quietly losing their minds over this for months. You'd burn through your Codex limit while your Chat quota sat untouched — or vice versa. Classic "left hand, meet right hand" product design. The kind of friction that makes you question whether anyone at OpenAI actually uses their own tools daily.

The real story here isn't the fix. It's what it signals. OpenAI is finally treating ChatGPT like one coherent product instead of a Frankenstein of features that never fully talked to each other. Small housekeeping. Big implication.
