💰 A one-year-old startup just hit a $300M valuation — by betting that AI's real bottleneck isn't the model, it's the people who understand the data.

Trajectory just closed a $45M Series round with a straightforward pitch: let doctors, supply chain managers, and other domain experts fine-tune AI models themselves — no ML engineers, no data science team required. You bring the expertise. The platform handles the rest.

It sounds simple. It's not. "No-code AI training" is a crowded, competitive space with well-funded players already fighting for enterprise budgets. But that $300M valuation on a company that didn't exist 12 months ago tells you exactly how much money is chasing this problem right now.

Because here's the thing — foundation models are good enough. The gap isn't model quality anymore. It's that the people who actually understand the nuances of a business can't speak to the model directly. Whoever builds that bridge wins the enterprise AI race.
