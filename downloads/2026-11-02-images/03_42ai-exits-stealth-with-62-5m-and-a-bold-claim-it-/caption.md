💡 A startup just raised $62.5M to predict what you'll do next — even in situations it's never seen before.

42AI came out of stealth this week with a war chest and a claim that's either the future of AI or the beginning of a very uncomfortable conversation. Their "world model" doesn't just pattern-match your past behavior — it says it can forecast what you'll do in entirely new scenarios. That's a very different, very bigger thing.

The use cases they're dangling are massive: marketing, healthcare, security. But the technical details are thin. Right now it's a lot of ambition wrapped in a sleek deck, and the AI world is full of those. The difference here is $62.5M gives them enough runway to prove it — or blow up trying.

Behavioral prediction at this scale would be commercially enormous and ethically explosive. Unicorn or lightning rod. Probably both.
