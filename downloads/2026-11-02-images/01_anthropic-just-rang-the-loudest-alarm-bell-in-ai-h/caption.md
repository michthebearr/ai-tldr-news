💡 Anthropic just warned the world that their own AI might be approaching the point of no return.

RSI — Recursive Self-Improvement — is the moment AI gets good enough at training itself to trigger an exponential, self-sustaining capability explosion. It's been a theoretical boogeyman for years. Anthropic is now saying it might be close. About their own product.

That's not a doomer on Reddit. That's the lab that built Claude raising its hand and saying: we may be approaching a threshold that breaks every timeline, every regulation, and every safety framework currently on the table. When the people building the thing are the ones sounding the alarm, you don't get to look away.

If they're right, we don't need updated policies. We need entirely new ones — fast.
