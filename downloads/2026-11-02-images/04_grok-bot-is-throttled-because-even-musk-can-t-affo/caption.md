🏎️ Elon Musk built the fastest-growing AI product he's ever seen — and now he can't afford to run it.

Grok Bot is being throttled. Usage limits are live. The reason? Musk himself admitted the infrastructure costs are "unsustainably high." This is the same man who owns a rocket company and is building his own AI data centers.

Let that sink in.

It's easy to laugh at the irony, but the real story is bigger than Musk. Even a well-capitalized AI lab with serious compute ambitions can't keep up when a consumer product goes viral. Inference costs — the price of actually running these models at scale — remain one of AI's dirtiest unsolved problems. Every company in this space is quietly wrestling with the same math.

Growth is easy to celebrate. Paying for it is a different conversation entirely.
