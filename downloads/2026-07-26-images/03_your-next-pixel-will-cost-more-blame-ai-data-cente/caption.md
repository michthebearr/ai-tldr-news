💡 AI data centers are making your next phone more expensive — and Google just admitted it.

Google VP Shakil Barkat basically confirmed the Pixel 11 is getting a price hike, pointing to a global RAM shortage as the reason. The culprit isn't a bad harvest or a factory fire — it's the explosive, relentless buildout of AI infrastructure eating up RAM supply faster than manufacturers can keep up.

And it's not just Google. Apple, Nintendo, Microsoft, and Roku have all raised prices for the same reason. The chips powering ChatGPT and Gemini and every other model you use daily are competing directly with the chips inside your phone, your console, and your streaming stick.

The AI arms race has a consumer tax. You're already paying it.
