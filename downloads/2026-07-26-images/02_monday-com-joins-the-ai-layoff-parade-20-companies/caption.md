💡 Monday.com just blamed AI for layoffs — and it's far from alone.

Monday.com cut headcount this week and pointed directly at AI as a factor. That might sound like a one-off, but TechCrunch is tracking over 20 major tech companies in 2026 alone that have cited AI as a stated reason for reducing their workforce.

This is no longer a hypothetical. For years, the "AI will take jobs" conversation lived in op-eds and panel discussions. Now it's showing up in press releases and severance packages. The pattern is documented, accelerating, and very real.

2026 is shaping up as the year workers actually feel it.
