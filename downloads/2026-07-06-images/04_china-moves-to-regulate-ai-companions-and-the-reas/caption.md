💡 China just regulated AI girlfriends — and the real reason has nothing to do with content moderation.

Beijing has dropped new rules targeting AI companion apps — the kind designed to simulate ongoing, personal relationships with users. Not a one-time chatbot. A product engineered to make you emotionally dependent on it, every single day.

The regulations aren't framed around harmful content. They're framed around social stability. Read between the lines and you'll find something more uncomfortable: the Chinese government is worried that people are choosing AI relationships over human ones — and that it's showing up in birth rates.

That's a different kind of policy problem entirely.

Western governments are still dancing around questions like "is this chatbot safe?" China just skipped ahead to the harder one: what happens to society when AI companionship becomes a genuine substitute for human connection? We don't have an answer. China decided it couldn't wait for one.
