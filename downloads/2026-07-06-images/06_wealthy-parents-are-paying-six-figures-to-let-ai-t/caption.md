💡 Wealthy parents are spending six figures to let AI raise their kids' GPA — and maybe their entire future.

A small but growing group of America's richest families are enrolling their children in AI-first schools like Alpha School and Forge Prep. Tuition runs tens of thousands a year. The pitch: AI tutors, personalized curricula, and a head start on a world being reshaped by the very technology their parents can afford to bet on.

The irony is hard to miss. The families most protected from AI's economic disruption are the ones leaning into it hardest — using their kids as the first test subjects for a model that could eventually reach every classroom in the country.

That's the part that actually matters. Rich kids are the pilot program. The results — whatever they are — will determine how AI-native education scales to the rest of the world.
