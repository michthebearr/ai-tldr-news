💡 Microsoft just cut 4,800 jobs — and the timing is telling.

The layoffs landed today, day one of Microsoft's new fiscal year. Xbox took the heaviest blow, with four game studios — including Double Fine and Compulsion Games — spun off as independents. Commercial sales got hit hard too. About 2.1% of the global workforce, gone.

This is the second year in a row Microsoft has opened its fiscal year with a major reduction. That's not a one-off restructuring. That's a pattern. And when the world's most visible AI company keeps cutting headcount while posting record AI revenues, the narrative writes itself.

The "AI replacing jobs" story has been theoretical for a while. Microsoft is making it feel very real.
