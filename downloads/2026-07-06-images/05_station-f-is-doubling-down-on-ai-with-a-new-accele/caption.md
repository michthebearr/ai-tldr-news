💡 Europe doesn't want to rent its AI future from Silicon Valley — and Station F is betting it doesn't have to.

Paris's Station F just launched a new edition of its F/ai accelerator. For the uninitiated: Station F is the world's largest startup campus, and this program is its direct play to become the go-to launchpad for early-stage AI companies on the continent.

The timing isn't accidental. US giants are dominant. Chinese players are locked out of most Western markets. That leaves a real opening — and a real urgency — for Europe to build AI infrastructure it actually owns. France has been louder than most about this, and Station F is one of the few institutions with the scale and network to back the ambition with action.

Sovereign AI isn't just a buzzword. It's a geopolitical question. And right now, Europe is still writing its answer.
