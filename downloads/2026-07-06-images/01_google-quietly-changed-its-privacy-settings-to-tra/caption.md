💡 Google just quietly opted you into AI training — and they were counting on you not to notice.

Google updated its privacy settings this week in a way that automatically uses your data to train its AI models. No loud announcement. No obvious prompt. Just a subtle default change burying the opt-out deep enough that most people will never find it.

We're talking about your search history, your Google Docs, your activity data — all potentially feeding the model unless you go dig through settings and manually toggle it off. TechCrunch has the exact walkthrough on how to find it, and it's worth doing right now.

This is how normalization works. Not with a controversial policy announcement you can rally against — but with a quiet checkbox, a confusing menu, and the assumption that your inertia is consent.
