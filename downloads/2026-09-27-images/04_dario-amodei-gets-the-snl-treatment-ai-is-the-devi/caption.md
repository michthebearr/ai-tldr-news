🏎️ SNL just roasted Dario Amodei — and the joke writes itself.

Saturday Night Live took aim at the Anthropic CEO this weekend with a sketch built around one brutal punchline: "AI is the devil and I its maker." The bit captures the exact paradox Amodei lives in — a man who genuinely believes he might be building one of the most dangerous technologies in human history, and keeps building it anyway.

That tension isn't new. But SNL making it a punchline? That is.

When late-night comedy starts naming AI founders by name, something has shifted. AI risk isn't just a conference-room conversation anymore — it's mainstream cultural material. And that matters, because public perception shapes regulation, political pressure, and trust in ways that no earnings call or safety report ever could.

Dario Amodei spent years being the "responsible" AI guy. Now he's a sketch comedy villain. Welcome to the mainstream.
