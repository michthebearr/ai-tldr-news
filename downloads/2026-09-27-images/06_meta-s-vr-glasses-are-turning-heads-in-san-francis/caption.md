💡 Meta's VR glasses just impressed the people who've been roasting them for years.

The Verge went hands-on with Meta's new VR Glasses in San Francisco this week — and came away notably impressed. That matters more than it sounds. This is a publication that has spent years documenting every stumble, pivot, and overpriced headset in Meta's wearables graveyard.

So when they call the hardware impressive, you pay attention.

Here's why this is bigger than a product review: Meta has quietly been building a full AI assistant layer designed to live inside wearable devices. The vision was always there. The hardware just kept letting it down. If that gap has finally closed, Meta isn't just selling glasses — it's selling the next interface for everything its AI can do.

The platform bet Zuckerberg has been making for years might actually be starting to land.
