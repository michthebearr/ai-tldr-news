🏎️ Google just turned Gemini into a checkout button — and Amazon should be nervous.

Google is testing a feature that lets users in India buy products from Flipkart directly inside Gemini and AI Mode search. No redirect rabbit holes. No separate app. Just ask, browse, buy — without ever leaving Google's ecosystem.

This is bigger than it sounds. Google has spent decades being the place people go before they shop. Now it wants to be the place they actually shop. That's a fundamental shift — from directing traffic to owning the transaction.

If this scales globally, Google doesn't just win search. It wins the purchase moment — the most valuable real estate in digital commerce.
