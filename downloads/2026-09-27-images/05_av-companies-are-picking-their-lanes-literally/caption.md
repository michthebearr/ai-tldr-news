💡 The "full self-driving everywhere" pitch is officially dead — and that's actually good news.

TechCrunch's mobility team just mapped out what the AV shakeout actually looks like on the ground. Robotaxi companies are doubling down on specific cities. Trucking AV firms are locking into defined freight corridors. And personal vehicle players have quietly retreated to ADAS features rather than swing for full autonomy.

The companies that tried to win everywhere are gone or dying. The ones still standing? They picked a lane — sometimes literally — and refused to leave it. Discipline beat ambition.

The industry is finally growing up. A clearer map means smarter investment, more realistic timelines, and survivors who actually know what business they're in. Five years from now, the AV companies that exist will be the ones that stopped trying to do everything.
