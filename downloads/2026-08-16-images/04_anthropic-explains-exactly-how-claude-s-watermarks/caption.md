🧠 Anthropic just showed its work on AI watermarking — and the honesty is refreshing.

The company published technical details on how watermarks will be embedded into Claude's outputs. The system hides imperceptible signals inside generated text that can survive light editing — a meaningful step toward tracking where AI-written content actually came from.

But Anthropic didn't oversell it. Heavy rewriting breaks it. Code is its own problem, since syntax tweaks and reformatting are just part of how developers work. That kind of candor is rare, and it matters — because publishing the methodology is essentially an open invitation for researchers and adversaries to stress-test it in public.

Watermarking isn't a silver bullet. It's a starting point.
