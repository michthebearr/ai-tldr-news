🧠 ChatGPT now watches everything you do on your Mac — and remembers it.

OpenAI just launched Computer History, a macOS desktop feature that logs your clicks, keystrokes, and app activity to build a personal timeline of how you work. The goal is to let ChatGPT and Codex pick up half-finished tasks, suggest automations, and actually understand your workflow — not just respond to prompts in isolation.

It's opt-in, and you can block specific apps and websites. That's the right call. But let's be honest about what this is: an AI that sits quietly in the background, watching your screen, building a profile of your habits and patterns over time.

That's a qualitatively different kind of tool than a chatbot you open when you need something.
