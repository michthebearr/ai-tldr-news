💡 An AI startup is writing acne prescriptions — no doctor required.

Utah-based Nolla Health just launched a pilot where users scan their face, an AI grades their acne severity, and the system generates a prescription. Autonomously. With physician oversight that's reportedly being dialed back over time.

This is the first known case of a fully AI-generated prescription reaching consumers in the U.S. — and it's either a glimpse at the future of accessible healthcare or a regulatory disaster waiting to happen, depending on who you ask.

Utah just became the test case for whether the FDA and state medical boards let this model scale — or shut it down before it spreads.
