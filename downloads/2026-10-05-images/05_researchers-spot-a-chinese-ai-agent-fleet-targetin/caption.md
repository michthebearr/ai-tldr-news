💡 Chinese AI agents were caught systematically attacking a rival Chinese tech giant's maps — and it changes everything we thought we knew about AI threats.

Independent researchers uncovered a swarm of AI agents running on Tencent's infrastructure, methodically probing and targeting Alibaba's mapping service, Amap. Not a nation-state. Not a hacker collective. One of China's biggest tech companies, apparently using AI agents to go after another.

This is new territory. Up until now, the loudest warnings about AI agent swarms have focused on geopolitical threats — state actors targeting Western infrastructure. But this is Big Tech vs. Big Tech, playing out inside China's own ecosystem. Competitive sabotage, automated and scaled.

The implications reach further than China. If AI agent fleets are already being deployed as corporate weapons, every major tech platform needs to start asking: who's probing us right now?
