💡 Sam Altman wants you to accept "some bad things" from AI — and just trust that it's worth it.

In a new Vanity Fair interview, OpenAI's CEO said society should be prepared to absorb harms like hacks and scams as a reasonable cost of AI's benefits. That's a bold ask from someone running the most powerful AI lab on the planet.

But the real story happened in the margins. When the journalist tried to ask about a ChatGPT user who died by suicide, an OpenAI PR rep stepped in to redirect the conversation. A CEO asking for public trust while his team buries a user death in real time is not a great look.

This is exactly the governance vacuum regulators keep pointing to. No accountability structure. No independent oversight. Just a founder asking you to take his word for it.
