🧠 OpenAI just added watermarks to AI text in the EU — and they already found the loophole.

Starting now, ChatGPT and Codex outputs for EU users carry invisible, machine-readable watermarks under OpenAI's "textGrain" system. It's a direct response to the EU AI Act's push for AI content provenance — regulators want to know when you're reading something a machine wrote.

OpenAI says textGrain matched or beat Google DeepMind's SynthID in benchmarks. Sounds solid. Except they also quietly admitted that editing the text degrades the watermark's detectability. Which means anyone who wants to strip it just... edits the text.

That's not a safeguard. That's a speed bump.

This gap — between regulatory intent and technical reality — is about to become the next big lobbying and enforcement war. Expect lawyers, lobbyists, and loopholes to follow.
