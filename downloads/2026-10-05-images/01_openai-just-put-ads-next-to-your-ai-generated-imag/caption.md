🧠 OpenAI just put ads next to your AI-generated images — and it was only a matter of time.

Starting later this month in the U.S., visual ads will appear alongside image generation results in ChatGPT. Think sponsored products sitting right next to whatever you just dreamed up with DALL-E. It's subtle. It's strategic. And it changes everything about what ChatGPT actually is.

OpenAI has been burning billions on compute while handing out free tiers like candy. That was never a business model — it was a land grab. Ads are the inevitable reckoning.

This isn't just a monetization move. It's a signal that ChatGPT is graduating from "cool AI tool" to full-blown platform — one that's going to reshape how brands reach people the same way Google and Instagram did before it.
