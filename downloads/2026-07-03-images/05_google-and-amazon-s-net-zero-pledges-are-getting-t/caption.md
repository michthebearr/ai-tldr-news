💡 Google and Amazon's net-zero pledges are being quietly dismantled — by their own AI ambitions.

Both companies made big, public commitments to hitting net-zero carbon targets. Bold timelines. Press releases. The whole thing. Then AI happened.

A new analysis shows that the energy demands from AI training and inference are blowing so far past original carbon accounting plans that neither company can realistically stay on track. Data centers are being built faster than clean energy can supply them, and the emissions gap is widening every quarter.

The environmental cost of AI is no longer a footnote. It's the headline — and regulators are starting to notice.
