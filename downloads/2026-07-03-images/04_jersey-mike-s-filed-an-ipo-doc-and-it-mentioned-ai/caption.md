💰 Jersey Mike's just mentioned AI in its IPO filing — and that tells you everything about where we are right now.

TechCrunch went digging through the sandwich chain's public offering documents and found exactly what you'd expect in 2026: an AI shoutout. Not because Jersey Mike's is building models or training agents. Because no company can go public anymore without bowing to the algorithm gods, even if they're mostly just making subs.

This is the buzzword bubble in its final, beautiful form. When a regional sandwich chain has to pepper its SEC filing with AI language to keep investors happy, the disclosure has become completely untethered from reality. It's not information anymore — it's ritual.

And that's the real problem. If every company from enterprise software to cold cuts is claiming AI relevance, how are regulators and analysts supposed to separate the signal from the noise? The answer, right now, is: they can't.
