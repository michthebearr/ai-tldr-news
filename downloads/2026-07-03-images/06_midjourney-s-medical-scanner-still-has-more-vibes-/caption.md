🏥 Midjourney is building a medical scanner — and they'd really prefer you not ask if it works.

The image-gen company best known for turning text prompts into fantasy art dropped a behind-the-scenes look at its ultrasound scanner this week. The device is designed for deployment in physical spaces, and the video shows controlled phantom scans that look impressively clinical.

What it doesn't show: any clinical validation data. No peer-reviewed evidence. No proof that this thing actually performs when it matters. Just vibes, good lighting, and a very confident announcement from a company whose entire track record is in pixels.

That gap should concern everyone. AI expanding into healthcare hardware is a massive deal — and "trust us, it looked cool in the demo" is not the bar we should be accepting.
