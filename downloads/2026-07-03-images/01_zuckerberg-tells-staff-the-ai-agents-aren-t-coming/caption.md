💡 Zuckerberg told Meta staff the AI agents aren't coming fast enough — and that's a bigger deal than it sounds.

At an internal all-hands, Zuck reportedly admitted that Meta's AI agent development has fallen behind his own expectations. No specifics leaked on where exactly things are stalling, but the signal alone is significant. This is a company that has been louder than almost anyone about agents being the next major platform shift.

When you've spent billions, restructured teams, and staked your public identity on AI being Meta's future — telling employees it's not moving fast enough is a rare crack in the confidence. CEOs don't usually walk into all-hands to celebrate how behind they are.

The hype around AI agents has been relentless. This is a reminder that building them — reliably, at scale — is a very different thing.
