💡 Anthropic is done waiting on Nvidia — it's building its own silicon.

Anthropic is in early talks with Samsung to develop a custom AI chip. No deal signed yet, but the conversations are loud enough to matter. This comes just days after OpenAI announced its own custom chip partnership with Broadcom, and the timing is not a coincidence.

Every major AI lab is waking up to the same reality: if you're renting your compute from Nvidia, you're renting your future. Custom silicon means lower inference costs, faster training, and a performance edge that compounds over time. It's the kind of structural advantage that's almost impossible to claw back once a competitor locks it in.

The chip wars are no longer just a hardware story. They're the AI story.
