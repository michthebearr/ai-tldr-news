💡 Anthropic just signed an $11.6 billion cloud deal — and walked away with equity too.

Anthropic is paying Akamai $11.6 billion over seven years for cloud infrastructure, with room to scale that number to $20 billion. But the real headline isn't the price tag — it's that Akamai handed Anthropic up to a 5% equity stake that grows the more they spend. That's not a vendor relationship. That's a bet on each other.

The other twist: this is a CPU-heavy deal at a moment when every major AI player is in an arms race for GPU clusters. Anthropic is deliberately zigging. And by spreading its infrastructure across Akamai alongside AWS and Google, it's quietly reducing the leverage any single cloud giant has over its operations.

One deal. $11.6B. Equity. CPU compute. Strategic independence. Anthropic is playing a longer game than most people realize.
