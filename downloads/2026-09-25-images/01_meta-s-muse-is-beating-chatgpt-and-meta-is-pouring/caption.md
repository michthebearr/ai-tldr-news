🧠 Meta's Muse is beating ChatGPT on the charts — and Meta is just getting started.

While OpenAI dropped GPT-6 updates and Anthropic shipped Opus 5.5 in the same week, Meta's personal AI agent Muse quietly topped the app store charts and is adding users faster than ChatGPT ever did at this stage. That's not a fluke. That's a strategy working.

Meta is cross-promoting Muse across Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp simultaneously — three of the most-used apps on the planet. No AI lab can buy that kind of distribution. The "AI Tamagotchi" approach — a persistent, personality-driven agent that actually feels like *yours* — turns out to be exactly what consumers want. Who knew people preferred a digital companion over a search bar with a PhD.

If Muse holds this trajectory, Meta doesn't just compete in consumer AI. It owns it. OpenAI and Anthropic can fight over enterprise contracts and API revenue while Meta becomes the AI your mom actually uses every day.
