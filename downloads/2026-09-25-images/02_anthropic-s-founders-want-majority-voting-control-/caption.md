💰 Anthropic's founders want to lock in control before Wall Street gets a vote.

The company is asking shareholders to approve a structure that hands its seven co-founders a combined 50.1% voting stake on most major decisions. It's the same playbook Google and Meta ran before their IPOs — cement founder authority before public markets can complicate the mission.

And to be clear, this isn't subtle. They're not hiding the ball. Dario and Daniela Amodei have spent years positioning Anthropic as the "responsible" AI lab — the adults in the room. This structure is them saying: trust us enough to let us keep running it our way, indefinitely.

The real stakes here aren't about governance optics. Whoever controls that voting bloc controls what "safe AI development" actually means in practice — for decades. That's a lot of power to hand seven people, no matter how well-intentioned.
