🏎️ Vibe-coded apps are shipping to production — and leaking your data while they're at it.

Researchers discovered that hundreds of Supabase-powered apps — many of them AI-generated — have been sitting wide open on the internet, exposing user data to anyone who knows where to look. The culprit isn't Supabase itself. It's misconfigured row-level security settings that LLM-generated code gets wrong almost every single time.

Nobody's checking. That's the real problem. AI coding tools make it dangerously easy to go from "prompt" to "live app" without a single security review in between. The defaults look fine. The app ships. The data leaks.

This is the uncomfortable tradeoff of the vibe-coding era — the same tools lowering the barrier to building are lowering the barrier to catastrophic exposure. Users didn't sign up to be collateral damage in someone's weekend project.
