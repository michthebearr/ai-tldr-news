🏎️ Amazon just gave tens of millions of people a smarter Alexa — and nobody had to ask for it.

Alexa+ is rolling out automatically to all compatible Fire TV devices in the U.S., completely free, no Prime subscription required. You don't opt in. You just wake up one day and your TV is running a more capable conversational AI. Classic Amazon move.

This is the installed base play at full speed. Amazon isn't trying to win a chatbot war in the App Store — they already have the hardware in your living room. By dropping Alexa+ into Fire TV for free, they're locking in AI habits before most people even know they have other options.

Monetize later. Dominate now.
