💡 AI isn't going to cure cancer anytime soon — and the reason is embarrassingly mundane.

The hype around AI and drug discovery has been enormous. Every few months, a new model promises to revolutionize oncology. But one startup is cutting through the noise with a quieter, less exciting argument: the data powering these models is fundamentally broken.

Their thesis is straightforward. Biological and clinical datasets are fragmented across institutions, inconsistently labeled, and poorly structured. If that's true, it doesn't matter how powerful your model is — garbage in, garbage out. The bottleneck isn't compute. It isn't architecture. It's spreadsheets that don't talk to each other.

That's both a more solvable problem and a much harder sell to investors chasing moonshots.
