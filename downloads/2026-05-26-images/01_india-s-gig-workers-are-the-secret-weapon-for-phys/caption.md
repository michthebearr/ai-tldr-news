🤖 India's Gig Workers Are the Secret Weapon for Physical AI.

Human Archive, founded by UC Berkeley and Stanford researchers, is equipping gig workers in India with camera-equipped caps and sensor devices to capture real-world physical data for AI and robotics labs. The startup plugs into India's existing gig economy infrastructure to collect the kind of embodied, messy, real-world data that robots desperately need to learn from.

It's crowdsourced robot training at scale — and it's cheaper than building controlled labs in San Francisco. Why it matters:Physical AI is bottlenecked by real-world training data, and whoever solves that supply chain problem first will have a massive head start in robotics.
