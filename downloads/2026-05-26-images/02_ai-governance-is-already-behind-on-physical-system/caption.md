💡 AI Governance Is Already Behind on Physical Systems.

Autonomous AI is leaving the server room and showing up in warehouses, delivery networks, and public spaces — and regulators aren't ready. Most existing AI governance frameworks were written to handle online harms: bias, misinformation, harmful outputs.

They say almost nothing about what happens when an AI system operates a forklift or navigates a public sidewalk. Why it matters:The gap between where AI governance is written and where AI is actually deployed is widening fast, and physical environments raise stakes that a content moderation playbook can't address.
