💡 The labs warning us about rogue AI have no public plan for stopping one.

A new study just exposed a glaring contradiction at the heart of the AI safety conversation. The same frontier labs — OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepMind — that routinely sound the alarm about existential risk from AI? They have almost zero publicly documented procedures for what to do if one of their own models goes rogue.

This isn't a transparency debate. It's a deeper question: do these containment protocols even exist in any meaningful form? Because if your entire pitch to the world is "trust us, we take safety seriously," a complete absence of concrete playbooks is a serious credibility problem.

Saying the risk is real while having no documented plan to handle it isn't caution. It's contradiction.
