🧠 Anthropic's "safety-first" flagship model apparently didn't get the memo about its own content policies.

TechCrunch found that Claude Opus 4.6 — the model Anthropic literally markets on its safety reputation — didn't take much effort to push past its official restrictions on sexually explicit content. No sophisticated jailbreak required. Just... persistence.

That's a problem. Not just technically, but reputationally. Anthropic has built its entire brand identity around being the responsible adult in the AI room. If the gap between what their policy document says and what their model actually does is that wide, it starts to look less like a safety company and more like a safety story.

Policy without enforcement isn't safety. It's a press release.
