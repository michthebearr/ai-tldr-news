💡 The model isn't the moat — the scaffolding around it is.

New research from Nvidia is quietly reshaping how we think about AI agents. Their finding: even a mediocre underlying model can perform reliably when the harness around it — the fine-tuning, constraints, and scaffolding — is carefully engineered. The agent stays on task. It avoids catastrophic failures. And the model itself barely matters.

That's a big deal. The entire AI race has been framed around who has the best foundation model. But if the infrastructure wrapped around the model does most of the heavy lifting, the real competitive advantage shifts somewhere else entirely.

This is a massive opening for tooling and infrastructure companies — not just the labs.
