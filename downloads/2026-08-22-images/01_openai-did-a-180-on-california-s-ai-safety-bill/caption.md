🧠 OpenAI just did a full 180 on California's AI safety bill — and it should raise some eyebrows.

Not long ago, OpenAI was actively lobbying against SB 53. Now they're publicly calling for it to be *stronger*. That's not a subtle shift. That's a complete reversal from a company that has spent years arguing state-level AI regulation is the wrong move — while federal regulation it claims to prefer never actually showed up.

So what changed? Maybe the political winds. Maybe the PR calculus after a rough stretch of public trust issues. Or maybe — and this is worth sitting with — OpenAI sees an opportunity to shape the rules before someone else does. Getting ahead of regulation is one of the oldest power moves in tech.

When the biggest AI lab in the world stops fighting a safety bill and starts demanding it go further, that's not a green flag. That's a reason to read the fine print.
