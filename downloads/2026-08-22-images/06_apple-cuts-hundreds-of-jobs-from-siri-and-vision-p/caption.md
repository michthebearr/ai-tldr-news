💡 Apple just laid off 200+ people from Siri and Vision Pro — and that tells you everything.

The cuts hit both the Vision Pro gaming team (largely shut down) and the immersive content team (significantly trimmed). Apple called it a "resource realignment." They didn't say toward what.

When a company quietly axes headcount across two of its biggest bets at the same time, the message is hard to miss: neither is hitting internal targets. Siri has been lapped by ChatGPT and Gemini for years. Vision Pro shipped to polite applause and thin demand. The math wasn't working.

Two moonshots. One round of layoffs. No replacement plan announced.
