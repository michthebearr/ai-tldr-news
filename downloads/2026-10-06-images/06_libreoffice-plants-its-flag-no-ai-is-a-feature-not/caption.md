💡 LibreOffice just made "no AI" their entire personality — and honestly, respect.

While Microsoft, Google, and pretty much every other productivity suite are cramming AI copilots into every corner of your screen, LibreOffice is going the other direction entirely. The open-source document editor announced it has zero plans to add AI to its default setup, and it's leaning into that decision hard.

Their reasoning is simple: privacy. When your tool isn't phoning home to a model, your documents stay yours. No training data. No cloud processing. No "helpful" suggestions you didn't ask for.

The real bet here is that there's a sizable, underserved crowd of users — lawyers, journalists, governments, privacy-conscious businesses — who actively want software that just does the thing without watching them do it. In a market this loud about AI, silence might actually be a differentiator.
