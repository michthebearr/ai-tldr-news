💰 Lambda is raising $4 billion at a $14.5 billion valuation — and a 2027 IPO is already on the calendar.

The Nvidia-backed GPU cloud startup just pulled in one of the biggest AI infrastructure rounds of the year, led by Coatue and Blackstone. That's not a Series A. That's a war chest.

The pitch is simple: AI compute is still undersupplied, hyperscalers can't serve everyone, and Lambda wants to be the infrastructure layer that fills the gap. Institutional money is clearly buying it — $14.5B pre-money says this isn't a speculative bet, it's a conviction play.

The 2027 IPO timeline is the real signal. Lambda isn't burning this capital to survive. It's building the runway to go public on its own terms.
