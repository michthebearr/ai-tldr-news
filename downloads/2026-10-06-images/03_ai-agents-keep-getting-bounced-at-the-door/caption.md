💡 AI agents can't actually do much for you yet — and it's because websites keep slamming the door in their face.

The pitch sounds incredible: an AI agent that books your flights, grabs dinner reservations, and handles your online shopping while you do literally anything else. Demos look clean. The vision is compelling. But the moment these agents hit the real internet, they run straight into a wall of CAPTCHAs, anti-bot filters, and sites that want nothing to do with them.

The problem isn't the AI. It's that there are no agreed-upon rules of engagement between agents and the web. So websites treat them like scrapers — because right now, they kind of are. A new interoperability standard is being proposed to fix this: a framework where verified agents can be selectively let in, without every bad actor getting a free pass alongside them.

Until that handshake exists, the "AI does everything for you" future is mostly a demo reel.
