💡 Europe just built a 1-trillion-parameter AI model — and it's gunning for GPT-4.

French lab Mistral dropped Mistral Large 4 this week, and the specs are not subtle. One trillion parameters. Full multimodal capability. And benchmarks the company says put it ahead of both American heavyweights and China's best open-weight models.

Mistral has always been Europe's most credible AI bet — but credible and competitive aren't the same thing. This release feels like a line in the sand. They're not positioning themselves as a scrappy alternative anymore. They're positioning themselves as the answer.

If the benchmarks hold under independent scrutiny, this is a big deal — not just for Mistral, but for the entire narrative that AI leadership has to live in San Francisco or Shenzhen.
