💡 Authors just won an AI copyright settlement — and now they're fighting to actually see the money.

Anthropic settled with a group of authors over using their work to train AI. That should've been a win. Instead, it's opened a whole new fight — because publishers and agents are allegedly moving in to claim major cuts of the payout before individual authors see a dime.

Here's the uncomfortable question this surfaces: who actually owns the harm? The writer whose words were scraped, or the intermediaries who licensed and sold that work along the way? Publishers and agents will argue they have contractual standing. Authors will argue they're the ones who got used.

This isn't just messy — it's precedent-setting. However this shakes out will shape how every future AI copyright settlement gets divided across the publishing world. Get it wrong, and authors become the last people paid in a system built on their labor.
