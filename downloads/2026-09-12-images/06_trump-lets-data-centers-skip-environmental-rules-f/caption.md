💡 The Trump administration is letting AI data centers skip environmental reviews — and former EPA officials are sounding the alarm.

To speed up AI infrastructure build-out, the White House is carving out key environmental regulations for data center construction. That means faster permitting, faster power, and faster everything — except accountability.

Former EPA officials are pushing back hard, calling the move a real health risk for communities living near these facilities. They've proposed a "Data Center Health Protection Pledge" from the White House. It will almost certainly go nowhere.

Here's the part that sticks: the communities bearing the health costs have no seat at the table. And the precedent being set here — that AI infrastructure gets to play by different rules — could shape energy and environmental policy for decades.

Faster AI. Someone else pays the price.
