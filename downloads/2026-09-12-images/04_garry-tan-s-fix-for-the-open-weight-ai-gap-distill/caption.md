💡 Garry Tan wants America to beat DeepSeek at its own game.

YC's president is calling on U.S. open-weight AI labs to do what DeepSeek already did — use knowledge distillation to pull powerful, capable models out of closed frontier systems. His pitch: stop ceding the open-weight space to Chinese-origin models and start distilling from OpenAI and Anthropic instead.

The logic is clean. If DeepSeek can compress frontier-level performance into an open model that runs on consumer hardware, there's no reason America can't do the same — except it hasn't. Tan's argument is that the U.S. is losing the open-source AI race not because it lacks the technology, but because nobody's made distillation from American models standard practice.

The catch? Frontier labs get to decide whether anyone's allowed to distill from them at all. That's not a technical problem. That's a policy fight waiting to happen.
