🏎️ China's Moonshot AI just set a $2 billion revenue target — and it's not bluffing.

Moonshot AI, the company behind the Kimi chatbot and its flagship K3 model, is gunning hard for $2B in annual recurring revenue. This isn't wishful thinking on a pitch deck — OpenRouter data shows K3 pushing up to 300 billion tokens per day, making it one of the most heavily trafficked AI systems on the planet.

Yes, usage has dipped slightly in recent months. But the core argument here is scale. Token volume is becoming the metric investors actually want to see, and Moonshot is leaning into that narrative with everything it's got.

Here's the bigger picture: a Chinese AI lab seriously competing on monetization changes the whole game. The race to build and profit from frontier AI isn't Silicon Valley's race anymore. It's global — and it just got a lot more interesting.
