🤖 A two-year-old startup just hit a $500M valuation — by selling robot homework.

Mecka AI is closing a Sequoia-led round that values it near half a billion dollars. What do they make? Not robots. Not chips. Training data — the raw physical-world information that teaches robots how to actually move, grip, and function in the real world.

This is the LLM playbook all over again. OpenAI didn't win because it built the prettiest interface — it won because it trained on more and better data than anyone else. Robotics is heading toward the same choke point, and investors are sprinting to own it before the door closes.

Whoever controls the best training data controls the robots that run on it. That's the bet. And right now, the market is pricing that bet at $500M for a company most people have never heard of.
