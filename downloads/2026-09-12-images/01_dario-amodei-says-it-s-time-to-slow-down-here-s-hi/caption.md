💡 The CEO of an AI lab just publicly called for AI labs to slow down — including his own.

Dario Amodei dropped a long essay this week laying out a three-step plan he's calling "pacing the frontier." The core idea: deliberately slow AI training to give safety infrastructure time to catch up. Not vague vibes — an actual framework, with teeth.

The most notable part? Amodei is proposing that third-party evaluators like METR get direct access to Anthropic's models to audit whether the company is actually keeping its safety promises. That's a real accountability mechanism, not a press release.

Here's the tension: Anthropic is actively shipping frontier models and competing hard with OpenAI and Google. So watching its CEO put a concrete slowdown proposal on the table — in public — is genuinely unusual. Now OpenAI and Google have to either match the energy or explain why they won't.
