💡 Microsoft's AI chief said AI will take your job — then walked it back within 24 hours.

Mustafa Suleyman went on the Decoder podcast and said AI would automate the work of lawyers, accountants, and project managers. Pretty clear statement. Then, almost immediately, his team clarified he meant AI would *help* those workers — not replace them. The backpedal was fast, public, and transparent.

Which somehow makes it worse.

When the person running AI at the world's most valuable company can't hold a consistent message for a single news cycle, it's not a PR slip — it's a window. The industry still hasn't figured out how to talk honestly about job displacement, because the honest answer is complicated and the stakes are enormous. So you get half-truths dressed up as reassurance.

The real story isn't what Suleyman said. It's that he said it at all.
