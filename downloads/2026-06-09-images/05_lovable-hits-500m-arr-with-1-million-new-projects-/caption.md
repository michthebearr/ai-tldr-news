💰 Lovable just hit $500M ARR — and it's building a million new projects every week.

The AI-powered app builder Lovable crossed $500 million in annualized run-rate revenue, and the number that really matters isn't the money — it's the scale. One million new projects created per week, many of them replacing internal business tools that companies used to pay enterprise software vendors serious money to maintain.

That's not a side hustle platform. That's small teams quietly ripping out legacy software and replacing it with something they built themselves in an afternoon.

The bottom of the software market is getting eaten alive — and most people aren't watching it happen.

Lovable's numbers are the clearest signal yet that AI-native dev tools have crossed from "cool demo" to structural shift. The question isn't whether tools like this will disrupt traditional software. It's how fast — and how far up the stack they climb next.
