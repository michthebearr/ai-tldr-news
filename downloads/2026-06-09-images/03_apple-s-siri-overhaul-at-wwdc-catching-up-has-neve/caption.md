🏎️ Apple finally rebuilt Siri from the ground up — and it mostly looks like 2024.

WWDC 2026 gave us a brand new "Siri AI," plus a wave of AI features across Photos, Shortcuts, and Safari. The demos were polished, grounded, and refreshingly honest about what the tech can actually do. That last part matters more than it sounds.

Apple just settled a $250M lawsuit over AI demos that overpromised and underdelivered. So yes, the conspicuously hands-on keynote was a message as much as a presentation. We're showing you real things now. Noted.

The hard truth: most of what Apple announced, ChatGPT, Google, and Samsung users have had for two years. But Apple has 1.5 billion active devices. When they ship something — even late — it doesn't trickle out. It floods.
