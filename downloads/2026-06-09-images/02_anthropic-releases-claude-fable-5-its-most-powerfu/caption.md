🧠 Anthropic just put its most powerful public model into the world — and built a cage around it at the same time.

Claude Fable 5 is Anthropic's first Mythos-class model available to everyday developers and researchers. It's described as exceptional at software engineering, knowledge work, and vision tasks — the kind of capability jump that doesn't go unnoticed in a competitive market.

But here's the tell: Anthropic shipped it with hard guardrails blocking outputs in high-risk domains like cybersecurity and biology. That's not a standard disclaimer. That's a company that has publicly warned AI is becoming genuinely dangerous — and then releasing powerful AI anyway, with the locks visible on purpose.

That tension is the whole story.

Opening up a Mythos-class model to the public is a meaningful escalation in what's accessible outside of labs and enterprise deals. Whether the guardrails hold — and who tests them first — is the part worth watching.
