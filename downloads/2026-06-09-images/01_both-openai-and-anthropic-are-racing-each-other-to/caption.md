🧠 OpenAI and Anthropic both filed for IPOs within days of each other — and that's not a coincidence.

OpenAI filed confidentially for a public offering this week, hot on the heels of Anthropic doing the exact same thing. Two of the most powerful AI labs on the planet are now in a race to the public markets — and the timing tells you everything about the pressure both companies are under.

These are businesses burning through cash at a historic pace, carrying sky-high valuations built almost entirely on future potential. Going public forces a reckoning. Suddenly you're not just pitching VCs on the dream — you're answering to shareholders who want to see a path to profit.

Whoever prices first sets the benchmark for the entire AI industry. That's a lot of weight for two IPOs to carry.
