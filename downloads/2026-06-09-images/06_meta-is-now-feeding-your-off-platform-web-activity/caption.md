💡 Meta is now using what you do outside its apps to shape what its AI tells you — and most people have no clue.

Meta just expanded its off-platform data program. It already tracked your activity on third-party sites and apps for ad targeting. Now that same behavioral data — the games you play, the things you buy, the sites you browse — is feeding your Facebook and Instagram feeds directly. And more importantly: it's shaping the responses you get from Meta AI.

This isn't a tweak. This is a fundamental shift in how AI personalization works. Before, surveillance data sold you ads. Now it's curating your reality and your AI assistant at the same time.

Most users opted into this without knowing it exists.
