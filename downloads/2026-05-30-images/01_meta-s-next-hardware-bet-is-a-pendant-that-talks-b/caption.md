💡 Meta is building an AI pendant — and it wants to live on your body, not just your screen.

After the Ray-Ban smart glasses quietly became one of the more compelling AI products on the market, Meta isn't slowing down. The company is reportedly developing an AI-powered pendant — a wearable that talks back, listens in, and keeps you connected to its AI layer without lifting your phone.

Details are thin right now. But the signal is loud: Meta is dead serious about ambient AI hardware. The pendant isn't just a gadget play — it's a land grab for the context layer. Whoever's device sits closest to you owns the most valuable real estate in AI: your daily life.

The race is on. Apple has its vision. Google has its history. Meta has momentum.
