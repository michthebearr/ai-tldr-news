💰 Groq is raising $650M — and quietly admitting where the real AI money is.

The chip startup is reportedly closing a $650M internal round as it shifts its focus from building hardware to running AI inference at scale. Translation: less "we make the chips," more "we make your AI respond faster and cheaper than anyone else."

This pivot matters. In a landscape where Nvidia just reshaped the industry with $20B in deal activity, Groq is reading the room. The chip wars are brutal — but inference? That's recurring, scalable, and exactly where enterprise demand is exploding.

The real gold rush in AI isn't silicon. It's speed.
