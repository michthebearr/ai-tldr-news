💡 Developers are letting AI write the code — and slowly forgetting how to read it.

A new wave of research is flagging something uncomfortable: the more engineers rely on AI coding assistants, the less capable they're becoming at the fundamentals. We're not just talking about speed. We're talking about atrophying the instincts needed to know when something is wrong.

And AI is wrong a lot. It just doesn't act like it. These tools ship confident, clean-looking code that can hide logic errors, security holes, and edge cases that an experienced developer would've caught immediately. The problem is — that experienced developer is increasingly out of practice.

The real time bomb isn't bad AI output. It's a generation of engineers who can't tell the difference.
