💡 Executives are automating jobs they've never done — and calling it a strategy.

ClickUp just cut 22% of its workforce to hand roles over to AI agents. They're not alone. Tech layoffs in 2026 are already nearly matching the entire headcount of 2025 — and we're not even halfway through the year.

Box founder Aaron Levie has a name for what's driving this: "AI psychosis." It's the boardroom condition where executives, running on pure hype and quarterly pressure, confidently slash roles they've never actually performed. No operational context. No real understanding of what gets lost. Just a deck full of AI ROI projections and a Slack message to HR.

When the person pulling the trigger has never done the job being eliminated, the damage doesn't show up until it's too late to reverse.
