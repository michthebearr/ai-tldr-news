🧠 Google built a useful AI assistant — then made it impossible to understand why it exists.

Gemini Spark is Google's new 24/7 AI assistant. It handles your inbox, your calendar, your weekend plans. TechCrunch's reviewer called it "actually pretty useful," which, honestly, is a win for Google these days.

The problem: it's a completely separate product from the Gemini assistant you already know. And no one — not reviewers, not analysts, seemingly not even Google — can give a clean answer for why.

This is how you lose a category you should own. OpenAI is building one coherent AI experience. Google is building a maze.
