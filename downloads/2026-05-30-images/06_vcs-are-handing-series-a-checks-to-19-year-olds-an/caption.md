💡 VCs are handing Series A checks to 19-year-olds — and they're the first to admit it's insane.

Three top investors sat down and said the quiet part loud: if you're 22 and building AI in San Francisco, a seed term sheet is probably already in your inbox. If you're 19, you might have a Series A. No traction required. Just vibes and a pitch deck with "AI-native" somewhere on slide two.

Here's the part that should worry everyone: these investors *know* it's a bubble. They said so. But they keep writing checks anyway — because sitting out feels riskier than overpaying. That's not conviction. That's fear of missing out with a Bloomberg terminal attached.

When the correction hits, it won't be the funds that feel it. It'll be the founders who raised $10M on a prototype and a dream, suddenly needing to grow into a valuation that was always fiction.
