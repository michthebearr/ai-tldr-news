🧠 A third of the internet written since ChatGPT launched is AI-generated — and that changes everything.

A new study just dropped a number that's hard to sit with: roughly one in three web pages published since November 2022 show signs of AI authorship. Not a few spam blogs. Not some dark corner of the web. A third of it.

The implications stack fast. Search is getting noisier. Readers are losing the ability to trust what they find. And here's the part that should genuinely concern you — AI models are now being trained on content that AI models wrote. That's not a hypothetical future problem. That's already happening, at scale, right now.

Nobody has a clean fix for it.
