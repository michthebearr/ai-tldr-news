📱 Two of YouTube's biggest filmmaking names just got torched by their own community for promoting AI video tools.

Matti Haapoja and Sam "Kold" Kolder — creators who built massive audiences teaching people how to *make* great video — posted sponsored content for Higgsfield's Seedance 2.5, an AI video generation platform. The backlash from fellow creators was immediate and brutal.

The charge: betrayal. The argument being made against them is that promoting tools designed to automate video production undermines the very craft they built their careers on. When your whole brand is "here's how to become a better filmmaker," getting paid to showcase tech that skips the filmmaking part hits different.

This is the tension that's been simmering under the surface of creative industries finally getting a very public face. Creator economics vs. creator solidarity. Sponsored posts vs. the people who trusted you to protect the craft.

The check cleared. The community noticed.
