💡 Google broke publisher traffic with AI search — now it's handing them a band-aid.

Google just announced a "follow" button that lets users designate preferred news sources, pushing that content higher across Search, Discover, and Google News. On the surface, it sounds like a win for publishers. But context matters here.

AI Overviews — Google's move to answer questions directly on the search page — has gutted referral traffic to news sites. Publishers are hemorrhaging clicks they built entire business models around. This new feature doesn't reverse that. It just gives readers a preference dial and lets Google say it tried.

The real story: this sets a precedent for how platforms quietly manage the political fallout of AI-driven traffic destruction without actually fixing anything.
