🏎️ A data labeling company just hit a $500M revenue run rate — and that sentence would've been laughable three years ago.

Micro1 supplies the human-generated, human-verified data that AI models need to get smarter. Unglamorous work. Massive business. The company just crossed a $500 million gross run rate, and it's not alone — multiple rivals are reportedly tracking similar numbers as demand for quality training data outpaces the industry's ability to supply it.

This is the AI boom's quietest gold rush. Everyone's focused on the models, but the real infrastructure layer being built right now is the data pipeline underneath them. Whoever locks that in early could hold a durable advantage when the market inevitably consolidates.

Think early cloud wars. AWS didn't win because it was flashiest — it won because it got there first and made switching painful.
