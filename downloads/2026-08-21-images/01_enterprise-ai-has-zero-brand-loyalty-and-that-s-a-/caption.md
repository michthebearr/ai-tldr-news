🏎️ Enterprise AI has zero brand loyalty — and that should terrify every investor in this space.

New data shows OpenAI is winning back business customers it lost to Anthropic. Sounds like a win for OpenAI, right? Look closer. The real story is how fast companies are jumping ship in the first place — hopping between providers every time a shinier model drops.

That's not a competitive market. That's a commodity market. And commodity markets are brutal, especially when you're burning VC cash at the rate these companies are. Sticky enterprise software is supposed to have switching costs baked in — long contracts, deep integrations, workflows that take months to rebuild. AI APIs apparently have none of that.

If your entire moat can be drained by a rival's next model release, you don't have a moat.
