🧠 ChatGPT can now text your friends for you — and that should stop you cold.

OpenAI just launched an Apple Messages integration that lets ChatGPT draft and send iMessages on your behalf. You describe what you want to say, it handles the rest. On the surface, it sounds like a convenience feature. It isn't.

This is the moment agentic AI quietly crossed a threshold most people weren't watching for. Not "help me write an email" — but "go send this, on my behalf, to a real person in my life, right now." An AI with unsupervised write access to your social relationships is a fundamentally different animal than a chatbot that answers questions.

The scariest tech shifts never announce themselves dramatically. They arrive as texting features.
