💡 Meta built a 6,500-person AI army — and it's already falling apart from the inside.

Engineers inside Meta's newly consolidated AI division are calling it a "soul-crushing gulag." Morale has cratered. People don't know what they're building or why. And the whole thing is reportedly teetering on the edge of open revolt — just months after it was created.

Here's the irony: the consolidation was supposed to fix Meta's AI focus problem. Instead of multiple teams pulling in different directions, one big unified unit would drive clarity and speed. What they got instead was a 6,500-person bureaucratic black hole where accountability goes to die.

This isn't just an HR story. OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic are all actively hunting Meta's best people. When your top AI engineers are using Soviet labor camp metaphors to describe their Monday mornings, you don't have a morale problem — you have a talent exodus waiting to happen.
