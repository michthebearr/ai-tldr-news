💰 Mistral is quietly becoming a $23 billion empire — and Europe is finally being taken seriously.

The French AI startup is rumored to be raising €3 billion at a €20 billion valuation. That's nearly double what it was worth at its last funding round. No deal confirmed yet — but if it closes, Mistral will have pulled off one of the fastest valuation doublings in recent AI history.

This isn't just a big number. It's a signal. Mistral has built a lean, open-weight alternative to GPT and Claude while its American rivals have been burning through cash at a scale that makes your eyes water. Enterprises are paying attention — and now, so is serious capital.

Europe has spent years talking about AI sovereignty. Mistral is turning that conversation into a balance sheet.
