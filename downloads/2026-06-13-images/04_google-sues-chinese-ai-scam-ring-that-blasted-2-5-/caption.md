💰 Google just sued a Chinese AI scam ring that sent 2.5 million fraudulent texts in two weeks.

The operation — dubbed "Outsider Enterprise" — used AI tools to blast hundreds of thousands of victims with SMS scams at a scale that would've been impossible just a few years ago. Two weeks. Two and a half million texts. That's not fraud. That's a factory.

Google's lawsuit is one of the most aggressive legal moves a major tech company has made against AI-powered crime. And it's not just about winning in court — Google wants discovery. They're trying to pull back the curtain on exactly how these AI tools were weaponized, so the playbook can't just be repackaged and reused elsewhere.

The bigger signal here: AI-supercharged fraud is outrunning law enforcement, and big tech is done waiting for regulators to catch up. They're going on offense.
