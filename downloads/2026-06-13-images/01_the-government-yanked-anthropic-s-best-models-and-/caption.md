💡 The government just yanked Anthropic's best models offline — and Anthropic is fighting back.

A federal order forced Anthropic to cut off access to its Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models for everyone — customers, partners, even its own employees. The reason: a discovered jailbreak vulnerability that regulators flagged as a national security concern. No warning. No patch window. Just: shut it down.

Anthropic went public with its frustration fast, arguing that "a narrow potential jailbreak should not be cause for recalling a commercial model deployed to hundreds of millions of people." That's a strong statement — and a reasonable one. But here's the twist: Anthropic spent years positioning itself as the safety-first AI lab. That reputation may be exactly why regulators felt confident enough to pull the trigger on them first.

This is the first time a government has ordered a major AI lab to kill commercial model access outright. Whatever you think of the call, the precedent is now set.
