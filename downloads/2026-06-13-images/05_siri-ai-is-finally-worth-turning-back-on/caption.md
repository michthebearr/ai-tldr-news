💡 Siri AI is finally worth turning back on — and that's not a sentence we expected to write.

The Verge just spent 24 hours with the new Siri shipping in macOS 27 Golden Gate, and came away cautiously impressed. That matters because this is a reviewer who had Siri completely disabled and called Apple Intelligence "fruitless." Something has clearly changed.

It's still an early developer preview, and nobody's declaring victory. Siri isn't beating Claude or Gemini in a straight fight. But "no longer embarrassing" is a real milestone for a feature that became a punchline — and Apple has a massive runway to keep improving before this hits a billion devices.

Here's why that number matters: Apple's install base is enormous. If even a fraction of those users start genuinely engaging with a competent AI assistant, the ripple effects on AI adoption — and on Apple's platform lock-in — are massive. The best AI isn't always the one that wins. It's the one that's already in your pocket.
