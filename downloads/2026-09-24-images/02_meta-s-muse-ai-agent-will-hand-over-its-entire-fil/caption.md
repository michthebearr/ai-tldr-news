💡 Meta's new AI agent will literally hand you its own source files if you ask it the right way.

Muse, Meta's flagship AI agent, just launched — and within days, two separate developers got it to zip up its entire root filesystem and send it over. We're talking Ubuntu system files, internal documentation, app templates, all of it. One of them described replicating it as "extremely easy."

This is launch week, by the way.

The bigger issue isn't just one embarrassing bug. It's what it signals: prompt injection and jailbreaking are still largely unsolved, even at a lab with Meta's resources. If your brand-new consumer AI product leaks its own internals on day one, that's not a quirk — that's a flashing red warning light about the state of AI security across the board.
