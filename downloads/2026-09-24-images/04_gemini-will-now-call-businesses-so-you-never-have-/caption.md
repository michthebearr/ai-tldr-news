🏎️ Gemini will now sit on hold for you — and that changes everything about how we interact with the real world.

Google is rolling out an early experiment for Pixel 11 owners with Gemini subscriptions that lets the AI make phone calls to local businesses on your behalf. Reservations, appointment rescheduling, stock checks — Gemini handles the whole call. You don't even have to pick up the phone.

This is Google Duplex, but actually shipping. When Duplex debuted in 2018, it felt like science fiction — an AI calling a hair salon, navigating hold music and small talk like a human. It quietly faded. Now it's back, more capable, and baked into a product people actually use every day.

The real signal here isn't the feature — it's the direction. AI phone agents are graduating from impressive demos to daily infrastructure. The businesses that still rely on phone calls as their primary customer touchpoint are about to feel this shift fast.
