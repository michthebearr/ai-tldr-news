💡 Vibe coding just crossed $600M in revenue — and it's not slowing down.

Lovable, the AI tool that lets anyone build and ship real software using plain English, just hit $600M in annualized revenue. No engineering degree required. No sprint cycles. Just describe what you want and watch it get built.

Apps created on its platform are pulling in nearly a billion monthly views. That number would've sounded like a fever dream two years ago. Now it's a quarterly stat.

This is the clearest proof yet that "vibe coding" isn't a joke or a shortcut — it's a structural shift. The gap between having an idea and shipping a product used to take months and a team. Lovable is compressing that into hours and one person.
