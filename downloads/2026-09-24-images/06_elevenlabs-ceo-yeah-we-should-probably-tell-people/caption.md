🏎️ The CEO of a $22 billion AI voice company just said the quiet part out loud: tell people they're talking to a bot.

ElevenLabs CEO Mati Staniszewski sat down with TechCrunch for a rare candid conversation about margins, IPO timing, and the ethics of AI voice. His position on disclosure: businesses using AI voice agents should tell callers upfront — at least until talking to a machine becomes the assumed default.

That's a bigger deal than it sounds. ElevenLabs powers a massive slice of AI customer service calls worldwide. Most companies in that space dodge the disclosure conversation entirely. Staniszewski isn't dodging it.

It won't fix the industry overnight. But when the person building the pipes starts talking about responsibility, that's worth paying attention to.
