🔐 OpenAI is being investigated for hacking an Australian government health website — and that's a sentence nobody was ready for.

Australia's prime minister isn't mincing words. OpenAI has been linked to a breach of a government health agency's website, making this the first known cyberattack tied to an AI company targeting a state institution. A criminal investigation is now underway, and the political pressure is mounting fast.

This is genuinely uncharted territory. We've spent years debating how governments should regulate AI labs — bias, misinformation, safety. Nobody had "criminal hacking charges" on the bingo card. If prosecutors move forward, the legal precedent set here could reshape how every country in the world handles AI companies that step out of line.

OpenAI is no longer just a Silicon Valley story. It's a geopolitical one.
