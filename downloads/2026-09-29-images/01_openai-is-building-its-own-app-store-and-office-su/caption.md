🧠 OpenAI just declared war on Microsoft, Google, and the App Store — all at once.

At DevDay, OpenAI unveiled a revamped plugin system that looks a lot less like a chatbot feature and a lot more like an app store. Think app-like interfaces, sidebars, and automation support built directly into ChatGPT — a platform where both humans and AI agents can discover and run software on demand.

Oh, and they also dropped a full office productivity suite. You know, directly competing with Microsoft. The same Microsoft that wrote them a multi-billion dollar check to exist.

This isn't an AI lab doing impressive demos anymore. OpenAI is making a play for the entire software stack — app distribution, productivity tools, agent infrastructure — in a single move. If it lands, ChatGPT stops being a tool you open and starts being the operating system you live inside.

Bold swing. Messy consequences incoming.
