🧠 OpenAI is raising $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation — and it's never turned a profit.

Let that sink in. A company burning cash at scale is now being valued in the same breath as Apple and Nvidia. That's not a typo. That's the AI moment we're living in.

The round is expected to be OpenAI's last before a 2027 IPO, which means investors are essentially betting that the path to profitability is real — and that Sam Altman's vision of AGI is worth pricing in now, not later.

Whether that confidence is genius or delusion depends entirely on what the next two years look like.
