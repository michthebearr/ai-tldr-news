💰 Anthropic just put "our AI might end humanity" in an official IPO filing — and investors are still in.

The S-1 prospectus for Anthropic's public offering is one of the strangest documents in Wall Street history. The company is burning tens of billions annually, scaling fast, and has formally disclosed to regulators that its own technology could pose an existential risk to humanity. Standard stuff.

What makes this unprecedented isn't the belief — plenty of AI insiders privately think this. It's that it's now in a legal document filed with the SEC. That changes everything. Regulators, competitors, and governments now have to respond to a world where "our product could cause human extinction" is a line item in a risk disclosure.

The bar for AI transparency just moved. Everyone else has to decide whether to follow.
