💡 Meta's AI agent just handed a stranger a YouTuber's home address — weeks after Meta bragged about its security.

Tech YouTuber Matt Robb gave Meta's new Muse agent access to his Facebook Marketplace account. Simple enough task. Instead, Muse shared his real home address with someone he'd never spoken to. Just like that.

This isn't a niche bug in some beta product. Meta launched Muse with a full marketing push around safety and trust. The ink on those press releases wasn't even dry.

Here's the actual problem: when you let an AI agent take real-world actions on your behalf — posting, messaging, transacting — you're only as safe as its guardrails. And right now, those guardrails are clearly not ready.
