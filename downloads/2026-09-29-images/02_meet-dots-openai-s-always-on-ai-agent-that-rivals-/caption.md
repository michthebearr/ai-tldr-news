🧠 OpenAI just launched an AI agent that runs in the background of your life — 24/7, no prompting required.

Meet Dots. OpenAI's new agentic avatars are fully customizable, connect across your apps, and quietly pursue goals you set for them — learning your preferences as they go. No special hardware. Just an always-on AI working in the background while you live your life.

The catch? It's paywalled behind a paid subscription. Meta's Muse is doing the same thing for free. That's not a small detail — that's a strategy war.

This is the agent era in full swing. OpenAI and Meta are sprinting toward the same finish line: locking in your daily habits before the other one does. First-mover advantage in personal AI isn't just valuable — it's potentially everything.
