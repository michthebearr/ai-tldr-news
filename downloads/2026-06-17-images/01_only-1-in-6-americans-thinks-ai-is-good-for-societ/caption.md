💡 Only 1 in 6 Americans thinks AI is good for society — and the industry has no one to blame but itself.

Pew Research dropped a number that should be rattling boardrooms today: just 16% of Americans believe AI will have a positive impact on society. Not a majority. Not even close. One in six.

Meanwhile, Wall Street and venture capital are pouring billions into the space like the hype cycle will never end. The disconnect between investor enthusiasm and public trust has never looked this stark — or this dangerous for the industry's long-term survival.

AI companies need social license to operate at scale. Right now, they're burning through it fast and doing almost nothing to earn it back.
