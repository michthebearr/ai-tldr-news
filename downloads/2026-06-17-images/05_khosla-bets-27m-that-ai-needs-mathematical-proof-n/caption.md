💡 Khosla just dropped $27M on a startup that wants to mathematically *prove* your AI isn't lying to you.

Pramaana Labs closed a $27M seed round to bring formal verification to AI outputs. That's the same technique used to prove software and hardware systems are mathematically correct — now being applied to AI in fields like law, drug discovery, and tax prep.

The problem they're solving is real. Hallucinations in a chatbot are annoying. Hallucinations in a legal brief or a drug interaction model are catastrophic. "Pretty good" accuracy doesn't cut it when the margin for error is zero.

This could be the unlock that finally gets regulated industries off the sidelines. Banks, hospitals, law firms — they've been watching AI from a distance precisely because they can't afford unpredictable errors. Mathematical guarantees change that calculus entirely.

Vibes got AI here. Proof might take it the rest of the way.
