💡 Anthropic is beefing with the White House — and it's great for business.

The Trump administration came after Anthropic's AI storyworld project Fable, and instead of backing down, Anthropic pushed back. Now spending data from Ramp shows enterprise customers are rewarding that defiance with bigger contracts.

Turns out, political friction can be a product differentiator. In a market where Microsoft and Google are deeply embedded with federal power, Anthropic is quietly positioning Claude as the anti-establishment choice — and B2B buyers are buying in, literally.

The right fight, picked at the right moment, is worth more than any ad campaign.
