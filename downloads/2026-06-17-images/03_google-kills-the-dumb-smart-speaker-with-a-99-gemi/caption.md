🏎️ Google just killed the dumb smart speaker — and it only cost $99.

Google's new Home Speaker isn't running the same tired voice command playbook. It's powered by Gemini, which means actual back-and-forth conversation instead of the robotic "I didn't catch that" loop you've been tolerating for years. At $99.99, it's priced to compete directly with Amazon's Echo lineup.

And this is bigger than a speaker launch. If Gemini can hold a real conversation in your living room — remembering context, handling follow-ups, not making you repeat yourself like it's the first time you've met — it changes what people expect from every smart home device going forward.

Amazon's Alexa upgrade timeline just got a lot more urgent.
