💡 Canadian pension funds are buying into India's data center boom — and that tells you everything about where AI infrastructure is headed.

A major Canadian pension fund is picking up an 8.2% stake in CtrlS, one of India's largest data center operators, running 15+ facilities across the country. This isn't a VC moonshot or a startup bet. This is the most conservative money in the world — retirement savings — flowing into AI compute capacity.

That's the signal. Pension funds don't chase trends. They buy toll roads. They buy pipelines. They buy things people will *need* for the next 30 years. The fact that they're now treating data centers the same way says the smart money has already decided: AI infrastructure demand isn't a bubble — it's a utility.

India just became the new battleground for that thesis.
