💡 A Databricks veteran just left to build what might be the most important AI company nobody's talking about yet.

Ali Ghodsi helped build Databricks into a $62B giant. Then he walked away — and started a startup claiming it can cut AI's energy consumption by 1,000x. That's not a typo.

The first proof point is Un0, an image-generation tool that reportedly matches conventional AI output while running on a tiny fraction of the power. The architecture is entirely new. The claim is enormous. And the demo is public.

Here's why this matters more than almost any AI story this year: power costs aren't just an inconvenience — they're the hard ceiling on how far AI can scale. Data centers are already straining power grids globally. A credible path to 1,000x efficiency doesn't just save money. It rewrites the entire roadmap.

Even if the real-world number is 10x, that's a massive deal.
