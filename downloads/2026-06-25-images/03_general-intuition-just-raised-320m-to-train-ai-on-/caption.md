💰 General Intuition just raised $320M on a wild bet: Fortnite might be the key to teaching robots how the world works.

The startup — now valued at $2.3B — is training AI agents on millions of hours of gameplay footage. The logic is surprisingly compelling: video games are packed with dense, consequence-rich action. Every jump, collision, and physics interaction is labeled by the game engine itself. That's a level of cause-and-effect signal that's incredibly hard and expensive to recreate in the real world.

The problem with most robotics training data is that it's scarce, costly, and slow to collect. General Intuition's thesis flips that entirely — use the billions of hours of gameplay that already exist as a proxy for physical intuition. If a model can learn from a character falling off a ledge in Fortnite, maybe it can generalize that to a robot navigating stairs.

Big if. But a $2.3B valuation suggests serious people think it's a real one.
