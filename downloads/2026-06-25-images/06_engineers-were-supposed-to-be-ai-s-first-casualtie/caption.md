💡 The "AI is killing engineering jobs" narrative just ran into some inconvenient data.

New research from SignalFire shows engineers are actually making up a *larger* share of new hires in tech — not a smaller one. While the layoff headlines have been loud, the hiring signal underneath them tells a very different story.

Turns out companies aren't racing to replace engineers with AI. They're racing to hire engineers who *know how to use it*. The demand for people who can build, deploy, and wrangle AI systems is outpacing the displacement story almost entirely.

The "AI kills coding jobs" thesis just got its first serious data challenge. And the answer has major implications for how we think about where this is all heading.
