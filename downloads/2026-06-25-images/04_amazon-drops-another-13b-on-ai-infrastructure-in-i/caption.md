💡 Amazon just dropped another $13 billion on AI infrastructure in India — and the land grab is getting serious.

AWS's latest commitment brings Amazon's total announced investment in India to a number that should make every competitor uncomfortable. This isn't a goodwill gesture or a PR play. It's a calculated bet on owning the pipes that India's AI economy runs through.

Whoever controls India's AI infrastructure layer over the next five years doesn't just win a market — they build a structural moat in one of the fastest-growing tech economies on the planet.
