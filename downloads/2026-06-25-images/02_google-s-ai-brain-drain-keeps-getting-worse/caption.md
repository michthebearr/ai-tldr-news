💡 Google keeps losing its best AI researchers — and they're all going to the same place.

Jonas Adler and Alexander Pritzel are the latest to jump ship from Google to Anthropic. Add them to a list that already includes Noam Shazeer and Nobel Prize winner John Jumper, and you've got a pattern that's impossible to spin as coincidence.

This isn't one unhappy team. Exits are coming from both Google DeepMind and Google Research — two separate orgs with different cultures, leadership, and missions. That breadth is what makes this alarming. You can fix a bad manager. You can't as easily fix a company-wide talent leak at the exact moment the frontier model race is most competitive.

No compute budget in the world fixes the morale math.
