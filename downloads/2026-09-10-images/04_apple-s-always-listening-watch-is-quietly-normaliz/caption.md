💡 Apple's new Watch can transcribe your conversations — and you never agreed to be recorded.

The Watch Series 12 has a feature that listens, transcribes, and summarizes ambient conversations in real time. Apple insists no raw audio is saved. But here's the problem: the person wearing the watch consented. Everyone else in the room didn't.

This isn't a bug. It's the product. And Apple is rolling it out quietly, carefully, and at enormous scale — to one of the most trusted consumer brands on the planet. That combination is exactly what makes it so consequential.

Once always-on ambient transcription becomes a standard feature, the social contract around private conversation doesn't bend — it breaks. We've barely started having that debate.
