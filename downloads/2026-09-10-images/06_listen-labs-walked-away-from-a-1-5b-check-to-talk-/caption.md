💡 Listen Labs just turned down $1.5 billion to have a different conversation.

The AI research startup had a signed Series C term sheet from Menlo Ventures on the table — reportedly valuing the company at $1.5B. They tore it up. Not because the deal fell through. Because Salesforce came calling, and Listen Labs decided that conversation was worth more.

No acquisition has been announced yet. But the fact that they walked away mid-process, from a signed deal, to chase enterprise talks tells you everything about where AI startup ambitions are right now. Going it alone at $1.5B apparently feels smaller than what a Salesforce deal could unlock.

This is what the AI land grab looks like in 2025 — startups turning down generational money because the right acquirer feels like the bigger bet.
