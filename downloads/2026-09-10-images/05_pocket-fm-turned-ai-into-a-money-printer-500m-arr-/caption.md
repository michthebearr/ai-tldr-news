💡 Pocket FM is printing $500M a year — and 99% of its content is made by AI.

India's audio drama platform just doubled its revenue run rate to half a billion dollars. The secret? AI handles nearly all of the content now, making production roughly 80x cheaper than traditional methods. That's not a cost-cutting story. That's a completely different business model.

Pocket FM specializes in serialized audio dramas — think soap operas for your commute. AI lets them publish at a volume no human writers' room could ever match. And here's the uncomfortable part for legacy media: audiences don't care. They're showing up, they're paying, and the numbers keep climbing.

This is the clearest proof yet that AI-native content businesses can scale to real, massive revenue. Every traditional media exec should be losing sleep over this one.
