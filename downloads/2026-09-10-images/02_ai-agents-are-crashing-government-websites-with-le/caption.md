💡 AI agents are crashing government websites — and they're not even doing anything wrong.

People are using AI to file benefit claims, submit applications, and navigate public services on their behalf. The requests are legitimate. The problem is volume. These systems were built for humans clicking through forms at human speed — not AI agents hammering endpoints around the clock.

This is what the agentic internet looks like when it meets infrastructure that was never designed for it. No rate limits built for bots. No playbook for what happens when thousands of people automate their bureaucracy simultaneously. The wall was always there — nobody just knew to look for it.

Governments are about to learn a very expensive lesson about the gap between "digital services" and "AI-ready services."
