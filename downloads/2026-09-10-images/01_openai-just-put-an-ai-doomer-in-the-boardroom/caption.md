🧠 OpenAI just put one of AI's biggest doomsayers on its board — and that's worth paying attention to.

Paul Christiano is joining the OpenAI Foundation's board of directors. If that name doesn't ring a bell: he's one of the most respected alignment researchers in the field, a former OpenAI employee who left to start the Alignment Research Center, and someone who has publicly stated he thinks AI could genuinely pose catastrophic risks to humanity. This isn't a safety researcher who hedges. He means it.

The timing is loud. OpenAI just completed its restructuring into a for-profit company — a move that drew significant criticism from safety advocates worried that commercial pressure would override caution. Dropping a serious safety hawk into the boardroom right after that either signals a real course correction, or it's the most strategically timed credibility purchase in Silicon Valley history.

The press release won't tell you which one. The decisions will.
