💡 TSMC just admitted it can't build chips fast enough — and that changes everything.

TSMC CEO C.C. Wei stood in front of shareholders this week and said the quiet part out loud: demand from AI companies is so overwhelming that even a massive US factory expansion can't close the gap. "Customer demand is so high, and we can only support so much."

Let that sink in. The single most important chipmaker on the planet — the company that manufactures silicon for Apple, Nvidia, and basically every AI lab that matters — is tapped out. This isn't a funding problem or an ideas problem. It's a physical, atoms-and-machines problem.

The real bottleneck in the AI race isn't code. It's concrete and cleanrooms.
