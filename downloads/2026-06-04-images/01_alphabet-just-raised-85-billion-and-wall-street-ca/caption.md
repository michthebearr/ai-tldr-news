💰 Alphabet just raised $85 billion in a single stock sale — and Wall Street couldn't hand over the money fast enough.

This isn't a startup funding round or a quiet bond offering. Google's parent company just pulled off the largest stock sale in history, and the entire thing is tied to its AI business. That's not a footnote — that's the whole story.

Public markets have officially joined the AI trade. For years, the big AI bets were happening behind closed doors in venture capital. Now institutional money — pension funds, asset managers, the serious long-term capital — is writing nine and ten-figure checks out in the open. That changes the game entirely.

When money moves at this scale, it doesn't just validate a trend. It sets the floor for where AI investment goes for the next decade.
