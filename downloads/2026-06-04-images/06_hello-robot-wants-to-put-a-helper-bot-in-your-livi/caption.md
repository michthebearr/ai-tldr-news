🤖 The home robot moment might actually be here — and this machine wants to live in your living room.

Hello Robot just unveiled Stretch Gen 4, the latest version of its wheeled home assistant. It fetches items, opens doors, and is built specifically to help people with limited mobility. Tall, practical, and now smarter than ever thanks to the AI models powering it under the hood.

For years, home robots were a punchline. Too clunky. Too dumb. Too expensive for what they could actually do. But Hello Robot has been quietly iterating through hardware generations while everyone else was still rendering concept videos — and that kind of real-world rep matters.

The bigger picture: as AI gets genuinely capable, the bottleneck shifts from software to hardware. Companies with field-tested robots already in homes have a serious head start. Hello Robot is one of the few that can actually claim that.

Science fiction is running out of excuses.
