💡 Lovable just quietly handed Google Cloud a massive vote of confidence — and nobody was supposed to know.

The AI app-building startup signed a multiyear deal with Google Cloud that quintuples its infrastructure footprint. No press release. No announcement. TechCrunch's sources had to drag it into the light.

The deal also includes expanded access to Anthropic's Claude models, which tells you everything about where serious AI dev tools are being built right now. Lovable has been one of the fastest-growing platforms in the space — and Google just made sure it's growing on their infrastructure.

This is Google Cloud playing the long game. While everyone watches the OpenAI-Microsoft partnership, Google is quietly signing the next wave of AI-native companies before AWS or Azure even get a meeting.

Smart money moves quietly.
