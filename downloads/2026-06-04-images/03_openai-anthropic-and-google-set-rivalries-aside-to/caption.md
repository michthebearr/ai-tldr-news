🧠 OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google just agreed on something — and that alone should get your attention.

Three of the most competitive companies in tech signed an open letter to Congress this week warning that AI could soon help bad actors build biological weapons. Dario Amodei, OpenAI leadership, and Google execs are calling for urgent legislation to close what they're describing as a dangerous — and very real — biosecurity gap.

These companies fight over everything. Talent, benchmarks, compute, customers. When they stop competing long enough to co-sign the same letter to lawmakers, you don't dismiss it as a PR move.

The message is simple: AI is advancing faster than the rules designed to contain its most dangerous applications. Bioweapons are the sharpest edge of that problem.
