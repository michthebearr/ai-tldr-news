💡 Jensen Huang is out — and Asia's governments are quietly panicking.

Huang's departure from Nvidia has set off a scramble across Asia. Governments are rushing to lock in chip supply commitments, calling in favors, and stress-testing whether their national AI programs are actually as stable as they thought. Spoiler: some of them aren't sure.

The problem runs deeper than optics. Nvidia's GPUs are the literal infrastructure behind Asia's most ambitious AI initiatives — national programs, state-backed data centers, sovereign AI strategies. When the man who built that empire walks out the door, procurement pipelines don't just get awkward. They get political.

And it gets messier. At least some governments are still navigating active regulatory investigations tied to Huang's tenure. That's not a footnote — that's a liability that could reshape how deals get done across the region.
