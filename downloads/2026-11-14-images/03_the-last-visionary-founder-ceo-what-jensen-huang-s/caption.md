💡 Jensen Huang wasn't just running Nvidia — he was the last rockstar of the AI era.

The Verge is making a bold call: Huang's exit from the CEO role isn't just a leadership change, it's the end of something bigger. The founder-CEO who could pack arenas, move markets with a leather jacket and a keynote, and personally will a chipmaker into the most valuable company on Earth. That archetype is basically extinct now.

His CES 2025 moment — 14,000 people watching a chip reveal like it was a concert — may have been the high-water mark. As AI matures, the industry is sliding toward something more institutional. More boardroom, less stadium. The suits are taking over, and the showmanship is quietly being retired along with the founders.

The chip stack will keep running. The vision just got a little quieter.
