🧠 The AI graveyard is getting crowded — and the newest headstones belong to Apple and OpenAI.

TechCrunch is keeping a running list of AI projects that have flamed out or badly missed expectations. The latest additions aren't obscure startups burning through seed funding. They're flagship bets from two of the most powerful companies on the planet — Apple's endlessly delayed Siri overhaul and OpenAI's chaotic attempt at building a consumer super app.

These were supposed to be the products that proved AI was ready for everyone. Instead, they're cautionary tales. Apple has been promising a smarter Siri for years and keeps pushing the goalposts. OpenAI moved fast, generated headlines, and then quietly watched the vision fall apart before it launched.

The graveyard isn't just a vibe check — it's a reality check. Even trillion-dollar companies are struggling to ship AI that actually works for regular people. Hype is easy. Products are hard.
