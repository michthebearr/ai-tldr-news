🧠 The woman who fired Sam Altman says the problem isn't fixed — and she's not done talking.

Helen Toner was one of four OpenAI board members who voted to oust Altman in November 2023. She's now sitting down with The Verge to explain exactly why she did it — and why the conditions that made it necessary still exist.

Her argument is straightforward and uncomfortable: AI lab CEOs have too much power and not enough accountability. The structures meant to keep them in check are weak, and no one has seriously fixed that since the Altman saga played out in public.

This isn't abstract governance theory anymore. When AI labs have the reach and influence of small governments, the question of who holds the CEO accountable becomes a policy emergency — not a boardroom footnote.
