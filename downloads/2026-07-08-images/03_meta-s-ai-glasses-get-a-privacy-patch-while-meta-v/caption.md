💡 Meta added a privacy feature to its AI glasses — and somehow made itself look shadier in the process.

The Ray-Ban Meta glasses just got a safeguard to make secret recording harder. On the surface, that sounds like a win. And technically, it is. Meta did a thing that makes the product less creepy. Credit where it's due.

But here's the problem: the announcement dropped at the exact same time Meta is quietly expanding how much of your personal data its AI systems collect and use across its platforms. So while the press cycle runs "Meta protects your privacy," the actual data strategy is moving in the opposite direction.

This is the playbook. Consumer-facing gesture. Friendly headline. Expanding data moat in the background.
