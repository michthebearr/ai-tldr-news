💡 A French startup just took direct aim at Nvidia's software stranglehold — and open-sourced the weapon.

ZML, backed by Yann LeCun and a team of French engineers, dropped ZML/LLMD this week: free, open-source inference software that accelerates AI models across virtually any chip — not just Nvidia's.

That's a bigger deal than it sounds. Nvidia's dominance isn't only about making the best GPUs. It's about CUDA — the developer ecosystem that makes switching chips feel like starting over. Every time a company runs AI on Nvidia hardware, they sink deeper into that lock-in. ZML is trying to cut that chain.

A credible cross-chip inference layer that actually performs is a direct attack on Nvidia's moat. If it works at scale, the calculus on AMD, Intel, and custom silicon gets a lot more interesting overnight.
