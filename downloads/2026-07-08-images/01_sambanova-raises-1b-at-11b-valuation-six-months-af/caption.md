💰 Intel almost bought SambaNova for $1.6B — six months later it just raised at $11B.

That's not a typo. AI chip maker SambaNova closed a $1B Series F this week at an $11 billion valuation. Earlier this year, Intel was reportedly circling the company at roughly $1.6B. Intel passed. The market didn't.

Five months. Seven-fold valuation jump. That's the AI infrastructure boom in one case study. The window for acquirers to scoop up serious compute talent at bargain prices is closing — fast. Every quarter you wait, the math gets worse.

The real signal here isn't just SambaNova's growth. It's that investors are betting hard on non-Nvidia AI compute alternatives. The race for AI infrastructure isn't winner-take-all anymore, and the money is starting to reflect that.
