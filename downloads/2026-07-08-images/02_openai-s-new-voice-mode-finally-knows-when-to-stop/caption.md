🧠 OpenAI's new voice model finally learned the most human skill of all — knowing when to shut up.

GPT-Live-1 dropped this week, and it's a meaningful step beyond the awkward, stilted voice AI we've all been quietly tolerating. It interrupts less, holds space when you pause mid-thought, and — here's the real unlock — can speak and listen at the same time.

That last part sounds like a small detail. It isn't. Every voice AI before this has been running on polite turn-taking: you talk, it waits, it responds. Real human conversation doesn't work like that. It's messy, overlapping, and full of half-finished sentences. Simultaneous speak-and-listen is the architectural prerequisite for voice AI that can actually keep up.

The immediate use case OpenAI is pointing to is live translation. Makes sense — that's exactly where you can't afford a lag or a fumbled handoff.
