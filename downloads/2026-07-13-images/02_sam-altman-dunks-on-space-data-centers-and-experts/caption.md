💡 Sam Altman just called space data centers what they are — a scam.

When someone floated the idea of building data centers in orbit, OpenAI's CEO didn't hold back. The internet clapped back fast, reminding Altman that he's no stranger to big swinging bets himself. Fair point. But here's the thing — on this one, he's actually right.

Infrastructure experts have been saying it quietly for a while: orbital compute is not a near-term engineering reality. The costs, the latency, the physics — none of it works at scale. What it *does* work for is a pitch deck and a press cycle.

In a space drowning in AI infrastructure hype, having the CEO of OpenAI publicly name the vaporware is a rare and genuinely useful moment of sobriety.
