🧠 Anthropic just stopped treating India like an afterthought.

Claude subscriptions are now priced in Indian rupees — a small operational detail that signals something much bigger. Anthropic isn't just passively collecting international signups anymore. It's actively competing on the ground in its largest market outside the US.

India is price-sensitive in a way that US dollar rates simply don't work for. A flat global price sounds neutral, but in practice it's a wall. Localized pricing tears that wall down — and opens Claude up to millions of developers and consumers who were never really in play before.

Whoever locks in mindshare in India's AI market early builds a moat that's very hard to cross later.
