💡 Hackers turned prompt injection into a weapon — so defenders just turned it back on them.

Security researchers have flipped one of AI's most dangerous attack techniques into a defensive move. It's called "context bombing" — and the idea is beautifully simple: flood an AI hacking agent's context window with so much junk that it thinks it's already done its job, or that it should stand down entirely.

Same vulnerability. Different direction.

What makes this so interesting is the asymmetry. Attackers have spent years exploiting the way AI models trust their context window. Now defenders are weaponizing that exact blind spot — tricking malicious agents into self-terminating before they can cause real damage. It won't stop everything, but in a world where agentic AI threats are scaling fast, buying time matters.

The best defenses often look like this: not a wall, but a mirror.
