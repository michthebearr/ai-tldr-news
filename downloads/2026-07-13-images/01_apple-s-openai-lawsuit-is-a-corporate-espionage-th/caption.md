🧠 Apple just sued OpenAI — and the allegations read like a corporate espionage thriller.

The claim: OpenAI's hardware team allegedly asked Apple job candidates to bring unreleased product samples and confidential documents to interviews. Not exactly the kind of recruiting strategy that holds up well in a deposition.

Apple is accusing OpenAI of stealing proprietary hardware information and manipulating a trusted partner into performing under false pretenses. That's a serious escalation — we're not talking about a standard IP dispute. These are the kinds of allegations that could mean criminal liability, not just a settlement check.

The timing couldn't be worse for OpenAI. It's in the middle of chasing massive enterprise deals, and nothing kills a sales pitch faster than a headline accusing you of corporate theft.
