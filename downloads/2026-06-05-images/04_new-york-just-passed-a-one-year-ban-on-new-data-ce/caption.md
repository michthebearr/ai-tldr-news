💡 New York just hit pause on data centers — and it could reshape the future of AI infrastructure.

New York State's legislature passed a one-year moratorium on large new data centers this week. It's the first statewide ban of its kind in the U.S., and it's now sitting on Governor Hochul's desk waiting for a signature.

The idea: slow down long enough to actually study what all this new compute capacity is doing to the energy grid and the environment before more of it gets locked in permanently. That sounds reasonable. The timing, though, is anything but.

AI demand is exploding right now. If this becomes law — and especially if other states follow — it could put a real brake on U.S. AI infrastructure buildout at exactly the moment every major tech company is racing to scale.
