💡 The NSA is reportedly deploying Anthropic's AI — in possible violation of a federal ban.

According to new reporting, the NSA is preparing to use Anthropic's Mythos model for active cyber operations. That's significant on its own. The fact that it may be happening despite a federal restriction on using Anthropic's technology makes it something else entirely.

This isn't a research pilot or a sandbox experiment. We're talking about one of the most powerful intelligence agencies in the world quietly arming itself with a frontier commercial AI model — and apparently deciding the rules don't apply to them.

The precedent here is what should concern everyone. If the NSA can sidestep federal policy to weaponize commercial AI, you can bet other agencies — and other governments — are watching closely.
