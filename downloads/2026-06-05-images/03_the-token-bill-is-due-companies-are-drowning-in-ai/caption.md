💡 Companies went all-in on AI — now the invoices are arriving and nobody wants to explain them.

Enterprises that built their workflows around AI agents and long-context models are having a very awkward moment with their CFOs. The "move fast, use all the tokens" era is over. What's replaced it: emergency budget reviews, usage audits, and a frantic search for anything that can make the unit economics make sense.

A whole new industry is cashing in on the chaos. Cost-optimization tools are rushing into the gap, promising to help companies claw back margins without torching the AI roadmap they just spent two years selling to the board.

This is the quiet crisis nobody's talking about loudly enough. If companies can't get inference costs under control, the entire AI ROI narrative starts to unravel — and fast.
