💡 Anthropic just posted numbers that make every AI skeptic look very, very silly.

In May, Anthropic's annualized revenue hit $47 billion. Five months earlier, it was sitting at roughly $9 billion. That's a 5x jump — not over years, not over quarters, over months. Most startups would kill for that kind of growth in a decade.

Co-founder Daniela Amodei isn't losing sleep over the critics asking whether AI spending will ever translate into real returns. Her answer is basically: read the room. When your revenue is scaling this fast, the burden of proof shifts to the doubters. And with an IPO reportedly on the horizon, Anthropic is about to let public markets weigh in on that argument.

If those numbers hold, this won't just be a big IPO — it could be the defining tech offering of the decade. The trillion-dollar bet on foundation models needs a proof point. Anthropic might be it.
