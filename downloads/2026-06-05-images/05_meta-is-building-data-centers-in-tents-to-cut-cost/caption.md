💡 Meta is housing its AI infrastructure in tents — and that tells you everything about this moment in tech.

Meta is reportedly setting up temporary tent structures to hold data center hardware while permanent facilities are still being built. It's a page ripped straight from Tesla's "production hell" era, when Elon Musk famously stood up assembly lines in tents to hit Model 3 targets.

The goal is simple: get compute online faster than concrete allows. Traditional data centers take years to build. AI doesn't wait years.

When the world's largest social network is literally pitching tents to keep up with demand, the compute race isn't a metaphor anymore — it's a logistics emergency.
