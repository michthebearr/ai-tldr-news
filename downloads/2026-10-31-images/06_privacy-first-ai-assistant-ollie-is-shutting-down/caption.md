💡 Ollie built a privacy-first AI assistant — and the market killed it anyway.

Ollie is shutting down within the week. The founder didn't bury the lead: the AI assistant space is too crowded, too well-funded, and too brutal for a niche product to survive on principle alone.

Here's the uncomfortable truth. Privacy is a feature people say they care about — until they have to pay for it, or give up convenience for it. Ollie had the right values. It just didn't have Google's distribution or OpenAI's war chest.

This isn't just one startup failing. It's a signal that the AI assistant market is consolidating fast, and the window for scrappy challengers may already be closing.
