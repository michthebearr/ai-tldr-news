💰 OpenAI just quietly lowered its own fundraising target — and that's a bigger deal than it sounds.

The company that practically invented the era of trillion-dollar AI hype is reportedly struggling to close its latest funding round at the number it originally set. Not a collapse. Not a crisis. But a pullback — and from OpenAI, that's enough to raise eyebrows.

This is a company that has never had trouble getting investors to throw money at it. Oversubscribed rounds, record-breaking valuations, a waitlist of VCs desperate to get in. So when they quietly trim the target, it isn't just a rounding error. It's a signal.

Investor appetite for AI is cooling at the edges — even for the biggest name in the game.
