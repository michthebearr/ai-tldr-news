💡 Nvidia might have to start cosigning loans just to keep its own chips selling.

Here's the situation: Nvidia is reportedly considering acting as a financial guarantor for Nscale's AI data center project in West Virginia. On the surface, it sounds like a generous partnership move. Dig one layer deeper and it's a red flag.

Data center developers are struggling to secure traditional financing for these massive GPU-packed builds. Banks are hesitant. So Nvidia — the company that *sells* the GPUs that fill those facilities — may have to underwrite the risk itself just to keep demand alive.

That's not a healthy supply chain. That's a company backstopping loans so customers can afford to buy its own product.
