💰 Anthropic is heading into its IPO carrying junk-grade credit — and that's a bigger deal than it sounds.

A Blackstone forecast, reported by the FT, predicts credit rating agencies will slap Anthropic with a high "junk" rating post-IPO. That's not an automatic disaster, but it's not nothing either. Junk ratings mean borrowing costs go up — significantly.

Here's why that stings: Anthropic isn't a company that can afford to make capital more expensive right now. They're burning cash at scale while trying to compete directly with OpenAI and Google — two of the best-funded entities on the planet. Every dollar they need to raise just got structurally pricier.

Going public was supposed to be the moment the money gets easier. Not like this.
