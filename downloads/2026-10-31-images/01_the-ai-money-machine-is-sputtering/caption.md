💡 Big Tech has spent hundreds of billions on AI — and the math still doesn't add up.

The AI buildout has been one of the most aggressive capital bets in tech history. Hyperscalers and model labs have been pouring money into GPUs, data centers, and infrastructure at a pace that makes the dot-com era look cautious. The problem? Revenue hasn't come close to keeping up.

Mounting debt, persistently negative cash flows, and a growing chorus of skeptical investors are forcing an uncomfortable question: is this sustainable, or is the industry just buying time? The pattern is sector-wide — companies racing to build capacity for a demand curve that hasn't arrived yet.

If investor sentiment turns, the capital keeping this whole ecosystem alive could dry up fast. And it's not just the big players who'd feel it — foundation model labs, data center operators, and the entire AI supply chain would be caught in the squeeze.
