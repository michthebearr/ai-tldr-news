💡 The Pope just entered the AI art debate — and he's not pulling punches.

Pope Leo XIV dropped a formal statement this week declaring that AI-generated images are categorically not art. His argument isn't just aesthetic — it's philosophical. He writes that there's "an ontological difference" between what a human creates and what an algorithm produces through statistical calculation. In other words: it's not about whether it looks good. It's about what's missing at the source.

That missing thing, according to the Vatican? The spark of humanity.

When a 2,000-year-old institution starts issuing precision-worded statements about generative AI, something has shifted. The "is AI art really art?" debate used to live on Reddit threads and design Twitter. Now it has theological weight behind it — and a Pope willing to draw a hard line.
