💡 Trump just renamed AI "super intelligence" by executive order — and made billionaires pinky-promise to be safe.

The White House pulled off quite a show this week. Zuckerberg, Bezos, Musk, Amodei, and nearly every other major tech CEO gathered to sign an AI safety pledge that Trump himself called "morally binding." Not legally binding. Morally. As in: no courts, no teeth, no consequences.

Then Trump signed an executive order officially rebranding artificial intelligence as "super intelligence." Not because the underlying technology changed. Not because scientists agreed. Because vibes, apparently, are now policy.

Here's the problem: the same CEOs who signed that pledge are in an all-out sprint to ship autonomous agents, replace workers, and outcompete each other at every cost. A moral handshake from people whose business model *is* the race means precisely nothing. When governments start renaming technologies by decree, you're not watching leadership — you're watching politics eat science alive.
