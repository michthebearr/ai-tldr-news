🧠 OpenAI just launched an AI agent platform — and it's basically enterprise software that occasionally grabs you lunch.

Meet Dots: OpenAI's new agent platform built around customizable AI characters that can manage business workflows, handle internal processes, and yes, throw in some personal assistant tasks on the side. Starting at $100/month, the hands-on vibe is clear — this is workplace software first, personal helper second.

Compare that to Meta's Muse agent, which is coming at this from the opposite direction: consumer-friendly, approachable, built for daily personal use. Two completely different bets on how AI agents actually become habits.

That's the real game here. Enterprise wallets are big, but consumer habits are sticky. Whoever gets people using agents *every single day* — for work or life — wins the long term.
