💡 Trump reportedly asked Grok whether to capture Venezuela's president — and it said go for it.

According to new reporting, President Trump consulted Elon Musk's AI chatbot Grok as a foreign policy sounding board before ordering military action to capture Nicolás Maduro. Not a general. Not the CIA. A consumer chatbot.

This is, by every measure, unprecedented. Grok has no access to classified intelligence, no diplomatic training, and zero accountability for the consequences of its outputs. And yet it apparently had a seat at the table for a decision with serious international implications.

This is what AI misuse at the highest level of power actually looks like — not a sci-fi scenario, a real one.
