💡 Apple just admitted that AI agents are a real security threat — and macOS is changing because of it.

Apple is tightening Full Disk Access permissions in macOS, and the reason is blunt: AI agents have gotten too powerful to trust with the old model. Right now, an app can get sweeping access to your files, messages, mail, and browsing history with little more than a checkbox. That era is ending.

Under the new rules, granting that level of access will require "very explicit user action" — not a buried toggle in System Settings. Apple is essentially acknowledging that the permission systems built for apps weren't built for agents that can read, reason, and act across your entire machine.

This is the first major OS-level security response designed specifically around AI agent risk. It won't be the last.
