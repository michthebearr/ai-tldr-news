💡 While AI labs debate the apocalypse, one startup is focused on the harm already happening — to your kids.

Circuit Breaker Labs is building what they call "crash-test dummies" for psychological AI harm. Instead of synthetic users testing for misinformation or jailbreaks, these personas are designed to probe AI companions and chatbots for emotional manipulation, unhealthy attachment patterns, and outputs that could genuinely damage vulnerable users — before any real person sees them.

It's unglamorous work. It doesn't get keynotes or billion-dollar valuations. But AI companions are already in the hands of teenagers, and the documented harms — dependency, manipulation, distorted emotional development — are real and measurable right now.

Someone has to do the boring, necessary stuff. Turns out that someone is a startup most people haven't heard of.
