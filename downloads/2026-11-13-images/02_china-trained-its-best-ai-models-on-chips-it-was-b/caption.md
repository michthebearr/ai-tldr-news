💡 China trained its best AI on chips the U.S. banned it from having.

New research confirms what a lot of people quietly suspected: China's most advanced AI models were built on export-controlled Nvidia chips — the exact hardware Washington restricted to pump the brakes on China's AI development. The restrictions didn't hold.

The chips got through anyway. Whether it was supply chain leakage, third-party workarounds, or controls that simply arrived too late, the result is the same: China's frontier labs got what they needed, and the ban may have done little more than buy time — if that.

That's a massive problem for U.S. strategy. The entire export control playbook was built on the assumption that cutting off access to advanced hardware would create a meaningful gap. If China's top labs are already running on restricted silicon, that gap may never have existed.

The AI arms race just got a lot more complicated.
