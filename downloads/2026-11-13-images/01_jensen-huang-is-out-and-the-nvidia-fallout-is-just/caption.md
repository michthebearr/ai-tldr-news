💡 Jensen Huang is out at Nvidia — and the timing couldn't be worse for American AI.

The Nvidia CEO and cofounder has resigned after reports surfaced of an affair with an alleged Chinese spy. That's not a PR problem. That's a national security crisis at the company that literally runs the AI race.

Congressional investigations are already underway. His handpicked successor, Colette Kress, was reportedly described by Huang himself as a "deer in headlights." And if that wasn't enough, Nvidia's environmental record is now getting dragged into the story too. This isn't a stumble — it's a full institutional meltdown.

Here's what makes this bigger than any one executive: Nvidia's chips power virtually every major AI lab, cloud provider, and government AI program on the planet. Leadership chaos here doesn't stay at Nvidia. It ripples into everything.

The backbone of global AI just cracked.
