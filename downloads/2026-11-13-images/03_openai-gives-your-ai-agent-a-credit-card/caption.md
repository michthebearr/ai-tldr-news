🏎️ OpenAI just gave your AI agent a credit card — and that changes everything.

ChatGPT's new Wallet feature lets OpenAI's Dots agents browse, choose, and actually *buy* things on your behalf using payment cards linked to your account. U.S. Pro subscribers with Amex, Chase, Mastercard, or Visa cards are first in line.

This isn't just a convenience feature. It's the clearest signal yet that AI agents are graduating from "helpful assistant" to "autonomous economic actor." Your AI doesn't just recommend the flight anymore — it books it.

And that opens a whole can of worms. Who's liable when the agent buys the wrong thing? What happens when it gets phished, manipulated, or just confidently wrong? The fraud and accountability questions here are genuinely unsolved — and OpenAI is rolling this out anyway.
