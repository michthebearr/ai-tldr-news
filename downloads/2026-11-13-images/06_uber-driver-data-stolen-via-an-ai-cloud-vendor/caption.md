🏎️ Uber drivers' license data was stolen — and Uber's own systems were never touched.

Thousands of driver license records were lifted in a breach confirmed by Uber this week. The twist: the data wasn't sitting on Uber's servers. It was stored by Fireworks AI, a third-party cloud infrastructure vendor — and that's who the hackers actually went after.

Uber did everything "right" on their end. Didn't matter. The moment sensitive data left their perimeter and landed with an AI vendor, it became someone else's security problem too. That's the whole trap of third-party supply chain attacks.

This is the new threat model. As companies rush to plug AI infrastructure into their operations, they're handing over sensitive data to vendors whose security practices they don't fully control. Every new integration is a new door. And right now, a lot of those doors aren't locked.
