🏎️ ElevenLabs just walked into the enterprise meeting room — and it might actually belong there.

ElevenLabs built its reputation on voice cloning and audio AI that genuinely sounds human. Now it's launching ElevenAI, a meeting notetaker and dictation tool aimed squarely at teams and enterprises. That puts it in a ring with Otter, Fireflies, and Notion AI — a crowded space that's been printing money for anyone who can crack it.

Here's the thing: most meeting transcription tools are still embarrassingly bad at accents, crosstalk, and anything remotely technical. ElevenLabs has spent years solving exactly those problems. If that edge carries over, this isn't just another notetaker — it's a category upgrade.

The pivot is bold. But the tech might actually justify it.
