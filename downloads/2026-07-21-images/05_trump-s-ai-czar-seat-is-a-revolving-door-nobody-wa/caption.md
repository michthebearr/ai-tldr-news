🏎️ The U.S. AI czar seat just lost another occupant — and nobody seems eager to fill it.

The director of the Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI) has resigned. Again. Since David Sacks exited his AI czar role, the position has become a revolving door spinning so fast it's hard to keep count. No stable replacement is on the horizon. No clear succession plan. Just another vacancy at the top of U.S. AI governance.

This isn't just an HR problem — it's a strategy problem. While China is executing a coordinated, government-backed national AI roadmap, the United States can't keep a policy director in their chair long enough to make a dent. Every leadership gap is a policy gap. And policy gaps have consequences.

China is playing chess. The U.S. can't find someone to sit at the board.
