💡 Anthropic just paid $1.5 billion to make a copyright lawsuit go away — and it didn't even buy clarity.

A federal judge approved the settlement between Anthropic and a group of authors who accused the company of training Claude on pirated books. Each author walks away with roughly $3,000 per book. Not nothing — but not exactly compensation that rewrites the economics of being a writer in the AI era.

Here's the part that matters most: this settlement sets zero legal precedent. The core question — whether training an AI on copyrighted material is legal at all — remains completely unanswered. Anthropic wrote a massive check specifically to avoid a ruling that could've reshaped the entire industry.

Every AI lab just got a new number to pencil into their risk models.

$1.5B is now the baseline cost of large-scale copyright litigation. OpenAI, Google, Meta — they're all watching. More settlements are coming, and none of them will settle the actual debate.
