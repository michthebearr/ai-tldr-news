🧠 Google just dropped three new Gemini models — and somehow still avoided the one everyone actually wants.

This week, Google released Gemini 2.5 Flash, 2.5 Flash-Lite, and a new Flash Cyber model built for security use cases. Fast, cheap, and designed for enterprise agent pipelines. Solid releases, technically speaking.

But the elephant in the room? Still no Gemini 2.5 Pro. The flagship model the AI industry has been waiting on is conspicuously absent — and at this point, calling it a "delay" is being generous. This is starting to feel like a deliberate positioning choice that Google just hasn't found the words to explain yet.

The strategy is becoming clearer though: flood the market with best-in-class cheap inference while OpenAI and Anthropic fight over the premium tier. Google's bet is that enterprise agentic workloads are won on cost and volume — not on who has the flashiest flagship.

Maybe they're right. But you don't build mindshare by shipping everything except the thing people asked for.
