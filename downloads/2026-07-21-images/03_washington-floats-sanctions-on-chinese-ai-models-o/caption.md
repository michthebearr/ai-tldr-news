💡 The U.S. just threatened to sanction Chinese AI models — and the timing is everything.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent floated the idea of sanctioning Chinese open-weight AI models this week, citing intellectual property theft. The trigger? Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 dropped last week — the largest open-weight model yet released — and immediately put Washington's patience to the test.

This isn't just political noise. If sanctions actually land, it would be the most aggressive use of U.S. trade policy against Chinese AI to date. Enterprises that are currently evaluating Chinese open-weight models as cost-effective alternatives to OpenAI or Anthropic now have a real legal risk sitting over those decisions.

The window to pick a side may be closing fast.
