💡 More than half of what's being uploaded to Deezer every day isn't made by humans.

In June, over 90,000 AI-generated tracks hit the platform daily — and that number crossed the 50% threshold of total uploads. Let that land for a second. The majority of new music hitting one of the world's major streaming platforms is now synthetic.

Deezer knows it's a problem. They don't have a fix. At that volume, there's no clean way to filter AI content from human-made music at scale — which means the royalty system, the discovery algorithm, and the monetization model are all quietly breaking in real time.

This isn't a warning shot. It already happened.
