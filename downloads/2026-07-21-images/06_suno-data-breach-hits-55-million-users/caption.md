🔐 55 million Suno users just had their names, phone numbers, and home addresses exposed in a data breach.

This isn't your average credential dump. Physical addresses are in the dataset — which puts this in a different threat category entirely. We're not just talking phishing emails. We're talking real-world targeting. That's a meaningful line to cross.

Suno has been one of the fastest-growing AI music generators on the market. Tens of millions of users in a short window is impressive. It's also a massive honeypot if your security infrastructure isn't keeping pace with your growth curve — and for a lot of AI consumer apps right now, it isn't.

This is the story the AI industry needs to reckon with. Scaling fast is the goal. Securing what you build apparently isn't always part of the plan.
