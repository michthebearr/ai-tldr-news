💡 The Trump administration just created something called the "Super Intelligence Force" — and no one can tell you what it actually does.

The White House unveiled a new AI task force this week, framed as its official answer to the growing conversation around AI safety and oversight. The name is cinematic. The policy substance? Essentially nonexistent. The announcement was long on branding and short on literally anything else.

That gap matters more than it might seem. How the U.S. government structures AI oversight — who has authority, what gets regulated, how national security intersects with model development — will shape the entire trajectory of American AI policy. A vague task force with a bold name is still a task force. It's a signal, even if nobody's sure what it's signaling.

Watch this space. Because whether this becomes a meaningful oversight body or a PR wrapper around inaction, the answer will tell us a lot about where this administration actually stands on AI.
