💡 A disgraced politician just tried to use AI as his alibi — and it's as bad as it sounds.

Former New Jersey Lt. Governor Dale Caldwell was forced to resign amid a sexual harassment investigation. His response? Hit the media circuit and cite an AI chatbot as proof of his innocence.

That's not how any of this works. Asking an AI a leading question and getting a favorable response isn't evidence. It's not exoneration. It's a search engine with better grammar telling you what you wanted to hear.

But here's why this matters beyond one politician's bad media strategy: this is the playbook we're going to see over and over. AI as rhetorical shield. AI as manufactured credibility. The technology doesn't validate claims — it just sounds authoritative enough to muddy the water for anyone not paying close attention.
