💰 Someone may have been running a Chinese spy operation targeting Jensen Huang himself.

A senior executive at a Singapore-based AI cloud company — already under fire for allegedly smuggling Nvidia chips to China — was reportedly investigated as a potential Chinese intelligence asset. The target? Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. The New York Times broke it, citing government sources familiar with the probe.

Chip smuggling is serious. But this is a different tier entirely. Running an intelligence operation against the CEO of the most strategically critical semiconductor company on the planet isn't export control evasion — it's geopolitical warfare.

Nvidia sits at the center of the global AI arms race. Whoever controls the chips controls the future, and apparently some people are willing to go very far to gain an edge.
