🔐 Mistral just dropped a trillion-parameter cybersecurity model — and gave away the keys.

The French AI lab released its biggest model yet, purpose-built for security operations and fully open-weight. That second part is the whole story. When you're running sensitive threat detection or vulnerability analysis, handing your data to a closed API is a massive liability. Mistral's answer: here are the weights, deploy it yourself, trust no one.

At a trillion parameters, this isn't a hobbyist tool. It's enterprise-scale firepower that until now only lived inside proprietary platforms. Palo Alto, CrowdStrike, and others have been selling AI-powered security at a premium — Mistral just put a comparably sized model on the table for anyone with the infrastructure to run it.

The moat just got a lot harder to defend.
