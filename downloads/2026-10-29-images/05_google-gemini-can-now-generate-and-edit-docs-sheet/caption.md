🧠 Gemini just stopped being a chatbot and started being your entire workspace.

Google quietly shipped one of its biggest Gemini upgrades yet — you can now create, preview, and edit Google Docs, Sheets, PDFs, slide decks, Markdown files, and code directly inside a Gemini conversation. No switching tabs. No exporting. No breaking your flow.

This is a bigger deal than it sounds. Gemini has always had the horsepower — it just felt like a fancy search bar stapled to Google Drive. This update turns it into something closer to a genuine AI-native productivity layer, the kind of thing power users have been asking for since day one.

ChatGPT and Claude already do this. Now Google's playing catch-up on its own turf — and with Workspace integration baked in, it might actually win.
