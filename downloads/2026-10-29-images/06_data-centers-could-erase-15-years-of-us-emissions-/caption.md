💡 AI's energy bill is about to erase 15 years of climate progress.

A new Sierra Club analysis dropped a number that should stop everyone in the industry cold: US power sector CO2 emissions could surge 42% by 2030 — not because of population growth or manufacturing, but because of data centers built to run AI.

That's 618 million additional tons of CO2. A decade and a half of hard-won emissions reductions, gone — fueled by fossil fuel buildout scrambling to keep up with AI's electricity appetite.

This is no longer a future problem. It's happening now, and the regulatory and political backlash is coming for an industry that's largely operated without scrutiny on this front.
