💰 OpenAI just bought its way into 6 million homes — and your security camera will never be the same.

Thrive Holdings, backed by OpenAI, is acquiring ADT in a $15.3 billion deal. The plan isn't just to own a home security company — it's to rebuild one of America's most boring-but-essential industries from the inside out using AI.

Think smarter threat detection that learns your patterns. Automated emergency response that doesn't wait for a human to pick up the phone. Personalized security profiles across tens of millions of households. ADT's legacy infrastructure becomes the distribution layer. AI becomes the brain.

This is what the race to put AI agents in the physical world actually looks like — not a chatbot, not a smart speaker. A system watching your front door.
