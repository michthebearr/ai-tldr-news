🧠 OpenAI's AI has solved over 100 open math problems — and the people advising it can't do a thing to stop it.

OpenAI quietly assembled a group of elite mathematicians to advise its AI research. Sounds responsible, right? Here's the catch: the advisory group is purely consultative. No authority. No veto power. No ability to pump the brakes on anything.

It's an oversight board with no oversight.

And the math itself? We're not talking about textbook problems. These are previously unsolved questions that have stumped researchers for years. AI cracking open problems at that level isn't a party trick — it's a preview of what frontier science looks like when AI is the one doing the discovering.

The structure here says the quiet part loud: OpenAI is moving fast on this, and no outside voice is going to slow it down.
