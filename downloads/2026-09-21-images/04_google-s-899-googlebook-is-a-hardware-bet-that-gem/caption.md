🧠 Google just dropped five AI-native laptops — and it's betting Gemini alone is worth the upgrade.

The Googlebook lineup landed this week: five laptops from Acer, Asus, Dell, HP, and Lenovo, starting at $899. Preorders open today, units ship October 4th. But the hardware is almost beside the point — what Google is really selling is Gemini baked so deep into the OS it's woven into your cursor, your dictation, your widgets, and how you interact with the machine at a fundamental level.

The standout move? Deep Android phone integration that actually makes the laptop and your phone feel like one device. It's the kind of seamless cross-device UX Apple users have had for years — and Windows has never quite pulled off.

This is the same bet Microsoft made with Copilot PCs. But Google has tighter OS control and a cleaner implementation — and that might be the difference between a gimmick and a genuine upgrade cycle.
