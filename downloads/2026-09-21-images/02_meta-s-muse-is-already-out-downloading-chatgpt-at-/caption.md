🧠 Meta's AI agent is already out-downloading ChatGPT — and it hasn't even broken a sweat.

Muse, Meta's new AI agent, has pulled more downloads and daily active users in the U.S. and Canada than ChatGPT did over the same post-launch window. That's not a small stat. ChatGPT was arguably the fastest-adopted consumer tech product in history — and Muse is beating its early numbers.

Here's the thing: this was never going to be a fair fight. Meta isn't launching into a cold market. It's funneling Muse directly into the attention streams of billions of people already living inside Instagram, WhatsApp, and Facebook. Distribution is the moat, and Meta's moat is enormous.

If those users build daily AI habits through Muse before they ever open ChatGPT or Gemini, the race for AI's default interface might already be over.
