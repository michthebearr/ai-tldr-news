💡 Amazon just slammed the door on Meta's AI agent — and the message is loud and clear.

Meta's Muse, an AI agent designed to browse and buy on your behalf, has been blocked from accessing Amazon.com. Not a glitch. Not a miscommunication. A deliberate decision by Amazon to keep a competitor's AI out of its ecosystem entirely.

And honestly? It makes perfect sense. Amazon has its own foundation models. Its own inference business. Its own AI ambitions. Handing Meta's agent the keys to the world's largest e-commerce platform would be like letting a rival set up shop in your own store.

This is the opening shot. Every major tech incumbent is about to start drawing hard lines around their turf — and AI agents are going to hit those walls one by one.
