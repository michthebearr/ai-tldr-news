💡 World model startups are raising hundreds of millions of dollars — and refusing to tell anyone what they're building.

World models — AI systems designed to simulate physical reality — are one of the hottest bets in tech right now. Robotics, self-driving cars, scientific simulation. The potential is enormous. The funding is real. The transparency? Almost nonexistent.

TechCrunch dug into the space and found something unusual even by Silicon Valley standards: founders won't talk, investors won't talk, and even the companies supplying these startups with data won't talk. That's a lot of locked doors for a sector this loud about its own importance.

Two explanations. Either these teams are sitting on genuinely breakthrough approaches they're racing to protect. Or the valuations got way ahead of the actual science, and nobody wants to be the first to crack the curtain.

The silence itself is the story.
