💡 A former accountant built an AI to replace himself — and he might actually pull it off.

Tabby is a real-time AI bookkeeping platform that handles client paperwork and spits out live profit-and-loss data. No waiting on your accountant. No chasing invoices. Just clean financials, on demand.

The founder spent years doing this work by hand. That matters. The best AI automation isn't coming from engineers guessing at broken workflows — it's coming from insiders who lived them. Tabby isn't automating a single task. It's going after the entire full-service bookkeeping relationship.

Accounting is a massive, underserved market. And the person most dangerous to an industry is always the one who knows exactly where the time gets wasted.
