💡 Nvidia is down 40% — and the interim CEO's big response was "we've got this."

Nvidia's stock has dropped to around $150/share after a painful post-earnings slide — nearly 40% off its recent highs. For a company that became the face of the AI boom almost overnight, that's a number that demands more than a confident sign-off on an earnings call.

But that's what investors got. Interim CEO Colette Kress closed out Q3 with "we've got this" — a phrase that's either reassuring or deeply concerning depending on how you feel about AI hardware demand holding up at Nvidia's valuation.

Here's the real story: when the stock most synonymous with the AI arms race drops 40%, it's not just a Nvidia problem. It's a stress test for the entire AI investment thesis — and right now, that thesis is sweating.
