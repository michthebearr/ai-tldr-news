💡 Apple is building an AI agent — and this time, they might actually mean it.

A new job listing from Apple is asking for an engineer to lead development of an agentic AI assistant. We're talking web browsing, code writing, cross-app workflows, and external API calls. This isn't a Siri refresh. This is Apple going directly at OpenAI's Operator, Google's Project Mariner, and Anthropic's computer use feature.

The stakes are high for a reason. Siri has been a punchline for years — a decade of promise, underwhelming delivery. Apple knows it's behind. But here's the thing: if they actually ship something capable, they don't have a distribution problem. They have a billion devices already in people's pockets.

Everyone else is fighting for users. Apple already has them.
