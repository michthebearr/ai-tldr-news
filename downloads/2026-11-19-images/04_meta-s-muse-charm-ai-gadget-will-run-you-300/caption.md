💡 Meta's first AI gadget costs $300 — and that price might be its biggest problem.

The Meta Muse Charm is real, it's coming in December, and Zuckerberg finally stopped dodging the number. Three hundred dollars for a small screen-equipped device built around Meta's Muse AI agent. Holiday season target locked.

Here's the awkward part: $300 buys you something pricier than an Amazon Echo Show and less capable than the phone already sitting in your pocket. That's a hard sell when consumers are still figuring out what standalone AI hardware is even *for*.

But the price isn't really the story — the bet is. This is Meta's first genuine attempt at putting AI into a physical product consumers can buy. If it lands, Meta becomes a hardware player. If it flops, it was just a very expensive press release.
