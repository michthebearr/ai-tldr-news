💡 Google just moved the search box out of the spotlight — and replaced it with a chatbot.

Google is quietly rolling out a collapsible AI chat sidebar directly on its homepage. A gray bar on the left side of the screen. Click it, and you're in a conversation. Traditional search results are still there — but now they're the supporting act.

This is a small UI tweak that carries enormous weight. For 25 years, that search box has been the most valuable piece of real estate on the internet. The front door to everything. Google is now slowly, deliberately moving it aside.

Here's why that matters: Google's entire business — the ads, the links, the billions — is built around that box. When a chat interface becomes the default way people ask questions, the old model starts cracking. No links clicked means no ads served. Google knows this better than anyone, which makes watching them do it anyway genuinely fascinating.

The search engine isn't dead. But it just got demoted.
