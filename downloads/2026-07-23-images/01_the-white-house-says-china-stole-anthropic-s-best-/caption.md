💡 The White House says China stole Anthropic's best model. Researchers say that's not how any of this works.

The US Treasury is threatening sanctions against Chinese AI lab Moonshot AI, claiming its Kimi K3 model was built by distilling Anthropic's Fable — essentially reverse-engineering its capabilities without permission. It would be the first time the US government has moved to sanction a company over alleged AI model theft.

There's just one problem: the experts aren't buying it. Researchers told TechCrunch that distillation alone can't account for how capable Kimi K3 actually is — and that the model almost certainly reflects real, independent research progress coming out of China. In other words, Washington may be reaching for a theft narrative to explain something more uncomfortable: that Chinese labs are genuinely catching up.

The bigger story isn't the sanctions threat. It's that this is what happens when geopolitics meets AI benchmarks — and policymakers don't fully understand either.
