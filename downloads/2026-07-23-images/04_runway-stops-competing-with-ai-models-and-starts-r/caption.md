💡 Runway just stopped trying to win the AI model war — and started building the roads instead.

Runway launched the Runway Media Router today, a single API that routes developers to a curated marketplace of third-party image, video, and audio models. Instead of betting everything on their own models, they're becoming the layer that sits above all of them.

This is a sharp strategic move. Model quality in generative media shifts every few weeks — what beats everything in January might be mid by March. By routing between the best available tools rather than locking users into one, Runway makes itself useful no matter who's winning the benchmark wars.

Be the plumbing, not the pipe.
