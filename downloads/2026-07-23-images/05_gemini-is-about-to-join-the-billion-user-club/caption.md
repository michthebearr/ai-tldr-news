💰 Gemini is about to hit one billion users — and that number changes everything.

Google just revealed that Gemini crossed 750 million monthly active users back in February. It's now closing in on a billion, which would make it only the second Google product ever to hit that milestone alongside Search and Maps. Let that sink in.

To be fair, this isn't all organic enthusiasm. A huge chunk of those users are getting Gemini because it's baked into Android, Google Workspace, and Search — not because they went looking for it. Google is essentially handing out AI the same way it handed out browsers and search bars.

But here's the thing: it doesn't matter how you got to a billion. A billion users means a billion data points, a billion feedback loops, and a distribution moat that OpenAI, Anthropic, and everyone else simply cannot buy their way into. Benchmark wars are fun. Scale wins.
