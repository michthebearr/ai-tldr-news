🎵 Meta made an AI hype ad and accidentally scored it with a song about the apocalypse.

The ad is glossy, optimistic, and very Meta — all warm lighting and human potential and AI-as-savior vibes. The soundtrack? David Bowie's "Five Years." A song about a man learning that Earth has exactly five years left before it's gone.

The internet noticed immediately. Either Meta's marketing team never actually listened to the lyrics, or they did and figured nobody else would. Neither option is great for a company trying to convince a skeptical public that its AI is something to celebrate.

This is a small mistake with a loud message. The biggest players in AI are still getting the communication catastrophically wrong — on a technology that already has people deeply uneasy. If you can't nail an ad, how are you nailing the bigger conversation?
