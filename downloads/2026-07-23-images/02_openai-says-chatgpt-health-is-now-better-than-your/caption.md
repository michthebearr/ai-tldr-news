🏥 OpenAI just told the world that ChatGPT is smarter than your doctor — and rolled it out to everyone.

ChatGPT Health is now live for all US users. Connect your medical records, Apple Health, MyFitnessPal, or Function data, and the model will reason through your health information in ways OpenAI's VP of health product is calling "better than clinician level."

That's a massive claim. And when reporters pressed for the benchmarks to back it up? Nothing concrete was offered. No peer-reviewed studies. No transparent testing framework. Just confidence.

Here's the tension: AI that can genuinely democratize healthcare access is one of the most exciting ideas in tech. But rolling out clinical-grade claims to millions of consumers — without the receipts — is exactly how this story goes wrong fast. Regulators are already watching closely.
