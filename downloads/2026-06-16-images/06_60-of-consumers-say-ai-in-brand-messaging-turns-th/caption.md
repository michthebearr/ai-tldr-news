💡 Slapping "AI-powered" on your product isn't a selling point anymore — it's a red flag.

A new survey from WordPress VIP found that 60% of US consumers react *negatively* when brands lead with AI in their marketing. Three in five people. That's not a niche concern — that's a majority telling companies to stop.

And yet, most brands aren't listening. Marketing decks are still loaded with "AI-powered," "AI-driven," and "AI-enhanced" — as if the label itself signals quality rather than cutting corners. Consumers have clearly figured out the game, even if the CMOs haven't.

The trust gap is real. And it's growing.
