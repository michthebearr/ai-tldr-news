💡 Meta just turned Facebook into an AI assistant — and it's using everything you've ever posted to power it.

Meta is rolling out "AI Mode" across Facebook, and the twist is what's fueling it: public data from Facebook, Instagram, and Threads — all three platforms, all feeding the same engine. This isn't a standalone chatbot. It's AI baked directly into the places you already scroll, search, and share.

That distinction matters more than it sounds. Most AI companies are racing to build better models. Meta is doing something different — it's embedding AI into a social graph that already has over 3 billion daily users. You don't need the best model if you have the best real estate.

The distribution play is Meta's real weapon here.
