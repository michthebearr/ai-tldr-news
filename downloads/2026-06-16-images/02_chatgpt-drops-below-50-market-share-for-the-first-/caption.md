🧠 ChatGPT just fell below 50% market share — and that's a bigger deal than it sounds.

OpenAI's flagship still pulls 1.1 billion monthly users. That's not a stumble, that's a giant. But market share slipping under 50% for the first time means the story has officially changed — from "ChatGPT is AI" to "ChatGPT is *one of the* AIs."

Gemini is sitting at 662 million users. Claude at 245 million. The gap is still massive, but the direction of travel is clear. OpenAI built this market from scratch, and now it's watching competitors use that blueprint against it.

Once you lose majority share, you stop being the default and start being a choice. That shift changes how developers build, how enterprises buy, and how everyday users think. OpenAI knows this better than anyone — it's exactly what happened to Google Search.
