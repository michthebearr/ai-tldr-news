🔐 The DOJ is now defending Elon Musk's illegal gas turbines — and calling them a national security asset.

xAI's Colossus supercomputer cluster in Memphis is powered by unpermitted gas turbines. That means they were installed and operating without the environmental approvals required of, well, everyone else. Regulators came knocking. Then the Justice Department stepped in — not to prosecute, but to protect.

The DOJ's argument: these turbines are critical to Pentagon AI operations, so environmental rules should take a back seat. In other words, the military needs the compute, so look the other way.

This is the part that should make everyone pay attention. "National security" is becoming a get-out-of-regulations-free card for AI infrastructure — and once that precedent is set, it doesn't stay narrow for long.
