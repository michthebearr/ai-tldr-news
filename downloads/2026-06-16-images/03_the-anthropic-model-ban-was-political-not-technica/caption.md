💡 Anthropic got its cybersecurity models pulled by the Trump administration — and the official reason doesn't pass the smell test.

The stated justification was jailbreak risk. The problem? Analysts who dug into the technical claims say they don't hold up. Anthropic, arguably the most safety-obsessed AI lab in the game, built these models with more guardrails than most. The security argument looks less like a finding and more like a fig leaf.

So what was this actually about? The leading theories are reactionary politics, retaliation, or both. Whatever the reason, the message landed clearly: even playing by the rules won't protect you if you end up in Washington's crosshairs.

That's the real story here. If the government can kill an AI product for opaque political reasons dressed in technical language, every lab now has to treat D.C.'s mood as a product risk. That's a dangerous new variable in an industry already navigating enough uncertainty.
