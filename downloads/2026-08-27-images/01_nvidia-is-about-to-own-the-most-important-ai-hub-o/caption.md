💡 Nvidia just agreed to buy Hugging Face — and that should make every AI researcher nervous.

The $12.9 billion deal would hand Nvidia the keys to the internet's most important open-source AI hub. Hugging Face is where models live, where researchers share work, where the open AI ecosystem breathes. And now the world's most powerful chip company wants to own it.

Think about what that means. Nvidia already sells the hardware that runs AI. Now it could control the platform where AI models are built, shared, and accessed. That's not just vertical integration — that's owning the whole supply chain, from the GPU to the model weights.

The conflict of interest writes itself.
