💡 Google just decided it doesn't want to find you a flight — it wants to book it for you.

AI Mode quietly became something much bigger this week. Google upgraded it to track flight prices in real time and help users complete hotel bookings without ever leaving Google. This isn't a search update. It's a business model update.

Think about what that shift actually means. Google has always been the front door to travel. Now it wants to be the whole building. Expedia, Booking.com, Kayak — every OTA that built its empire on Google traffic just watched Google decide to cut them out of the transaction entirely.

The search giant already owns the on-ramp. Adding checkout is just removing the last reason to leave.
