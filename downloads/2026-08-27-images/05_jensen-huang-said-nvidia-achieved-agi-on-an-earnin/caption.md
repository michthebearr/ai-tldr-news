🏎️ Jensen Huang just announced AGI on an earnings call — then said the word was "senseless."

In the same breath, on Nvidia's latest earnings call, CEO Jensen Huang casually claimed his company had "achieved AGI" and then immediately dismissed the milestone as meaningless. Classic Huang move: drop the bomb, defuse it, watch the headlines write themselves.

Here's the thing — he's not entirely wrong to dismiss it. AGI means something different to every person who uses it. OpenAI has a definition. Demis Hassabis has another. Half of Silicon Valley has quietly redefined it to mean whatever their current model does best. When a term is infinitely flexible, announcing you've crossed the finish line is basically announcing nothing.

But that's exactly the problem. The CEO of the world's most valuable AI infrastructure company can say "we achieved AGI" and walk it back in the same sentence — and nobody blinks. The word has become a vibes metric, not a scientific one.
