💡 The next big AI interface isn't an app — it's your text messages.

A new wave of AI agents are skipping the app store entirely and living directly in SMS. No download, no account, no onboarding flow. Just a number you text and a response that comes back in seconds.

The field is already crowded — general assistants, family tools, travel planners, work agents. The bet these startups are making is simple: the lowest-friction surface on your phone is also the most underrated one. SMS open rates crush email. Everyone already knows how to use it. And critically, it doesn't ask anything of the user before delivering value.

That last part is what kills most AI apps. Retention dies at the login screen.
