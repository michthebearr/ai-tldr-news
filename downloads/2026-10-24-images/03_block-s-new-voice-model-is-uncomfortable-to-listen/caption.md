💡 Block just built a voice AI that sounds eerily human — and it never touches the cloud.

Jack Dorsey's Block dropped a voice model this week that runs entirely on a MacBook M4 Max. No server call. No upload. Just disturbingly convincing AI speech, processed locally on consumer hardware sitting on your desk.

That's a bigger deal than it sounds. Voice AI has always come with a quiet asterisk: your words leave your device. Every query, every command, every awkward dictation — shipped to someone's cloud. Block just made that trade-off optional.

If on-device voice quality hits this level at scale, cloud-dependent voice platforms have a problem.
