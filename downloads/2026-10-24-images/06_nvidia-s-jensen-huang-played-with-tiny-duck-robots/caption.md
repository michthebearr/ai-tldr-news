🤖 Jensen Huang played with a tiny duck robot — and 20,000 people immediately bought one.

Microduck is a palm-sized AI-powered bot that stomps, somersaults, falls asleep, and responds to your voice. It's equal parts toy and tech demo. And the moment Nvidia's CEO was spotted playing with one, the internet lost its mind.

Twenty thousand units sold out almost instantly. They're already being called a frontrunner for the hottest toy of Christmas 2026 — and honestly, it's not hard to see why. Consumer robotics has been overpromised for decades, but something is clearly different now.

That something is price. Voice-commanded, behaviorally expressive robots selling out at scale means the hardware and AI costs have finally dropped to a point where regular people will actually buy them. That's the real story here — not the duck.
