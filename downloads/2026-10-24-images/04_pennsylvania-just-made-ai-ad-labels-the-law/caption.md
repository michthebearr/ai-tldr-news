💡 Pennsylvania just made AI ad disclosures the law — and the rest of the country is watching.

Governor Josh Shapiro signed the Artificial Intelligence Deceptive Advertising Act this week, requiring any ad that was "significantly modified or generated by AI" to carry a visible disclosure. Video, audio, image, text — doesn't matter. If AI touched it in a meaningful way, you have to say so, and the label has to match the format of the ad itself.

This is one of the first state-level laws with actual enforcement teeth around AI-generated advertising. No more quietly slipping AI-generated content past consumers with zero accountability.

If it holds up to legal challenges, this becomes the template other states copy. That's how US tech regulation usually works — one state moves first, then the dominoes fall.
