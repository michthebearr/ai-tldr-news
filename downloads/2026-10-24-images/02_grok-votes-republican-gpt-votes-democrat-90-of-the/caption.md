🧠 Grok votes red. GPT votes blue. And it's happening 90% of the time.

A new study put Grok and ChatGPT through nearly 500 real election races — forcing each model to pick a candidate. The result? The two AI systems disagreed more than 9 times out of 10. Grok skewed Republican. GPT skewed Democratic. Consistently, measurably, at scale.

Neither xAI nor OpenAI programmed this in explicitly. It's emergent — baked into training data, reinforcement choices, and the political DNA of the cultures that built these models. No one flipped a switch. It just happened.

That's the part that should make you uncomfortable. Hundreds of millions of people are asking AI systems questions during active election cycles right now. The bias isn't theoretical. It's already in the answer.
