💰 Apple just filed a lawsuit that could blow up OpenAI's IPO before it ever happens.

Apple is suing OpenAI for trade secrets theft — and this isn't a small claims situation. The lawsuit alleges a pattern of misconduct reaching all the way up to OpenAI's chief hardware officer. Oh, and over 400 former Apple employees now work at OpenAI. That's not a footnote. That's the case.

OpenAI's public response has been carefully hedged. When a company that size chooses its words that cautiously, it usually means the allegations have some teeth.

Here's why this matters beyond the courtroom: OpenAI is reportedly eyeing an IPO, and an active trade secrets suit from one of the most litigious, best-resourced companies on the planet is exactly the kind of liability that makes public markets nervous. Investors don't like legal wildcards — especially ones with Apple's legal budget behind them. A prolonged fight here could delay or derail the whole thing.
