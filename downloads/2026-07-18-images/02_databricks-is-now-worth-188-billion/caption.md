💰 Databricks just hit a $188 billion valuation — and it's gunning straight for OpenAI's enterprise lunch.

The data pipeline company that quietly became an AI powerhouse just closed a new funding round that makes it one of the most valuable private companies on earth. That's not a rounding error. That's a statement.

Here's the play: Databricks is betting hard on open-weight models, publishing research showing enterprises can get serious AI performance for coding at a fraction of the cost of closed models. When your CFO is staring at an OpenAI bill and asking "what are we actually getting?" — that's a very good pitch to walk in with.

Every dollar Databricks raises is a vote against the idea that enterprise AI has to run through one of a handful of closed-model gatekeepers. The incumbents should be paying attention.
