🔐 People are hacking Zoom just to stop AI from transcribing them — and that tells you everything.

A workaround is quietly spreading that lets Zoom users flag themselves as unwilling to be recorded or summarized by AI. No official opt-out exists. So people built their own.

This is what happens when AI features get shipped as defaults and consent gets treated as a footnote. Meeting bots are now standard infrastructure — quietly transcribing, summarizing, and logging conversations that most people assume are just... conversations. The platforms never really asked. They just turned it on.

And here's the question buried in all of this: if every meeting is being summarized by AI, is anyone actually reading any of it? At some point "productivity tool" starts to look a lot like surveillance nobody asked for.

The backlash is real and it's growing. Platforms will either build genuine opt-out mechanisms or regulators will build them for them.
