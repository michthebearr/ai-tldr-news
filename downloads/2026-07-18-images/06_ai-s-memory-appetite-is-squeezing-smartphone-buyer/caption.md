💡 The AI arms race is making your next phone more expensive — and most people have no idea why.

India is one of the world's biggest smartphone markets, and it's hitting a wall. Sales are slowing, budget devices are getting pricier, and a big part of the reason has nothing to do with local demand or manufacturing costs. It has to do with AI.

The memory chips being hoarded to power enterprise AI infrastructure — the stuff running your chatbots and copilots — are the same chips that go into affordable smartphones. When hyperscalers vacuum up supply, prices rise across the board. Manufacturers serving India's cost-sensitive buyers are getting caught in the crossfire of decisions made in server rooms on the other side of the world.

This is what the AI buildout actually looks like at street level. Not just billion-dollar GPU clusters — but a first-time smartphone buyer in Mumbai paying more for less.
