💡 A top VC just admitted what Silicon Valley has been avoiding — AI's wealth problem isn't going away.

Neil Rimer of Index Ventures is saying the quiet part out loud: the staggering wealth being minted by AI will be redistributed. The only question is whether the industry does it on its own terms — or has it done to them through taxes and regulation.

That's a striking admission from someone sitting at the top of the VC food chain. Rimer isn't predicting doom. He's predicting a reckoning. And the fact that insiders are starting to talk like this signals that the pressure around AI's inequality problem has crossed from activist talking point to boardroom reality.

When the people profiting most start warning about redistribution, it's not altruism. It's pattern recognition.
