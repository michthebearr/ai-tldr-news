🤖 Agility Robotics just set up camp in Tesla's backyard — and it's not a coincidence.

Agility Robotics is opening a new training center for its Digit humanoid robot in Fremont, California. That's the same city where Tesla builds its cars and quietly develops Optimus, its own humanoid robot play. This is a deliberate move, and everyone involved knows it.

Fremont is a talent magnet — robotics engineers, manufacturing specialists, the kind of people who build things that actually work at scale. Agility is planting its flag there to compete for the same brain trust Tesla is recruiting. Digit is already on the floor in Amazon warehouses, so this isn't a concept car moment. This is a scaling play.

The humanoid robot race is getting crowded fast. Boston Dynamics, Figure, Tesla, 1X — everyone wants the enterprise contract. Agility just made clear it's not watching from the sidelines.
