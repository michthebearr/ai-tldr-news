💡 Roblox just let anyone build a game with a single sentence.

The platform launched "Build," a new mobile feature that turns a text prompt into a basic, playable game — no coding required. It's live in the app right now, and it's aimed directly at the 88 million people logging into Roblox every day, most of them under 17.

That's a massive number of curious, creative kids who previously hit a wall the moment they tried to actually make something. Learning Lua takes time. Typing a sentence doesn't. Roblox just removed the biggest barrier between wanting to create and actually creating.

The flip side? An already enormous platform is about to get flooded with AI-generated games at a scale nobody fully understands yet. Quality control, originality, discovery — all of it gets harder when anyone can ship a game in 30 seconds.

This isn't just a product update. It's a bet on who gets to call themselves a game developer next.
