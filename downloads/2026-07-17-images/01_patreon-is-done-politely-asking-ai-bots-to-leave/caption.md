💡 Patreon is done asking AI bots nicely — now it's slamming the door in their face.

Patreon just partnered with Cloudflare to actively block AI training bots from scraping its platform. This isn't a polite "please don't" in a robots.txt file that bots can just ignore. This is a bouncer at the door.

The shift matters because the honor system was never really working. AI labs built crawlers that ignored opt-out signals entirely, and platforms had no real recourse. Patreon is betting on technical enforcement over trust — and that's a fundamentally different posture.

If major platforms follow, AI labs are going to find the open internet a lot less open.
