💡 San Francisco is done watching robotaxis run its streets on someone else's terms.

After a Waymo fleet caused hours of gridlock in the city, Mayor Daniel Lurie sent a formal letter to state regulators demanding more local control over autonomous vehicle operations. He wants SF to have real authority over when and where robotaxis can operate — not just sit on the sidelines while Sacramento calls the shots.

For years, cities have basically been spectators in the AV policy game. State regulators approve the permits, companies deploy the fleets, and locals deal with the fallout. Lurie pushing back this publicly is a signal that the patience is running out.

The robotaxi era is scaling fast. The fight over who actually governs it is just getting started.
