📱 Google just let you clone yourself for work meetings — and it's as unsettling as it sounds.

Google is rolling out personalized AI avatars inside Google Vids, its AI-powered video creation tool for Workspace users. You scan yourself once, and from then on, Gemini can generate videos starring a digital version of you — scripted, edited, and ready to send.

No camera. No recording. Just… you, but not really you.

The pitch is obvious: faster internal comms, scalable training videos, async updates without the effort. But the questions this raises are immediate — who owns your likeness inside a corporate suite, what happens when HR sends a "personal" video that wasn't actually recorded, and how long before this gets misused in ways Google's terms of service can't keep up with?

Enterprise deepfakes are here. They just came wearing a Google Workspace badge.
