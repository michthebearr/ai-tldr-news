🤖 Zoox's robotaxi got confused by smoke — and had to issue a federal recall because of it.

Zoox quietly recalled its autonomous vehicle software after one of its robotaxis encountered heavy smoke and behaved unexpectedly. Not a crash. Not a cyberattack. Smoke. The kind of thing that's become a near-annual reality across the Western US as wildfire seasons get worse.

Federal safety regulators are already watching. They've been specifically warning AV companies about vehicles that interfere with first responders during emergencies — and a confused robotaxi in the middle of a wildfire evacuation zone is exactly the nightmare scenario they're flagging.

This is the edge case problem in one story. AVs get trained on millions of miles of clean, predictable data. The real world has smoke, floods, and chaos. The gap between those two things is where trust gets lost.
