🧠 The EU just handed ChatGPT the same regulatory treatment as Google Search — and OpenAI has to comply.

The European Commission has officially designated ChatGPT as a "Very Large Online Search Engine" under the Digital Services Act. That's not just a label — it's a legal framework with real consequences: mandatory risk mitigation around minors, mental health, and illegal content, plus audits and potential fines if OpenAI falls short.

This is the EU moving from strongly-worded guidelines to actual teeth. The DSA gives regulators the power to suspend products, not just write letters. For a generative AI tool used by hundreds of millions of people, that's a significant shift in accountability.

Other jurisdictions are watching closely. Whatever compliance framework OpenAI builds here could quietly become the global template.
