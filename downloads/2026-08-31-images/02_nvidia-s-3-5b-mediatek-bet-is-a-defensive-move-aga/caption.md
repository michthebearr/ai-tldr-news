💡 Nvidia just dropped $3.5 billion on MediaTek — and it has nothing to do with innovation.

Google, Amazon, and Microsoft are all quietly building their own AI chips. The goal? Stop writing massive checks to Nvidia. It's the single biggest threat to Jensen Huang's empire, and he knows it.

So Nvidia is playing defense. By investing deep into MediaTek — one of the world's most important chip design houses — Nvidia embeds itself into the supply chain at a level that's hard to cut around. It's not about making a better GPU. It's about making Nvidia structurally unavoidable.

$3.5 billion isn't a bet on the future. It's insurance against losing the present.
