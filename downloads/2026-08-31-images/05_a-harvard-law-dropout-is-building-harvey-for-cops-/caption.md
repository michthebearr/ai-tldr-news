💰 A Harvard Law dropout just raised $6M to put an AI lawyer in every patrol car.

Blue Voice is vertical AI built specifically for law enforcement — trained on department policies, local ordinances, and jurisdiction-specific protocols that ChatGPT has never seen. Think Harvey AI for BigLaw, but swap the corner office for the front seat of a squad car.

The founder walked away from Harvard Law to build it. That's either a founding myth or a red flag, depending on how this plays out.

Here's why it actually matters: the "GPT wrapper" era is dying. The AI tools winning right now are the ones trained on private, domain-specific data that general models can't touch. Law enforcement is exactly that kind of vertical — highly specialized, deeply local, and operating in situations where a wrong answer isn't just embarrassing. It's dangerous.

Getting this right could meaningfully improve how officers navigate policy in real time. Getting it wrong, in one of the highest-stakes domains imaginable, could be catastrophic.
