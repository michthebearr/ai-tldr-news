📱 A three-year-old AI startup just raised $15M — after already turning a profit.

Clipto builds AI-powered search for video archives. Think terabytes of raw footage that media companies, broadcasters, and enterprises are sitting on but can't actually use. Clipto makes it searchable. Simple idea. Massive market.

The numbers tell the real story: $15M ARR, profitable, *then* raised. That sequencing matters. In a funding climate exhausted by companies burning cash on the promise of future revenue, Clipto is doing the opposite — proving the model works before asking for more fuel.

The unsexy problems are often the biggest ones. Unstructured video is everywhere and almost nobody can search it efficiently. Clipto is quietly building the picks-and-shovels infrastructure for the video era — and enterprises are clearly willing to pay.
