📱 Instagram is coming for AI influencers who've been hiding what they are.

Meta just announced it will throttle the reach of AI-generated accounts on Instagram that don't disclose they're artificial. No label, no reach. The message is simple: if you're faking a human persona and hoping no one notices, the algorithm is no longer on your side.

This didn't come out of nowhere. User frustration with polished, eerily perfect AI influencers has been simmering for months — people are tired of engaging with content only to realize there's no real person behind it. Meta is finally responding with something that actually has teeth.

The ripple effects matter more than the policy itself. Instagram setting this precedent puts every other major platform on notice. Twitter, TikTok, YouTube — they either follow suit or spend the next year explaining why they haven't.
