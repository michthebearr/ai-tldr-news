🧠 The Pentagon now has its own ChatGPT, Grok, and Gemini — and that changes everything.

The US Department of Defense has quietly added OpenAI's ChatGPT and xAI's Grok to its central AI portal, joining Google's Gemini which was already in the mix. These aren't consumer apps handed out to soldiers — they're government-grade deployments with security controls baked in. Think of it as a classified app store for frontier AI.

But here's the bigger picture: the world's most powerful military is now standardizing on the same three AI providers dominating your phone. Frontier model access isn't a perk anymore — it's infrastructure. The same way the DoD runs on GPS and encrypted comms, it now runs on large language models.

That raises the geopolitical stakes considerably. Which models a military uses, who builds them, and who controls access just became a national security conversation.
