💰 Together AI just raised $800M — and its valuation more than doubled in under two years.

Together AI, the neocloud built around hosting open-source models, closed a massive funding round this week, pushing its valuation from $3.3B to $8.3B. That's a $5 billion jump in roughly 18 months.

The reason enterprises are flocking here is simple: they want the flexibility of open-source AI without the headache of running their own GPU infrastructure. Together AI sits right in that gap — and apparently, that gap is worth billions.

This isn't a hobbyist story anymore. Open-source AI infrastructure is now a full-blown business category, and investors are betting it becomes a permanent alternative to the walled gardens of OpenAI and Anthropic.
