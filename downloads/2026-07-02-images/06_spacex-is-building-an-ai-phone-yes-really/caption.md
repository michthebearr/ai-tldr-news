💡 SpaceX is building an AI phone — and it's not really about the phone.

Elon Musk's rocket company reportedly showed investors a "handset-like" AI device prototype ahead of its long-anticipated IPO. Details are scarce, but the signal is clear: SpaceX isn't trying to out-iPhone Apple.

This is a play to own the entire wireless stack. Starlink already has direct-to-cell ambitions — beaming connectivity straight to devices from orbit. Add proprietary hardware and an AI assistant layer on top, and you have something no traditional carrier can touch. Not Verizon. Not AT&T. Nobody.

Here's the real kicker: most of the planet still lacks reliable cellular coverage. If SpaceX ships a Starlink-native AI device, it doesn't need to win in New York or London. It just needs to win everywhere else.
