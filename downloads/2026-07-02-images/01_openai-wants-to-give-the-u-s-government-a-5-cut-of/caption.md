🧠 Sam Altman wants to give the U.S. government a piece of OpenAI — and it's not as generous as it sounds.

Altman reportedly floated offering a 5% equity stake in OpenAI to the U.S. sovereign wealth fund. The pitch? Let the American public share in the AI boom instead of watching a handful of insiders cash out. Noble framing. Very calculated move.

This is damage control dressed up as patriotism. OpenAI is in the middle of converting from a nonprofit to a for-profit structure, and the political heat — from regulators, from critics, from the Trump administration — is real. A government equity stake doesn't just buy goodwill. It buys a reason for Washington to want OpenAI to win.

No deal yet. But the fact that Altman is even floating this tells you everything about how much pressure is building. If it goes through, it rewrites the rulebook: AI companies don't get regulated, they negotiate equity stakes instead.
