💡 Microsoft just dropped $2.5B to own AI deployment — and it's a shot across the bow at every enterprise software company on the planet.

Microsoft has launched a dedicated AI deployment company with $2.5 billion committed to the effort. It's not building a new model. It's building the infrastructure to get AI into the hands of businesses at scale — and doing it faster than anyone else can catch up.

Here's the thing: Amazon, OpenAI, and Anthropic have all made the same move. Deployment — not research, not model benchmarks — is where the real moat gets built. Whoever controls how enterprises *use* AI controls the stickiest, most lucrative layer of the entire stack.

For any company still debating whether to build internal AI tools or buy from a vendor, that calculus just got a lot harder. When the biggest names in tech are all racing to own your deployment pipeline, "neutral" options are running out fast.
