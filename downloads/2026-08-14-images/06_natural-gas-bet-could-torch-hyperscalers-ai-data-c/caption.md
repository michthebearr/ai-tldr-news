💡 Natural gas prices could triple — and Big Tech's AI data centers are directly in the blast radius.

Microsoft, Google, Amazon, and the rest of the hyperscaler crowd made a massive bet on natural gas to power their AI buildout. It was supposed to be the practical bridge fuel: cleaner than coal, available enough to keep the lights on as AI demand exploded. A new forecast is now warning that bridge could get three times more expensive in parts of the US.

That's not a rounding error. Energy is one of the single biggest cost inputs in AI economics — it directly shapes what it costs to run inference, serve API calls, and keep those sprawling data centers humming 24/7. A tripling of gas prices doesn't just dent margins. It reshapes the entire profitability math behind cloud AI services.

The companies that moved fastest and built biggest may now be the most exposed.
