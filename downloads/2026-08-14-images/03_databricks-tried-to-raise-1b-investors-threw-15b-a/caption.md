💰 Databricks asked for $1B. Investors showed up with $15B. They settled on $5B.

That's not a fundraise. That's a controlled flood.

Databricks just closed a $5B round at a $190B valuation — and the wild part is they didn't even want that much money. CEO Ali Ghodsi told TechCrunch straight: "AI is expensive." When demand came in 15x over, he did the math and said yes to more than he planned.

What this signals is hard to overstate. Databricks isn't a flashy model company. It's picks-and-shovels AI infrastructure — the unglamorous but essential layer that enterprises actually run on. A $190B valuation for that kind of business means investor appetite for foundational AI plays hasn't just held. It's still accelerating.

Everyone's betting on the gold rush. Databricks is selling the shovels.
