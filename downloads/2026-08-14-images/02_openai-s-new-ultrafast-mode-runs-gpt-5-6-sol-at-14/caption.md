🧠 OpenAI just made "too slow for production" an excuse enterprises can no longer use.

Ultrafast is a new inference mode for GPT-5.6 Sol — OpenAI's most powerful model — running at 14x normal speed. It's built for enterprise teams who've been forced to downgrade to smaller, weaker models just to hit latency targets. That tradeoff is now officially dead.

This isn't just a performance upgrade. It's a strategic move. Chinese AI competitors have been aggressively undercutting US labs on price, and frontier model adoption in production environments has lagged because speed was always the bottleneck. OpenAI is betting that removing that last barrier locks in enterprise customers before rivals can.

The message is simple: you don't have to choose between smart and fast anymore.
