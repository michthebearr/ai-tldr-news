💡 Apple built a secret AI model for China — and Alibaba helped train it.

Reuters sources reveal Apple quietly developed a China-specific large language model in partnership with Alibaba, trained on Alibaba's infrastructure. It's how Apple plans to bring Apple Intelligence features to its massive Chinese user base while staying on the right side of Beijing's strict data rules.

The catch: Apple's AI is now literally two different products depending on which side of the firewall you're on. The same company. The same iPhone. Different intelligence.

This is one of the clearest examples yet of what geopolitical pressure actually does to tech — it doesn't just slow companies down, it splits them in half.
