💡 Meta says AI should be "for everyone" — just not the good stuff.

This week, Meta dropped Glimmer, an open-weight model you can download and run locally. Sounds like a win for democratization. Then you notice Muse Spark — the more powerful model — stays locked behind a proprietary API. Zuckerberg even published a letter about AI belonging to the people.

The letter and the product roadmap are telling two different stories. Open-source when it's cheap to give away. Closed when openness starts eating into the business. That's not a philosophy — that's a pricing strategy with good PR.

Real conviction doesn't come with a ceiling.
