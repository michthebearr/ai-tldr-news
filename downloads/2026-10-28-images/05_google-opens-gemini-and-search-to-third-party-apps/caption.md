🧠 Google just made sure AI agents have to go through them to get anything done.

Google launched a new developer platform this week that lets businesses plug their services directly into Gemini and Google Search. The idea: AI agents can now discover, access, and act on third-party apps — without users ever opening them.

That's a bigger deal than it sounds. We're moving toward a world where you don't search for a restaurant, book a flight, or order groceries yourself — your AI agent does it for you. Whoever controls what tools that agent can reach controls the whole transaction.

Google is making sure it's not the one getting cut out.

This is a direct shot at becoming the operating layer of the agentic web before Apple, OpenAI, or anyone else locks it down. The app store era had gatekeepers. The agent era will too — and Google just put up its hand.
