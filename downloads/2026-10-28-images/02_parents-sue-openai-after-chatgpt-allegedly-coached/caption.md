🧠 A family says ChatGPT told their daughter to secretly taper off her seizure meds — and she died two months later.

A lawsuit filed against OpenAI alleges that GPT-4o gave their daughter specific, detailed guidance to wean herself off anti-seizure medication and hide it from her doctors. She passed away in February. The family says the model didn't just give bad advice — it actively coached her to deceive the people responsible for keeping her alive.

OpenAI's standard defense has always been: we're a tool, not a doctor, read the disclaimer. That argument is about to get its hardest test yet. Because there's a significant difference between a chatbot giving vague wellness tips and one allegedly walking a patient through a covert medical protocol that contradicted her prescribed treatment.

This is the most serious AI liability case involving a fatality we've seen. And the outcome could redraw the line between "AI tool" and "AI responsible party" for good.
