💡 Chrome can now read your Gmail to fill out your forms — and yes, that's as wild as it sounds.

Google just updated Chrome's autofill to pull data directly from your Gmail inbox. Flight confirmations, delivery addresses, order tracking numbers — if it lives in your email, Chrome can now surface it and pre-fill forms on the web automatically.

You have to opt in. But once you do, your browser has a live feed into one of the most personal data streams that exists: your inbox.

This is Google collapsing the wall between your email and your browser into one seamless AI layer. Convenient for users, sure. But also extraordinarily valuable for Google — which now has a cleaner picture than ever of what you're buying, where you're going, and what you're doing.

Helpful product. Massive data play.
