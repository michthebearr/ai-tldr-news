💡 AI just broke one of computer science's most important unsolved problems — and researchers are saying it's real.

The Exponential Time Hypothesis has been a cornerstone of theoretical computer science for decades. It essentially sets a hard floor on how fast certain computational problems can ever be solved. Frontier AI labs are now claiming their models have refuted it — meaning that floor might not exist where we thought it did.

MIT brought in one of its top computer scientists to verify the result. His verdict: yes, it appears to check out.

If this holds up under peer scrutiny, it's not just a win for AI — it rewrites the rulebook for what's computationally possible. We're talking about ripple effects across cryptography, algorithms, and the fundamental limits of computing itself.

This might be the most consequential thing AI has ever done.
