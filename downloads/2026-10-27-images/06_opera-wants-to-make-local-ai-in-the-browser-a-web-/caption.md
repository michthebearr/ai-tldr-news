💡 Opera wants to make running AI locally in your browser a universal web standard — not a feature one company owns.

The browser wars just got a new front. Opera is proposing WebAI, an open standard that would let any browser run AI models directly on your device — no cloud, no proprietary lock-in, no asking nicely for access.

The idea is big: make on-device AI as native to the web as video or 3D graphics. If WebAI catches on the way WebGL did, it stops being "Opera's AI thing" and becomes something every developer can build on, regardless of which browser their users open.

That's the dream, anyway. The hard part is getting Google, Apple, and Mozilla to play along.
