💰 Anthropic wants Wall Street's money — just not Wall Street's influence.

As its IPO approaches, Anthropic is quietly engineering governance structures designed to keep shareholders from pressuring the company to move fast and break things. The goal: raise billions in public capital without handing over the wheel to investors who care more about Q3 earnings than existential risk.

It's a genuinely hard thing to pull off. Public markets are not known for their patience with "trust us, safety takes time." The moment growth slows or a competitor ships faster, the pressure to compromise will be loud and relentless.

But here's what makes this bigger than just one company's IPO. If Anthropic threads the needle, it hands every safety-focused AI lab a playbook. If it doesn't, it proves that mission-driven AI and public markets are fundamentally incompatible.

Either way, the whole industry is watching.
