💰 Disruptive just raised $10 billion to bet on a handful of AI companies — and that tells you everything about where the money is going.

The VC firm behind early investments in Groq and ElevenLabs closed a new $10B fund this week. But this isn't a diversified portfolio play. It's a concentrated, high-conviction fund designed to back at least 10 companies — meaning average check sizes of around $1 billion each.

That number is the headline. We've moved past the era of spreading small bets across 50 startups and hoping one hits. The new meta is picking your winners early and writing checks big enough to keep them competitive in a game where compute and infrastructure costs are measured in the billions.

Winner-take-all investing, for a winner-take-all moment.
