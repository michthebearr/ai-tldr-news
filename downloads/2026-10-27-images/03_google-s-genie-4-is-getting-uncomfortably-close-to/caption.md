💡 Google's Genie 4 can now build you a playable world in real time — and it's getting hard to ignore.

The latest version of Google's Genie world model just landed with a serious upgrade: interactive environments with audio, better physics, more realistic characters, and — the big one — two full minutes of scene coherence. That's double what the previous version could hold together.

Two minutes doesn't sound like much until you realize how quickly AI-generated worlds fall apart. Keeping a coherent, interactive simulation alive for that long, steered by natural language commands, with Gemini 3.8 Flash doing the reasoning underneath? That's a different category of capability.

This is quietly becoming one of the most important AI projects nobody's talking about enough. A credible, AI-native game engine isn't a distant concept anymore — it's looking like a roadmap.
