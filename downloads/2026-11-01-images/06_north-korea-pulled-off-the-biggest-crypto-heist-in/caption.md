💡 North Korea just pulled off the biggest crypto heist in history — and they're already cleaning the money.

The FBI has officially tied the $6.3 billion Binance breach to North Korean state-sponsored hackers operating under the name "TraderTraitor." This isn't a basement operation. This is a nation-state running a sophisticated financial crime unit at a scale that makes every previous crypto hack look like pocket change.

The stolen funds are already moving. Hackers have begun converting tokens to Ethereum and are preparing to launder billions — with AI-assisted techniques that make real-time tracking and freezing nearly impossible. Binance says it's covered 95% of the loss and has resumed withdrawals, but that's almost beside the point.

The real story: state-sponsored actors aren't just hacking for intelligence anymore. They're funding governments. And crypto is the perfect vehicle.
