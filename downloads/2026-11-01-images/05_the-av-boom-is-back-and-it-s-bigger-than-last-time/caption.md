💡 The autonomous vehicle industry is back — and this time, it actually might work.

The first AV boom (2018–2020) was all hype and hardware. Lidar sensors, HD maps, and big promises. Then reality hit, and most of the industry quietly retreated to regroup.

This wave is different. AI-driven decision-making has matured to the point where robotaxi deployments aren't just demos anymore — they're scaling. New players are entering with purpose-built stacks, and legacy automakers who ghosted the space are suddenly very interested again. The underlying tech isn't just incrementally better. It's categorically better.

That's what makes the stakes higher this time. The first boom failed loudly but cheaply. This one has too much momentum — and too many passengers — to get wrong.
