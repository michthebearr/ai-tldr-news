🏎️ California's AI infrastructure boom might just get a free pass on environmental review.

Prop 45 is a California ballot measure being sold as a way to fast-track "essential" infrastructure. But buried in the broad language is what critics say is a massive loophole — one that could let data center developers skip meaningful environmental review and strip residents of their legal right to challenge projects.

We're not talking small builds. We're talking 100MW hyperscale campuses landing in communities with little to no public recourse. Environmental advocates are raising the alarm, and the timing couldn't be worse — AI infrastructure investment is flooding into California faster than regulators can keep up.

If this passes, the state with the most to gain from AI growth becomes the state least equipped to question where that growth lands.
