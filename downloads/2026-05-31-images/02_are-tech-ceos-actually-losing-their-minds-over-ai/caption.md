💡 Are the people betting billions on AI actually losing the plot?

TechCrunch's Equity podcast went there this week — asking out loud whether tech executives are "uniquely prone to AI psychosis." It's a spicy diagnosis for a pattern a lot of us have noticed: leaders making increasingly grandiose, untethered claims about what AI is, what it will do, and how fast it will reshape everything.

This used to be the kind of conversation that only happened in group chats and conference hallways. Now it's getting said into microphones.

And it matters more than it sounds. When the people controlling billion-dollar AI budgets can't be trusted to reason clearly about the technology they're funding, the consequences don't stay inside the boardroom. Bad assumptions at the top become bad products, bad policy, and bad outcomes for everyone downstream.
