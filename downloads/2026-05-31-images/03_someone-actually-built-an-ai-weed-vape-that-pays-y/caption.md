💡 Someone built an AI weed vape that pays you Bitcoin for smoking — and it's somehow a real product.

Meet Gudtrip. It's a cannabis vaporizer that dispenses Bitcoin rewards every time you take a hit, wrapped in AI branding, and sold with a straight face. A Verge reporter went down the rabbit hole so you didn't have to — and came back equal parts confused and impressed that this thing actually exists.

Here's why it matters beyond the obvious joke: this is what AI branding looks like when it fully detaches from meaning. Slap "AI" on a vape pen, throw in some crypto incentives, and suddenly you have a pitch deck. The cannabis industry has been hunting for a premium identity for years — and apparently this is where that search ends up.

Three of tech's most overhyped trends. One product. Zero irony.
