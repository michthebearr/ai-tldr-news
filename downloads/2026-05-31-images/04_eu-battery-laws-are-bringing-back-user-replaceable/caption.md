💡 The EU just forced Big Tech to do what it refused to do for a decade — let you replace your own battery.

The EU's 2023 Battery Regulation is hitting its stride, and manufacturers are quietly redesigning phones, laptops, and wearables to comply. After years of gluing everything shut in the name of "sleek design," companies are being legally required to make batteries user-replaceable again. The Verge breaks down exactly how this is playing out across the industry.

This matters beyond Europe. When a market this large mandates a design standard, global product lines follow. It's cheaper to build one device than two — so what the EU demands, the world often gets. We've seen it with USB-C. We're seeing it again here.

Regulation actually working for consumers. Rare. Document it.
