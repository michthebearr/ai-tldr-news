💰 Black founders just hit a 4-year funding high — but one good quarter doesn't fix a broken system.

New Crunchbase data shows Black founders raised their highest quarterly total since 2022. On the surface, that's progress worth noting. Look closer, and researcher Gené Teare is waving a flag: these gains are fragile, and the headline number is doing a lot of heavy lifting.

The real barrier isn't check size. It's warm introductions. It's who gets into the room before the pitch deck even exists. In an AI funding boom where early relationships between founders and investors can determine a company's entire trajectory, access gaps don't just persist — they compound.

One strong quarter is a data point. Structural exclusion is a pattern.
