💰 SoftBank just wrote Europe a €75 billion check — and France is cashing it.

SoftBank announced plans to pour up to €75 billion into data centers across France, targeting 5 gigawatts of new capacity. For scale: that's roughly the entire current data center footprint of the UK. Built in one country. One bet.

This isn't a feel-good investment tour. SoftBank is planting a flag in what's quickly becoming the next front of the global AI infrastructure war. The US and China have dominated that conversation — but Europe has been screaming about AI sovereignty for years, and someone finally showed up with real money.

France just became a very serious player in that fight.
