🏎️ Everyone hates the Ferrari Luce — and Ferrari could not care less.

TechCrunch Mobility just published a piece that reframes the whole Luce backlash, and it's worth paying attention to. The argument: public opinion on a Ferrari is essentially noise. Ferrari doesn't sell to the people dunking on it in comment sections. It never has. The outrage is real. The relevance of that outrage is not.

But the deeper story here is about AI. As artificial intelligence reshapes automotive design pipelines, the cars being built are increasingly optimized for a tiny slice of ultra-wealthy buyers — not the enthusiast culture that grew up around the brand. AI-assisted design can precision-target a niche demographic with frightening accuracy. The people left out of that equation are just... left out.

The gap between cars people want to exist and cars rich people actually buy is widening. AI is about to make it a canyon.
