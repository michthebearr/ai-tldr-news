💡 Google just hired an entire AI startup — and quietly handed it the keys to Chrome.

Relay, an AI automation startup, is shutting down this week. But this isn't a failure story. Founder Jacob Bank and the full team are walking straight into Google's Chrome division, with Bank already teasing "ambitious plans to help you work with AI in Chrome."

Read between the lines: Google isn't just adding another chatbot to its browser. It's bringing in a team whose entire focus was building AI that handles real workflows — multi-step tasks, automation, execution. That's agentic AI. And it's coming to the most widely used browser on the planet.

The browser might be about to become your AI's operating system.
