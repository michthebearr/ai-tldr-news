🧠 OpenAI just built a safer ChatGPT for teens — three years after teens flooded onto the original one.

ChatGPT for Teens launched this week with age-appropriate guardrails, parental controls, and features designed to discourage academic cheating. It's cleaner, more restricted, and clearly built with regulators in mind.

The problem is the timeline. Teens have been using standard ChatGPT since 2022 — largely unmoderated, with no protections built for their age group. OpenAI knew this. The internet knew this. And now, after years of criticism and mounting regulatory scrutiny, there's finally a teen-safe version.

Better late than never isn't the defense OpenAI thinks it is.
