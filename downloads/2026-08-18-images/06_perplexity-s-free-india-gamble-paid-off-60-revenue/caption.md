🏎️ Perplexity gave India free access — and India gave it back 60% revenue growth.

Here's a freemium story that actually has a happy ending. Perplexity partnered with Airtel to hand Indian users free access to its AI search product. No friction, no credit card, just try it. Downloads spiked. Then the free tier closed to new subscribers — and instead of falling off a cliff, India revenue jumped roughly 60%.

The users who converted? They stayed. And they paid. That's the part every AI company needs to underline. In a price-sensitive market where most Western AI products die on the pricing page, Perplexity found the unlock: get people hooked first, worry about the paywall second.

This is now a playbook. Carrier partnerships as a distribution wedge into emerging markets — India today, Southeast Asia, Latin America tomorrow. Every AI company chasing global scale is going to be studying this one closely.
