🔐 OpenAI just built an AI specifically to fight other AIs — and that tells you everything about where cybersecurity is headed.

OpenAI is expanding its cybersecurity program Daybreak and dropping a new model trained exclusively for cyber defense. Not a general-purpose tool with a security plugin bolted on — a purpose-built AI defender designed from the ground up to go toe-to-toe with AI-powered threats.

Here's why that matters: attacks are no longer slow, script-kiddie stuff. AI-generated threats are faster, more adaptive, and harder to detect than anything that came before. The old playbook is already obsolete.

OpenAI is essentially admitting that you can't fight an AI arms race with human-speed tools. Specialized AI defenders aren't a nice-to-have anymore — they're the new baseline.
