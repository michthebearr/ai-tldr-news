💰 OpenAI just handed its employees $7 billion — and that's not even the wild part.

The company has completed a massive employee tender offer, letting staff sell shares back at a valuation that cements OpenAI as the most valuable private tech company in history. We're talking a number so large it would've been considered science fiction for a startup just five years ago.

Here's the real signal: insiders aren't dumping everything. They're taking some chips off the table — which is smart — but largely staying in. That's a loud vote of confidence from the people who actually know what's happening inside the building.

The downstream effect on AI talent? Brutal for everyone else. When your competitors can offer employees life-changing liquidity *and* a rocket ship trajectory, recruiting against OpenAI in San Francisco just got a whole lot harder.
