🏎️ Anthropic is quietly stamping Claude's fingerprints on everything it generates.

Starting now, Claude will embed invisible watermarks in all generated text and C2PA provenance metadata in generated images — across both new and older models. You won't see them. But machines will.

The push is partly about EU AI transparency rules, but Anthropic is going further than compliance requires. The bet: if enough AI companies adopt this standard, platforms finally get a reliable technical signal to detect AI-generated content at scale — something every detection tool so far has failed to consistently deliver.

That last part is the real story. Watermarking only works if it becomes infrastructure, not a differentiator. Anthropic is essentially daring the rest of the industry to follow.
