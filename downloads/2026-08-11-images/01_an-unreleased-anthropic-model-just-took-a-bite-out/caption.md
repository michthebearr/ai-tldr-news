💡 An Anthropic AI just made progress on a math problem that's been unsolved for 165 years.

The Riemann Hypothesis is the white whale of mathematics. Posed in 1859, it's one of the Millennium Prize Problems — worth $1 million and, more importantly, the kind of thing that defines careers. Humanity's greatest mathematical minds have taken their shot and walked away empty-handed. For generations.

An unreleased Anthropic model reportedly didn't solve it — but made meaningful forward progress. That distinction matters. In math, "meaningful progress" on a problem this old isn't a consolation prize. It's a historic event in its own right. The catch: Anthropic hasn't published the work yet, so independent verification is still pending.

But if this holds up, the implications are enormous. We're not just talking about AI writing code or summarizing documents anymore. We're talking about AI pushing the frontier of human knowledge into territory we've never been able to reach.
