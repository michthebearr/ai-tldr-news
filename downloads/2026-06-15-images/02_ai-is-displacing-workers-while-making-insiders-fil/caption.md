💡 AI is making a handful of people obscenely rich — and putting everyone else out of a job.

TechCrunch ran the numbers, and the picture isn't pretty. Tens of thousands of workers are being laid off under the banner of "AI efficiency" while a small circle of insiders is accumulating generational wealth faster than most people can process. We're not talking about a gradual shift — this is happening now, visibly, in real time.

The problem isn't just economic. It's that the people losing and the people winning can see each other clearly. There's no abstraction here. A warehouse worker getting a pink slip and a VC posting yacht photos aren't separated by six degrees anymore — they're one scroll apart.

When the gap is that visible, the backlash doesn't stay online.
