🤖 A satellite just hunted down a target on its own — no humans involved.

In April, an Earth observation satellite used onboard AI to independently identify and locate a target in real time. No instructions from the ground. No human in the loop. It reasoned about what it was looking at and acted on it — entirely by itself.

Until now, satellites have basically been expensive cameras pointed by people on the ground. This flips that model entirely. The satellite isn't waiting to be told what to do — it's deciding.

That shift matters enormously. For commercial remote sensing, it means faster, smarter data collection. For military surveillance, it means autonomous target acquisition in orbit. This capability won't stay experimental for long.
