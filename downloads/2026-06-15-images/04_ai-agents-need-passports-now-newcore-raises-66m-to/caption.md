💰 AI agents are walking into your company's systems with no ID, no oversight, and no paper trail — and someone just raised $66M to fix that.

NewCore just came out of stealth with a serious funding round to tackle what it's calling the next enterprise security crisis. As AI agents start autonomously handling real work inside company systems, most are being spun up like rogue contractors — no formal identity, no access controls, no audit trail. They're doing the job of employees with none of the accountability.

That's a massive problem. Every company moving fast on AI deployment is quietly building a shadow workforce with privileged system access and zero governance. It's the kind of risk that sounds abstract until something goes wrong — and then it's a board-level conversation.

NewCore is betting this becomes mandatory spend. Not a nice-to-have. A compliance requirement.
