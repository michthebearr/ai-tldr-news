💡 The US just pulled the plug on two of the world's most powerful AI models — and every government that trusted American infrastructure is freaking out.

On June 13, US export controls took Anthropic's Fable and Mythos offline for users across the globe. Briefly, that even included Anthropic's own foreign-born employees. One government directive. Instant blackout. No warning.

Europe and Canada are now asking the uncomfortable question they should have asked years ago: if the AI your hospitals, banks, and defense agencies run on is built on American models — who actually controls the off switch? Turns out, Washington does. And it just used it.

Dozens of cybersecurity veterans have already petitioned the White House to reverse course, arguing that the people who need these models most aren't the bad guys — they're the defenders. This is the first time a US directive has physically yanked frontier AI access mid-deployment. "AI sovereignty" stopped being a policy paper topic and became a live emergency overnight.
