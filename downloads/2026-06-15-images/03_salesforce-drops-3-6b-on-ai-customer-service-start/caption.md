💡 Salesforce just paid $3.6 billion to skip the homework.

The CRM giant is acquiring Fin, an AI-powered customer service platform, and plugging it straight into Agentforce — its enterprise agent-building product. No multi-year R&D roadmap. No internal experimentation. Just a check and a shortcut.

That's the move. Salesforce looked at what it would take to build best-in-class agentic AI for customer service and decided buying was faster than building. At $3.6B, they're clearly not the only ones who think Fin's tech is worth something.

Here's the signal everyone should be paying attention to: when a company drops that kind of money on a customer service AI startup, it means the enterprise agentic AI market has officially shifted. The "wait and see" phase is over. Consolidation is here.
