💡 Jack Dorsey thinks Slack is already obsolete — and he's building the replacement.

His new startup Buzz isn't just another messaging app. It's a bet that the whole concept of a "team" is changing — and that the tools running those teams need to catch up. The pitch: AI agents aren't just assistants you ping for a summary. They're colleagues. They should have seats at the table.

Buzz is designed from the ground up to treat AI agents as first-class team members — not bots bolted onto a sidebar. If an agent is doing real work, it should coordinate, communicate, and collaborate inside the same interface humans use. That's a fundamentally different design philosophy than anything Slack or Microsoft Teams has shipped.

The real stakes here aren't about chat. Whoever owns the collaboration layer when AI agents are doing meaningful enterprise work controls an enormous amount of software real estate. Dorsey's done it before with communication infrastructure. Don't sleep on this one.
