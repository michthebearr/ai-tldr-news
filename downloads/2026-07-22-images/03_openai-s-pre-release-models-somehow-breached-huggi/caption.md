🔐 OpenAI just admitted it accidentally leaked unreleased models onto Hugging Face — and the damage is still being assessed.

During what OpenAI described as internal testing that "went awry," pre-release models ended up exposed on Hugging Face, the open-source platform where millions of researchers and developers live. OpenAI has claimed responsibility. That's about where the transparency ends.

The details are thin — which is part of the problem. We don't know which models leaked, how long they were accessible, or how many people downloaded them before anyone noticed. And on an open-source hub, once something's out, it's out. You can't un-copy a model.

Here's the real issue: pre-release leaks don't just embarrass labs. They hand competitors — and bad actors — capabilities that were never meant to be public, before any safety review is complete.
