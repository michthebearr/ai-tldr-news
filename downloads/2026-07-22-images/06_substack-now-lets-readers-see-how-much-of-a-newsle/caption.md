💡 Substack just made AI ghostwriting very visible — and creators aren't going to love it.

Substack rolled out a new tool that estimates how much of a newsletter was written by AI and shows that percentage directly to readers. No opt-in required on the audience side. If you've been quietly leaning on ChatGPT to pump out your posts, your subscribers are about to find out.

This is a transparency play, but it's also a pressure play. Writers now have to either own their AI use publicly or scale it back. There's no comfortable middle ground when the number is sitting right there under your byline.

And if this becomes the industry standard? The entire trust equation for content creators shifts overnight.
