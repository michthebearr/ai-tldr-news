💡 One US AI company is saying the quiet part loud: Chinese AI models aren't the boogeyman Washington thinks they are.

Arcee, an American open-source AI firm, is pushing back hard against the political narrative that models like DeepSeek are inherently dangerous. Their argument isn't naive — it's technical. The risk framing, they say, is being driven by politics, not by what's actually inside the models.

And the timing matters. DeepSeek is already showing up in US enterprise environments. The debate has moved from think tanks to boardrooms, and regulators are under pressure to act fast. Fast decisions and nuanced technology rarely mix well.

The next 12 months of AI policy could lock American companies out of genuinely useful tools — or leave real security gaps wide open. Both mistakes are expensive.
