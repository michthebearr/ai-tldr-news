💰 OpenAI just committed $750 billion to AI infrastructure — that's not a typo.

Through 2030, OpenAI's infrastructure spend has ballooned to three-quarters of a trillion dollars. For context, that's roughly the entire GDP of Sweden. This isn't a budget line. It's a land grab.

The strategy is clear: treat compute like a moat. Lock in the data centers, the power contracts, the supply chains — and make it structurally impossible for competitors to catch up. By the time a rival raises their next round, OpenAI will have already signed the leases.

This is what a monopoly looks like before it's declared one.
