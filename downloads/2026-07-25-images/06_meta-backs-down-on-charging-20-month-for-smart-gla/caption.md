🎵 Meta tried to put a $20/month paywall on a feature that doesn't even use the internet.

The hearing-enhancement tool on Meta's smart glasses runs entirely locally — no cloud, no servers, no ongoing cost to Meta. And yet, the company quietly planned to charge users a monthly subscription for it anyway. That's not a service fee. That's a toll booth on a road they already own.

After backlash, Meta hit pause. But they were careful to say subscriptions aren't off the table — just delayed. So this isn't a reversal. It's a retreat with the option to reload.

The timing couldn't be worse. Meta's glasses are already facing serious heat over privacy concerns and harassment incidents. Adding a paywall on local features would've handed every wearables skeptic exactly the ammunition they needed.
