💡 One downed power line almost knocked out the AI capital of the world.

Northern Virginia isn't just a place — it's the beating heart of global AI infrastructure. More data centers are packed into that one corridor than anywhere else on Earth. And last week, a single fallen power line exposed just how fragile the whole thing is.

Turns out, these data centers have no real coordinated plan when the grid hiccups. No synchronized failover. No smart load-shifting. Just massive, competing power draws and a regional grid that was never designed to absorb them. The industry has spent billions building AI capacity and almost nothing building resilience around it.

The fix exists — better islanding technology, smarter interconnection agreements — but it's not here yet. Until it is, one bad storm in Virginia could mean latency spikes, outages, or worse across half the AI services you use daily.
