🏎️ OpenAI just shipped a physical AI keypad — and it's already dividing the room.

The device is a programmable input tool that sits on your desk and triggers AI actions, shortcuts, and commands at the press of a button. Think of it as a macro pad, but built specifically for the AI-native workflow. For developers and terminal-dwellers, it's a genuinely clever toy.

For everyone else? It's a peripheral with no obvious reason to exist yet. No clear setup story, no mainstream use case, no real on-ramp for the average person who just wants AI to work without buying more hardware.

But that might be the whole point. OpenAI is quietly probing the hardware category — and this keypad looks less like a finished product and more like a question: will developers pay for dedicated AI-native input before a polished consumer version ships?
