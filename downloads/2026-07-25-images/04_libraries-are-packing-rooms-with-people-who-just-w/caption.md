💡 Libraries are hosting "how to avoid AI" workshops — and they're the hottest ticket in town.

Public libraries across the country are filling rooms with people who just want out. Not out of the internet. Out of AI. Attendees are showing up to learn about alternative search engines, non-AI writing tools, and ways to stop their personal data from feeding the machine.

Librarians say the demand is unlike anything they've seen before. These aren't tech-illiterate holdouts — they're people who understand exactly what's happening to their data and have decided they don't want any part of it.

Consumer AI fatigue just got a mailing address.

What's interesting here is who's leading the resistance. Not regulators. Not Big Tech competitors. Librarians. The same institutions that fought for reading privacy decades ago are now teaching digital self-defense — and people are showing up in droves.
