🏎️ Cognition just bought a startup — not for its tech, but for its personality.

Cognition, the company behind Devin the AI coding agent, acquired a conversational AI startup called Poke this week. No flashy model architecture. No proprietary data. Just vibes — literally. Poke's whole thing was *how* it talked to you, and that's exactly what Cognition wanted.

The plan is to transplant Poke's interaction style directly into Devin. Because it turns out that an AI agent being pleasant, natural, and intuitive to talk to might matter just as much as whether it can actually write good code.

This is the first major acquisition where "personality" is the product being purchased. A signal that the next AI arms race isn't about raw capability — it's about UX, tone, and how it *feels* to work with these things.
