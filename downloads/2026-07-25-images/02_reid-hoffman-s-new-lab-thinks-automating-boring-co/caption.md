💡 Reid Hoffman doesn't think coding is AI's biggest opportunity — clicking and form-filling might be.

Prentis, a new AI lab co-founded by Hoffman and Mark Pincus, is in talks to raise $100M. Their bet: automating the soul-crushing desktop grunt work — form fills, workflow clicks, repetitive computer tasks — will dwarf coding assistance as the dominant AI use case.

No product is live yet. But the thesis is loud. Most AI hype has orbited developers and code generation. Prentis is looking past that, at the hundreds of millions of workers who will never write a line of code but spend half their day doing mindless computer tasks that AI could handle in seconds.

When the LinkedIn founder shifts his chips from coding to clicking, that's a signal worth paying attention to.
