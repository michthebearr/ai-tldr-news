💡 Flock Safety is offering buyouts to avoid layoffs — and it's a warning sign for the whole AI infrastructure space.

Flock Safety, the surveillance camera and fleet management company, is reportedly giving employees voluntary buyout packages to trim headcount without pulling the layoff trigger. The catch: if not enough people take the offer, cuts are "almost certainly" coming anyway.

Flock raised heavily during the AI and public safety tech boom. The problem is that being well-funded and being profitable are two very different things — and a lot of companies are just now figuring that out the hard way.

This isn't just a Flock story. It's a canary in the coal mine for every AI infrastructure company that rode the hype wave, scaled fast, and is now staring down a reckoning.
