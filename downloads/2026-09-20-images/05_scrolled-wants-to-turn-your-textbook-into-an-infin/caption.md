💡 A startup just turned your textbook into a TikTok feed — and it might actually work.

ScrollEd is a Palo Alto startup founded by student couple Utsav Gupta and Rebecca Neff, and they're pitching a simple but sharp idea at TechCrunch Disrupt: use AI to automatically reformat traditional textbooks into a scrollable, TikTok-style feed — videos, audio, embedded quizzes and all.

The logic is hard to argue with. Students already spend hours in infinite scroll. Fighting that attention pattern hasn't worked. So why not build education inside it instead of against it?

If ScrollEd scales, it puts real pressure on legacy edtech platforms and textbook publishers who've been slow to move. AI handling content transformation automatically isn't just a feature — it could become the baseline expectation for how learning materials are delivered.

Meet students where they are, or get left behind.
