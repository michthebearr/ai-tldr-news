🔐 AI isn't the cybersecurity villain — humans using AI are.

A new report is cutting through the noise on energy grid security, and the message is uncomfortable: your power infrastructure was already wide open to attack before AI showed up. The vulnerabilities were there. The negligence was there. AI just handed attackers a turbocharger.

Security experts are now watching bad actors use AI tools to probe grid systems faster, smarter, and at a scale no human team could match manually. Meanwhile, defenders are still catching up. The gap is widening in real time.

And here's the part that stings: while policymakers debate sci-fi scenarios about rogue superintelligence, the unglamorous, urgent work of actually hardening critical infrastructure keeps getting deprioritized. The threat isn't a robot uprising. It's a guy with a laptop and a very good AI assistant.
