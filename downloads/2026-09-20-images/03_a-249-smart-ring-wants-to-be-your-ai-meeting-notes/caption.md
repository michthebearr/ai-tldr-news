💡 A $249 ring wants to silently transcribe your entire workday — and your coworkers never have to agree to it.

Vocci just debuted at TechCrunch Disrupt with a smart ring that passively listens during meetings and spits out AI-generated notes. No tapping, no prompting — just wear it and let it capture everything. At $249, it's pitched as a frictionless productivity upgrade for the always-in-meetings professional.

The form factor is clever. Rings don't look like recording devices, which is exactly the problem. Every other person in that room — colleague, client, job candidate — is being captured by a piece of jewelry they probably didn't notice. Consent in ambient AI wearables is already murky. A discreet ring makes it murkier.

This is the ambient AI wearable space in 2025: the hardware is getting smaller, the capabilities are getting bigger, and the ethical framework is still nowhere in sight.
