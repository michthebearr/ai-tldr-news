💡 Trump wants to rebrand AI, build an "AI Force," and install an AI czar — and he's just getting started.

In a Truth Social post, Trump announced plans to create a dedicated "AI Force" led by an appointed AI czar, framing the move as his administration going all-in on AI development. Anyone pushing back? He's already calling it a Democratic hoax.

This tracks directly with his earlier executive order scrapping Biden's AI safety guidelines. The message is clear: the White House isn't here to regulate AI — it's here to champion it. Trump even floated ditching the term "AI" entirely, though he left the rebrand details suspiciously vague.

The politics of AI just got a lot louder. With a dedicated force, a czar, and the full weight of presidential messaging behind it, AI governance is no longer a wonky policy debate — it's a culture war.
