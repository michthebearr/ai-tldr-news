💰 Enterprises are deploying AI agents at scale — and nobody has figured out how to secure them yet.

HiddenLayer just closed a $100M round, and the timing tells you everything you need to know about where AI risk actually lives right now. This isn't a company building firewalls or running penetration tests on your cloud infrastructure. They're focused on something newer and messier: protecting AI models and agents while they're actively running.

Think prompt injection attacks, model theft, adversarial inputs that make your AI behave in ways you never intended. These are runtime threats — and they're hitting systems that most security teams weren't built to handle.

Attackers already know enterprises are moving fast and securing slow. That $100M bet says a lot of smart money knows it too.
