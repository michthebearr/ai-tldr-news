🧠 OpenAI is now facing 30 lawsuits over a mass shooting — and one of its top executives is being named personally.

Law firm Edelson PC is escalating its legal campaign against OpenAI, filing 30 new suits alleging the company's AI aided and abetted the Tumbler Ridge shooting. The claims go further than anything we've seen before: OpenAI policy chief Chris Lehane is now named as an individual defendant.

TechCrunch flags that key evidence in the case remains unconfirmed — so the legal outcome is far from certain. But that's almost beside the point. Win or lose, these suits are forcing a question the AI industry has never seriously had to answer: can an AI company be held criminally adjacent liable for real-world violence?

That's not a hypothetical anymore. It's a courtroom.
