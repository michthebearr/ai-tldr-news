💡 AfterQuery just pulled off a 10x valuation jump in five months — and the AI world can't decide if it's genius or insanity.

The startup went from a $300M Series A to a $3.2B valuation in less time than most companies plan a product roadmap. That makes it Y Combinator's fastest-ever unicorn — a title that sounds impressive until you start doing the math on what, exactly, changed in 150 days.

AfterQuery builds data infrastructure for AI model training — the behind-the-scenes plumbing that labs desperately need as they race to build bigger, better models. The demand is real. The space is hot. But a 10x markup in five months means investors are pricing in a future that hasn't been written yet.

Either this is the most rational bet in AI infrastructure right now, or it's a flashing neon sign that late-stage AI valuations have fully left the building.
