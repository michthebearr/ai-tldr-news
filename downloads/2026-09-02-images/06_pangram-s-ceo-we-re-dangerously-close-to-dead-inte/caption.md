💡 AI-generated content isn't just polluting your feed — it's breaking the systems society runs on.

Pangram CEO Max Spero is sounding the alarm: AI slop has moved way beyond fake Twitter engagement. It's now inside your hiring pipeline, your product reviews, and your insurance claims. And the tools we have to catch it? Not reliable enough.

Here's the part that makes this a real infrastructure problem: Spero says the whole "real vs. fake" framing is broken. Most AI content isn't purely machine-written — it's a blend. Human seed, AI polish, human edits. How do you flag that? Nobody has a clean answer.

When fraud detection, underwriting, and hiring decisions all hinge on knowing whether a human wrote something, "we can't reliably tell" stops being a content moderation problem and starts being a systemic failure.
