🧠 The US government just picked a side in the biggest AI copyright fight in history — and it's not the New York Times.

The Trump administration filed a brief in the NYT v. OpenAI lawsuit this week, arguing that restricting AI training on copyrighted material would hurt America's ability to compete with China. Translation: the federal government is now officially in the room, backing OpenAI's "fair use" defense.

This is a massive deal. Every major AI lab — OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta — has copyright lawsuits hanging over them right now. If the government's position lands with the court, it could effectively give the entire industry a liability shield. The legal cloud that's been following AI for two years might just evaporate.

The NYT thought it was suing a startup. It's now arguing against Washington.
