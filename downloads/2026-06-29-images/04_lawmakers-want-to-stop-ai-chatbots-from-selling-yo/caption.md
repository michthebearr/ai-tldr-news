🏥 You're telling your AI chatbot things you wouldn't tell your doctor — and someone might be selling it.

Senators Elizabeth Warren and Rep. Mary Gay Scanlon just reintroduced the Health and Location Data Protection Act, updated specifically for the AI era. The bill takes direct aim at companies like OpenAI and Anthropic, prohibiting them from selling or brokering the health and location data users share during conversations.

Here's the uncomfortable truth: millions of people are already using AI chatbots as informal health advisors — describing symptoms, asking about medications, sharing mental health struggles. Most have no idea that data could be packaged and sold to the highest bidder. There's currently no law stopping it.

This bill wants to close that loophole before it becomes a scandal.
