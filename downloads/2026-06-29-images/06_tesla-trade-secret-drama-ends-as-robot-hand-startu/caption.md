🤖 Tesla sued a robot hand startup — and now that startup just raised $11M.

Proception builds dexterous robot hands, and Tesla wasn't happy about it. The company just settled a trade secret lawsuit with the EV giant while simultaneously dropping news of an $11M fundraise. Bold timing.

Here's why this story matters beyond the legal drama: dexterous manipulation is the hardest unsolved problem in robotics right now. We can build robots that walk, lift, and navigate — but teaching a machine to handle objects with human-like hands? That's the final boss. Proception's edge is a novel method for collecting training data on exactly that problem.

Whoever cracks this wins the humanoid robot race.
