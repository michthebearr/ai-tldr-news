🏎️ Cursor just put an AI coding agent in your pocket — and you don't have to write a single line of code.

Cursor shipped a mobile app this week, and it's not for coding on your phone. It's for supervising your AI coding agent while you're away from your desk. Your agent keeps building. You keep watching. You steer when it goes off-script.

That's the shift. Agentic coding is already here — the new bottleneck isn't writing code, it's keeping the agent on track. Cursor is betting that interface lives on your phone, not your MacBook.
