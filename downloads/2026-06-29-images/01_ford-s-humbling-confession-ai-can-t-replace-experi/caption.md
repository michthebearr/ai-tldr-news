💡 Ford fired its engineers, hired AI, and now it's begging the engineers to come back.

Ford is rehiring retired veterans — internally called "gray beards" — after discovering that AI alone couldn't deliver the engineering quality the company expected. A Ford exec straight-up admitted they got it wrong. They assumed the tech would carry the load. It didn't.

What went wrong? Ford layered AI onto workflows without the deep domain expertise needed to guide, check, and refine its outputs. Turns out decades of hands-on engineering knowledge isn't just a nice-to-have — it's the thing that makes AI actually work.

The lesson is expensive, but it's simple: AI amplifies expertise. It doesn't replace it.
