🎵 TIDAL just decided AI-generated music doesn't deserve a paycheck.

The streaming platform is cutting off monetization for AI-generated tracks entirely — making it one of the first major services to draw a hard line between human-made music and machine-made content. If you used AI to generate your songs, TIDAL's not cutting you a check.

It's a bold move, and the ripple effects could be massive. Right now, AI music is flooding streaming platforms — cheap to make, easy to upload, and quietly siphoning royalty pools away from human artists. TIDAL just said enough.

The real question: if Spotify, Apple Music, and Amazon follow suit, the economics of AI music collapse overnight — and the industry finally has to answer what "authorship" actually means in 2025.
