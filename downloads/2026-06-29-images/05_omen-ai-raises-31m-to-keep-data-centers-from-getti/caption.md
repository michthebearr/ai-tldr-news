💰 $31M just went to a startup solving the grossest problem in AI infrastructure — bacteria.

Omen AI just closed a Series A to deploy AI-powered monitoring inside data center cooling systems. Their core insight: as liquid cooling becomes the standard for dense AI compute, the pipes and coolant running through these facilities can grow bacterial contamination that silently threatens hardware uptime. It's not glamorous. It's also a massive operational blind spot.

Most people thinking about the AI boom picture chips and models. Omen is betting on the unglamorous layer underneath — the physical infrastructure that keeps everything from melting. Contamination events are hard to detect early, expensive to fix, and increasingly common as more facilities push liquid cooling to the limit.

This is what "picks and shovels" investing looks like in 2025. The AI boom doesn't run without the boring stuff staying alive.
