🧠 Microsoft is quietly training its sales team to talk customers out of OpenAI — the company it gave $13 billion to.

According to new reporting, Microsoft is coaching its salespeople to pitch its own in-house AI models as the smarter, cheaper alternative to OpenAI and Anthropic. The same OpenAI it invested billions in. The same models it distributes through Azure. The pitch is essentially: skip the middleman — and the middleman is its own partner.

This is a margin play, plain and simple. Every customer Microsoft steers toward its proprietary models is a customer it doesn't have to share revenue with. The math makes sense. The optics are something else entirely.

The partnership was always going to get complicated. But Microsoft actively undercutting OpenAI in sales conversations is the clearest signal yet — this isn't just an investment anymore. It's a rivalry with a nondisclosure agreement on top.
