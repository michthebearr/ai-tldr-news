💡 Google Search just stopped being a search engine.

Google is rolling out a major upgrade to AI Mode — one that lets you actually *do* things inside Search, not just look things up. We're talking task completion, third-party app integrations, and an interface that's slowly starting to look less like a search bar and more like an operating system.

This is Google going fully agentic. Instead of Search handing you off to Notion, Spotify, or your calendar, it wants to be the place where the action happens. No redirect. No new tab. Just done.

The business threat buried in this update is enormous. Google has always been the referral engine keeping SaaS products alive with traffic. If it starts completing tasks itself, that pipeline quietly disappears.
