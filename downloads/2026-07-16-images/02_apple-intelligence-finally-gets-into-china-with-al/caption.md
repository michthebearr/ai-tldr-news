💡 Apple Intelligence is finally coming to China — and it had to make a deal with the devil to get there.

After more than a year of rumors, Apple just got regulatory approval to launch Apple Intelligence in China. The catch? It's not running on Apple's own models. Alibaba and Baidu are powering the features on the ground, giving Beijing the local oversight it requires.

This was a forced move, not a bold one. Without it, Apple was selling increasingly hollow iPhones to hundreds of millions of users who couldn't access the headline feature of the entire product line. That's a brutal position for a company trying to justify premium pricing in its second-biggest market.

The playbook is now official: if you want AI in China, you localize. Apple chose to play ball.
