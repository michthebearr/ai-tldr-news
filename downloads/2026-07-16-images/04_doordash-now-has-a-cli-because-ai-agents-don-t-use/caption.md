💡 DoorDash just built a CLI — because AI agents don't tap through apps.

The company launched a limited beta of `dd-cli`, a command-line tool that lets developers and AI agents search stores, build carts, and place orders straight from the terminal. No UI. No scrolling. Just structured commands and a burrito on the way.

This isn't a side project for nerd points. DoorDash is explicitly designing for a world where the customer placing your order isn't human. The bet: agentic commerce is moving fast enough that infrastructure needs to exist before the wave fully hits.

When a $20B delivery platform starts building agent-native interfaces, it's not a signal — it's a memo to the entire industry.
