🏥 Daniel Ek just raised $700M to scan your entire body with AI — and he's bringing it to New York.

Neko Health, the preventive care startup co-founded by the man behind Spotify, just closed a massive Series C led by Lightspeed. The pitch: walk into a clinic, get a full-body scan, blood work, proprietary sensor readings, and a clinician review — all in one visit. AI does the heavy lifting on analysis.

This isn't a wellness app or a chatbot telling you to drink more water. It's a physical clinic network backed by serious medical imaging technology, and $700M says investors think it can scale like a consumer brand.

The bigger signal here: AI diagnostics is no longer a niche play for the ultra-wealthy worried well. VCs are now betting it's a mainstream consumer health category — and the US market is the proving ground.
