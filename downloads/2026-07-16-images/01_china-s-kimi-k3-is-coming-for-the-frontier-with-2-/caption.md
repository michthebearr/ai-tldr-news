💡 China just built the biggest open AI model on the planet — and it's gunning for Anthropic.

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 is reportedly dropping with somewhere between 2 and 3 trillion parameters. To put that in perspective: most frontier models don't publish their parameter counts at all. Moonshot is not only going big, they're going open.

The Financial Times says K3 is expected to close the gap with Anthropic's Opus 4.8 — which isn't a mid-tier benchmark flex, that's one of the best models in the world. If that holds up at this scale, this isn't China catching up. This is China arriving.

Open weights at frontier level changes everything. It means researchers, startups, and governments anywhere on earth get access to capability that was, until very recently, locked behind a handful of San Francisco API keys.
