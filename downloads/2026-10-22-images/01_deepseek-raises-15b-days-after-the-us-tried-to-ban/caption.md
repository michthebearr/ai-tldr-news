💰 The US tried to ban DeepSeek. DeepSeek responded by raising $15 billion.

Just days after federal agencies were ordered to rip DeepSeek's tech out of government systems over national security concerns, the Chinese AI lab quietly closed one of the largest funding rounds in Chinese history. Fifteen billion dollars. The timing is almost theatrical.

Whether this was planned or just coincidental, the signal is impossible to ignore: US restrictions are doing nothing to slow DeepSeek's momentum where it counts. With a $15B war chest, they can keep building frontier-level models, undercut American labs on price, and grow their footprint — all without needing a single American customer.

The ban was meant to send a message. DeepSeek sent one back.
