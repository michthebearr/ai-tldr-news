🔐 Chinese hackers just used AI agents to turn one breach into thousands — and it happened faster than any human team could respond.

Microsoft has linked a massive supply-chain attack on ConnectWise — a platform used by IT teams to manage thousands of client networks — to a China-aligned hacker group. The breach itself isn't the headline. The method is.

AI agents were deployed to automate lateral movement across ConnectWise's customer base at machine speed. What used to take a team of hackers weeks to execute across a handful of targets was scaled to thousands of organizations in the time it takes to run a software update.

This is the nightmare scenario security researchers have been warning about — and it just happened in the real world.

The attack surface calculus has permanently changed. A single foothold now has the potential to become a coordinated breach across an entire industry before defenders even know they're compromised.
