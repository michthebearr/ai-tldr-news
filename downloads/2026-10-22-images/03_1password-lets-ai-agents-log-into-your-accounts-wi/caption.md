🏎️ 1Password just solved one of the messiest problems in the agentic AI era — and most people haven't noticed yet.

As AI agents start handling real tasks — booking travel, managing files, logging into services on your behalf — someone had to answer the obvious question: how do you give an AI access to your accounts without handing it your actual passwords?

1Password's answer is a dedicated credential layer built specifically for AI agents. Instead of your real password, the agent gets a tokenized credential — enough to authenticate, not enough to expose your secrets. Your vault stays locked. The agent gets the job done. It's a clean separation that the industry desperately needed.

This isn't a nice-to-have feature. It's the infrastructure layer that makes agentic computing safe enough to actually use at scale. Without this kind of delegation framework, every AI agent touching a personal account is a security incident waiting to happen.
