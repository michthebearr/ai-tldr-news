🧠 Uber just went all-in on ChatGPT — for every single one of its 30,000 employees.

Uber has rolled out OpenAI's enterprise agent platform, ChatGPT Work, across its entire workforce after a smaller pilot. That's not a test anymore. That's a full commitment — and one of the largest enterprise AI deployments we've seen from a single company.

The "AI pilot" era is quietly dying. What's replacing it: big companies picking a platform, standardizing on it, and moving on. When 30,000 people start building their workflows around one AI tool, you don't just adopt software — you create deep lock-in that's very hard to undo.

For every enterprise still running cautious little pilots? Uber just made that position a lot harder to defend.
