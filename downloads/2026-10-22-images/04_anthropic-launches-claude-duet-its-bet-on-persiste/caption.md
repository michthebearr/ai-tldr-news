🧠 Anthropic just made its biggest bet yet that the future of AI isn't a chatbot — it's an agent.

Claude Duet is Anthropic's new app experience built around long-running, autonomous AI interactions. It's rolling out to Pro and Max plan users via waitlist, details are still sparse, and that's kind of the point — Anthropic is planting a flag before the full picture is even clear.

The message is deliberate: single-turn chat is yesterday's product. Duet is designed for sustained, agentic work — Claude operating across time, tasks, and context in ways that look less like Q&A and more like a persistent collaborator.

Every major lab is now in this race. OpenAI has operator-style agents. Google has its Gemini agentic suite. Anthropic just stepped into the ring with Duet — and the differentiation war starts now.
