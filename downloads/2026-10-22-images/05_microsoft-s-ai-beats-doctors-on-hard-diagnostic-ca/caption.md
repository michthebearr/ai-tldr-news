💡 Microsoft's AI just beat doctors at diagnosis — but the fine print changes everything.

A new study in *Science* found Microsoft's AI diagnostic system outperformed generalist physicians on tough medical cases. Mustafa Suleyman is already calling it a step toward "medical superintelligence." The headlines are loud. The caveats are louder.

Here's the problem: TechCrunch dug into the methodology and found that most of the performance gains appear to come from the underlying foundation models — not from anything Microsoft uniquely built on top. In other words, the AI is winning because GPT-4-class models are powerful, not necessarily because Microsoft's diagnostic layer is doing anything special.

That distinction matters more than it sounds. If the secret sauce is just the base model, then Microsoft's system isn't particularly defensible — and every competitor with access to the same models can close the gap fast. The real race isn't who has the best medical wrapper. It's who controls the foundation.

Impressive result. Complicated story.
