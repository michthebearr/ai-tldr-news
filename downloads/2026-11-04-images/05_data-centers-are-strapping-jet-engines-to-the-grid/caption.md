💡 AI needs so much power that data centers are literally strapping jet engines to the grid.

Not a metaphor. Operators are repurposing aircraft engines to generate electricity on-site because waiting years for a utility hookup isn't an option when you're racing to build out AI infrastructure. These engines work. They're just slower, dirtier, and more expensive than anything you'd actually want to use long-term.

Here's the real problem: grid permitting in the U.S. is so broken that companies are making environmentally ugly decisions just to stay competitive. The AI compute boom is moving faster than the energy system was ever designed to handle — and the gap is widening every quarter.

Stopgap solutions become permanent ones if the underlying problem doesn't get fixed.
