🧠 ChatGPT just got one step closer to replacing every creative tool on your phone.

OpenAI quietly rolled out native GIF generation inside ChatGPT, powered by its new Images 2.6 model. Text prompt, uploaded photo, whatever you've got — it'll animate it. You can tweak the speed, edit individual frames, and share straight from the app.

On the surface, it looks like a small quality-of-life update. But zoom out and it's part of a bigger pattern: OpenAI is systematically eating the lunch of every specialized creative tool in the market. First image generation. Now animation. The gap between "general AI assistant" and "full creative suite" is closing fast.

The floor for what people expect from ChatGPT keeps rising — and that's a problem for every niche tool still charging a subscription.
