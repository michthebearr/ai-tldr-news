🏎️ Google just told schools: use our AI or lose Search — and the internet is furious.

Google announced this week that it will cut off Search access for schools that choose to block its AI features. Not throttle it. Not restrict it. Cut. It. Off. For kids.

The backlash was immediate and loud. NYC mayor Zohran Mamdani, former FTC chair Lina Khan, and a wave of educators and policy voices called it exactly what it looks like — a monopoly using its grip on essential infrastructure to force AI adoption on institutions that never asked for it.

This is textbook bundling behavior. And with Google already under antitrust scrutiny, handing regulators a story this clean might be the most self-destructive move the company has made in years.
