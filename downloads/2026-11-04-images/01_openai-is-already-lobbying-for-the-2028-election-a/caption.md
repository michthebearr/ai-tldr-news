🧠 OpenAI is already playing 2028 election politics — and calling it a "social contract."

OpenAI's policy chief Chris Lehane is out here pitching AI governance proposals explicitly tied to the 2028 election cycle. The framing is generous: economic benefits for everyday Americans, a fairer AI future, a new deal between tech and society.

The fine print is less generous. Every policy outcome Lehane is championing just happens to keep OpenAI positioned at the center of how AI gets regulated and funded in America. That's not a social contract — that's a lobbying strategy with better branding.

A major AI lab openly trying to shape electoral politics two years before the election is a new frontier. And if OpenAI is already this organized, you can bet the rest of the industry is watching closely.
