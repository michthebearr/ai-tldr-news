💡 Meta just bought an entire AI dev team — and it's not human.

Meta has acquired Conductor, a startup that lets you deploy not one AI coding assistant, but a coordinated crew of specialized agents working in parallel. One handles the backend. One handles the tests. One handles the docs. All at once.

This isn't copilot-style autocomplete anymore. This is Meta betting that the future of software development looks less like a smart suggestion and more like an autonomous engineering department you can spin up on demand.

The terms weren't disclosed — but the signal is loud and clear.
