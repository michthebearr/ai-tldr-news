💡 Anthropic wants to make sure you actually know when you're talking to Claude.

The company is building a system called FlashProve — a cryptographic verification layer that lets anyone confirm whether an AI response genuinely came from the model it claims to be from. Think of it as a digital fingerprint baked into the output itself.

This might sound niche. It isn't. As AI-generated content floods every corner of the internet and bad actors get better at spoofing trusted models, the ability to verify provenance isn't a nice-to-have — it's infrastructure. The industry has been building skyscrapers without checking who signed the blueprints.

Anthropic is betting that trust, not just capability, is the next competitive frontier.
