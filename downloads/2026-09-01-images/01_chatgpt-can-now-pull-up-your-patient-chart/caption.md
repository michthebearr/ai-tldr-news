🏥 ChatGPT just got access to your medical records — and that's a bigger deal than it sounds.

OpenAI's ChatGPT Health product has integrated with Epic, the electronic health records system powering most major U.S. hospitals. Clinicians can now pull patient data directly into ChatGPT for read-only access — the AI can see your chart, but it can't touch it.

That guardrail matters. But what matters more is the door this opens. Epic isn't just one EHR vendor — it's the backbone of American hospital infrastructure. Getting into Epic means getting into the workflow of doctors, nurses, and care teams at scale. ChatGPT isn't a consumer toy anymore. It's auditioning to be medical infrastructure.

If this gains traction, OpenAI won't just be in your pocket. It'll be at the bedside.
