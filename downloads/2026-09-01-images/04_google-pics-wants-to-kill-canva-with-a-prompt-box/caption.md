💡 Google just built a Canva killer — and hid it inside an app you already use.

Google launched Google Pics this week, a Gemini-powered design tool baked directly into Workspace. The pitch is simple: describe what you want, get a professional image back. No layers, no templates, no drag-and-drop. Just a prompt box.

It runs on Google's Nano Banana generative model and it's going straight at the business users Canva and Adobe have been battling over for years. That's not a small market — that's the whole game.

Here's why this is dangerous for the incumbents: Google doesn't need to be better. It just needs to be good enough — and already open on your screen.
