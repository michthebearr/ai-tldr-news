🏎️ Apple says OpenAI destroyed evidence — and has the MacBook to prove it.

Apple's lawsuit against OpenAI just escalated into something much darker than a trade secret dispute. A former employee at the center of the case allegedly had conversations about "destroying forensic data" — on a MacBook that OpenAI only recently turned over. That's a problem.

Apple is now pushing for expedited discovery, arguing evidence isn't just at risk — it's actively disappearing. When a company has to tell a court "they're deleting things right now," the legal situation has moved well past uncomfortable.

If the destruction-of-evidence allegations hold up, OpenAI isn't just defending against theft claims anymore. Spoliation of evidence can torch a legal defense faster than the original charge ever could. This one's about to get very messy, very fast.
