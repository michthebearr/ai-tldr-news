💡 Amazon just turned Alexa into a shopping assistant that hunts you down — politely.

The new "Update Me When" feature lets you tell Alexa what you're waiting for — a product drop, a book release, a tour announcement — and it'll tap you on the shoulder the moment it happens. Sounds handy. It is. But let's not pretend this is purely for your convenience.

Amazon built Alexa to sell things. That's always been the quiet truth. What's changed is that Alexa is no longer waiting for you to ask — it's now engineered to catch you at the exact moment you're most likely to open your wallet.

A reactive assistant just became a proactive purchase funnel. That's a big shift dressed up in a very friendly UI.
