🔐 Nobody knows what their AI agents are actually doing — and that's a $50M problem.

AIR just closed a $50M funding round to solve one of enterprise AI's most quietly terrifying issues: companies are deploying AI agents at scale with almost zero visibility into what those agents are up to. AIR automatically discovers every agent running inside an organization, vets the third-party plugins and skills they're using, and shuts down any behavior that falls outside company policy.

It's basically an IT firewall — but built for the age of autonomous agents. And the timing makes sense. As businesses spin up hundreds of agents to handle everything from customer support to internal workflows, the attack surface grows fast. One rogue plugin or misconfigured agent can cause serious damage before anyone even notices.

Agent governance is the next big frontier in enterprise security. AIR is betting it becomes as non-negotiable as antivirus software.
