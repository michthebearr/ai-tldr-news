💡 Everyone's obsessing over AI models and chips — but the real bottleneck might be the internet itself.

TechCrunch just published the infrastructure piece nobody wants to read but everyone needs to. The argument: AI's hunger for compute, bandwidth, and real-time data is about to slam into an internet that was never built for any of this. Legacy systems, aging data centers, brittle APIs — all of it held together, as they put it, by "glue and dreams."

The AI scaling conversation almost always lives at the model layer. Bigger parameters, faster chips, cheaper inference. But underneath all of that is a physical and digital infrastructure that hasn't kept pace — and AI workloads are uniquely good at finding every crack in a system.

The unsexy layer is about to become the most important one.
