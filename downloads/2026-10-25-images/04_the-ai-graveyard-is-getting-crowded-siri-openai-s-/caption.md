🧠 The AI graveyard is filling up fast — and some big names are in it.

TechCrunch is keeping a running obituary of AI projects that never made it, and it's a genuinely humbling list. Siri's long-promised upgrades have been delayed so many times they've become a tech industry joke. OpenAI's "super app" ambitions? By most accounts, a chaotic stumble.

The pattern is the same every time: a splashy announcement, a wave of headlines, then silence. Shipping AI that actually works — at scale, reliably, for real users — is brutally hard, even when you have billions in the bank.

The graveyard doesn't lie.
