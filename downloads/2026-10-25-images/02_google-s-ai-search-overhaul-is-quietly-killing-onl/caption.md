💡 Google's AI search isn't just changing how we find information — it's quietly dismantling who gets paid to create it.

The Verge ran a deep-dive this week into how Google's AI-generated search summaries are hollowing out independent publishers and media outlets. The mechanic is simple and brutal: Google reads the article, summarizes it, and hands the answer to the user. No click required. No traffic delivered.

Sites that spent years and real money optimizing for Google's own rules are now being outranked by Google itself. The content still powers the answer. The creators just don't get anything for it.

And if that model scales — if AI search becomes the destination instead of the directory — the economics that fund most online journalism collapse entirely. No traffic means no ad revenue. No ad revenue means no journalists. The machine keeps learning. The humans who fed it go broke.
