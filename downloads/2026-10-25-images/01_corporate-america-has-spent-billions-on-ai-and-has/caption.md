💰 Corporate America has spent billions on AI — and an economist at one of the world's biggest investment firms just said the quiet part out loud.

Apollo Global's chief economist is calling it: for most companies outside of Big Tech, AI has delivered "zero financial payoff." Not slow returns. Not modest gains. Zero. Despite massive spend on infrastructure, software, and talent, the productivity bump simply isn't showing up in the numbers for your average Fortune 500 firm.

The people winning right now? The ones selling the shovels. Nvidia, Microsoft, the cloud providers — they're cashing in while everyone else waits for the ROI that was supposed to justify all of this. It's the gold rush dynamic playing out in real time.

What makes this notable isn't the argument — skeptics have been making it for a while. It's who's making it. This isn't a contrarian blogger or a tech pessimist. It's a senior economist at a major financial institution with billions of dollars riding on getting this right.

The hype and the reality have never looked further apart.
