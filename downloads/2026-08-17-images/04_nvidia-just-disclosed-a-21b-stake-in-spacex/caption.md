💡 Nvidia just revealed it owns $21 billion worth of SpaceX — and that changes everything.

Nvidia filed disclosures this week showing a massive $21 billion stake in SpaceX. This came on the heels of Elon Musk announcing an exclusive deal to outfit SpaceX data centers with Nvidia chips. So Nvidia isn't just a vendor here. It's a major investor in one of its own biggest customers.

That's a significant line to cross. When GPU supply is tight — and it still is — who decides which companies get chips first? If Nvidia holds a $21 billion stake in SpaceX, that question gets a lot more complicated. Competitors and regulators are going to notice.

Nvidia is quietly becoming something bigger than a chipmaker. It's positioning itself as a power broker across the entire AI ecosystem — and with that comes serious conflict-of-interest territory.
