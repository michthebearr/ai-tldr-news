🧠 OpenAI quietly killed the team responsible for catching its most dangerous AI risks.

The Preparedness team — the group tasked with evaluating whether OpenAI's models could cause catastrophic harm — was dissolved at the end of July. No fanfare. No press release. Just gone.

Safety responsibilities have been split up and redistributed into domain-specific silos: bio risks here, cyber risks there. The argument from OpenAI is presumably that specialized teams are more effective. The counterargument writes itself — a fragmented safety structure means no one is looking at the full picture when a model crosses multiple risk categories at once.

The timing is what makes this hard to ignore. Models are getting more capable by the month, and this is the moment OpenAI chose to restructure the team built to sound the alarm.

Defensible strategy or massive blind spot — we'll find out.
