💡 Stripe is reportedly buying OpenRouter for $7B+ — and this might be the most strategic AI acquisition you haven't heard about yet.

OpenRouter is an AI gateway that lets developers route requests across dozens of models — think GPT-4, Claude, Gemini — from a single API. It's infrastructure. Unglamorous, essential, and wildly underrated.

The CEO literally described his own company as "Stripe for AI." Stripe apparently took notes. If this deal closes, Stripe doesn't just process payments for AI products — it owns the layer that sits between developers and every major model on the market.

That's not a fintech play. That's a chokepoint.

If AI API spending keeps compounding the way everyone expects, controlling how developers access and pay for inference could be worth multiples of $7B. Stripe just spotted the toll booth — and bought it before anyone else realized what it was.
