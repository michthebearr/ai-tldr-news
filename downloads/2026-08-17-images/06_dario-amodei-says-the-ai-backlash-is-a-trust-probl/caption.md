💡 Dario Amodei doesn't want to be the AI doomer anymore — but he can't exactly un-ring that bell.

The Anthropic CEO is out pushing a new frame: the public's growing anxiety about AI isn't a reasonable response to his own apocalyptic warnings, it's a "crisis of trust." He's trying to separate himself from the backlash while staying credible as the industry's foremost safety voice. That's a tricky pivot.

Here's the tension: Amodei built Anthropic's entire brand on taking AI risk seriously. Now that skepticism is mainstream and spilling into real resistance, he's on defense about his own messaging. You can't spend years warning about existential catastrophe and then act surprised when people start distrusting the technology.

When the safety guy has a PR problem, the whole industry has a problem.
