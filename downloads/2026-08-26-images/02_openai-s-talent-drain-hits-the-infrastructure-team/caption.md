🧠 OpenAI is losing the people building its future — and the spin isn't landing.

The company's top data center executive just left, and OpenAI's official line about "reorganizing its infrastructure organization" is doing a lot of heavy lifting. It's the kind of corporate language that translates pretty cleanly to: we don't want to talk about this.

Here's why it matters more than a typical exec departure. Data center buildout isn't a side project for OpenAI right now — it's the whole game. Scale is the strategy. Compute is the moat. Losing the leader steering that ship isn't a footnote, it's a crack in the foundation.

And this isn't happening in isolation. Senior talent has been walking out the door across functions for months. At some point, a string of departures stops being coincidence and starts being a signal.
