🤖 Bill Gates wants to tax robots — and lock certain jobs away from AI forever.

Gates just dropped a new essay with two concrete ideas: a robot tax to fund social safety nets, and a formal "Human Reserved" job category — roles society deliberately keeps out of AI's reach because human connection and dignity matter more than efficiency.

That second one is the real story. Most tech-billionaire AI takes are long on concern and short on specifics. "Human Reserved" is an actual policy mechanism — a line in the sand that says some jobs shouldn't be automated, full stop. Think elder care, therapy, teaching. Roles where the human *is* the point.

When someone with Gates's policy reach starts naming specific mechanisms, legislators listen.
