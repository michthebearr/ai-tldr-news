🧠 Claude finally remembers who you are — and that changes everything.

Anthropic just shipped persistent memory across Claude's chat and Cowork products. Your projects, your preferences, your context — it all carries over now. No more re-explaining yourself every single session like the AI has amnesia.

This sounds small. It isn't. Persistent memory is the line between an AI tool and an AI assistant. Without it, every new session is a blank slate — and you're doing half the work before you've even started. That friction is exactly why "AI-powered workflows" have felt more like a demo than a replacement for real tools.

With this update, Cowork gets a lot more serious.
