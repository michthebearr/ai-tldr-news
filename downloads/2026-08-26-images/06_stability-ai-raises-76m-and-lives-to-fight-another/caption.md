💰 Stability AI just raised $76M — which sounds impressive until you remember how close they came to dying.

The company behind Stable Diffusion has closed a new funding round, bringing its total raise to $232M. After a chaotic stretch — founder drama, mass employee departures, and very public questions about whether the lights would stay on — this is less a victory lap and more a quiet sigh of relief.

Stability still has real assets: Stable Diffusion's name recognition is enormous, and its open-source community is genuinely loyal. But the competitive landscape has shifted fast. Closed-model rivals have been moving aggressively while Stability was busy surviving.

The raise matters beyond just one company. If Stability goes dark, the open-source image generation ecosystem loses its most recognized player — right when it can least afford to cede ground to closed platforms.

Still here. Still relevant. For now.
