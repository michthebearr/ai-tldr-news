🤖 Waymo just did what Apple, Google, and Tesla all had to do — it built its own chip.

The robotaxi company is no longer just running self-driving software on off-the-shelf hardware. Waymo has developed custom silicon tuned specifically for its fleet — and that's a bigger deal than it sounds.

Here's why: the gap between a sensor detecting something and the car reacting to it has to be milliseconds. Off-the-shelf chips aren't built with that obsession. Custom silicon is. Owning that layer means Waymo controls performance, slashes latency, and cuts long-term hardware costs — all at once.

This is a company that just decided it's in the infrastructure business forever. That's not a startup move. That's a moat.
