🧠 DeepMind alumni just built an AI that outperformed both OpenAI and Anthropic — at science.

British startup Inherent released Faraday this week, an AI agent built to do something deceptively hard: replicate scientific research papers. It beat models from OpenAI and Anthropic on the task. The team is made up of DeepMind veterans, and they're positioning Faraday not as a tool, but as a research teammate.

Replication is the unglamorous backbone of science. Before you can build on a finding, someone has to verify it holds up — and that process is slow, expensive, and massively bottlenecked. Automating it well is harder than it sounds, which is exactly why most labs haven't cracked it.

If Faraday can do this reliably at scale, the question stops being "can we reproduce this?" and becomes "what do we build next?" That's a meaningful shift in how fast science actually moves.
