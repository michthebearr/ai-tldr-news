💡 Harvard is selling AI clones of its professors — and they'll coach you at 2am without complaint.

HBS Foundry, a $699 startup bootcamp from Harvard Business School, just dropped something genuinely weird: AI avatars modeled directly on its actual instructors. Pitch your startup idea, run a mock board meeting, get real-time feedback. No scheduling. No office hours. Just a digital version of a Harvard professor telling you your go-to-market strategy needs work.

It's part edtech product, part experiment in what "access to expertise" even means anymore. For $699, you're not getting a Harvard degree — but you are getting something that looks, sounds, and responds like faculty on demand. That's a strange new category.

When Harvard starts cloning its professors, everyone else will follow.
