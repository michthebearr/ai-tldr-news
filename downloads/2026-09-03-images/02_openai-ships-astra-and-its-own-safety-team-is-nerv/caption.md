🧠 OpenAI just shipped a model its own safety team is nervous about.

Astra — internally GPT-6 — dropped this week, and OpenAI is calling it a "generational leap." That alone would be headline news. But buried in the announcement is something harder to spin: this is the first model OpenAI has officially flagged as hitting a "critical cybersecurity capability threshold."

The reason for the alarm is a new technique called "recurrent depth." It lets the model reason outside the sequential thinking patterns that current safety frameworks are literally built to monitor. In other words, OpenAI shipped a model that outpaces its own tools for watching it.

When the people building the guardrails are the ones raising the red flag, that's not a PR problem. That's a structural one.
