💡 Nvidia just bought the neighborhood where every AI model lives.

Nvidia has confirmed it's acquiring Hugging Face — home to 3 million models and 18 million developers — for $12.9 billion. This isn't just a big number. It's a statement: the company that makes the chips powering AI now also owns the platform where most of the world's AI models are built, shared, and discovered.

Think about what that means. Nvidia already dominates the hardware layer. Now it controls the marketplace layer too. Every researcher uploading a model, every startup shopping for one — they're all operating on Nvidia's turf.

This is the picks, shovels, and the general store. All one company.
