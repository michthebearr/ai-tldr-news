💡 Meta will slash your AI bill by 95% — if you let it watch everything you do.

Meta just launched a deeply discounted pricing tier for its new Muse Spark agent model. The catch? You opt in to sharing your prompts and model outputs, which Meta then uses as training data. They're calling it voluntary. Technically, it is.

But when the alternative is paying full price, "voluntary" starts to mean something different. This is a clever move — Meta isn't just selling AI access, it's buying the agentic workflow data it needs to compete with OpenAI and Google. Developers get a discount. Meta gets the dataset.

It's not a pricing model. It's a data acquisition strategy with a loyalty card attached.
