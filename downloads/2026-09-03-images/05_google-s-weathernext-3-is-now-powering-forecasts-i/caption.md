🧠 Google just put its best AI weather model where 3 billion people will actually feel it.

WeatherNext 3 — Google's third-generation deep-learning weather model — is now live inside Google Search, Maps, and Gemini. Not a research paper. Not a benchmark. A real product, used by real people, every single day.

And it's beating the old guard. Traditional numerical weather prediction has been the backbone of forecasting for decades — built on physics equations, supercomputers, and armies of meteorologists. WeatherNext 3 learned the patterns instead. The result: measurably better forecasts, deployed at planetary scale.

This is what AI actually replacing legacy expert systems looks like. Quietly. Productively. No hype cycle required.
