💡 A startup just handed the keys of a spacecraft to an AI model — and told it to drive.

AstroForge, a space mining startup, is putting a transformer-based AI called Autonomy-1 in full operational command of its next space probe. Not a copilot. Not a backup system. The decision-maker.

That means real-time navigation choices, autonomous problem-solving, and mission-critical calls — all made by a model, in deep space, with no human hand on the wheel. The kind of environment where a bad decision doesn't just mean a bug report.

Here's what makes this a genuine milestone: transformer architectures were built for language. AstroForge is betting they can generalize to life-or-death physical systems millions of miles from Earth. If it works, the implications go way beyond mining asteroids.
