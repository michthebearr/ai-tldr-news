🧠 OpenAI just dropped two new models in a single day — and the race is getting uncomfortable to watch.

Meet GPT-6 Sol and Luna. Sol is the heavyweight: smarter, more capable, built for complex tasks. Luna is the leaner option — cheaper, faster, and designed for teams that don't need the full firepower. Both share architectural DNA with Astra, OpenAI's internal flagship model, which tells you they're not cutting corners to hit a lower price point.

This is the tiered model playbook now. Every major lab is doing it — Anthropic, Google, Meta. You get the premium option and the cost-efficient option, and enterprises pick their lane. What's different today is the sheer volume: three major model releases in a single 24-hour window, counting Anthropic's drop.

Most companies haven't finished evaluating last quarter's models.
