💡 Trump just renamed AI from the UN podium — no policy, no order, just vibes.

At the UN General Assembly, President Trump declared that the United States is officially calling artificial intelligence "super intelligence" going forward. No executive order. No regulatory framework. No definition. Just a rebrand, announced to the world from one of the most consequential stages on the planet.

Here's the kicker: "super intelligence" wasn't even one of the options in the social media poll Trump ran on this exact topic just days before the speech. The winning term came from nowhere — or rather, from the podium.

That's the problem. International AI governance is already a mess of competing frameworks, terminology disputes, and geopolitical tension. When the US president casually rebrands a technology from a global stage with zero substance behind it, it doesn't just look chaotic — it actively makes the hard work of global coordination harder.

Cool name. No meaning. Real consequences.
