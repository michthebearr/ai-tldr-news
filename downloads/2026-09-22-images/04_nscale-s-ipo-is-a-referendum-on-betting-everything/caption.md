💰 Nscale is going public — and its entire business basically rides on two phone calls.

The British AI data center company is heading for an IPO, and the pitch to Wall Street comes with a major asterisk: almost all of its revenue comes from just two clients — Microsoft and Anthropic. That's not a customer base. That's a dependency.

Investors are already nervous. Customer concentration risk is the kind of thing that looks fine in a bull market and catastrophic the moment one of those contracts gets renegotiated, delayed, or pulled. Nscale scaled fast by locking in hyperscaler deals instead of diversifying — and now the public markets get to vote on whether that was a smart strategy or a ticking clock.

The real stakes here go beyond Nscale. If this IPO stumbles, it sends a chill through every AI infrastructure company that built their whole model around one or two anchor clients and called it a business.
