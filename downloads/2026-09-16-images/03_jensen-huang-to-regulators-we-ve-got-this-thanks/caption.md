💡 The most powerful man in AI just told regulators to stay in their lane.

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang made his position crystal clear this week: AI doesn't need government oversight. In his view, safety isn't a policy problem — it's an engineering one. Every company building AI products should handle it themselves. Simple as that.

Coming from literally anyone else, this might read as a talking point. Coming from Jensen Huang — the man whose GPUs power OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Anthropic, and every other major lab on the planet — it's something closer to a verdict. He's not just in the room where it happens. He built the room.

His framing is slick: AI isn't some alien intelligence, it's hardware and software. Don't regulate the technology, trust the builders. But "trust the builders" is a tough sell when regulators worldwide are watching this industry sprint forward with no speed limit in sight.
