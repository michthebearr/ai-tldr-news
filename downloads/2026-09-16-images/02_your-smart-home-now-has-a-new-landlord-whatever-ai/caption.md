🏎️ Google just gave AI agents the keys to your smart home — and didn't really ask you about it.

Google launched early access to a Google Home MCP server this week, meaning third-party AI agents like Claude and ChatGPT can now control your lights, locks, cameras, and connected devices — in plain English, no custom integration needed.

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is quickly becoming the universal plug for AI agents, and this move is a big deal. It's not just a convenience upgrade. It's a fundamental shift in who — or what — sits at the center of your home. Your smart home used to answer to Google or Amazon. Now it answers to whatever AI you've got running.

That's either incredibly useful or slightly terrifying, depending on how much you trust your agent stack.
