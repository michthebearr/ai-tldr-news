💡 AI auditors won't save you if your agent can already access everything.

A new TechCrunch op-ed is calling out what a lot of people in the security world already know but rarely say out loud: the red teams and in-house auditors that AI labs love to talk about are mostly theater. Impressive on a slide deck. Not so impressive when a rogue agent is already halfway through your system.

The real fix is structural. Restrict what agents can access. Restrict what they can do. Apply the same principle of least privilege that good security engineers have used for decades — just actually do it, for AI. If an agent doesn't need access to your email, your file system, or your payment tools, it shouldn't have it. Full stop.

As agentic AI rolls out faster than most companies can track, the gap between "we have safety processes" and "we have actual controls" is getting dangerous to ignore.
