🧠 Claude just built Google Docs inside your chat window — and it's gunning for Microsoft and Google.

Anthropic quietly made a massive move this week. It merged its Claude chat and Cowork interfaces into one unified experience and dropped two new tools alongside it: Docs and Slides. You can now create, export, and share full documents and presentations without ever leaving a Claude conversation.

That's not a chatbot feature. That's a productivity suite.

This is Anthropic planting a flag in territory Google and Microsoft have owned for decades. The strategy is clear — stop making users switch apps and become the one window where work actually gets done. If Claude can pull it off, the competitive threat to Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 just got very real.

Chatbots are table stakes now. The fight is for your workflow.
