📱 AI dating apps are now running video call scams — and the fake personas are convincing enough to break your heart and empty your wallet.

Security researchers just exposed a wave of fraudulent dating apps using AI-generated personas to lure real users into emotional connections. We're not talking about a few fake profile pics. These apps are polished, the "people" feel real, and they'll hop on a video call with you — powered entirely by generative AI.

Then comes the ask. Money, gift cards, wire transfers. The classic romance scam playbook, now running at machine scale with a fraction of the overhead.

This is what happens when powerful generative AI meets zero guardrails and a profit motive. The cost of running romance fraud used to be limited by how many human scammers you could hire. That ceiling is basically gone now.
