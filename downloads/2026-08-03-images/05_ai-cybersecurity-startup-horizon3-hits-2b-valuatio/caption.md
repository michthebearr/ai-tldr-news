🔐 AI isn't just changing how we build software — it's changing how fast it can be attacked.

Horizon3 just raised $250 million at a $2 billion valuation, and the pitch is simple: the old model of testing your security once a year is dead. AI-powered attacks move too fast for that. Enterprises need continuous, automated validation running in the background — not a report that's already outdated by the time it lands on a desk.

That's exactly what Horizon3 sells. And apparently, companies are paying serious money for it.

This round is a signal. AI is generating new threat surfaces faster than most security teams can track — and the enterprises that understand that are writing big checks to close the gap.
