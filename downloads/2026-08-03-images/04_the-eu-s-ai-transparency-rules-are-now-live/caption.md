💡 The EU just made AI transparency the law — and the clock is already ticking.

As of August 2nd, the EU's AI Act transparency obligations are officially in force. Companies operating in Europe must now tell users when they're talking to a chatbot or consuming AI-generated content like deepfakes. No more quietly slipping AI into the experience without disclosure.

The European Commission didn't just flip the switch — they also dropped a set of official AI labels for companies to use, so there's no excuse for rolling your own ambiguous "AI-assisted" badge and calling it compliance. This is real regulation with real legal teeth.

First major AI labeling law in the world. Every global AI company just got a new compliance item on their roadmap.
