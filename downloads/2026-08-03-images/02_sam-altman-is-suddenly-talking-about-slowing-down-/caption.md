💡 Sam Altman is suddenly telling the AI industry to slow down — and nobody quite knows what to make of it.

The man who turned OpenAI into the most powerful AI company on the planet is now publicly calling for the industry to "pace the rate of AI development." That's a striking pivot from someone who spent years treating the race to AGI like a sprint with no finish line.

The debate it's triggered is intense. Is this a genuine safety reckoning from someone who's seen enough behind the curtain to be genuinely worried? Or is it a calculated move to position OpenAI as the "responsible" player right as regulators start sharpening their pencils? Silicon Valley is splitting on exactly that question.

Either way, when Altman talks, the industry listens — and deceleration language from him doesn't land quietly.
