🧠 The people writing America's AI laws are running their offices on ChatGPT — and the receipts are public.

House spending records confirm that OpenAI's ChatGPT is the dominant paid AI tool across congressional offices on Capitol Hill. Staffers are using it daily to draft memos, summarize legislation, and manage constituent communications. This isn't a test balloon. It's already the default.

Think about what that means. The same lawmakers tasked with regulating AI — deciding who gets oversight, what's allowed, what gets broken up — are operationally dependent on one specific AI company. Not AI in the abstract. OpenAI's product. Specifically.

That's not a fun fact. That's a conflict of interest hiding in plain sight.
