🧠 Alibaba just dropped its biggest AI model yet — and it's claiming OpenAI-level performance.

Meet Qwen Max. It's Alibaba's largest open-weight model to date, and the benchmarks are putting it in the same conversation as the top frontier labs in the US — think Anthropic and OpenAI territory. That's a bold claim. But the open-weight release means researchers and developers worldwide can download it right now and put it to the test themselves.

Here's why this one matters beyond the benchmark flex: open-weight models are hard to contain. Export controls can block chips, but they can't un-release a model that's already on the internet. If Qwen Max holds up under real-world scrutiny, the US strategy of maintaining an AI lead through hardware restrictions just got a lot more complicated.

The US-China AI race isn't coming. It's here.
