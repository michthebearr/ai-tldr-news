💡 Most enterprise AI projects don't fail because the model is bad — they fail because nobody can actually ship the thing.

A startup called June just came out of stealth with a $20 million pre-seed round to fix exactly that. Backed by Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff, June is building the infrastructure layer that sits between "we bought an AI tool" and "we have AI running in production." That gap, it turns out, is where most enterprise initiatives go to die.

The pitch is simple and the market is real: companies have spent heavily on AI but deployment friction — integrations, security, workflow fit — keeps killing momentum before anything reaches users. June wants to be the layer that makes that problem disappear.

A $20M pre-seed for a company that was invisible until this week is a serious signal. Early backers aren't betting on a model. They're betting that "AI for AI adoption" is one of the most important picks-and-shovels plays in enterprise tech right now.
