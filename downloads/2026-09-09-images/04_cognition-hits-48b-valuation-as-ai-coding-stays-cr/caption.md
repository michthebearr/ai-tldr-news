💡 Cognition just got valued at $48 billion — and its product is still figuring itself out.

The AI coding startup behind Devin, the autonomous software agent that made waves (and drew skeptics) when it launched, just closed a round at a valuation higher than Cursor's pre-acquisition multiple. That's a bold number for a company that's openly been on a winding road to product-market fit.

But here's what the real story is: investors aren't betting on one winner in AI coding. They're carpet-bombing the category. Cursor, Cognition, GitHub Copilot, Replit — capital is flowing to all of them simultaneously, because nobody believes the space is locked up yet.

A $48B valuation isn't just confidence in Devin. It's a signal that the market thinks AI dev tools could be one of the biggest categories in tech — and nobody wants to be the one who missed it.
