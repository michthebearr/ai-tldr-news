🏎️ Apple just held an AI conference — and called it a product launch.

New CEO John Ternus used the "Surprise and Shine" event to make one argument, loudly: the iPhone is still the best AI device on the planet. Then he spent the whole keynote proving it.

iPhone 18 Pro gets upgraded AI camera features. Apple Watch Series 12 gets "Audio Intelligence" that recaps your conversations. The Health app now calculates your "health age" using Apple Intelligence. And Apple Reference Image — a provenance tool that flags whether a photo has been AI-edited — might be the sleeper hit of the whole lineup.

This isn't AI as a bullet point on a spec sheet anymore. It's the organizing principle of every single product Apple ships — with a privacy-first, on-device angle that's a direct jab at every cloud-dependent competitor in the room.
