🎵 Suno is scrapping its entire AI music model — because the old one was legally indefensible.

Suno, one of the biggest names in AI-generated music, is rebuilding its foundation model from scratch. The new version, Suno v6, is trained exclusively on licensed music — a direct response to the pile of copyright lawsuits the company is currently facing over its previous scraping-based approach.

This isn't a patch or a policy update. It's a full teardown and rebuild. That's an enormous operational cost, and it's essentially Suno admitting out loud that the way it trained its earlier models couldn't survive legal scrutiny.

Here's why that matters beyond Suno: they're the first major generative AI company to proactively replace a model over copyright liability. Every AI image, video, and text company with a lawsuit on its desk just got handed a blueprint — and a warning.

The era of "scrape now, litigate later" may finally be ending.
