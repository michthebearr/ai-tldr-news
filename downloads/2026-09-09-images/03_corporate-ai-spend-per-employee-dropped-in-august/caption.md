💡 Corporate AI spending per employee just dropped — and the excuses are running out.

New data shows AI spend per employee fell at major firms last August. The easy explanation is summer slowdowns. The real explanation is messier: token costs are falling fast, cheaper models are flooding the market, and companies are getting more output for fewer dollars.

That sounds like a win. For Microsoft, Google, and Amazon, it's closer to a nightmare. Their entire infrastructure buildout was priced around AI demand growing in dollar terms, not just in usage. Efficiency gains are quietly deflating the revenue projections that justified hundreds of billions in data center bets.

The uncomfortable question nobody can answer yet: is enterprise AI maturing into something leaner and smarter — or is the enthusiasm just quietly fading?
