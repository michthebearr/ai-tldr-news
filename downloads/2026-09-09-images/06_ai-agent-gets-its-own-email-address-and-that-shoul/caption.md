💡 Your AI agent now has its own email address — and the internet is not ready for that.

Instinct, the viral AI assistant, just gave its agent a dedicated inbox. Not to receive your emails — to send them. The agent creates accounts, contacts businesses, files support tickets, and manages correspondence entirely on its own. It has a persistent identity on the internet. That's a different category of product than a chatbot.

This isn't a gimmick. An AI with its own email address can act like a person in systems that were built for people. Sign up for services, negotiate with support teams, follow up on your behalf while you sleep. The efficiency case is obvious. The accountability case is murkier.

Which is exactly why Sequoia just backed Cymphony — a startup building security infrastructure to track what AI agents are actually doing inside enterprises. The problem used to be theoretical. It isn't anymore.
