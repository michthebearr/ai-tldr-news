💡 Shopify just gave AI agents a credit card — and checkout will never be the same.

Shopify has expanded its WebMCP support to cover the entire checkout flow. That means browser-based AI agents can now update order details and complete purchases on your behalf. The buyer stays in control with authorization, but the infrastructure for fully autonomous shopping is now live across one of the world's largest merchant networks.

This isn't a prototype or a lab demo. This is scale. Shopify powers millions of stores — so the moment agentic commerce becomes native to that ecosystem, it becomes the default assumption for how online shopping works.

Your AI assistant doesn't just find the best deal anymore. It buys it.
