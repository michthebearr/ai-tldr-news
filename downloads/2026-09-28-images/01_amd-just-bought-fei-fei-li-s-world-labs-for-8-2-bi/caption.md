💰 AMD just paid $8.2 billion to stop being the chip company that almost matters.

The deal: AMD is acquiring World Labs — the spatial intelligence startup founded by AI icon Fei-Fei Li — for $8.2 billion. Li isn't just selling her company. She's joining AMD as EVP and Chief Scientist, which means AMD didn't just buy a startup. It bought a legend.

This is AMD drawing a line in the sand. For years, Nvidia has owned the full AI stack — hardware, software, research credibility, developer loyalty. AMD competes on chips, but keeps losing the narrative. Landing Fei-Fei Li, the researcher behind ImageNet who helped kickstart the modern deep learning era, is the kind of move that changes how the industry perceives you overnight.

The real play here isn't spatial intelligence. It's legitimacy.
