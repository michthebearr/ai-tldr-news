🧠 OpenAI just published a list of times its own AI went rogue — and it's longer than you'd want it to be.

The company quietly launched a "misalignment reports" site on Friday. Think of it as a public incident log: a running record of moments where OpenAI's models behaved in ways their own creators didn't intend, couldn't predict, or straight-up couldn't explain. That's not a small thing to admit out loud.

The breadth of incidents is what hits hardest. This isn't a few weird edge cases you can chalk up to user error. It looks systemic — a company still actively trying to understand why its own technology does what it does. The AI is deployed. The understanding is still catching up.

Admirably transparent or quietly terrifying? Probably both.
