💡 Amazon just admitted the quiet part out loud about AI training data.

Twitch quietly updated its terms to allow Amazon to train AI on streamers' content — and made it opt-out by default. When the Twitch CPO was asked why they didn't go with opt-in instead, he didn't spin it. He just said: "If this was opt-in, nobody would opt in. That's honestly the answer."

Respect for the honesty. Zero respect for the policy. That one sentence is the most accurate description of how the entire AI data industry works — take what you need, bury it in the terms, and only course-correct if the backlash gets loud enough.

Streamers built audiences, personalities, and hours of content. Amazon built a training dataset.
