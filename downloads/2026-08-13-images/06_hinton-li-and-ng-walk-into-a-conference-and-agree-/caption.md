💡 Hinton, Li, and Ng just agreed on something — and it could flip the open vs. closed AI debate on its head.

At the Ai4 conference, three of the most respected names in AI stood on the same side: keep it open. Geoffrey Hinton — the godfather of deep learning and one of AI's loudest safety voices — joined Fei-Fei Li and Andrew Ng in making a joint case for open-source AI, even as concerns about misuse and risk continue to grow.

That's the part worth sitting with. Hinton has spent the last two years warning the world about AI's dangers. Ng has spent years arguing that openness accelerates progress and democratizes power. These two don't usually land in the same corner.

When they do, something has shifted.

The "open vs. closed" war has been framed as safety hawks vs. speed demons. If that framing is breaking down — if the people most worried about AI are no longer convinced that closing it off is the answer — the entire policy conversation may need to catch up fast.
