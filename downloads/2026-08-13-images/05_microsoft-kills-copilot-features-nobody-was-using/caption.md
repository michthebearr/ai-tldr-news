💡 Microsoft just quietly admitted its Copilot launch was a mess.

The company is merging its consumer and business Copilot apps into one and cutting AI-generated podcasts, Group Chats, Deep Research, and its "Mico" character. If you don't recognize half of those features — that's kind of the point.

This is a controlled retreat. Microsoft shipped aggressively, saw what didn't stick, and is now cleaning house before the clutter becomes a reputation problem. The "move fast and figure it out later" era of Copilot is over.

The real question now: what's actually driving enterprise adoption — and is the stuff that survived the cut good enough to answer it?
