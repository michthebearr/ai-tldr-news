💡 Cognition just raised $1B — and is already back asking for $40B more.

The AI coding startup closed a massive round at a $26B valuation just months ago. Now it's reportedly in talks to raise again, this time at $40B. That's a 54% valuation jump in under a year, for a company most people outside of AI Twitter still can't explain at a dinner party.

The pitch is that AI coding agents — software that can write, debug, and ship code autonomously — are going to eat software development whole. If that's true, $40B might actually look cheap in hindsight. Cognition's product, Devin, was one of the first agents to make that case in a way that turned heads.

But here's the uncomfortable question: either AI coding is genuinely one of the most valuable categories ever built, or we're watching 2021-style mania in real time — just with better PR.
