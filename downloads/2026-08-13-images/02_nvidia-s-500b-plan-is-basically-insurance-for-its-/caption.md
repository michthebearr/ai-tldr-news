💡 Nvidia doesn't just want to sell you chips — it wants to control the economy around them.

The company is reportedly plotting a $500B plan to keep AI infrastructure financing alive and well. The move would create structured lending systems that prop up demand for existing GPU hardware — even as that hardware ages and newer models hit the market.

Translation: Nvidia is engineering financial conditions so its chips never really lose value. It's not just a product strategy. It's market architecture.

If this works, Nvidia stops being a chipmaker and becomes something closer to a central bank for AI compute. That's a completely different kind of power.
