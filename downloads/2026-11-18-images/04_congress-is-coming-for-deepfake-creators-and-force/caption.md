💡 Congress is finally going after deepfake creators — and the platforms that have been protecting them.

A Senate Judiciary subcommittee held hearings this week targeting two interconnected problems: the rise of AI-generated deepfake harms and the forced arbitration clauses platforms use to keep victims out of court. Roblox got called out by name on the arbitration front. That's not a great look.

The proposed Defiance Act would strip platforms of that legal shield for specific online harms — including AI-generated content used to harass or exploit people. Right now, buried in most platform terms of service is language that essentially says: got hurt? Can't sue us. The Defiance Act would change that.

If it passes, this would be one of the first U.S. laws with real enforcement power against deepfake creators and the companies that have quietly made it easy for them to operate consequence-free.
