💡 Trump just used a presidential state visit to plant America's AI flag in Southeast Asia.

During a trip to Manila, Trump announced a deal with the Philippines to build US-backed AI infrastructure across the country — data centers, chips, the full stack — under a venture called Pax Silica. It's dressed up as a bilateral tech partnership, but read the fine print: this is American companies expanding their AI footprint into a strategically located ally, with the White House providing the geopolitical cover.

The Philippines gets investment and infrastructure. The US gets another node in its global AI supply chain — one that sits uncomfortably close to China's backyard. That's not a coincidence.

The race to lock up AI infrastructure worldwide is moving fast, and the US is now openly using presidential diplomacy as a tool to win it.
