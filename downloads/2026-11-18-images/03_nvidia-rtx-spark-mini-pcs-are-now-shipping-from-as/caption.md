💡 Nvidia's pocket-sized AI supercomputer is now sitting on your desk — from three major PC makers.

The RTX Spark N1X chip combines a 6,144-core Blackwell GPU with a 20-core Grace CPU in a form factor small enough to fit in a mini PC. This week, Asus, Dell, and MSI started shipping desktops built around it. Acer, Gigabyte, HP, and Microsoft's Surface RTX Spark Dev Box are right behind them.

This matters because serious local AI compute has mostly lived in the cloud or inside expensive workstations. RTX Spark changes that equation — developers and prosumers can now run models locally, fast, without a rack of hardware or a monthly API bill.

The desktop AI era isn't coming. It's shipping.
