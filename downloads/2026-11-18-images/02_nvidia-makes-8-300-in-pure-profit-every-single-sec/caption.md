🏎️ Nvidia is making $8,300 in pure profit every single second — and the gap is only widening.

That's not a typo. While the rest of us are arguing about which AI model writes better emails, Nvidia is quietly printing money at a pace that makes most Fortune 500 companies look like lemonade stands. The Verge just dropped a data visualization on their financial ascent, and the revenue charts look less like a business and more like a SpaceX launch sequence.

The reason is simple: Nvidia owns the hardware layer of the AI boom. Every major model being trained, every inference query being run — almost all of it flows through Nvidia silicon. AMD and Intel are technically in this race, but calling it competitive at this point is generous. Nvidia isn't just ahead. It's in a different timezone.

The scariest part? This dominance has staying power. The more developers build on CUDA, the harder it gets to leave.
