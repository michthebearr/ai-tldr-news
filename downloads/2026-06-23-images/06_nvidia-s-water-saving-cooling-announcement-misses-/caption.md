💡 Nvidia just announced a "water-saving" cooling system — and quietly ignored AI's biggest water problem.

The new data center cooling tech does cut water usage inside the facility itself. That part is real. But it completely sidesteps where the majority of AI's water footprint actually lives: the fossil fuel power plants burning energy to run these data centers in the first place. Follow the water upstream and the win evaporates fast.

This is what selective sustainability math looks like. Optimize one visible metric, ignore the larger system, and let the press release do the rest. Nvidia isn't alone in this — it's becoming an industry playbook as AI energy demand scales into territory that's genuinely hard to spin.

The greenwashing battles in AI are just getting started.
