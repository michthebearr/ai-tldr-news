💡 Google DeepMind just wrote a $75M check to make AI filmmaking actually cool.

DeepMind is partnering with A24 — yes, the studio behind Everything Everywhere, Midsommar, and Hereditary — to build AI tools designed specifically for Hollywood production. This isn't a vague pilot program or a press release with no follow-through. It's real money behind a real creative partner.

And the choice of A24 matters enormously. This is a studio with serious artistic credibility and a fanbase that would riot if they thought AI was cheapening the work. If DeepMind can build tools that A24 filmmakers actually want to use, that's a proof of concept the rest of Hollywood can't ignore.

Most AI-in-entertainment deals trigger immediate backlash from the creative community. This one is structured to avoid that — and that might be the whole point.
