💡 AI agents that never stop — no prompts, no pauses, no off switch.

The next leap in agentic AI isn't smarter assistants. It's persistent agent loops — swarms of AI running continuously in the background, iterating and acting on their own without waiting for you to say "go" again. You authorize it once. Then it just... keeps going.

We've gone from chatbots to task-based agents to AI that has no natural endpoint. The "loop" architecture is exactly what it sounds like: agents working in cycles, indefinitely, making decisions and taking actions while you're doing something else entirely.

And that's where it gets complicated. The real question isn't whether these loops are powerful — it's how you turn them off. Once you hand over the keys, oversight becomes a much harder problem.
