💰 Nvidia basically poached Groq's best stuff without buying them — and Groq just raised $650M anyway.

Here's the wild backstory: Nvidia struck a $20B "not-acqui-hire" deal with Groq — licensing their talent and technology without ever actually acquiring the company. For most startups, that's a slow-motion death sentence. Groq is calling it a pivot.

The AI chipmaker just confirmed a $650M raise and is rebuilding its leadership team from scratch. The play? Double down on its neocloud business and own the inference speed narrative — positioning itself as the fastest alternative to Nvidia's own hardware in a market Nvidia otherwise dominates completely.

The fact that serious investors wrote $650M in checks after all of that chaos says something. The race for non-Nvidia inference infrastructure isn't over — it's just getting started.
