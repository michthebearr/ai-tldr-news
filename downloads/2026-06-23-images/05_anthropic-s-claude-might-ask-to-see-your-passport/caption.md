🧠 Anthropic wants to see your passport before you chat with Claude.

Buried in a quiet privacy policy update, Anthropic added language allowing Claude to request identity verification — think passport or driver's license — "in certain circumstances." They haven't explained exactly what triggers it. That vagueness is doing a lot of heavy lifting.

The leading theory is age-gating. As AI assistants get more capable — and more controversial — regulators are pushing harder for platforms to know who's on the other end of the conversation. Anthropic may be getting ahead of that pressure before it becomes a mandate.

But here's the bigger story: if the most trusted name in "safe AI" normalizes document-level ID checks, every other lab follows. The frictionless, anonymous chatbot era might be ending faster than anyone expected.
