💡 Google just quietly moved one of the most important people in AI off the day-to-day wheel.

Demis Hassabis — the co-founder of DeepMind and the closest thing the field has to a rockstar scientist — is being elevated to chair of Google DeepMind and chief scientist at Alphabet. Sounds like a promotion. It's more complicated than that.

Day-to-day control of the lab is passing to Koray Kavukcuoglu, DeepMind's former CTO. Hassabis stays visible, stays prestigious, but hands off the operational grind. Classic "kick upstairs" energy — and in a company moving as fast as Google right now, who's actually running the lab matters enormously.

This is the world's best-resourced AI lab in the middle of an arms race. The person steering it shapes what the whole field chases next.
