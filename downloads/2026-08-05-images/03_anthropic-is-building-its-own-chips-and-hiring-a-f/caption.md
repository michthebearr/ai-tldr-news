💡 Anthropic is building its own chips — and it's a bigger deal than it sounds.

The AI lab behind Claude is assembling an in-house silicon team to design custom hardware built specifically around how its models think and run. It's not a moonshot side project. It's a strategic bet on vertical control.

The playbook isn't new. Google has TPUs. Apple has its Neural Engine. Amazon has Trainium. Every major tech company that scaled compute-heavy workloads eventually realized: you can't rent your way to efficiency forever. Anthropic is arriving at that same conclusion — just later and with a lot more urgency.

At $10B+ in cloud compute deals, the economics are brutal. Custom silicon isn't just a performance upgrade — it's the only real lever Anthropic has to stop burning cash at the rate AI inference demands. Own the chip, own the cost structure.
