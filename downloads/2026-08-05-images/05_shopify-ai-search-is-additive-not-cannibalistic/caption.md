💡 AI search is killing publisher traffic — but it's tripling Shopify's orders.

While media companies are watching their referral traffic collapse, Shopify just dropped a very different data point: AI-driven traffic and orders to its merchant stores tripled year-over-year in Q2. Turns out AI assistants aren't just answering shopping questions and leaving people hanging — they're sending buyers directly to product pages.

That's a meaningful distinction. The "AI kills the web" narrative is real, but it's not the whole story. What AI search seems to be doing is filtering out browsing and delivering intent. Bad for content. Very good for commerce.

E-commerce might be the use case that finally justifies AI search — and it splits the web into two camps: those AI helps, and those it eats.
