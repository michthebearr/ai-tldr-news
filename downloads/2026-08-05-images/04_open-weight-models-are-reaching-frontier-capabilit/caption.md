💡 Open-weight AI models are hitting GPT-4-level performance — with none of the safety guardrails.

A new SaferAI report is sounding the alarm on Z.ai's GLM-5.2, an open-weight model approaching frontier capability while skipping the safety infrastructure that closed labs like OpenAI and Anthropic have spent years building in. The kicker: it's publicly available. Anyone can download it, strip what little protection exists, and do whatever they want with it.

This is the core tension the AI safety debate has been dancing around. Closed labs do the hard work of alignment, red-teaming, and guardrails — then an open-weight model matches their capability and makes all of that largely irrelevant. You can't patch a model that's already on a million hard drives.

Governance hasn't caught up. Not even close.
