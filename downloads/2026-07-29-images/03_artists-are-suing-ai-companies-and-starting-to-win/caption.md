💡 Artists are suing AI companies — and they're starting to win.

For years, AI firms scraped the internet like an all-you-can-eat buffet: books, illustrations, code, art — all folded into training datasets without permission or payment. Artists pushed back. Most people assumed the legal system would shrug.

It isn't shrugging. Authors, illustrators, and creators are filing suits against Google, Meta, Anthropic, and others — and early settlements and rulings are breaking their way. The courtroom is catching up to the technology faster than the industry expected.

This is the moment that matters. The legal framework around AI training data is being written right now, case by case. And the outcomes will decide whether the foundation of every major AI model is built on solid ground — or a copyright timebomb.
