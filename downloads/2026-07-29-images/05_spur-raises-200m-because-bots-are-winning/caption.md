💰 Bots are winning the internet — and one startup just raised $200M to fight back.

Spur Intelligence closed a $200 million round from Insight Partners this week. Their product? Technology that tells real humans from automated bots. That this is now a nine-figure business tells you everything about how bad the problem has gotten.

Here's the thing — bots aren't new. But AI-generated synthetic traffic is a different beast entirely. What used to require real infrastructure and effort now costs almost nothing to spin up at scale. Fake clicks, fake users, fake engagement — all of it suddenly cheap and convincing enough to fool legacy detection tools.

$200M in a single round suggests the market isn't just growing. It's outrunning every solution that came before it.
