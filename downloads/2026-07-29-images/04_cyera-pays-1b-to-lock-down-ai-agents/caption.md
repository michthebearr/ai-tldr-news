💡 AI agents are running loose inside enterprise systems — and someone just paid $1 billion to fix that.

Data security company Cyera is acquiring identity startup Oasis Security for $1B, its third acquisition this year. The target: the fast-growing, under-governed world of AI agents that are operating with real credentials, real access, and very little oversight inside corporate infrastructure.

This isn't a theoretical risk anymore. AI agents are being handed the keys to enterprise systems at scale — and most companies have no clear picture of what those agents are accessing, or what happens when something goes wrong. That's a massive attack surface, and the security industry is finally catching up.

A $1B deal sends one clear message: agent permissions are now a boardroom problem.
