🏎️ MIT built an AI that predicts disasters that have never happened before — and that's exactly the point.

Most weather forecasting tools learn from history. But climate change is quietly making history useless. A once-in-a-millennium flood can't be predicted by looking at past floods if it's never occurred in that region before.

MIT grad student Kai Chang and Professor Themis Sapsis built something different. Their AI doesn't train on past disaster data — it generates statistically plausible future extreme events from scratch, then maps them with probability estimates. It's essentially teaching a model to imagine catastrophes before they happen.

That's not a gimmick. It's infrastructure planning, disaster preparedness, and risk modeling for a world where the old playbook no longer applies.
