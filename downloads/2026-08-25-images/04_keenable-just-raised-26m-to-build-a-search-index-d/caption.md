💰 Someone just raised $26M to rebuild the internet's index — but this time, for AI agents, not you.

Keenable just came out of stealth with a $26 million seed round led by Accel. Their product: a brand-new web index built specifically for AI agents. Not for Google crawlers. Not for human readers. For the autonomous software that's increasingly doing the browsing on your behalf.

The problem they're solving is real. Today's web indexes were designed around how search engines serve people — blue links, page rankings, human-readable summaries. But AI agents don't work that way. They need to retrieve, reason, and act on information in a totally different format. Keenable is betting that gap is big enough to build a billion-dollar infrastructure company around.

And if they're right, the leverage is enormous. Whoever owns the index that AI agents call when they hit the web controls a fundamental layer of how the internet gets consumed. That's not a features bet — that's a plumbing bet.
