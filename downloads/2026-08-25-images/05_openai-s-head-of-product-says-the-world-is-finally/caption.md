🧠 OpenAI's head of product just said the quiet part out loud: the bottleneck isn't the AI anymore — it's us.

Thibault Sottiaux, OpenAI's head of product, sat down with TechCrunch for one of the more candid product-level interviews we've seen from the company. His core argument: after years of AI tools that promised a lot and delivered inconsistently, users and enterprises are finally ready to hand real, complex tasks to agents.

The capability gap has closed. The trust gap hasn't. Sottiaux says the hard problem now is UX — how do you build interfaces that let people delegate meaningfully without feeling like they've lost control? That's where OpenAI's product attention is actually pointed right now.

Not the model. The handoff.

It's a significant signal. When your head of product is talking about interface design and trust architecture instead of benchmark scores, it tells you exactly where the next competitive battleground is shaping up.
