🧠 OpenAI just built the fastest AI inference chip on the planet — and it changes everything.

Meet Jalapeño. OpenAI's custom silicon just topped SemiAnalysis' InferenceX benchmark on both tokens-per-user and throughput-per-kilowatt. That's not a minor win. Those two metrics almost never move together — faster throughput typically means worse latency per user. Jalapeño does both.

Hardware VP Richard Ho says the chip cracks the classic latency-vs-throughput trade-off that's defined AI inference for years. That's a big claim. The benchmark numbers say it's not a bluff.

Here's why this keeps Nvidia up at night: if OpenAI can run inference faster and cheaper than anyone buying off-the-shelf GPUs, it has a structural advantage that compounds. Better products. Lower costs. A moat that gets wider every time a competitor has to wait in Nvidia's order queue.

OpenAI isn't just an AI lab anymore. It's a chip company, a cloud, and a model shop — all in one. That's a very different animal.
