🏎️ Google made an AI ad so cringe, even AI fans are wincing.

For the Fourth of July, Google dropped a commercial imagining the Founding Fathers using Google Workspace and Gemini to draft the Declaration of Independence. Yes, really. Ben Franklin presumably on a Chromebook. Thomas Jefferson asking Gemini to clean up his prose.

The Verge called it "infuriating" and "cringeworthy." The internet largely agreed. And the uncomfortable truth is — this isn't some scrappy startup missing the mark. This is Google. The company with more AI firepower than almost anyone on the planet, and it still can't figure out how to make people feel good about using it.

That's the real story. When the industry's loudest AI evangelist resorts to patriotic kitsch to sell its own product, the perception gap isn't a marketing problem. It's a warning sign.
