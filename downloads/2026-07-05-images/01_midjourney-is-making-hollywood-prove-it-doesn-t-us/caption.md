💡 Midjourney is tired of being sued — so now it's asking Hollywood a very uncomfortable question.

Three major studios are going after Midjourney for copyright infringement. Standard story. Except Midjourney just flipped the script by demanding the studios prove they don't use AI themselves.

It's a classic legal "you too" move — and it's sharp. If any of these studios are quietly running AI tools internally while publicly crusading against AI companies in court, their moral high ground evaporates fast. Suddenly the narrative shifts from "Hollywood vs. the machines" to "Hollywood vs. the machines they also use."

The implications go way beyond this one case. A ruling or disclosure that catches studios in that contradiction could reshape how AI copyright litigation plays out across the entire industry.
