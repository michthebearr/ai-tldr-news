🔐 The biggest AI labs just got left out of an AI security alliance — and that's the whole story.

Nvidia, Microsoft, IBM, and SpaceX have formed the Open Secure AI Alliance, a new coalition building open-source tools to defend against threats from frontier AI models. The founding moment: the Hugging Face breach, which exposed just how vulnerable the AI infrastructure layer really is.

But look at who's missing. OpenAI, Google, Anthropic — the labs actually building the most powerful frontier models — aren't in the room. For an alliance explicitly designed to defend against frontier AI risks, that's a strange guest list.

This isn't just a technical initiative. It's a political one. The industry is fracturing over who gets to write the rules on AI safety — and this alliance just drew a very visible line between the open-source camp and the closed-model giants.
