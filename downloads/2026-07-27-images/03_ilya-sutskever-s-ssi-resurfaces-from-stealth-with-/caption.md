🏎️ Ilya Sutskever just broke two years of silence — and he brought Nvidia with him.

Safe Superintelligence has been one of the most mysterious labs in AI. Founded in 2024 by former OpenAI chief scientist Ilya Sutskever, SSI went almost completely dark after launch — no demos, no blog posts, no product teasers. Just vibes and a very loaded name.

Now they've surfaced with a long-term compute partnership with Nvidia. That's not a small move. Nvidia infrastructure at scale means SSI is building something real, and building it fast. For a lab that's made silence its entire brand, this is the loudest thing they could have done short of shipping a model.

The timing is brutal for OpenAI. As its safety credibility takes hits in the press, SSI is quietly positioning itself as the lab that actually means it — now with the compute to back it up.
