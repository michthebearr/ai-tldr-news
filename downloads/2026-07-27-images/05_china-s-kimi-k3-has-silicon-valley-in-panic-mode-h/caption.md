💡 China just open-sourced a frontier AI model — and it might be the most aggressive move in the global AI race yet.

Moonshot AI dropped Kimi K3 this week, a Chinese model that's reportedly going toe-to-toe with the best systems Silicon Valley has to offer. Investors are rattled. Technologists are paying close attention. And for good reason.

Here's the real play: China's top labs aren't just building competitive models — they're giving them away as open weights. Free. That's not charity. That's a calculated move to gut the pricing power of US closed-source competitors, flood global markets with Chinese AI infrastructure, and make their stack the default before anyone can stop it.

US companies built moats around proprietary models. China just decided to commoditize the entire market.

When open source is also a geopolitical weapon, the rules of competition change completely — and right now, American AI labs don't have a clean counter-move.
