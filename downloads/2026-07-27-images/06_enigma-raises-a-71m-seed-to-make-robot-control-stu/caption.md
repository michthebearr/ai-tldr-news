🤖 A robotics startup just raised $71M at seed stage — and they haven't even shipped yet.

Enigma closed one of the largest seed rounds in robotics history this week, backed by Index Ventures, Ribbit Capital, and Sarah Guo's Conviction Partners. Their bet: the next big unlock in robotics isn't the hardware — it's the interface.

The pitch is dead simple. Operating a robot should feel like turning a volume knob, not writing G-code. Right now, deploying industrial robots requires specialists, long setup times, and a tolerance for pain. Enigma wants to make it something anyone can do.

That's the real bottleneck nobody talks about. The robots are getting good enough. The humans controlling them aren't set up to keep pace.
