💡 Google's AI Overviews now appear in nearly half of all searches — and publishers are quietly panicking.

New data shows AI-generated answer summaries are showing up in 43% of Google searches, a sharp jump from just a year ago. That means almost half the time someone searches for something, Google hands them the answer before they ever think about clicking a link.

This isn't a test anymore. It's the default. And the web's entire traffic economy — built on the assumption that people would click through to read things — is quietly being dismantled query by query.

Publishers are getting less referral traffic. News sites, blogs, how-to guides — all watching their Google-driven visits erode while the answer to the question they wrote lives inside a gray AI summary box at the top of the page.

The search bar didn't disappear. It just stopped sending people anywhere.
