💡 AI is writing math proofs now — and nobody's checking if they're actually true.

Axiom Math just did something quietly massive: they formally verified the entire Stacks Project, one of the largest and most complex libraries in algebraic geometry, using AI agents built specifically for mathematical proof verification. That's not a small feat. The Stacks Project took humans decades to build.

Their thesis is blunt — AI is already flooding mathematics with generated proofs, and most of them are going unverified. That's not a quirk. That's a crisis. If the foundations of mathematical knowledge start getting corrupted by confident-sounding but wrong AI output, the downstream damage to science, cryptography, and research could be enormous.

Axiom is betting there's a company worth building in the trust layer between AI and math. Formal verification isn't new — but doing it at this scale, with AI agents, is.

The math has to be right. Someone has to check.
