🧠 Claude just got access to your bank account — and that changes everything.

Anthropic launched a Personal Finance feature that connects Claude directly to your bank and credit card accounts through Plaid. Ask it plain questions, get real answers — where your money went, how your spending breaks down, why your budget looks the way it does. It's your financial life, but now with an AI that can actually read it.

This is a genuinely big deal. Most AI tools operate on information you give them. This one pulls live data from one of the most sensitive corners of your life — without you having to paste in a spreadsheet.

The utility is obvious. The privacy stakes are equally obvious.
