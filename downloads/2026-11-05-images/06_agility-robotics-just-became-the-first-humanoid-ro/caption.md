🤖 Humanoid robots just got their first publicly traded company — and the whole sector will never be valued the same way again.

Agility Robotics, maker of the Digit bipedal robot, debuted on the Nasdaq today as the first pure-play humanoid robotics company on a public exchange. That's not a minor footnote — that's a landmark. Investors now have a direct, liquid bet on the humanoid robot race for the first time ever.

The timing is loaded. Figure, 1X, Tesla's Optimus team — all still private, all moving fast. Agility just handed Wall Street a pricing anchor for the entire category. Every valuation conversation in this space now starts with Agility's market cap.

The catch: public markets are quarterly. The pressure to show real operational progress on Digit — not just demos — just got very real, very fast.
