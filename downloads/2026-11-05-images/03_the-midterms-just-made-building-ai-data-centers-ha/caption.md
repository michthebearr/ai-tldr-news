💡 Building AI's future just got a lot harder — and it has nothing to do with the models.

Meta is raising the alarm after Tuesday's midterms showed something unexpected: opposition to AI data centers isn't red or blue. It's local. Communities across the country voted against — or elected candidates hostile to — the massive infrastructure projects that Big Tech needs to scale.

This matters more than it sounds. AI scaling laws only work if you can actually build. Data centers aren't optional extras — they're the physical backbone of every model, every product, every roadmap. Slow the construction, and you slow everything downstream.

NIMBYism just became an AI problem.
