🧠 OpenAI is eyeing the public markets — and it could happen as soon as 2027.

Greg Brockman confirmed this week that an IPO is on the table, with the company pointing to accelerating enterprise revenue and what he described as a genuine business turnaround. For a company that's been burning cash at an almost incomprehensible rate, that's a bold claim — but the numbers are apparently starting to back it up.

Here's why this is a bigger deal than a typical tech listing: an OpenAI IPO would force the company to open its books to the public for the first time ever. No more vague revenue hints and hand-picked press briefings. Real scrutiny.

And whatever valuation Wall Street lands on won't just matter for OpenAI — it becomes the benchmark for the entire AI industry.
