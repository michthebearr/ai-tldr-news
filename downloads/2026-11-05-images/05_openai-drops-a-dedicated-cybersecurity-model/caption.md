🔐 OpenAI just built an AI model specifically for cybersecurity — and not everyone's convinced that's a good thing.

The new model is tuned for security tasks: analyzing vulnerabilities, reasoning through attack chains, supporting threat detection. It's OpenAI planting a flag in one of the fastest-moving corners of AI application, and the technical capability is real.

The problem is the obvious one. A model that can reason about how systems break is useful to defenders and attackers in equal measure. OpenAI hasn't landed a satisfying answer to that tension yet — and launching the product doesn't make the question go away.

Specialized AI from a frontier lab hits different than a generic model. The stakes are higher, the misuse surface is wider, and "we have safety guidelines" isn't the reassurance it used to be.
