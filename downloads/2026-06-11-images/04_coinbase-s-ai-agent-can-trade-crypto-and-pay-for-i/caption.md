💡 Coinbase just built an AI agent that pays its own bills — and it changes what "autonomous" actually means.

The agent uses Coinbase's x402 protocol to trade crypto assets and independently purchase access to premium data and APIs. No human approving invoices. No billing friction. It acquires what it needs, when it needs it, and keeps moving.

That's the real flex here. We've been talking about autonomous AI agents for years, but most of them still hit a wall the moment they need to access a paid resource. Coinbase just removed that wall by giving agents a native way to spend crypto in real time — essentially funding their own operations mid-task.

An AI that can pay for its own research is a fundamentally different kind of tool.

This is also Coinbase making its clearest argument yet for why AI and crypto belong together. Micropayments at machine speed, no middlemen, no approval chains. If this model catches on, the economic layer underneath autonomous AI starts looking a lot like a blockchain.
