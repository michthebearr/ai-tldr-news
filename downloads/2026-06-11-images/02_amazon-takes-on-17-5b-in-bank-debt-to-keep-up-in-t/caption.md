💡 Amazon just borrowed $17.5 billion from banks — and it's barely newsworthy anymore.

Fresh off a bond sale, Amazon is back at the table, this time pulling $17.5B in bank debt to fund its AI infrastructure push. Data centers, chips, power — the buildout is relentless, and the bill keeps growing.

Here's what makes this moment worth paying attention to: when the world's largest cloud provider has to borrow at this pace just to stay in the race, it tells you something about how expensive this race actually is.

Most companies couldn't dream of spending at this scale. For Amazon, it's a Tuesday.
