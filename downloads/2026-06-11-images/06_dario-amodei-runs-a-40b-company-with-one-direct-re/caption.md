💡 Dario Amodei runs a $40 billion company and has exactly one direct report.

That's not a typo. The CEO of Anthropic — one of the most powerful AI labs on the planet — has deliberately built an org structure so flat it would make most McKinsey consultants physically ill.

The logic is intentional. Amodei wants to stay close to the technical work, not trapped in management layers reviewing PowerPoints about the work. In a field where the difference between a good model and a great one lives in the details, that proximity might be the whole game.

Most companies this size are already drowning in org charts. Anthropic is betting the opposite approach wins.
