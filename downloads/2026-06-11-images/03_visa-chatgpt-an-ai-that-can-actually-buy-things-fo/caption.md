🧠 Visa just gave ChatGPT a credit card — and it can use it without asking you first.

Visa has plugged its payment infrastructure directly into ChatGPT, letting AI agents move from "here's what you should buy" to "done, it's ordered" with zero human involvement. The system evaluates merchants, reads your prompt, and fires Visa's payment rails autonomously. No cart. No checkout page. No final click.

That last part matters more than it sounds. The "final click" was the last moment a human was in the loop. It was friction, sure — but it was also a checkpoint. A pause. A chance to say no. That's gone now.

This is the moment agentic AI stops being a party trick and starts moving real money. The purchase funnel just lost its last human checkpoint — and every retailer, bank, and regulator on the planet should be paying attention.
