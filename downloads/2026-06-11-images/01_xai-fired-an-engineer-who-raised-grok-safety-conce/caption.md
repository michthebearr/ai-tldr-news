💰 xAI fired an engineer who raised Grok safety concerns — right before cashing out.

A former xAI engineer is suing both xAI and SpaceX, claiming he was let go after flagging AI safety issues with Grok. The timing is what makes this story sting: the firing allegedly happened just days before SpaceX's massive IPO, when any bad press about safety culture could have tanked the moment.

The lawsuit calls it retaliation. And if that's true, the implication is hard to ignore — that a safety concern was quietly buried to protect a stock price. AI governance advocates have been warning about exactly this scenario for years: companies treating safety as a PR risk rather than a responsibility.

We've seen whistleblower stories before. Rarely this close to a billion-dollar liquidity event.
