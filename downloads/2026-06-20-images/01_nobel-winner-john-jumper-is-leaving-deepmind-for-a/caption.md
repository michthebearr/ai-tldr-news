💡 The man who won a Nobel Prize for AI just left Google for its biggest rival.

John Jumper — the researcher behind AlphaFold, the protein-folding breakthrough that genuinely changed biology — is leaving DeepMind to join Anthropic. And he's not the only one. TechCrunch reports he's part of a growing wave of high-profile exits out of Google's flagship AI lab.

That's a brutal headline for DeepMind. AlphaFold is the kind of achievement labs frame on the wall. It's the proof point Google trots out whenever someone asks if AI is doing anything real. Losing the Nobel laureate behind it isn't just a personnel move — it's a narrative hit.

When researchers at that level start choosing your competitor, the talent war stops being about comp packages and prestige. It's about where the most interesting problems are being solved — and right now, that answer seems to keep pointing away from Google.
