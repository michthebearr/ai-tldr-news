🤖 The guy who fixed video forever is now fixing robotics infrastructure.

Jean-Baptiste Kempf built VLC to play any video file, on any device, without friction. Now his new company, Kyber, is taking that same philosophy to remote robots, drones, and industrial hardware — building the real-time infrastructure layer that lets you actually control them reliably.

The problem isn't the robots themselves. It's the plumbing. The space is fragmented, latency-plagued, and held together with duct tape. Kempf is treating it exactly like he treated incompatible video formats in 2001 — open-source-first, infrastructure-obsessed, deeply unsexy, and absolutely necessary.

The most important builders in tech are often the ones solving problems nobody wants to talk about.
