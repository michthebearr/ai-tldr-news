💡 The government wants to lock down Anthropic's new cybersecurity AI. History says good luck with that.

Anthropic built Mythos, a model purpose-built for cybersecurity applications. Now there's serious pressure to slap export controls on it — the idea being that if adversaries can't get it, we're safer. Sounds reasonable. Except TechCrunch just traced 30 years of the same playbook, and the results are pretty embarrassing.

PGP encryption. Commercial spyware. Dual-use tech after dual-use tech. Every time, the pattern is identical: controls get imposed, the technology spreads anyway through other channels, and the people who actually feel the pain are legitimate researchers, businesses, and allies — not the adversaries the rules were meant to stop. There's no serious evidence AI is different enough to break that streak.

The real danger here isn't Mythos getting out. It's policymakers locking in a broken framework before the AI industry pushes back hard enough to stop it.
