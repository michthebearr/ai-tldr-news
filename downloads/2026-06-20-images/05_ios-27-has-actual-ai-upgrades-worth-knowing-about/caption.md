💡 iOS 27 just dropped, and the AI upgrades are actually worth paying attention to.

Apple shipped iOS 27 this week with a meaningful round of Apple Intelligence and Siri improvements. Nothing that's going to make your jaw drop — but that's never really been Apple's game.

The real story isn't any single feature. It's the distribution. Within months, whatever AI Apple bakes into an update lands in billions of pockets worldwide. No other company in the world can say that. Incremental beats dramatic when your install base is that size.

TechCrunch notes the headline AI features come bundled with quieter quality-of-life additions that never make it to the keynote stage — the kind of stuff that just makes the phone feel smarter over time. That's the Apple playbook, and it still works.

Slow and steady doesn't just win the race. It shapes the entire track.
