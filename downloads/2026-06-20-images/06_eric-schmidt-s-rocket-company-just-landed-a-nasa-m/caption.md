💡 Eric Schmidt just got NASA to hand him a Mars mission.

Relativity Space — the rocket startup now backed and led by former Google CEO Eric Schmidt — was selected by NASA to launch the Aeolus payload to Mars in 2028. And this isn't a simple rideshare deal. Relativity is providing the spacecraft, the rocket, and cruise operations end-to-end. That's a full mission contract, not a taxi ticket.

For a company that's had a rocky road to orbit, this is a serious vote of confidence. NASA is essentially betting that a private venture can handle deep-space logistics that used to live entirely inside government agencies.

That's the bigger story here. NASA isn't just hiring private companies to cut costs anymore — it's handing them the keys to interplanetary exploration. Whoever builds that infrastructure now shapes how humanity expands into the solar system for decades.

Schmidt isn't just in the AI race. He's playing for Mars.
