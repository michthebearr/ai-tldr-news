🤖 Japan just handed a taxi app $570M to replace its own drivers.

Go, Japan's dominant ride-hailing platform, just pulled off the country's biggest IPO of 2026 — raising ¥88.6 billion to go all-in on robotaxis. Not to grow the business they already have. To transform it entirely.

Japan's driver shortage isn't a looming problem — it's already here. An aging population and shrinking workforce have made human-operated taxis increasingly unsustainable. That's exactly what makes this moment different: Go isn't betting on autonomy because it sounds exciting. It's betting on it because the economics actually work right now.

Most mobility companies use public markets to scale what's already working. Go used them to fund what's coming next.
