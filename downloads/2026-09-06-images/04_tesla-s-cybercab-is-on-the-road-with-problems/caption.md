🔐 Tesla's Cybercab is finally on public roads — and it's already having a moment.

The robotaxi Elon Musk has been promising for years has officially launched. No steering wheel. No LiDAR. Just Tesla's vision-only system and a whole lot riding on it working exactly as advertised.

It isn't — at least not yet. Details are still emerging, but TechCrunch is reporting the rollout has hit problems out of the gate. For a vehicle Musk has positioned as the centerpiece of Tesla's entire autonomous future, a rocky debut is about the worst way to start.

This matters beyond the headlines. Tesla's bet against LiDAR has always been the controversial core of its self-driving strategy. Every stumble the Cybercab makes in the real world hands ammunition to the critics — and gives regulators another reason to slow things down.
