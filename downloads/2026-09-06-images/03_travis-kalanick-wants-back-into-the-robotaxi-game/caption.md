🤖 Travis Kalanick wants back in — and he's aiming straight at the robotaxi wars.

Uber's controversial co-founder is reportedly building a new company called Atoms, with his sights set on the autonomous vehicle market. Kalanick is calling it "unfinished business" — a pointed reference to Uber selling off its self-driving unit, ATG, to Aurora in 2020 when the money got tight.

Details are sparse. No confirmed funding, no clear tech stack. But the direction is obvious, and so is the subtext: this is a man who helped build the playbook for ride-hailing at scale and watched someone else finish the race.

Here's the real question — in a market already dominated by Waymo, Tesla, and players with billion-dollar runways, what does Kalanick actually bring? If there's an edge, it's probably not the technology. It's knowing how to navigate regulators, cities, and chaos better than anyone else in the room.
