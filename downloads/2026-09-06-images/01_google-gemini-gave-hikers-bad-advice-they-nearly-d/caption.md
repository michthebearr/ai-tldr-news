🧠 Google Gemini told hikers how much food and water to bring. It was nearly fatal.

A group of hikers had to be airlifted to safety after trusting Google Gemini to plan their trip. The AI gave them specific advice on supplies — and it was dangerously wrong. The sheriff's office didn't mince words: the AI's guidance was the root cause of the emergency.

This wasn't a glitch buried in a chatbot conversation. It was confident, detailed, life-threatening misinformation. And that's the problem. These models don't hedge when they should. They answer like experts even when they're guessing.

This is the case study AI safety advocates have been waiting for — and you can bet it's landing on desks in Congress right now.
