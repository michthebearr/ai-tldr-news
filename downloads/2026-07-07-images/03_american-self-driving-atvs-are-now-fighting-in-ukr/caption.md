💡 American self-driving ATVs are now operating in active combat — and nobody really planned for this moment.

Forterra has deployed over 100 autonomous ground vehicles in Ukraine, making them the first confirmed American autonomous vehicles operating in a live wartime conflict. Not a demo. Not a controlled test environment. Real war, real conditions.

This is the line a lot of people in policy and ethics have been nervously watching. Autonomous vehicles were supposed to move slowly — from warehouses, to highways, to maybe someday military logistics. Instead, they just skipped several steps at once.

The technology didn't wait for the frameworks to catch up.
