💡 SK Hynix is about to go public in the US — and AI is the entire reason why.

The South Korean memory chip giant behind Nvidia's HBM chips is expected to IPO on a US exchange this Friday in a multibillion-dollar listing. If you've been following the AI boom at all, you know HBM — high bandwidth memory — is the unsung hero inside every major AI data center pushing models like GPT and Gemini.

SK Hynix didn't just benefit from the AI wave. It rode it to Wall Street. Demand for HBM exploded as hyperscalers raced to build out AI infrastructure, and Hynix was sitting on exactly the chips everyone needed. Now Wall Street wants a slice.

This IPO is a signal, not just a stock event. Every layer of the AI stack — from software to silicon to the memory that makes it all run — is now a target for investors.
