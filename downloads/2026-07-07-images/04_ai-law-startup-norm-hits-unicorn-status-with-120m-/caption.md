💡 Legal AI just got its first unicorn — and it's coming for the billable hour.

Norm, an AI-native legal startup, closed a $120M Series C led by Khosla Ventures this week, vaulting its valuation to $1.2 billion. The company builds AI-driven workflows for a legal industry still running on paper, PDFs, and eye-watering hourly rates.

Law is one of the last major professional services verticals where no AI startup has planted a definitive flag. Medicine has its contenders. Finance has its disruptors. Legal has been stubbornly resistant — expensive, complex, and deeply relationship-driven.

Norm is betting that's exactly why the opportunity is massive.
