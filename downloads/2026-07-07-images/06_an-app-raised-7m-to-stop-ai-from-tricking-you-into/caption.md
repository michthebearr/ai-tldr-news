💰 Someone just raised $7M because AI can now fake your kid's voice well enough to convince you they've been kidnapped.

Savi just launched on iOS and Android with $7 million in seed funding — and its entire reason for existing is one of the most disturbing use cases AI has unlocked: voice cloning scams. We're talking about criminals who can clone a loved one's voice in seconds and call you mid-afternoon demanding ransom money while "your child" screams in the background.

This isn't theoretical. These scams are already happening and already working. People have wired real money to criminals who never touched their kids — because the voice on the other end sounded exactly right. Savi is the first consumer product built specifically to detect and defend against this in real time.

The fact that this app exists tells you more about the current AI threat landscape than any policy paper will.
