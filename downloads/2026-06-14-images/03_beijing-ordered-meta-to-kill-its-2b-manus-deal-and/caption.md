💡 Beijing just killed a $2 billion Meta deal — and Meta is going along with it.

Meta is unwinding its acquisition of Manus, the Chinese AI agent startup, after Beijing stepped in and demanded the deal be reversed. That's not a negotiation. That's a veto. From a foreign government. Over a U.S. tech acquisition.

This is the part that should make every investor and acquirer in the AI space stop scrolling. China just demonstrated it has regulatory reach that extends beyond its own borders — and that even a company the size of Meta has limited options when Beijing says no. The $2 billion is almost secondary to the precedent being set here.

The deeper issue: when you acquire AI talent and IP with roots in China, you may not actually be acquiring clean ownership of anything. Beijing gets a say. And now everyone knows it.
