💡 Anthropic flipped a switch — and India suddenly had no lights.

When Anthropic suspended access to its newest models, Indian developers didn't just lose a tool. They lost infrastructure. Engineers mid-build, startups in production, and tech teams with live dependencies were locked out overnight, with no warning and no fallback. It was a reminder of something most people knew but hadn't felt yet: you don't own what you rent from someone else's server.

The episode is being called India's "Huawei moment" for AI. The same way U.S. chip restrictions exposed how deeply China's telecom ambitions depended on American hardware, this suspension exposed how deeply India's AI ambitions depend on American foundation models. The debate about AI sovereignty just stopped being theoretical.

The uncomfortable truth: every country building a digital future on U.S.-controlled AI infrastructure is one policy decision away from this exact problem.
