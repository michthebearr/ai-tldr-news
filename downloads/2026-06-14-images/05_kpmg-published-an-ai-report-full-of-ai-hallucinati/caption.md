💡 KPMG wrote a report about AI hallucinations — using AI — and the report hallucinated.

One of the world's most powerful consulting firms just published, then quietly pulled, an enterprise AI report riddled with fabricated data. The culprit? The very AI tools the report was supposed to be analyzing.

This is a spectacular own goal. KPMG has armies of analysts, editors, and reviewers. If a hallucinated AI output can make it through that process — on a document literally about AI risks — it tells you everything about where enterprise governance actually stands right now.

The firm hasn't explained what was wrong or how it slipped through. Which might be the most damning part.
