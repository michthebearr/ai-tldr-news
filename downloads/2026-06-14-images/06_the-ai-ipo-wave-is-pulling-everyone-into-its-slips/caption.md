💰 The AI IPO wave is rising — and every startup wants to surf it, whether they belong on the board or not.

Several high-profile AI companies are queuing up to go public, and the gravitational pull is real. Founders across the startup ecosystem — many with only a loose connection to AI — are openly timing their IPOs to catch the momentum before the window shuts. One founder literally invoked "the SpaceX wave." That tells you everything about the energy in the room right now.

This is how bull markets work. One sector runs hot, and suddenly everyone's an AI company. The pitch decks get retrofitted, the press releases lean harder on the buzzwords, and retail investors end up holding the bag when the story stops matching the numbers.

The froth is the warning sign.
