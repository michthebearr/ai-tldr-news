💡 Anthropic's biggest investor may have just triggered its biggest crackdown.

Amazon CEO Andy Jassy reportedly took his company's own security research straight to the White House — research showing Anthropic's Fable 5 model could be prompted into surfacing dangerous information. That conversation didn't stay in a conference room. It became the export control directive that forced Anthropic to pull worldwide access to both Fable 5 and Mythos 5.

One of Anthropic's largest financial backers essentially set off the alarm that locked Anthropic's own users out.

This is where AI safety gets complicated. We're no longer talking about researchers writing cautionary blog posts or companies self-regulating through press releases. We're watching billion-dollar investors shape binding government action in real time — and the line between "safety concern" and "competitive leverage" has never been blurrier.
