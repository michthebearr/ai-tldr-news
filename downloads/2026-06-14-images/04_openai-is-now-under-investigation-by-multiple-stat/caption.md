💰 OpenAI is under investigation by multiple state attorneys general — and the timing couldn't be worse.

A coalition of state AGs is now probing OpenAI across a surprisingly wide range of issues: ad practices, health data handling, and broader consumer protection concerns. The scope of this isn't narrow. This looks like a coordinated sweep designed to find something — anything — that sticks.

Here's why that matters. Federal AI regulation is completely gridlocked, so state attorneys general are stepping into the vacuum. A multi-state investigation has real teeth — it can demand documents, depose executives, and generate the kind of headlines that spook IPO investors. OpenAI is already in the middle of a messy for-profit restructuring. Add a regulatory pile-on and you've got a company fighting on every front simultaneously.

Washington may be slow. The states are not.
