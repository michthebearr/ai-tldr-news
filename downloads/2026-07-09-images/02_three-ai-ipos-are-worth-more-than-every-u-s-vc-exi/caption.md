💰 Three AI companies are about to be worth more than every U.S. startup exit combined — for the last 25 years.

Anthropic, OpenAI, and SpaceX are tracking toward IPOs that would collectively eclipse the total value of all U.S. venture-backed exits since 2000. Not in a single sector. Not in a single decade. All of them. Combined.

Let that number breathe for a second.

This isn't a bubble story — bubbles spread value thin and pop. This is the opposite: a historic concentration of wealth into a handful of companies moving faster than any regulatory framework, investor playbook, or competitor can keep up with. The rules of venture capital were written for a different era. That era is over.

For founders, investors, and regulators, the takeaway is the same: the game has changed, and most people are still playing by the old rules.
