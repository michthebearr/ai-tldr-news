📱 Meta has been training its AI on your Instagram photos — and you probably never knew.

This week it came out that Meta quietly launched an AI image generator built on public Instagram content. No announcement. No consent pop-up. Just a buried opt-out setting most users have never seen — and a massive training dataset that's been growing ever since.

This is the move. Launch the product, collect the data, and make opting out just inconvenient enough that most people won't bother. By the time anyone notices, hundreds of millions of photos are already in the pipeline.

Your brunch shots. Your travel photos. Your face. All potentially fuel for Meta's models.
