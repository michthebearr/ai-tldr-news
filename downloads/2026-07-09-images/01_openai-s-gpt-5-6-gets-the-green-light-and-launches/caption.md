🧠 GPT-5.6 is live — and it needed government sign-off to get there.

After weeks stuck in regulatory limbo, the Trump administration cleared OpenAI's latest model for public release. And OpenAI, true to form, didn't just drop the model — they dropped an agent too. "ChatGPT Work" is a new enterprise-focused AI agent launching alongside GPT-5.6, aimed squarely at corporate users who want AI embedded into their workflows.

Sam Altman called GPT-5.6 "the best model we have ever produced." He says that every time. Every. Single. Time.

But here's what actually matters: a major AI model requiring government approval before going public is a new norm — and it just got normalized quietly. If this becomes standard practice, the U.S. government now has a hand on the throttle of AI development in a way it didn't before. That's worth paying attention to regardless of which way you lean on regulation.
