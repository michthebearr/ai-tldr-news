🏥 A fake image of Mitch McConnell in a hospital bed went viral — and AI caught it before the damage was done.

A fabricated photo showing Senator McConnell hooked up to tubes in a hospital bed spread across social media this week. It looked convincing enough to fool people. But Google's deepfake detection system flagged it as AI-generated and stopped the narrative in its tracks.

This is a big deal — not because of McConnell specifically, but because of what it proves. AI-generated political disinformation is no longer theoretical. It's here, it's convincing, and it's being deployed. The fact that detection infrastructure caught this one, cleanly, before it caused lasting damage? That's the proof of concept the industry has been waiting for.

We're heading into major election cycles globally. This is what the arms race looks like.
