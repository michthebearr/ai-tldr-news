💰 Ollama just raised $65M — and it's proof that not everyone wants their AI in the cloud.

The local AI tool closed a Benchmark-led round this week, and the numbers behind it are hard to ignore: 9 million users, 176,000 GitHub stars, nearly 17,000 forks. That's not a marketing campaign. That's a developer community that found something that actually works and ran with it.

Ollama lets developers run large language models directly on their own machines — no cloud API, no data leaving your device, no per-query costs. For a certain kind of builder, that's not just convenient. It's the whole point.

The raise signals something bigger: there's a massive underserved market of developers who want powerful AI on their own terms. Ollama just got the capital to own it.
