💡 The White House just decided when you get to use the next big AI model — and that's a first.

OpenAI's GPT-5.6 won't be dropping into your ChatGPT tab anytime soon. The Trump administration asked the company to hold back a public launch, releasing the model only to select partners for now. The stated reason: safety concerns.

Whether you take that framing at face value or not, the mechanics here are unprecedented. A sitting executive branch directly intervened in an AI product release timeline — and a major lab complied. That's not a policy paper. That's not a Senate hearing. That's power being exercised in real time.

Every AI lab on earth is watching this closely. Because if it happened to OpenAI, it can happen to anyone.
