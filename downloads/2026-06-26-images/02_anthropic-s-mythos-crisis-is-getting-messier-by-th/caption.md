💡 The Trump administration took Anthropic's most powerful models offline two weeks ago — and nobody's talking about it.

On a Friday evening, the administration handed Anthropic an ultimatum: pull the Mythos-class models, now. Anthropic complied. Executives flew to DC. Then... silence. No updates, no public timeline, no criteria for what it would even take to bring them back.

Two weeks later, the models are still down and Anthropic has declined to comment repeatedly. An entire family of frontier AI models — just gone, with no resolution in sight.

This isn't regulation. This is governance by threat. And the fact that one administration can quietly hold a model family hostage, with no public process or accountability, should alarm everyone paying attention to how AI power actually gets exercised.
