📱 A $2.3B bet that video games might be the secret to teaching AI how to actually think.

General Intuition just raised $320M on a wild thesis: train AI agents on millions of hours of gameplay footage, and you get something closer to real intuition than anything coming out of traditional pipelines. The logic is that games generate dense, moment-to-moment decision data — the kind of messy, reactive, consequence-driven thinking that's nearly impossible to manufacture any other way.

And right now, that matters more than almost anything else in AI. Quality training data is the scarcest resource in the entire industry. Synthetic data is the workaround everyone's chasing — and if gameplay translates to real-world agent performance, General Intuition just found a pipeline no one else has at scale.

It's either a genuinely clever unlock or a very expensive hunch.
