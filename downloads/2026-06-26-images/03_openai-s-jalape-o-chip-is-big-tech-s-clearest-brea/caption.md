🧠 OpenAI is building its own chip — and Nvidia should be paying attention.

The chip is codenamed Jalapeño, and it's a custom inference processor built with Broadcom. Translation: OpenAI is no longer content to just buy compute from Nvidia. It wants to own the silicon layer entirely.

This isn't a one-off. Google has TPUs. Apple has its Neural Engine. SpaceX is deep in custom hardware. The pattern is clear — every company that gets big enough on Nvidia eventually tries to engineer its way out. OpenAI just crossed that threshold.

When the world's most aggressive AI spender starts designing its own chips, Nvidia's stranglehold on AI infrastructure doesn't look like a permanent condition anymore. It looks like a problem that money and ambition can solve.
