💰 Patronus AI just raised $50M to build crash-test dummies for AI agents — and the timing couldn't be more urgent.

The startup, founded by former Meta AI researchers, is creating simulated "digital worlds" that put AI agents through hell before they ever touch the real one. Think stress-testing, edge cases, failure modes — all the things that currently happen in production, at your expense.

Investors are describing demand as nearly insatiable. That tracks. The AI agent wave is crashing into enterprise and consumer products fast, but the infrastructure to evaluate whether those agents are actually trustworthy? Barely exists.

That's the gap Patronus is betting $50M it can own.

Agentic AI is only as reliable as the testing layer behind it. Right now, that layer is mostly vibes and hope. If Patronus executes, they don't just build a product — they become the foundation every serious AI deployment has to run through first.
