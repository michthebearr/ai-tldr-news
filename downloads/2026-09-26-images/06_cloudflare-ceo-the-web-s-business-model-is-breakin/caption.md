💡 The CEO who sees more internet traffic than almost anyone says AI is quietly breaking the web — and he's not wrong.

Matthew Prince, CEO of Cloudflare, is sounding the alarm. His company sits at the infrastructure layer of the internet, handling a staggering share of global web traffic. So when he says AI bots are consuming the web while giving almost nothing back, that's not speculation — that's a front-row observation.

The core problem: AI has severed the deal that made the open web work. Users used to visit websites, see ads, generate revenue, and fund the creators who made content worth reading. Now AI scrapes that content, synthesizes the answer, and keeps the user on its own platform. No click. No ad. No paycheck for the creator.

Search traffic is evaporating. Ad models are cracking. And the people who actually built the internet are watching their work get strip-mined for free.
