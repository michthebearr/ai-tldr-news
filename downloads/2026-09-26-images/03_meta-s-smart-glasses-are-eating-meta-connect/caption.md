💡 Meta Connect just became a smart glasses keynote — and that should terrify Apple and Google.

At this year's Meta Connect, the Ray-Ban smart glasses weren't a side act. They were the whole show. Meta leaned into its expanding lineup of AI-powered wearables as the centerpiece of its ecosystem strategy — no phone required, no headset needed.

The play is obvious once you see it: if Meta can make AI glasses feel as normal as AirPods, it owns ambient computing before anyone else even ships a product worth wearing. Full AR headsets might be years away from mass adoption. Glasses you'd actually put on your face? Those are here now.

Meta is running the clock on its competitors.
