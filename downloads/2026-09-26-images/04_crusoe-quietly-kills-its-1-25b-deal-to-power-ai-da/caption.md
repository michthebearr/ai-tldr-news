💡 Crusoe just quietly killed a $1.25 billion deal — and it says a lot about AI's energy problem.

Crusoe Energy walked away from its splashy plan to power AI data centers using Boom Supersonic's stationary turbines. We're talking a billion-dollar-plus agreement, gone. Boom's own CEO confirmed the tech is no longer on Crusoe's near-term roadmap.

The deal had been framed as a clever workaround for one of AI's ugliest bottlenecks: the crushing, grid-breaking power demands of modern data centers. The pitch was innovative. The execution, apparently, wasn't there.

This is becoming a pattern. Big energy announcements get made alongside big compute ambitions — and then reality hits. The AI buildout is moving faster than any creative turbine partnership can keep up with. The industry still doesn't have a clean, scalable answer for where all this power is actually going to come from.
