🤖 Google DeepMind just taught a robot to use its entire body — and it's more impressive than it sounds.

Gemini Robotics 2 is DeepMind's latest model, and the upgrade is significant: it no longer just controls a robot's arms and hands — it controls everything. Feet to fingertips, the whole frame moves as one coordinated system.

They demoed it on Apptronik's Apollo 2 robot pulling a baseball glove off a shelf. Simple task on paper. Wildly complex in execution — because getting a humanoid body to balance, reach, and manipulate an object simultaneously is one of the hardest unsolved problems in the field.

Crack whole-body coordination, and suddenly humanoid robots become genuinely useful in warehouses, hospitals, homes — anywhere humans move through messy, unpredictable space.
