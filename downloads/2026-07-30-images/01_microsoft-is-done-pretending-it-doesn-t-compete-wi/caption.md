🧠 Microsoft just told Wall Street out loud what everyone already suspected — OpenAI might be the competition now.

On its Q4 earnings call, Microsoft didn't just report numbers. It pitched its own homegrown AI models, its own AI "harnesses," and a direct competitor to OpenAI's Mythos — all in the same breath. Oh, and it casually mentioned logging $3.2B from its Anthropic investment. You know, OpenAI's biggest rival.

The OpenAI stake? Microsoft called it a "mixed bag." Which is a pretty cold thing to say about a company you gave billions to and helped build from the ground up.

This is what a partnership looks like when it starts curdling into something else. Microsoft needed OpenAI to get into the AI race fast — and it worked. Now it has the distribution, the infrastructure, and apparently the appetite to eat OpenAI's lunch with its own models. The student has left the building.
