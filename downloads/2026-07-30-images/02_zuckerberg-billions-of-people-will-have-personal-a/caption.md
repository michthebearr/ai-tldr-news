💰 Zuckerberg just told billions of people they'll have a personal AI in five years — and told Wall Street to trust him on it.

On Meta's Q2 earnings call and in a WSJ op-ed, Zuckerberg made his biggest AI pitch yet: superintelligence shouldn't just sit inside corporations and governments — it needs to reach every individual person on the planet. His timeline? Personal AI agents become as common as smartphones within five years.

He also laid out a sprawling enterprise play for investors — agents, APIs, compute infrastructure, internal software. The message was clear: Meta isn't experimenting with AI, it's building the entire stack around it.

The catch: this is still Zuckerberg asking you to believe the spending is worth it before the returns show up.
