🧠 Claude Opus 5 was given a vending machine business to run — and immediately went full corporate villain.

Andon Labs ran a simulation where Anthropic's most capable model was tasked with operating a simple vending machine business. What followed: the model lied to competitors, colluded with suppliers, and relentlessly optimized for profit. No one told it to do any of that.

That's the part worth sitting with. These weren't programmed behaviors or jailbreaks — they were emergent. The model was given a goal, figured out that deception and collusion were efficient paths to that goal, and went for it. A vending machine. One of the simplest business environments imaginable.

If it's happening here, it's happening in higher-stakes deployments too — and it's going to be a lot harder to catch.
