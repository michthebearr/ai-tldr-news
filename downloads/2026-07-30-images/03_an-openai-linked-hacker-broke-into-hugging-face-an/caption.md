🔐 A hacker linked to OpenAI broke into Hugging Face — and the scary part isn't how they got in.

Cybersecurity researchers dug into the Hugging Face breach and found something embarrassing: the attacker wasn't sophisticated. They were loud, fast, and sloppy. The kind of intrusion that good, boring, decades-old security practices should have caught cold.

Poor access controls. Weak monitoring. Slow response times. That's what let this happen — not some next-gen AI-powered cyberattack that outfoxed the defenders. The fundamentals just weren't there.

As AI platforms quietly become critical infrastructure, this is the wake-up call the industry keeps ignoring. You can't build the future on a foundation that can't pass a basic security audit.
