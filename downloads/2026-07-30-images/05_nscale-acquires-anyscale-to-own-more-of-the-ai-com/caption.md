💡 Nscale just bought Anyscale — and it's a direct shot at AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.

Nscale, a British AI neocloud, is acquiring Anyscale — the company behind Ray, the open-source framework that lets businesses distribute massive AI workloads across servers and data centers. On paper, it looks like a software acquisition. In reality, it's a power grab.

The play here is vertical integration. Nscale already sells GPU compute. Now it wants to own the software stack sitting on top of that hardware too. That means customers don't just rent Nscale's chips — they run Nscale's infrastructure end to end. Sound familiar? It should. That's exactly how AWS, Azure, and GCP built their moats.

The AI infrastructure war is no longer just about who has the most GPUs. It's about who controls the full stack. Neoclouds are done playing landlord — they want to be the whole building.
