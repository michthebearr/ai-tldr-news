💡 Hugging Face is sitting on $13B acquisition offers — and might turn them all down.

The open-source AI hub has reportedly fielded serious buyout interest at a ~$13 billion valuation. On paper, that's a no-brainer exit. In practice, it's a lot more complicated.

Hugging Face isn't just a company — it's infrastructure. Model repos, datasets, and the trust of hundreds of thousands of developers who built their workflows around a platform that promised to stay open. The founders know that selling to the wrong buyer doesn't just change the business. It potentially betrays an entire ecosystem.

That's a rare position to be in. And a harder call than the number suggests.
