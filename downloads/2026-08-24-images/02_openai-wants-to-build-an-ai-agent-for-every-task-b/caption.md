🏎️ OpenAI wants an AI agent for literally everything — but can regular people actually trust one?

OpenAI is pushing hard beyond Operator and Codex, building out a full lineup of autonomous AI agents designed to handle everyday tasks for everyday users. Not just developers. Not just power users. Everyone.

That's an enormous bet. Building the agents is the easy part — OpenAI has already proven it can do that. The harder problem is convincing a normal person to hand over their inbox, their calendar, or their browser to a piece of software they barely understand. Trust at scale is a completely different challenge than tech at scale.

If they crack it, the way humans interact with computers gets rewritten. If they don't, "AI agents" stays a developer toy and the whole category needs a serious reality check.
