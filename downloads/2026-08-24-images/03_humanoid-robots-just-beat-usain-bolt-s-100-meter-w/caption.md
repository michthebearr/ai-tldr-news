🤖 A humanoid robot just ran the 100-meter dash faster than Usain Bolt.

At the World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing, China's Tiangong Ultra clocked a time that beat Bolt's legendary 9.58-second world record — set in 2009 and considered by many to be untouchable. The same robot also broke the 400-meter world record at the same event.

This isn't a lab result or a simulation. It happened on a track, in competition, in front of an audience. Built by the Beijing Humanoid Robot Innovation Center, Tiangong Ultra just handed physical AI its clearest benchmark moment yet.

Humanoid robots aren't warehouse demos anymore. They're faster than the fastest human who ever lived.
