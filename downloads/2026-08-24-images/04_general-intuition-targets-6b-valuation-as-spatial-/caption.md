🤖 A $6 billion bet that one AI brain can run all robots — not a different one for each.

General Intuition is raising a massive new round at a $6B pre-money valuation, backed by Valor Ventures, Point72 Ventures, and Seven Seven Six. The startup builds foundation models for AI agents that understand and navigate physical space — and they're going all-in on robotics.

The big idea: instead of writing custom software for every robot and every task, you build one generalizable spatial reasoning model and let it do the heavy lifting across the board. Think of it like GPT-4 for physical AI — one model, endless applications.

$6 billion says the smartest money in the room thinks they're right.
