💡 The Nvidia scandal just handed Congress the excuse it's been waiting for on AI chip exports.

Bipartisan tech policy is basically a unicorn in Washington. But when there's a politically digestible scandal attached — one with executive testimony, China, and billion-dollar chips — suddenly both sides find common ground fast.

That's exactly what's happening. Democrats and Republicans are aligning on new legislation to tighten AI chip export controls, with Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer among those calling Nvidia executives to testify. The Huang scandal isn't just a PR headache for Nvidia — it's become the catalyst lawmakers needed to push restrictions that were already simmering.

The stakes are massive. Tighter controls could reshape how advanced AI chips flow to China and ripple across the entire global semiconductor supply chain. This one moves fast.
