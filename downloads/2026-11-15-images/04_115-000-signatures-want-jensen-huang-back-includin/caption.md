💡 115,000 people want Jensen Huang back — and Elon Musk is one of them.

A public petition demanding Nvidia's board reinstate Jensen Huang hit 115,000 signatures over the weekend. That's not a Reddit thread. That's a coordinated show of force from some of the most powerful names in tech, rallying behind a CEO ousted amid a China chip-smuggling scandal.

Let that sink in. Geopolitical red flags. Federal-level security implications. And Silicon Valley's response is essentially: we want our guy back.

It tells you everything about how this industry weighs personal loyalty against national risk. When you're the architect of the AI boom, apparently the rules bend a little differently.
