💡 China's self-driving industry isn't waiting around to find out if Nvidia survives its chaos.

Export restrictions have been tightening for months. Now, with Nvidia's leadership embroiled in scandal and supply uncertainty spiking, Chinese autonomous vehicle companies are done hoping for the best. They've been quietly building their backup plans — stress-testing alternative chips, courting domestic suppliers, and mapping out a future where Nvidia hardware simply isn't an option.

Here's the irony: the export controls designed to slow China's AI ambitions may have just lit a fire under them. The Huang scandal handed Chinese AV companies the perfect justification to accelerate domestic chip development — and the urgency to actually follow through.

The West wanted to slow China down. It may have just made them self-sufficient faster.
