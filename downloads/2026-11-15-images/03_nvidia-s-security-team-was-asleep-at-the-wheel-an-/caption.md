🔐 Nvidia's security team had a critical gap — right when it mattered most.

A former intelligence officer just explained to TechCrunch exactly how Nvidia got caught sleeping. The kind of leak that reportedly ensnared Jensen Huang doesn't come through hacked servers or stolen files — it travels through personal relationships and casual conversations. That's precisely the threat most corporate security teams aren't built to catch.

Here's the kicker: Nvidia's own security chief walked out the door in August. What followed was apparently the most damaging stretch of the entire episode. No one was watching the right doors.

And this isn't a Nvidia-specific failure — it's an industry-wide blind spot. Every major AI company is sitting on chips and IP that are strategically valuable to foreign adversaries. Most of them have the same gap. They just haven't found out yet.
