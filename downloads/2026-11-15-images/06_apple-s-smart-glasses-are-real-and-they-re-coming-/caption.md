💡 Apple's smart glasses are real — and they're coming for Meta's lunch.

Bloomberg's Mark Gurman just reported that Apple will announce its first smart glasses at WWDC in June 2027. Think slightly slimmer than Meta's Ray-Ban collab — no augmented reality, no spatial computing, no Vision Pro-level ambitions. Just a clean, wearable hardware play aimed directly at the mainstream market.

That's the whole point. Apple isn't trying to reinvent reality here. It's trying to own the entry point before Meta locks it in. By stepping into the affordable smart glasses tier, Apple validates the entire category — and suddenly a space that felt like a Meta science project becomes a full-blown platform war.

The consumer wearables market just got a lot more interesting.
