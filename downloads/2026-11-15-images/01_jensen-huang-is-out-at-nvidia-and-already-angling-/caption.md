💡 Jensen Huang is out at Nvidia — and apparently already trying to claw his way back in.

The CEO of the world's most powerful AI chip company was forced to step down after revelations about personal relationships, including one with an executive at a company suspected of routing Nvidia chips to China. His security chief had quietly left months earlier. Nobody said a word publicly until it all came apart at once.

This isn't a messy corporate breakup story. It's a national security story. Nvidia's chips are the backbone of modern AI — the most coveted, most controlled, most geopolitically sensitive hardware on the planet. The idea that the man at the top had a relationship potentially connected to Chinese chip smuggling is a five-alarm situation for Washington, not just Wall Street.

And yet — sources say Huang is already exploring a path back to the CEO seat. Bold move for someone who just got shown the door.
