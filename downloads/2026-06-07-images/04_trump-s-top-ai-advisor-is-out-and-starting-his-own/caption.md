💡 Trump's top AI advisor is leaving the White House — and setting up shop on the outside.

Sriram Krishnan, the senior policy advisor most responsible for shaping U.S. federal AI strategy under Trump, is stepping down. He's not retiring from the fight — he's launching a new institution to keep influencing the administration's AI agenda, just without a government badge.

Details on funding and structure are still thin. But the move raises a real question: does leaving the room make you more powerful or less? On one hand, independence means fewer political constraints and more freedom to push bold ideas. On the other, actual policy gets made by people with seats at the table.

Outside influence is still influence. Until it isn't.
