🧠 OpenAI just declared chat dead — and it's already building the replacement.

A senior OpenAI employee made the call this week: "chat is dead." That's a bold thing to say when your entire company is built on a chatbox. But OpenAI is apparently backing it up — they're actively developing a "super app" that goes way beyond asking questions and getting answers.

The vision is an AI-native app that manages tasks, runs your life, and replaces the cluttered mess of apps on your phone. Not a chatbot. A primary computing interface. The kind of thing Apple and Google have spent decades owning.

No launch date. But the internal conviction is clearly real — and if they pull it off, OpenAI stops being a chat product and starts being your entire phone.
