💰 YouTube just agreed to pay $3 billion to school districts for hooking kids on its platform.

Thousands of US school districts sued YouTube claiming its addictive design features caused real harm to children — and YouTube blinked. The settlement includes new teen safety measures, but the number itself is the headline: $3 billion is a seismic acknowledgment that how you build a product carries legal consequences.

This isn't just a YouTube story. It's a warning shot aimed directly at every AI-powered recommendation system on the internet. If an algorithm is designed to maximize engagement at the expense of wellbeing, that's no longer just an ethics debate — it's a liability.

The era of "it's just a feature" is over.
