💡 Trump wants AI data centers built in America — but America keeps saying no.

After his summit with Xi, Trump threatened sweeping tariffs on any US company that builds AI infrastructure outside the country. The message was blunt: keep the compute on American soil, or pay a steep price.

The problem? American soil isn't exactly rolling out the welcome mat. Local governments across the country are blocking new data center permits — citing energy demands, water usage, and community pushback. So companies are caught between a federal government demanding they build here and local governments making it nearly impossible to do so.

Two forces pushing in opposite directions. One massive bottleneck in the middle.

This matters because the entire US AI strategy — from OpenAI's expansion to Google's infrastructure plays — runs on compute. If that buildout stalls, the race with China doesn't slow down. It just shifts.
