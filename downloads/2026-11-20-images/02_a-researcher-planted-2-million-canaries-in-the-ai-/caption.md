💰 A researcher planted 2 million fake secrets inside AI pipelines — and watched them leak everywhere.

Security researcher drops the most damning proof yet that the AI supply chain has a serious data problem. The method: seed AI systems with 2 million uniquely trackable "canary" prompts — fake credentials, fake secrets — each one fingerprinted so it could be traced wherever it ended up. The results were exactly as bad as you'd expect.

Private inputs didn't stay private. Data bled across vendors, pipelines, and downstream systems at a scale that should make every enterprise CTO deeply uncomfortable. This wasn't a theoretical vulnerability or a one-off bug. It was systematic.

If you're using AI tools — for work, for products, for anything sensitive — the working assumption has to be: your inputs may not be yours alone.
