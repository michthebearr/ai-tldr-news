💡 Google Assistant is being put down — and it's taking your smart speaker with it.

Google just announced it's retiring Google Assistant from smart home devices next year. The Nest app gets the axe on January 25th, 2027. If you've built your home around Google's ecosystem, you're being migrated to the Google Home app and whatever Gemini-powered experience replaces what you had.

This is the quiet funeral for one of the original voice AI platforms. Google Assistant launched in 2016 and was once the gold standard — faster, smarter, and more context-aware than anything else on the market. Now it's being retired to make room for LLM-native AI that couldn't exist back then.

The first generation of voice assistants is officially over.
