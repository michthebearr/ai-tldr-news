💡 The first new browser engine in years just dropped — and it wasn't built by Google, Apple, or Microsoft.

Ladybird is a browser with a completely from-scratch engine, four-plus years in the making, now available in alpha on macOS and Linux. Developer Andreas Kling built the whole thing independent of any Big Tech backing. No corporate overlords. No existing engine to fork. Just a genuinely new piece of internet infrastructure.

That's a bigger deal than it sounds. Browser engine diversity has been quietly collapsing for years — Chrome, Safari, and Firefox are basically all that's left holding up the open web. A credible independent engine entering the picture matters for keeping the internet from becoming a monoculture controlled by a handful of companies.

It's alpha. It's rough. But it exists — and that's already remarkable.
