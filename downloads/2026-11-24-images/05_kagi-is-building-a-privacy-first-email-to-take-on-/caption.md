💡 Kagi is coming for Gmail — and they might actually be serious this time.

Kagi Mail just entered public beta. You get an @kagimail.com address, unlimited aliases, custom domain support, and a company that's legally on the hook to never run ads or surveil your inbox. No "we respect your privacy" marketing speak. Just no ads. Full stop.

This isn't a one-off product launch. Kagi has been quietly building an entire anti-Google stack — paid search, the Orion browser, and now email. That's the holy trinity of your digital life, and they're the only company right now actually trying to own all three without monetizing your data.

Email is the hardest piece of this puzzle. It's where network effects are brutal and switching costs are real. But if any company has the credibility to pull it off, it's the one that already convinced people to pay for search.
