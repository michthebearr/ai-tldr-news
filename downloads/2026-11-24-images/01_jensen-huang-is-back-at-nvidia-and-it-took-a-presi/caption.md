💡 Jensen Huang just got his job back — and it took a call from the White House to make it happen.

Nvidia's CEO resigned after reports surfaced of multiple affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy. The kind of story that ends careers. Except when you're running the most important chip company in the world during an AI arms race.

President Trump and VP JD Vance apparently made their feelings known to the Nvidia board. The board listened. Huang is back in the leather jacket, back at the helm, and the resignation is being treated like it barely happened.

Here's the part that should make everyone pause: the U.S. government effectively decided who runs a private company — because they couldn't afford not to. When the Oval Office is making boardroom calls, the line between Big Tech and Big Government in the AI race isn't blurry anymore. It's gone.
