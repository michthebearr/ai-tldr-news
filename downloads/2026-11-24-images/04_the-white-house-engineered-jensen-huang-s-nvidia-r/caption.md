💡 The White House didn't just influence Nvidia's leadership — it may have engineered it.

Here's the backstory nobody's leading with: before Nvidia's board made anything official, Trump floated Jensen Huang's reinstatement publicly on a radio show. That wasn't a slip. That was a signal — and the board heard it loud and clear.

This isn't just corporate drama. Nvidia's chips sit at the center of US AI strategy, export controls, and the global semiconductor race. The federal government apparently decided Jensen Huang is too important to that equation to let normal corporate governance get in the way.

The precedent here is the real story. When Washington can effectively thumb the scale on who runs America's most critical AI hardware company, the line between private enterprise and national tech policy just got a lot blurrier.
