💡 AI is quietly blowing up the world's most important climate negotiations.

At COP31 in Antalya, delegates trying to hammer out the next phase of global climate commitments ran into an uncomfortable problem: the math no longer adds up. The clean energy transition was already a stretch goal — but AI data centers are now consuming electricity at a scale that's breaking the original projections entirely.

This isn't a future concern. It's happening in the room, right now. Negotiators are being forced to revise demand forecasts mid-summit because the infrastructure powering AI is scaling faster than any renewable buildout can realistically match.

The cruel irony is hard to miss — the technology promising to help solve climate change may be actively delaying the agreements designed to prevent it.
