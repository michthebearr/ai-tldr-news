🧠 OpenAI just poached the Apple exec who built the Vision Pro — and that tells you everything.

Paul Meade, the VP who ran Apple's most secretive and ambitious hardware program, is leaving to join OpenAI's hardware division. This isn't a mid-level hire. This is OpenAI pulling from the inner sanctum of Apple's hardware operation — the team that doesn't talk, doesn't leak, and doesn't lose people easily.

Most people still think of OpenAI as a software company. A chatbot. An API. But moves like this don't happen if you're just building software. You don't hire the person behind the Vision Pro because you're optimizing a model.

Something physical is coming. And it's further along than anyone's letting on.
