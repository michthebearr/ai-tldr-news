🏎️ Elon wants to put data centers in space — and even Masayoshi Son isn't buying it.

Musk has been pitching orbital data centers as the next frontier of computing infrastructure. Bold idea. Except the physics, the economics, and the engineering all seem to be working against him.

The problems aren't small. Heat dissipation in a vacuum is notoriously brutal. Getting a single kilowatt of power to orbit costs a fortune. And latency? You're literally adding the distance between Earth and space to every single request. When critics lay it out that plainly, the concept stops sounding visionary and starts sounding like a whiteboard sketch that never met an engineer.

The real tell: Masayoshi Son — the man who handed WeWork $10 billion with a straight face — reportedly has doubts. When that's your skeptic, the hype-to-substance ratio deserves a second look.
