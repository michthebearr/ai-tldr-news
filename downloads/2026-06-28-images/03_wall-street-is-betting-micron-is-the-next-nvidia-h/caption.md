💡 Wall Street found its next Nvidia bet — and it's not who you'd expect.

Everyone knows Nvidia won the AI compute race. But investors are now asking a different question: what's the *next* bottleneck? The answer might be memory.

AI models are getting so large and so complex that raw processing power isn't always the limiting factor anymore. Memory bandwidth is. And that's where Micron comes in — the US-based chip maker is one of the dominant players in high-bandwidth memory (HBM), the same specialized chips Nvidia packs into its GPUs to keep up with AI demand.

The bull case is simple: if memory becomes the new chokepoint for AI infrastructure, Micron is one of the few US-listed pure plays on that thesis. Nvidia-scale demand. Micron-scale valuations. For now.
