💡 Apple is literally begging the US government to let it buy chips from a company the Pentagon blacklisted for arming China's military.

Apple has filed for a federal exemption to purchase RAM from CXMT — a Chinese memory maker flagged for ties to the People's Liberation Army. The reason? RAM and storage prices have surged so sharply that Apple already raised prices across nearly its entire product lineup this week. They're running out of options.

This is what a supply chain trap looks like in real time. The US government says CXMT is a national security threat. Apple says it can't build affordable products without them. Both things can be true — and that's exactly the problem.

This isn't just Apple's headache. As AI hardware demands exponentially more memory, every major tech company will face this same impossible choice: comply with US national security policy, or stay competitive in a world where China dominates memory manufacturing. Apple just made that tension impossible to ignore.
