💡 Every automaker on the planet is watching Tesla FSD right now — and they're nervous.

Tesla's Full Self-Driving system is under the microscope this week. Regulators are circling, competitors are closing, and the industry is quietly asking a question Tesla doesn't want asked: is the lead actually as big as they've been saying?

Tesla got here first. That matters. But "first" and "best" are different things, and Waymo is scaling fast with a model that actually removes the human from the equation. Legacy automakers aren't far behind with serious capital and serious pressure to deliver. The window for Tesla to cement FSD as the industry standard is narrower than it's ever been.

This one sets the benchmark for what "production-ready" autonomous AI looks like — for investors, for regulators, and for every startup betting on the space.
