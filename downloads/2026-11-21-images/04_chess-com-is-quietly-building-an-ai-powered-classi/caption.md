💡 Chess.com isn't just a chess app anymore — it's quietly building an AI-powered empire of classic games.

The platform is launching a "Classic Games" umbrella in public beta this weekend, adding 10 new titles: backgammon, go, mahjong, sudoku, shogi, solitaire, and more. Earlier this year they dropped a poker platform. The pattern is obvious — Chess.com is making a play to own the entire classic games category.

AI opponents and coaching tools are baked in from the start, extending the same model that made Chess.com one of the most genuinely useful AI integrations in gaming. It doesn't replace the human player — it makes them better.

That distinction matters. Chess.com has spent years proving AI can enhance competition without killing the fun. Scaling that philosophy across a dozen games with completely different mechanics, skill curves, and communities is the real experiment here.
