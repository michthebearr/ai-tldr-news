🔐 The FBI's crime database just got hacked — and it's not even the first time this year.

The National Crime Information Center (NCIC) was breached. This is the database that every level of law enforcement in the country relies on — warrants, missing persons, criminal records, all of it. CNN reports this is the FBI's second hack in just a few months. The USDA was also hit the same day.

This isn't just an embarrassing headline for federal agencies. The NCIC is a potential goldmine for bad actors — tamper with the records inside and you could interfere with active investigations, expose ongoing surveillance operations, or quietly alter criminal histories.

The country's crime infrastructure runs on this database. And someone just walked through the front door.
