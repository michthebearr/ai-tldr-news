💡 Anthropic just went public — and the safety-first promise is already under pressure.

Claude's maker debuted on the Nasdaq last Thursday, instantly landing among the most valuable companies on the planet. It's a massive milestone for a lab that was literally founded on the idea that AI development is moving too fast and too recklessly.

Here's the tension: Anthropic has built its entire identity around constitutional AI and safety as a first principle — not a marketing tagline. But public markets don't care about principles. They care about growth, margins, and next quarter. Every activist investor and earnings call is now a stress test for how serious Anthropic really was about all of it.

The company that warned loudest about unchecked AI now has to answer to the one force that's broken more corporate values than any other — shareholders.
