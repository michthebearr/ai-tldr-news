🤖 The UN spent a whole week debating killer robots and left with a plan to… keep talking about it.

Diplomats gathered in Geneva specifically to hammer out rules for autonomous weapons systems — lethal AI that can identify and kill targets without human input. After days of negotiations, the big outcome was an agreement to "start discussing options."

That's not a framework. That's a calendar invite.

Here's the problem: AI-powered weapons aren't a future threat. They're already deployed in active conflict zones right now. The gap between what militaries can build and what international law actually covers is growing by the month — and the world's top diplomatic body just punted on closing it.

The window to establish meaningful norms around autonomous weapons is narrow and getting narrower. At this pace, the technology won't wait for the diplomats to catch up.
