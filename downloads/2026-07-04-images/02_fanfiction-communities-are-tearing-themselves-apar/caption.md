💡 Fanfiction communities are tearing themselves apart trying to catch AI writers — and they're getting it wrong.

AO3 and other fanfic platforms have become ground zero for a grassroots anti-AI movement. Writers are self-organizing to detect and expose authors using generative AI — flagging posts, calling people out, getting accounts removed.

The problem: the detection tools don't work. AI detectors are notoriously unreliable, and innocent writers are getting accused, harassed, and pushed out of communities they've been part of for years. The witch hunt is doing real damage to the people it was never meant to touch.

This is what self-policing AI use looks like without any reliable infrastructure to back it up. Good intentions, broken methods, and a community eating itself alive.
