💰 A UK chip startup just raised $720M to go after Nvidia — and the timing is no accident.

Fractile, a British AI chip company most people hadn't heard of last week, has closed one of the largest funding rounds in European tech history. The goal: build purpose-built AI silicon that can genuinely compete with Nvidia's iron grip on the market.

Details on the actual chip architecture are thin. But here's the thing — $720 million doesn't come from cautious money. That's serious institutional conviction that the AI chip market is still up for grabs, and that someone outside Silicon Valley can take a real shot at it.

The backdrop makes this even more interesting. Nvidia is navigating export restrictions, antitrust scrutiny, and growing enterprise anxiety about single-vendor dependency. For a well-funded, credible European alternative, this might be the best possible moment to exist.

Nvidia's moat is real. But it's also getting a lot of people very nervous.
