💡 Trillions in AI data centers have been announced. A lot of them will never be built.

The AI infrastructure boom looks unstoppable on paper — sweeping announcements, eye-watering dollar figures, politicians cutting ribbons. But behind the press releases, the actual construction is hitting wall after wall. Local opposition, permitting nightmares, power grid bottlenecks, and shortages of transformers and cooling equipment are quietly killing projects before a single shovel hits dirt.

The political winds may have shifted post-election, but bureaucracy doesn't care who won. The gap between what's announced and what gets built is growing — and it's becoming one of the most underreported constraints on AI's growth trajectory.

If the infrastructure can't keep pace with demand, the whole industry hits a hard ceiling. And whoever cracks the efficiency problem first doesn't just win a construction contract — they win the AI race.
