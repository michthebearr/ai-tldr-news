💰 CoreWeave is making its customers fund its own infrastructure buildout — and that should raise a few eyebrows.

The AI cloud company's Q3 earnings revealed a funding playbook that's equal parts creative and concerning: customer prepayments used as working capital, debt borrowed against specific contracts, and a shift toward shorter-term deals that quietly transfer more cost onto the customer over time.

This is what it looks like when even a well-capitalized AI infrastructure giant is struggling to keep pace with its own ambitions. CoreWeave has raised billions and counts Microsoft as a major customer — and it's still finding ways to make others float the bill for its growth.

The uncomfortable truth: when a company starts treating customer prepayments like a financing tool, it's either a sign of explosive demand or creeping financial stress. CoreWeave's situation looks like both at the same time.
