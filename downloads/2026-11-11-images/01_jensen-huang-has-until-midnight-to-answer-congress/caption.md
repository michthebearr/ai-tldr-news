💡 Congress just gave Jensen Huang a midnight deadline — and the story behind it is wild.

House Republicans are demanding Nvidia's CEO agree to a closed-door briefing over his alleged ties to a Chinese spy. This isn't a rumor or a vague accusation — it's a formal congressional deadline, with Nvidia's board already rattled and governance experts calling the situation unprecedented at a company of this scale.

Here's what makes it worse: a separate report suggests Huang's behavior may have been an open secret. Senior industry figures reportedly knew — and were effectively looking the other way.

Nvidia is the company powering the entire AI infrastructure buildout. The chips behind your favorite AI tools, the data centers, the arms race — it all runs through them. A national security investigation landing here, right now, is about as bad as the timing gets.
