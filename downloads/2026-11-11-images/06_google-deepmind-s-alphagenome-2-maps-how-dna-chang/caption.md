💡 Google DeepMind just built an AI that reads your DNA and predicts how it behaves — cell by cell.

AlphaGenome 2 is DeepMind's new model that maps how specific DNA mutations affect gene activity across different cell types. And that last part is the hard part. The same genetic change can do something completely different in a liver cell versus a brain cell versus a cancer cell. That's what makes this problem so brutally complex — and why no model has cracked it cleanly until now.

This is a step beyond AlphaFold. That model told us what proteins look like. AlphaGenome 2 is trying to tell us why genes break — and in what context. That's the difference between a blueprint and a diagnosis.

If this holds up, it could become a core tool for understanding the cellular roots of diseases like cancer and diabetes. Not AI as a productivity hack. AI as actual science.
