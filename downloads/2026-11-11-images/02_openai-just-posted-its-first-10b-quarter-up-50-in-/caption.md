🧠 OpenAI just hit $10 billion in a single quarter — and it took them three months to get there.

Q3 revenue came in at $10B, up from $6.7B in Q2. That's nearly 50% growth in 90 days. For context, most enterprise software companies would celebrate that kind of jump over a full year.

Is OpenAI profitable? No. The costs — compute, talent, infrastructure — are enormous. But the revenue trajectory alone is making a point that's hard to dismiss: this isn't hype. This is a real business moving at an unreal speed.

Fastest-growing enterprise software company in history. The title's not official, but it's hard to argue with the math.
