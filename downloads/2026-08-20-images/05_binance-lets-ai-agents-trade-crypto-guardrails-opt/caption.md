💡 Binance just gave AI agents the keys to your crypto — and forgot to install a seatbelt.

Binance launched Agent OS this week, letting AI agents from ChatGPT, Claude, and Cursor execute real trades directly on its platform. No demo mode. No sandbox. Live money, autonomous decisions, go.

Here's the part that should make you pause: there are zero platform-level guardrails. No mandatory risk controls. No hard limits baked in by Binance. Whatever safety nets exist are entirely up to the user to configure — which, if history is any guide, most won't.

Autonomous agents with direct access to financial accounts and user-defined-only limits isn't a feature. It's a liability assignment dressed up as innovation. When something goes wrong — and with this setup, it's when, not if — Binance already has its answer ready: you set the rules.
