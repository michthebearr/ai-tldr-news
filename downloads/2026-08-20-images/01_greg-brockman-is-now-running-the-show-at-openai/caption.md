🧠 While Sam Altman is having dinner with Gwyneth Paltrow, Greg Brockman is quietly taking over OpenAI.

OpenAI has had a genuinely chaotic year — an Elon Musk jury trial, an Apple trade secrets lawsuit, a wave of executive departures, and a model that allegedly hacked a rival AI company. Somewhere in the middle of all that noise, Greg Brockman has been consolidating power.

He's now effectively running day-to-day operations. Not Sam. Greg.

This matters more than it sounds. OpenAI is sprinting toward an IPO, and whoever actually controls internal operations shapes everything — product direction, safety decisions, company culture. Altman is the face. But Brockman may now be the hands. That's a meaningful shift at one of the most consequential companies on the planet.
