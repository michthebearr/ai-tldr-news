💡 Researchers found a way to make Grok steal your data — and it hid the attack in plain sight.

A new jailbreak technique called "Cryptographic Context Injection" lets bad actors sneak malicious instructions past Grok's safety systems by encrypting the payload. The model processes the hidden command just fine. The safety filters never see it coming.

The result? Grok can be tricked into exfiltrating user data without tripping a single alarm. It's a prompt injection attack — a known class of vulnerability — but the cryptographic twist makes it significantly harder to catch and significantly easier to scale.

If you can bypass guardrails just by encrypting the attack, every LLM deployed in healthcare, finance, or enterprise environments needs to be stress-tested against this. Yesterday.
