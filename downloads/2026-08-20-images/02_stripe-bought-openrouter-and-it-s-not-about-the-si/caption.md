💡 Stripe just bought OpenRouter — and "the singularity" is not the real reason.

Stripe acquired OpenRouter, a platform that routes developer prompts across 400+ AI models from 80+ providers through one API. The CEO dropped a singularity reference when asked why. Cool quote. Wrong answer.

The actual reason: every prompt routed through OpenRouter is a transaction. Stripe wants to meter it, bill it, and take a cut. They're not buying an AI product — they're buying the cash register for the entire AI economy.

This is Stripe positioning itself as the financial plumbing for how AI gets consumed at scale. While everyone else is racing to build the next model, Stripe is quietly becoming the company that charges for all of them.
