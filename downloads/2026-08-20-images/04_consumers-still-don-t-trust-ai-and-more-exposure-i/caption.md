💡 The more people use AI, the less they trust it — and that's a massive problem for the industry.

A new analysis out this week found that consumer skepticism around AI is actually *growing* as AI becomes harder to avoid. Silicon Valley bet that familiarity would breed acceptance. It hasn't.

The "just get it in front of people" playbook — the one behind every aggressive product rollout and forced feature integration — isn't converting skeptics. It's creating them. Widespread deployment has produced widespread wariness, and the gap between enterprise enthusiasm and how everyday users actually feel has rarely been wider.

That's not a PR problem. That's a legitimacy problem — and no product launch fixes it.
