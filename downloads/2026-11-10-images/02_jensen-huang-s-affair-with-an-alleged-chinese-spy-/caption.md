🔐 The man who controls global AI infrastructure may have been sleeping with a Chinese spy.

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has confirmed an extramarital affair with a woman U.S. officials suspect is a Chinese intelligence operative. Reports indicate Huang quietly hired a crisis PR firm — one that specializes in high-profile sex scandals — more than a year ago, well before this story went public.

That timeline is doing a lot of work.

Huang isn't just any tech CEO. Nvidia's chips are the backbone of the entire AI arms race — the chokepoint between who leads and who falls behind. If China gained any intelligence access through this relationship, the damage wouldn't be a leaked memo. It could be a years-long head start in the most consequential technological competition in modern history.

We don't know what was accessed. We don't know what was shared. What we do know is that the stakes of this story are almost impossible to overstate.
