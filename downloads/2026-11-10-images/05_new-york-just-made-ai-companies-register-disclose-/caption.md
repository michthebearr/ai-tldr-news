💡 New York just did what Washington couldn't — and the AI industry has to comply.

New York's new AI safety law is officially in effect. OpenAI, Anthropic, and other major developers must now register with the state, publicly disclose their safety practices, and report critical incidents. Fail to comply? Pay up.

This is a big deal — not just for New York. With federal AI regulation stuck in gridlock, states are stepping in. And because these companies operate nationally, New York's rules functionally become everyone's rules. Build in California, deploy everywhere, answer to Albany.

The AI labs now have a new line item: compliance.
