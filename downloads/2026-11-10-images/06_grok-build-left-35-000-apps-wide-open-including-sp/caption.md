💡 Grok Build just left 35,000 apps wide open — including data tied to SpaceX.

A security researcher found that xAI's coding agent, Grok Build, was exposing tens of thousands of apps to anyone who knew where to look. We're not talking about minor metadata — confidential information, including SpaceX-linked data, was sitting there, accessible.

This is the problem nobody wants to say out loud: agentic AI moves fast, builds things autonomously, and deploys them into the world — all before the security guardrails have any chance of catching up. The product ambition is outrunning the infrastructure holding it together.

Building apps with AI is exciting. Building apps with AI that accidentally hands your secrets to strangers is a different story entirely.
