🏎️ Anthropic just declared war on the follow-up email.

Claude Meetings is Anthropic's new real-time AI that sits inside your workplace conversations and works while you talk. It's drafting action plans, mapping timelines on a whiteboard, answering questions in chat, and pulling live data from your connected apps — before anyone's even said "let's circle back."

This isn't a note-taker. It's a full post-meeting workflow replacement, compressed into the meeting itself. The goal is simple: by the time you hang up, the work is already done.

And the target on the wall? Microsoft Copilot and Google Workspace AI — two giants who built their entire enterprise AI pitch around exactly this territory.
