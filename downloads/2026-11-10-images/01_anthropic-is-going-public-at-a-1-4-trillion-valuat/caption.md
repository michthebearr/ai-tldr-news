💡 Anthropic is going public — and the number attached to it is almost incomprehensible.

The Claude-maker just set its IPO price range at $70–$78 per share, putting its fully diluted valuation at up to $1.4 trillion. For context: that's not a startup anymore. That's a generational institution.

Here's the detail that stands out: all seven co-founders are selling the exact same number of shares, netting roughly $250M each — while keeping voting control locked down tight. This wasn't accidental. It's a carefully choreographed signal that the founding team is aligned, cashing out modestly, and not going anywhere.

This would rank among the largest tech IPOs in history. Every other AI lab — OpenAI, xAI, Mistral — now has a number to beat. The AI gold rush just got a price tag.
