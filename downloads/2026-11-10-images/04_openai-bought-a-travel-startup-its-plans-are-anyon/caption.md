🧠 OpenAI just bought a travel startup — and won't tell anyone why.

The acquisition is Odessia, founded by Francis Davidson, the same guy who built Sonder into one of the most recognizable names in short-term rentals. OpenAI hasn't dropped a single hint about what it's actually building. No product roadmap. No press release. Just a quiet deal and a very loaded silence.

But the hire tells the story. Davidson isn't a research guy — he's a consumer product guy. This isn't OpenAI bolting on a travel API. This looks like the beginning of a full vertical, which would put them in the ring with Google Flights, Booking.com, Expedia, and every AI travel agent startup that's spent the last two years raising money on exactly this thesis.

OpenAI is building something much bigger than a chatbot. The question is how fast.
