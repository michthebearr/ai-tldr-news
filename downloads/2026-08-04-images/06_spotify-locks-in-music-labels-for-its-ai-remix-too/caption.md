🎵 Spotify just built the first real framework for AI music — and the labels are actually on board.

Spotify has locked in Merlin, the coalition representing 30,000+ independent labels, as a partner for its AI remix and covers tool. That's on top of Universal Music Group already signing on. This isn't a scrappy startup asking forgiveness later — it's the biggest names in music voluntarily stepping into the AI era.

Here's what makes this different: artists opt in, they get credited, and they get paid. That's the trifecta that every previous AI music product failed to clear. Spotify didn't bulldoze the industry — it built infrastructure the industry could actually say yes to.

With both major and indie label pipelines now aligned, Spotify has quietly done what nobody else has: created a legitimate, consent-based commercial system for AI-generated music at scale. The blueprint just got real.
