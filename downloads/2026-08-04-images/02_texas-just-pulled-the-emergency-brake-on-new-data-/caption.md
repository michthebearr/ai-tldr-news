💡 Texas just slammed the brakes on new data centers — and it's a warning shot for the entire AI industry.

Governor Greg Abbott has ordered ERCOT and the Public Utility Commission to audit every new data center proposal before it can touch the grid. The state that practically invented the "come build here, no questions asked" playbook is now asking a lot of questions.

Here's the irony: Texas won the AI infrastructure race so convincingly that it broke its own grid's ability to keep up. The same cheap power and light-touch regulation that made it the go-to destination for hyperscalers is now the reason capacity planning is in crisis.

If Texas can't absorb the boom, nowhere can. Every other state watching this will think twice before rolling out the welcome mat — and the industry's aggressive build-out timeline just got a lot shakier.
