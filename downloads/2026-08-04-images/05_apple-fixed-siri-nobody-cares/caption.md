🏎️ Apple finally fixed Siri — and nobody cares.

After years of being the punchline of every AI conversation, Apple has shipped a version of Siri that actually works. Context-aware, device-integrated, and genuinely capable. The reviews are solid. The response? A collective shrug.

Here's the problem: while Apple was quietly fixing things behind closed doors, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude were out in the world rewriting everyone's expectations for what AI is even supposed to feel like. "Competent" used to be a win. Now it's just the entry fee.

Apple didn't lose the AI race in a single moment — they lost it slowly, by letting the gap widen for years while the rest of the industry sprinted. Now they've caught up to a bar that's already moved.

Being good isn't enough anymore. You had to be first.
