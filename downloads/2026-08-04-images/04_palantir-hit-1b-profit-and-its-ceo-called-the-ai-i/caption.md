💡 Palantir just crossed $1 billion in profit — and its CEO spent the earnings call calling AI labs Marxists.

Alex Karp didn't hold back. On this week's earnings call, he went after frontier AI labs — OpenAI, Anthropic, the usual suspects — arguing their ideological makeup makes them fundamentally untrustworthy for serious enterprise work. His take: labs building general-purpose AI answer to their own vision of the future, not to the businesses cutting the checks.

It's a sharp pitch. And it's working. Enterprises spooked by the idea of feeding sensitive data into ChatGPT or Claude are lining up for Palantir's "we work for you, not humanity" model. Karp has essentially turned distrust of Big AI into a billion-dollar revenue stream.

The irony? The more the AI labs dominate headlines, the better Palantir's sales calls go.
