🧠 Apple's trade secrets case against OpenAI just got a whole lot messier.

New court documents reveal Apple has widened its investigation — and more former employees than originally named may have walked out the door with confidential data before landing at OpenAI. What started as a targeted legal dispute is starting to look like something much harder to contain.

This isn't just about a handful of disgruntled engineers anymore. If Apple's discovery process is turning up new names, it suggests the alleged IP leakage may have been systemic — happening across teams, over time, at the exact moment OpenAI was building its most sensitive technology.

The junction between Apple and OpenAI is one of the most consequential in all of tech. How this plays out could reshape how Silicon Valley thinks about employee mobility, NDAs, and what "trade secret" even means in the AI era.
