📱 Snap doesn't want to pay for AI video anymore — so it's making someone else's problem.

The company is spinning out its internal AI video team into a brand new standalone startup called Dotmo. The employees stay, the work continues — Snap just quietly removes the line item from its budget.

This is a masterclass in corporate optionality. Snap gets to tell investors it's cutting costs while keeping a foot in the door if AI video turns into something valuable. If Dotmo wins, Snap claims proximity. If it flops, Snap shrugs.

The real story here isn't Snap. It's the pattern. Big Tech is increasingly using the "spin it out" playbook to offload expensive AI bets without fully walking away from them. Clean balance sheet. Preserved upside. Minimal accountability.

Have your AI. Eat it too.
