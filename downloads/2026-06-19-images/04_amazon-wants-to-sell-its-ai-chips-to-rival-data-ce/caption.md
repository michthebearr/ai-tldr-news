💡 Amazon wants to sell its AI chips to rival data centers — and Nvidia should be paying attention.

AWS is in active talks to offer its in-house Trainium and Inferentia chips directly to third-party data centers. Not just use them internally. Sell them. To competitors. Andy Jassy has publicly called this a $50 billion opportunity, which means Amazon isn't tiptoeing around its ambitions here.

This matters because Nvidia has had an almost unchallenged grip on AI compute since the boom kicked off. If Amazon can get its silicon into external data centers at scale, it introduces real pricing competition into a market that currently has almost none. That's a structural shift — not just a product launch.

The shot has been fired.
