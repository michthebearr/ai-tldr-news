💰 OpenAI is building its IPO roster like it's assembling the Avengers.

In the same week, OpenAI quietly landed two massive names: Noam Shazeer, one of the eight co-authors of "Attention Is All You Need" — the 2017 paper that literally invented the Transformer architecture modern AI runs on — and Dean Ball, a former Trump administration AI policy official with direct ties to the current White House.

Shazeer had been at Google DeepMind. His arrival isn't just a talent win — it's a symbol. You don't hire the co-inventor of the Transformer unless you're telling the world you're still in the business of building the future, not just cashing in on it.

Ball is the other half of the story. With an IPO on the horizon and Washington paying closer attention to AI than ever, having someone with a direct line to the administration isn't a nice-to-have. It's infrastructure.

Two hires. One very clear message: OpenAI is coming to market loaded.
