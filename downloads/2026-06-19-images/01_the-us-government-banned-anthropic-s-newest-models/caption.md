💡 The US government banned Anthropic's two newest models — and apparently made them impossible to ignore.

Fable 5 and Mythos 5 were pulled after Amazon researchers reportedly found a way to break through Fable 5's safety guardrails. National security concern, regulators said. Case closed.

Except it wasn't. Cybersecurity researchers hit back with an open letter calling the ban counterproductive, and Anthropic pointed out the uncomfortable truth: the same jailbreaks exist in models from competing labs that faced zero restrictions. Usage numbers, meanwhile, haven't fallen. They've climbed.

This is the Streisand Effect meets AI regulation — and it exposes something more dangerous than a jailbreak. A safety crackdown that targets one company while leaving its competitors untouched isn't a safety policy. It's a market distortion. And it just set a precedent for how governments can quietly kneecap AI labs without ever having to prove the threat was real.
