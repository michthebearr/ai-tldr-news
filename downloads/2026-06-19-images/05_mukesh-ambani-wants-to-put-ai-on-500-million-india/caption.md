💰 Mukesh Ambani wants to put AI in the hands of 500 million Indians — and he already has the pipes to do it.

Reliance, India's telecom and retail giant, is embedding AI directly into its Jio platform. We're talking every call, every app, every connected home device across its network becoming an AI touchpoint. This isn't a product launch. It's an infrastructure move.

Most Western AI companies are still fighting for distribution — paying for ads, chasing downloads, begging for shelf space. Ambani doesn't have that problem. Jio is already the utility half a billion people use to exist online. He's not asking for access. He owns the pipes.

This is what population-scale AI actually looks like. Not a chatbot. Not a subscription tier. A utility, baked in at the base layer.
