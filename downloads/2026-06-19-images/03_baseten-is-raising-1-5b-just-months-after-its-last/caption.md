💡 Baseten just raised a massive round — and the ink from their last one is barely dry.

The AI inference startup is reportedly closing a $1.5 billion round at a $13 billion valuation, just months after its previous mega-raise. For context: Baseten builds the infrastructure that lets companies actually *deploy* AI models in production — the unglamorous, critical plumbing that sits between a model existing and a model doing something useful.

The speed here is the story. Back-to-back raises at this scale don't happen by accident. Investors are sprinting to own a piece of the inference layer because as model proliferation explodes, the bottleneck isn't building models anymore — it's serving them cheaply, reliably, and at scale.

Whoever owns that serving layer owns the margin.
