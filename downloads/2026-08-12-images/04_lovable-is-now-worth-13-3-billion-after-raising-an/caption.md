💰 Lovable just hit a $13.3 billion valuation — and it's only been around for a minute.

The no-code AI app-builder confirmed a $400 million raise this week, hot on the heels of hitting $500 million in annualized revenue back in June. That kind of trajectory doesn't happen in normal startup math. Lovable isn't just growing fast — it's growing at a speed that makes most SaaS companies look like they're moving in slow motion.

Here's what the number actually means: regular people are shipping real software without writing a single line of code. Lovable turned "vibe-coding" — a term that sounded like a joke 18 months ago — into a category that's now worth over $13 billion.

The bottom of the dev market is getting eaten alive. And the appetite isn't slowing down.
