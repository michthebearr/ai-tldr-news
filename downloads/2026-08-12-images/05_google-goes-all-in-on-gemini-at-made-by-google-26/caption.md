🧠 Google's hardware event wasn't really about hardware.

At Made by Google '26, the Pixel 11 got a new Tensor G6 chip, the Pixel Watch 5 can now track blood pressure and insulin resistance, and a $29 Pixel Tag is gunning for AirTag via Google's Find Hub network. Solid upgrades across the board.

But the real story? Every single one of these devices is a Gemini delivery mechanism. Google isn't building gadgets — it's building surfaces. More devices means more entry points for the AI assistant, and with 1 billion Gemini users already on the flywheel, the momentum is real.

Hardware as a Trojan horse. And it's working.
