💰 A 2-month-old AI startup just raised $1.1 billion — before shipping a single product.

River AI, founded by Igor Babuschkin — one of the co-founders of Elon Musk's xAI — closed a massive seed-ish round led by General Catalyst. The company is building personal AI agents. That's the pitch. That's it. Nine figures in the door on vibes and a résumé.

This is what AI pedigree buys you in 2025. No product. No users. No revenue. Just the right name attached to the right idea at the right moment — and apparently that's more than enough to unlock institutional money at a scale that would've been unthinkable two years ago.

The "prove it first" era is over.
