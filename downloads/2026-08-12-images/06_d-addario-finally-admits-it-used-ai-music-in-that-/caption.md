🎵 D'Addario spent two weeks lying about AI music — and just got caught.

The guitar string giant used Suno-generated AI music in a promotional video. When people noticed, D'Addario didn't come clean. They blamed "low-quality exports." They blamed "plugin combinations." They blamed everything except the actual answer.

Then the evidence became impossible to explain away. This week, they finally admitted it.

This is a big deal. D'Addario sells to musicians — people whose entire livelihood depends on the idea that authentic, human-made music has value. Getting caught secretly using AI-generated audio while publicly denying it isn't just bad PR. It's a betrayal of the exact audience that buys your product. And it's a landmark moment for AI music detection, proving these tools are now good enough to catch brands in the act.

Authenticity was supposed to be the whole point.
