🧠 OpenAI just lost the executive who quietly made it a business.

Brad Lightcap is out. After years as COO, he's leaving to "start something new" — which, in corporate speak, means the conversation about his future probably started a while ago.

Lightcap wasn't the face of OpenAI. Sam Altman has that locked. But Lightcap was the operator — the person translating billion-dollar vision into actual revenue, partnerships, and org structure. He helped turn a research lab into one of the fastest-growing software companies in history.

Losing your COO mid-hypergrowth isn't a footnote. It's a stress test.
