💰 Gemini just crossed 1 billion users — and most of them aren't typing a single word.

Google dropped a stat this week that should make every AI competitor nervous: Gemini has hit the one-billion-user milestone, and 63% of those users are engaging via voice. Oh, and the app is generating over 150 million images every day. These numbers landed alongside the Made by Google hardware event — perfectly timed, obviously.

Here's what the headline buries: ChatGPT may have defined what an AI assistant looks like, but Google is quietly defining how people actually use one. Voice-first, at a billion-user scale, is a completely different product than a chat window on a laptop.

Android is the unfair advantage nobody talks about enough. When your AI ships pre-installed on billions of devices in every market on earth, a billion users isn't a milestone — it's a starting point.
