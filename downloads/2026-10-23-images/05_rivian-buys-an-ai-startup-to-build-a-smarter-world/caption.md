💡 Rivian just bought an AI startup — and it's a direct shot at Tesla's self-driving crown.

Rivian acquired Third Dimension, a startup building AI "world models" — essentially digital twins of physical reality used as hyper-realistic driving simulators. The idea: instead of logging millions of real-world miles to capture rare edge cases, you simulate them. Cheaper, faster, and infinitely scalable.

Paired with Rivian's growing fleet data, this gives them the raw ingredients to build a serious autonomous driving training pipeline. That's not a driver assistance play. That's a full self-driving ambition.

World models are quietly becoming the most important battleground in autonomous vehicles. Whoever builds the most accurate simulation of reality controls the training data game — and the companies that crack it won't need the road to teach their cars anymore.
