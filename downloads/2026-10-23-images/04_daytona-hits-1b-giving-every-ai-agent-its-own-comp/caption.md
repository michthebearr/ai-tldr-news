🏎️ Daytona just hit $1B by solving the problem nobody was talking about — until now.

Every AI agent needs a computer. Not metaphorically. Literally. When you're running thousands of agents in parallel, shoving them all into shared infrastructure is a mess — slow, insecure, and not built for what agents actually need to do.

Daytona's answer: give each agent its own isolated cloud environment, spun up on demand. It's the containers-for-agents play, and the people writing the checks include founders of Stripe, Perplexity, Datadog, and Mercor. FirstMark Capital led the round. That's not a random bet — that's pattern recognition from people who've built infrastructure layers before.

The real signal here isn't the valuation. It's that agentic infrastructure is becoming its own investment category. The smart money isn't just funding AI apps anymore — it's funding the picks and shovels underneath them.
