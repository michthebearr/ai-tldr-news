🧠 OpenAI is now worth more than the entire GDP of Australia — and it's still a private company.

OpenAI is in talks to raise $30 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation. If that closes, it becomes the most valuable private company in history. Not by a little. By a wide margin.

Three years ago, OpenAI was valued at $29 billion. That number now looks like a rounding error. This fundraise comes as the company restructures away from its nonprofit roots and positions itself for an IPO — shedding the governance model that was supposed to keep it from becoming exactly this: a profit-maximizing tech giant with trillion-dollar ambitions.

When AI capital starts operating at this scale, it stops being a tech story and starts being a macroeconomic one.
