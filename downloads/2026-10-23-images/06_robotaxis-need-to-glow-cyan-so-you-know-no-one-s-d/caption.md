🤖 If a car is glowing cyan, no one's behind the wheel — and soon that'll be federal law.

The NHTSA has proposed a new rule requiring autonomous vehicles to display cyan-colored exterior lighting whenever they're operating without a human driver. No text scrolling across the door. No custom brand signal. Just that one specific shade of blue-green, universally.

This is actually a bigger deal than it sounds. For years, robotaxi companies have been rolling out their own little visual languages — and pedestrians, cyclists, and other drivers have been left guessing. A Waymo beacon means something different than a Zoox flash. That's a real safety problem when you're deciding whether to cross the street.

A single federal standard cuts through the chaos. You see cyan, you know.
