💡 Google just quietly rewrote the rules on your most personal data — and most people missed it.

On October 16th, Google updated its privacy policy to include a new exception to a promise it's held for years: your Gmail, Drive, and Google Photos data won't be used for ads. That promise still stands — unless you opt into Google's personalized AI features. Then it's fair game.

The update wasn't announced. It was buried in policy language and caught by Android Authority, not Google's PR team. That's not an accident.

This is the deal finally made explicit: you get smarter, more personal AI. Google gets your emails, your files, your photos — and a new pipeline straight into ad targeting. The most intimate data you've ever handed a tech company, now monetized.
