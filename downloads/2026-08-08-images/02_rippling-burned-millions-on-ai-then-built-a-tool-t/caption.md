💰 Rippling burned millions on AI tools before anyone asked if it was actually working.

When Rippling gave employees open access to AI, the bills piled up fast. No guardrails, no tracking, no visibility into who was spending what — just a growing line item and a lot of unanswered questions about ROI.

So they built the answer themselves. AI Spend Console tracks AI spending at the individual and team level, mapping costs to actual productivity outcomes. It's the kind of tool that shouldn't need to exist — but does, because most companies rolled out AI mandates before they rolled out any kind of financial oversight.

Every company telling employees to "just use AI" right now is probably bleeding the same way. Rippling just had the receipts to prove it — and turned the lesson into a product.
