💡 Roku just launched AI-generated TV channels, and yes, it's exactly as soulless as it sounds.

The streaming platform quietly added four new AI-produced channels to its free ad-supported lineup — including one called Fairground. No writers. No directors. No humans in a creative role whatsoever. Just algorithmically assembled content designed to fill screen time and serve ads.

Critics aren't being subtle about it. Words like "dystopian" are already showing up in early takes, and honestly, that tracks. This isn't AI assisting creators — it's AI replacing them entirely, at scale, on a platform with millions of daily users.

When a company as mainstream as Roku treats AI slop as a legitimate content category, it doesn't just lower the bar. It buries it.
