🎵 A rapper got caught using AI to make his song — and the receipts are undeniable.

Fenix Flexin spent weeks denying that his '80s synth-pop track "Rubberz" was AI-generated. Then the evidence piled up. Producer Medasin posted a detailed breakdown, and Treblo — the AI music tool allegedly used to create it — released its own detector that flagged the track as its output.

At that point, denial stopped making sense. Flexin has now effectively dropped the fight.

This is one of the clearest cases we've seen of a signed artist getting caught using AI-generated music. And it opens up a question the industry has been dodging: should artists be required to disclose when AI made their music — and if so, who actually enforces that?
