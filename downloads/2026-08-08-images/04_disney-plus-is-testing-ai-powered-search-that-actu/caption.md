💡 Disney Plus just made searching for something to watch actually not terrible.

Disney is testing a natural language AI search tool for Disney Plus — and it's a real one. You can type what you're feeling, ask by voice, or pick from smart prompts, and it'll serve up a personalized row of recommendations that actually matches what you meant. No more scrolling for 20 minutes and giving up.

The key word here is *intent*. This isn't "because you watched Moana, here are 40 more animated movies." It's the platform parsing what you actually want — a chill movie, something scary but not too scary, a show your whole family won't hate. That's a meaningfully different product.

If it scales, this becomes the new baseline. Every streaming platform is about to feel the pressure to match it.
