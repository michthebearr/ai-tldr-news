🧠 OpenAI just built its own chip — and yes, they named it Jalapeño.

Meet Jalapeño, OpenAI's first custom AI processor, developed in partnership with Broadcom. It's a purpose-built ASIC designed specifically for inference — the part where a trained model actually runs and responds to your prompts. Not flashy in theory, but massive in practice.

Here's why this matters: inference is where the money gets spent at scale. Every time you send a message to ChatGPT, that's inference compute churning in the background. Right now, OpenAI is heavily dependent on Nvidia for that workload — and Nvidia charges accordingly. Custom silicon is how you break that dependency, cut costs, and control your own performance roadmap.

Google has TPUs. Amazon has Trainium. Meta has MTIA. OpenAI just joined the club.
