🧠 Samsung banned ChatGPT in 2023. Now they're rolling it out to every single employee.

Samsung Electronics is deploying ChatGPT Enterprise and OpenAI's Codex across all Korean staff and its entire Device eXperience division worldwide — the teams behind your Galaxy phones, TVs, and home appliances. That's a company-wide bet on AI productivity at one of the biggest electronics manufacturers on the planet.

The backstory makes this hit different. Samsung's 2023 ban wasn't symbolic — it came after employees accidentally leaked sensitive internal code by pasting it into ChatGPT. The fact that they've now flipped to full enterprise adoption says one thing clearly: the productivity gains got too big to ignore, and enterprise-grade controls gave leadership the cover they needed.

When the company that made ChatGPT famous for the *wrong* reasons becomes one of its biggest corporate customers, the era of "should we even allow this?" is officially over.
