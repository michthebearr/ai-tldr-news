💰 Menlo Ventures just raised $3 billion — and one Anthropic bet is the reason why.

Back in 2024, Menlo went all-in with a $750 million concentrated bet on Anthropic. That's not a diversified portfolio play. That's a firm putting its reputation on the table and sliding it across to a single model company.

It paid off spectacularly. Menlo went from respected-but-mid to must-watch AI investor almost overnight. Now they've closed a $3 billion Fund VII — and you can bet it's going straight back into AI infrastructure, applications, and whatever the next big unlock looks like.

The real lesson here isn't about Anthropic. It's about conviction. One bold, concentrated call on the right company can completely redefine a firm's trajectory.
