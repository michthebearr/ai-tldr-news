💰 Oracle is laying off 21,000 people to pay for its AI future.

The company just announced one of the biggest headcount cuts in enterprise tech this year — not because the business is failing, but because it needs the money. Billions are being redirected toward AI infrastructure and data center expansion, and apparently, 21,000 salaries are what's funding the bill.

Oracle is carrying serious debt to make this bet work. The logic: shed the people who built the old product era, pour capital into the infrastructure that powers the new one, and hope enterprise AI workloads grow fast enough to justify the pain. It's a high-stakes gamble dressed up as a pivot.

This is the trade-off every legacy tech giant is quietly staring down. AI investment sounds clean in a press release. It looks different when it's 21,000 humans on the other end of it.
