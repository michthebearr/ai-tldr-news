🤖 Humanoid robots are heading to Wall Street — and the entire sector is watching.

Agility Robotics just announced a SPAC deal that values the company at $2.5 billion, with $620 million in expected cash proceeds. That's a serious number for a company most people outside robotics circles have never heard of.

But Agility isn't hype. It's an Oregon State spinout with Amazon backing, a bipedal robot called Digit already deployed in real warehouses, and one of the more credible track records in a space drowning in vaporware. This isn't a demo company going public — it's an operational one.

Here's why it matters beyond Agility itself: humanoid robotics is currently flush with private capital and sky-high valuations with nothing to anchor them. A successful IPO sets a public benchmark. Every founder, investor, and competitor in the space will be pricing themselves against this number.

The robots are clocking in. Now they're filing with the SEC.
