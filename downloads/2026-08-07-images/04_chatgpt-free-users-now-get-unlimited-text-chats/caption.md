🧠 OpenAI just removed the message cap for free ChatGPT users — and that's a bigger deal than it sounds.

Free users can now have unlimited text conversations. No more hitting a wall and staring at a "you've reached your limit" screen. On top of that, free and Go users are getting access to a "think" button — a deeper reasoning mode that was previously locked behind a paid plan.

That last part matters. OpenAI isn't just being generous. They're handing a taste of their most powerful features to millions of users who've never paid a dollar. That's a growth play, not a charity move.

The competitive signal here is loud. With Google, Anthropic, and a dozen others fighting for the same users, OpenAI is betting that locking people out costs more than letting them in.
