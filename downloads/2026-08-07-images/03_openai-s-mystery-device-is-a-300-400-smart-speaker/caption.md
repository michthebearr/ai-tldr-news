🏎️ OpenAI's next big move isn't software — it's a $300–$400 smart speaker sitting on your kitchen counter.

New details have emerged on OpenAI's mystery hardware device, and it's shaping up to be a premium smart speaker priced right in the awkward middle of the market — above an Amazon Echo, below an Apple HomePod. Designed to bring ChatGPT directly into your home as a dedicated appliance.

Here's the problem: the smart speaker market has been a graveyard for ambition. Amazon and Google spent years trying to make these things culturally essential, and consumers mostly shrugged. Now OpenAI thinks it can crack the category by leading with AI — not just music playback and timers.

It's either a genius bet or an expensive lesson.
