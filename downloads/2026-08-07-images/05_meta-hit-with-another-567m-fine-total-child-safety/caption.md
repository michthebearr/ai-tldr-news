💰 Meta just got hit with another $567M fine — and the total bill for child safety failures is closing in on $1 billion.

A New Mexico court piled on top of the existing $375M fine in the state's landmark case, bringing the running total to $942 million. The court ruled Meta's platforms are a "significant contributing cause" of a teen mental health crisis — and classified them as a public nuisance.

That last part is the one to watch. "Public nuisance" isn't just a legal insult — it's a legal template. The same framework used to extract billions from opioid manufacturers is now being applied to a social media company. Other states are paying close attention.

Meta can absorb a billion dollars. What it can't easily absorb is 49 other states — or foreign regulators — lining up with the same playbook.
