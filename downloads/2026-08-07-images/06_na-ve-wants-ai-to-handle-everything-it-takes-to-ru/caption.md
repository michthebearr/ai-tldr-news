🏎️ A startup just raised $28.5M to make running a company as easy as prompting a chatbot.

Naïve wants to automate everything that isn't the actual product — entity formation, compliance, payroll, vendor setup. All the operational sludge that slows founders down before they've even written a line of code. Their pitch: vibe-coding was just the beginning. Welcome to vibe-operating.

The logic tracks. If AI can write your software, why is a founder still manually filing paperwork and chasing payroll vendors? Naïve is betting the back office is the next thing to collapse — and that whoever owns that infrastructure owns the foundation every new company is built on.

The flip side is real though. Lower barriers mean more companies. More companies means more noise, more failure, more zombie startups that technically exist but probably shouldn't. Democratizing entrepreneurship and flooding the market with half-baked ideas are the same move.
