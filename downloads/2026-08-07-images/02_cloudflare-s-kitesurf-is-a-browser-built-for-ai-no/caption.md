💡 Cloudflare just built a browser that wasn't made for you.

Kitesurf is a cloud-hosted browser designed specifically for AI agents to navigate the web — not humans. No tabs. No bookmarks. Just infrastructure that lets thousands of autonomous agents browse simultaneously without melting your compute bill.

The key detail: it uses significantly less compute than Chromium for automation tasks. That might sound like a footnote, but when you're running AI agents at scale, efficiency gaps compound fast. Cheaper browsing means more agents, running longer, doing more.

This is the unglamorous stuff that actually determines how powerful AI becomes. Not the flashy models — the plumbing underneath them.
