💡 Hank Green just said out loud what a lot of us are quietly thinking about AI.

The science YouTuber — someone with millions of followers and a generally tech-friendly worldview — posted a candid admission this week: his use of LLMs has gotten "not healthy." He described the dopamine loop of chatting with AI as something he's been leaning on too hard, and called it bad for both him and the world.

That's a big thing to say publicly. Especially when you're not a doomer. Especially when your audience trusts you to be level-headed about this stuff.

This isn't a fringe voice sounding the alarm. When a science communicator with real reach starts pumping the brakes on his own AI habits, it means the conversation about LLM dependency is leaving the think-piece circuit and entering the mainstream. The reckoning about *how* we use these tools — not just whether they're powerful — is finally starting.
