🎵 A Billboard Top 100 hit might be AI-generated — and the artist isn't saying either way.

Fenix Flexin, rapper from Shoreline Mafia, just climbed to number 58 on the Billboard Hot 100 with his solo track "Rubberz." The problem? Fans and critics took one look at the cover art — unmistakably AI-generated — and started asking harder questions about what they were actually listening to.

The track's sound has only fueled the suspicion. Flexin hasn't denied it, hasn't confirmed it, and hasn't said much of anything. That silence is doing a lot of talking.

This is where it gets messy for the industry. If AI-assisted music is quietly cracking mainstream charts with zero disclosure, the already-blurry lines around authenticity just got a lot blurrier. There's no rule that says you have to tell anyone. And that might be the whole problem.
