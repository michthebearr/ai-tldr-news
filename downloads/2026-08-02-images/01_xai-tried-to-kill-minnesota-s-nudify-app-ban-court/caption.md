💡 xAI tried to use the First Amendment to kill a deepfake nude ban — and a federal judge just said no.

Minnesota passed a law targeting apps that generate non-consensual nude images. xAI went to court to stop it, arguing the ban was an unconstitutional restriction on free speech. The judge wasn't moved.

The injunction was denied. The law stands. And xAI is now on the hook in a state that's decided it won't wait around for the AI industry to self-regulate its way out of a very real harm.

This is one of the first real courtroom stress tests of a strategy AI companies have been quietly banking on — that free speech protections could shield them from deepfake legislation. Round one goes to the regulators.
