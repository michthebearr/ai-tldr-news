💡 AI companies are finally offering to pay artists — but is a royalty check enough to fix years of betrayal?

A new wave of AI startups, including Pippa and Seedance, are testing a model where illustrators actually get paid when their work is used to train generative AI. It sounds like progress. And for some artists, cautiously, it might be.

But money doesn't automatically equal consent. Artists have spent years watching their styles, their livelihoods, and their creative identities get scraped without so much as a heads-up — let alone a contract. Some are willing to explore the royalty model. Others see it as the industry trying to buy its way out of an ethical mess it created on purpose.

Here's what actually matters: if royalty frameworks gain real traction, they could rewrite the rules on AI training data — settling lawsuits, setting precedent, and giving companies the legal cover they've desperately wanted. Whether that's a win for artists or just a more palatable form of the same power imbalance is the real question.
