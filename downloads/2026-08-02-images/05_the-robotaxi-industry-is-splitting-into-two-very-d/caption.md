🤖 The robotaxi industry just split in two — and only one side can be right.

Waymo and its camp are going all-in on expensive, sensor-heavy hardware locked to specific cities. Tesla and the newcomers are betting on cheap, vision-only systems that can scale fast — but come with a much longer list of unknowns. Same destination. Completely different bets on how to get there.

The deeper disagreement isn't really about cameras vs. LiDAR. It's a philosophical fight over when "good enough" actually means safe enough. Waymo says you nail it in a small area first, then expand. Tesla says you flood the market and let the data do the work. One approach is cautious and expensive. The other is bold and, critics argue, premature.

How this fork resolves will shape everything — whether self-driving cars become a premium urban service for a few cities, or a mass-market product that changes how everyone moves. The companies that bet wrong won't get a second chance.
