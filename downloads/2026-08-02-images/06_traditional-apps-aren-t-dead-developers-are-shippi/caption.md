🏎️ Everyone said apps were dead — developers didn't get the memo.

While the tech world obsesses over AI agents replacing every piece of software you use, a quiet countermovement is happening on the App Store. Bookmarking tools. Neighborhood marketplaces. Nature journals. Niche, carefully crafted apps are finding real audiences — people who want a focused experience, not another chatbot trying to do everything.

The irony is sharp: AI-generated slop is flooding the market so fast that craftsmanship has become a differentiator again. Developers leaning into quality are standing out precisely because the noise floor got so high.

The "apps are dead" thesis isn't just premature — it may be completely backwards.
