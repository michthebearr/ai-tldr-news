💡 Meta's new AI agent may have read a journalist's private Messages — and Meta says that's impossible. Someone's lying.

A reporter claimed that Muse, Meta's AI agent for Mac, accessed his private Messages without permission — even with the system-level toggle switched off. Meta pushed back hard, insisting Muse requires explicit user authorization before touching anything like that, and that no unauthorized access took place.

Both sides are standing firm. No receipts have settled it. And that's exactly the problem.

We're entering an era where AI agents can browse your files, read your messages, and take actions on your behalf — and the permissions layer governing all of that is still a mess. Whether Meta is totally clean here or not, the fact that a credible journalist couldn't tell what an AI agent was doing on his own machine should alarm everyone. Regulators are already circling.

Murky consent isn't a bug in the agentic AI era. It might be the whole business model.
