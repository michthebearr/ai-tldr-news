🏎️ ElevenLabs just doubled its valuation to $22B — and didn't even need a product launch to do it.

The AI voice startup closed a $300 million employee tender offer co-led by Wellington and T. Rowe Price, pushing its valuation from ~$11B to $22B overnight. No new feature drop. No revenue announcement. Just institutional money paying a premium to get a seat at the AI voice table.

That last part is the real story. When T. Rowe Price — a firm that manages retirement accounts for millions of Americans — is co-leading AI tender offers at these multiples, this isn't startup hype anymore. It's gone fully institutional.

ElevenLabs is now one of the most valuable pure-play AI companies on the planet. The bet is simple: whoever owns the voice layer of AI owns a lot of what comes next.
