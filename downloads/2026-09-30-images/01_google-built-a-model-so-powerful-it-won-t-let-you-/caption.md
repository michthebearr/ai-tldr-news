💡 Google built a model so powerful it won't let you near it.

Gemini 4 Argon is Google's newest frontier model — designed for elite software engineering, legal and finance work, and cybersecurity defense. And you can't have it. Access is currently restricted to a handpicked group of "trusted cyber defenders" while Google figures out how to responsibly release something this capable.

That's not a soft launch. That's a lab admitting, out loud, that their best model is too dangerous to just ship.

This might be the clearest signal yet that we're entering a new era of AI — one where the most powerful tools aren't withheld because they're unfinished, but because they're too finished. The gap between what labs can build and what the public can access is quietly becoming one of the most important fault lines in tech.
