💡 Consumer AI has a dirty secret — the math just doesn't work.

Frontier labs keep quietly retreating from consumer AI products, and TechCrunch finally explains why: the cost to run inference at scale for millions of everyday users is nowhere close to what those users are willing to pay. This isn't a "the models aren't good enough yet" problem. The models are fine. It's a margin problem — and subscriptions alone can't dig them out of it.

Think about that for a second. The most hyped technology in a generation might be economically unviable for regular people. When the unit economics are this brutal, the money chases the one customer who'll actually pay: enterprise. Corporations with deep pockets and measurable ROI requirements.

Which means the AI revolution might reshape Fortune 500 productivity dashboards long before it meaningfully changes anyone's actual life.
