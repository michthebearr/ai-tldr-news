🤖 Meta is training robots to replace the humans keeping its data centers alive.

Meta is quietly piloting humanoid and autonomous robots inside live data center facilities — handling hardware swaps, cable management, and routine inspections. Tasks that currently require trained technicians showing up in person. Real pilots. Real infrastructure. Not a concept video.

The play is obvious once you see it: as Meta scales its AI ambitions, data center operations become a massive cost and bottleneck. Robots don't call in sick, don't need benefits, and can work at 3am without complaint. If this works, it doesn't just save Meta money — it rewrites the economics of running AI infrastructure at scale.

And where Meta goes, the rest of Big Tech follows.
