🤖 China just held a robot boxing tournament — and it's way more serious than it sounds.

At the 2nd World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing, humanoid robots went head-to-head in structured athletic events, including full boxing routines. It looked like a sci-fi film. It wasn't. It was a calculated show of force from a country that's moving faster on physical AI than most people realize.

Chinese robotics firms are closing the gap with Boston Dynamics and Figure — and in some cases, they're already winning on deployment volume. While the West debates regulation and runs demo videos, China is scaling hardware in the real world, fast.

The US-China AI race was never just about chatbots. Whoever locks down humanoid robotics controls the next layer of physical automation — warehouses, factories, logistics, defense. Beijing knows it. That tournament wasn't entertainment. It was a message.
