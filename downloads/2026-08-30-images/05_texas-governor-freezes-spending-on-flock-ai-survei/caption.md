💡 Texas just pumped the brakes on $30 million worth of AI surveillance cameras — and the governor doing it is a Republican.

Greg Abbott has frozen state spending on Flock Safety's AI-powered license plate readers after a Texas Tribune investigation exposed how the program actually got funded: a $1 surcharge quietly tacked onto insurance policies, sold to the public as a way to fight catalytic converter theft.

The cameras have been quietly spreading across the state for years, sweeping up license plate data and sharing it with law enforcement — with very little public debate about how that data is stored, used, or who can access it.

Here's the shift that matters: when a Republican governor in Texas starts hitting pause on AI surveillance tech, this stops being a "civil liberties left" issue. It's gone mainstream.
