💡 Elon Musk wants to manufacture his own turbine blades — so he can burn more gas, faster, to feed his AI.

A secretive SpaceX foundry is reportedly being tapped to produce gas turbine components in-house, shaving 18 months off the timeline for bringing new power online at Musk's xAI data centers. In the AI arms race, that kind of speed advantage is enormous.

But here's what's not being advertised: gas turbines are already generating lawsuits and public health studies in the communities hosting them. Musk isn't just cutting a supply chain — he's doubling down on a power source with real, documented externalities and betting that no one will hold him accountable for it.

The AI industry's energy crisis is forcing trillion-dollar decisions that will play out in people's lungs and local water tables for decades. Those decisions are being made fast, quietly, and almost entirely without public input.

Speed is a feature. Accountability is not.
