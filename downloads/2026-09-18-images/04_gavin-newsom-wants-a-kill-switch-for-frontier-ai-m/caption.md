💡 California's governor just put a kill switch on the table for frontier AI — and every major lab should be paying attention.

Gavin Newsom has issued an executive order directing a panel of experts to deliver AI safety recommendations within two months. The explicit ask: figure out how to mandate a kill switch for frontier models in state law. This isn't a think piece. It's a governor with a deadline.

The timing is no accident. Anthropic's own researcher went public with a doomsday-level warning, and Dario Amodei followed up with his "pace the frontier" proposal. Newsom is reading the room — and responding with policy teeth.

Here's why this is a big deal: California regulating frontier AI is the closest thing the US has to federal oversight right now. A mandatory kill switch wouldn't just be symbolic — it would force OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and every other lab to fundamentally rethink how they deploy models. What California does next could set the template for the country.
