💡 Microsoft called AI scraping "the largest theft of labor in human history" — while doing exactly that.

Newly unredacted court filings from the New York Times lawsuit against Microsoft and OpenAI reveal that Microsoft executives used that phrase *internally* — even as the company was actively scraping paywalled content to build AI training datasets. Someone inside those Slack channels knew exactly what they were doing.

Internal warnings reportedly flagged that the practice would devastate publishers. Microsoft said one thing in public and another behind closed doors. That's not a PR problem. That's a paper trail.

These filings could fundamentally change how courts treat AI training data — and every major AI lab should be paying close attention right now.
