🏎️ Disney just hired the former CEO of a company it sued — and made him their first-ever CTO.

Karandeep Anand, who was running Character.AI until recently, is now Disney's inaugural Chief Technology Officer, reporting directly to CEO Josh D'Amaro. Let that sink in.

Not long ago, Disney sent Character.AI a cease-and-desist for reproducing its characters without authorization. Now the man at the helm of that company is responsible for Disney's entire technology infrastructure. The lawsuit threat didn't end the relationship — it apparently started a job negotiation.

This is what aggressive AI pivoting looks like in real time. Yesterday's adversary is today's architect.
