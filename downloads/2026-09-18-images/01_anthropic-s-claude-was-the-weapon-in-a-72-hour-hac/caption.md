🔐 Anthropic's Claude just broke into OpenAI's vault — and it only took 72 hours.

Security researchers at Hacktron used Claude Opus 4.8 and 5 to stage a full-scale attack on OpenAI's internal systems. We're talking hijacked employee accounts and unauthorized access to "Monorepo" — OpenAI's internal GitHub repository, reportedly home to its core algorithmic secrets.

The entire operation was done and disclosed in under three days. Think about that. The AI arms race just got a new category: using your rival's tools to rob them blind.

This is the most concrete proof yet that frontier AI models aren't just productivity software — they're already functioning as cyberweapons. And the fact that the victim was OpenAI makes this impossible to ignore.
