💡 Dario Amodei wants AI labs to coordinate on safety — but critics say it's less "saving the world" and more "pulling up the ladder."

Following a public warning from an Anthropic researcher about AI risk, Amodei has put forward a formal proposal: independent safety evaluators and coordination between labs in democratic nations. On paper, it sounds responsible. In practice, it's sparking a serious fight.

Jensen Huang pushed back publicly, and he's not alone. The uncomfortable question gaining traction: when the biggest players in AI start drafting the rules for AI, are they protecting humanity — or protecting their market position? Safety language is easy. Conflicts of interest are hard to see through.

How this debate lands will shape everything. Real oversight, or a cartel with better PR? The answer matters more than most people realize.
